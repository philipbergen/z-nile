arcade/.tracker/issues/active/f0203/acceptance/look.spec.luau
	the defaults are pickman's: 0.004 rad a pixel, 80 degrees of pitch, 0.12 s a quarter turn
	wrap keeps an angle in (-pi, pi]
	approach goes the short way round, by at most its step, and lands on the target
	the eased turn moves a quarter turn in turnSeconds, starts at the target, and is frame-rate independent
	the mouse turns right for a drag right and looks down for a drag down, at radiansPerPixel
	pitch stops at maxPitch either way
	turn adds radians (yaw left, pitch up) and wraps the yaw
	a mouse step or a turn that is not a number leaves the look as it was
	snap recentres, and the starting values are at rest
	press sets or clears one arrow and leaves the given arrows alone
	the arrows look in quarter turns from the heading, positive left, two together between
	held arrows turn the look toward them at the turn rate, level the pitch, and ignore other look input
	let go, the look springs back to the heading at the same rate and then rests
	any other look input during the spring takes over from where it left the look
	the arrows' look is frame-rate independent, and a dt that is not a positive number does not move it
	alpha is the share of a snapshot interval gone, clamped, and 1 when it is not a number
	blend goes between two values, and blendYaw goes the short way round
	tune fills from the defaults, freezes, and refuses a tuning that cannot work
arcade/.tracker/issues/active/f0203/acceptance/pad.spec.luau
	the defaults are pickman's accepted gamepad tuning (f0146)
	a push past the on tilt sends its direction once, and holds it
	a tilt short of the on tilt never engages
	an engaged stick stays engaged down to the off tilt, and a new push after release sends again
	each sector is the dominant axis, a tie going vertical (x right, y up)
	an engaged stick changes sector only past the diagonal by the margin
	an engaged stick turned past the margin while short of the on tilt keeps its sector
	a reading that is not a number releases the stick
	a tuning's on tilt and margin are the ones the fold uses
	a horizontal fold reads the x tilt alone: left and right, never up or down
	a horizontal fold never holds the side the stick is not on: short of on across the centre, it releases
	the look stick turns at its rate: full tilt right turns right, full tilt up looks up
	the look stick has a radial dead zone and a squared response past it
	the look is frame-rate independent: two half frames turn as far as one frame
	invert flips the pitch alone, and a tuning's rates and dead zone are the ones used
	a resting look stick turns nothing, even with no dead zone
	the look turns nothing for a dt or reading that is not a positive finite number
	the most recently used gamepad drives; drift below the off tilt and other devices never take over
	only Gamepad1 to Gamepad8 are gamepads
	tune fills from the defaults, freezes, and refuses a tuning that cannot work

arcade/.tracker/issues/active/f0203/acceptance/rng.spec.luau
	the default stream from seed 42 gives the minimal-standard sequence
	a stream's multiplier, mix and offset pick the sequence (pickman's ghosts, picks and blaster streams)
	the same seed and stream give the same sequence; another seed gives another
	int draws whole numbers in 1..n and reaches every one of them
	int refuses an n that is not a whole number of at least 1
	float is the advanced state scaled into [0, 1)
	draw hands out a function that draws from, and advances, the same generator
	a generator is plain data: a copy continues the same sequence
	a seed that is not a finite number counts as 0, and a fraction is floored
	a negative seed and a huge seed land in 1..MODULUS-1, exactly
	the state stays in 1..MODULUS-1 and whole on every stream
	new refuses a stream that is not whole or whose products could leave a double's exact range
arcade/.tracker/issues/active/f0203/acceptance/touch.spec.luau
	its sizes are pickman's: a 48 px swipe, a 30 degree cone, a 44 px target
	a finger on a button presses it when it goes down and releases it when it comes up
	buttons are half-open rectangles, checked in the context's order
	a swipe on the swipe half sends its direction once, on release; screen-down is down
	a swipe counts from finger-down to finger-up, whatever the path between
	a swipe shorter than MIN_SWIPE or outside the cone round an axis sends nothing
	a finger on the look half sends its pixel steps as it moves, and nothing when it lifts
	each half's use comes from the context, and a half used for nothing takes no finger
	the halves split at width / 2, and a width that is not a positive number makes every point the right half
	one finger holds a zone: a second finger on the same button or half is ignored, down and up
	fingers on different zones work at once
	a begin for a finger already down, and a move or end for one never seen, change nothing
	the given state is never changed
	a begin or move at a point that is not a number is dropped; an end always lifts its finger
	a finger keeps the zone it went down in, so its lift releases even after the buttons change
	reset lifts every finger: a release for each held button in finger order, and no swipe
	toScreen moves an input position by the inset's origin, and screenSize falls back while the area is bad
