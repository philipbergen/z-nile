# d0405: Busy read keeps pass

## Problem
- A busy answer from `Wallet` (`arcade/src/server/Wallet.luau`) always clears the pass allowance, so a PLAY that is still busy after the portal's 10-second wait (`Play.CHECK_SECONDS`) writes no pass, and a player with nothing to pay with is teleported to the game place and sent back without being offered the purchase.

## Non-goals
- A busy answer given while the read in progress is not late (its store call has run for less than `Wallet.WAIT_SECONDS`, 5 seconds) still clears the pass allowance as today, so `arcade/tests/place/wallet-order.spec.luau:291` and `arcade/tests/acceptance/f0201/wallet.spec.luau:1029` keep passing.

## Solution
- A busy answer keeps the pass allowance when the read in progress for that player and title is late, meaning its store call was not back within `Wallet.WAIT_SECONDS` (5 seconds, the Wallet's own late flag), so a PLAY pressed during a slow store call ends with a pass written or the purchase offered.
