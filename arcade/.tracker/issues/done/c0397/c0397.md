# c0397: Narrow the test screen line pins for f0395

## Problem
- Once f0201 is done and its specs are promoted to `arcade/tests/acceptance/f0201/`, f0395 turns three of their cases red: the Prices case in `config.spec.luau` (an exact `toEqual` on three fields, where f0395 adds a fourth, `revive`), the test-screen-lines case in `config.spec.luau` (the literal play price line `"5 R$ 1 R$ SALE"`), and the test-mode ask case in `receipts.spec.luau` (the literal revive lines).

## Non-goals
- No file under `arcade/src` changes.
- f0201's `client.spec` and `check.sh` do not change (the check's labels still hold).
- No assertion on the struck price line itself is added, because those pins belong to f0395's own specs.

## Solution
- The Prices case reads `Prices.struck`, `Prices.price` and `Prices.sale` one by one with `toBe` in place of the whole-table `toEqual`, and keeps its other assertions.
- The test-screen-lines case stops pinning the price line and instead pins, for `PaidPlay.testLines("SNAKE", "play", Prices)`, the line count (3), line 1 (`"SNAKE: 1 GAME"`) and line 3 (`"TEST PURCHASE: NOTHING IS CHARGED"`), and keeps its revive title assertion.
- The test-mode ask case compares what Receipts sends with `{ "open", PaidPlay.testLines("INVADERS", "revive", Prices) }` in place of the literal lines, and keeps its other assertions.
- All three narrowed cases pass on main both before and after f0395's change, and no other case in either file changes.
- The issue overrides `arcade/tests/acceptance/f0201/config.spec.luau` and `receipts.spec.luau`, starts only once f0201 is done, is done before f0395's raft starts, and takes `arcade/.tracker/issues/f0395/experiments/c0397-narrowing.patch` as its starting patch.
