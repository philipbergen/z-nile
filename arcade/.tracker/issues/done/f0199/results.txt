arcade/.tracker/issues/active/f0199/acceptance/travel.spec.luau
	the defaults: 15 s after the engine call returns, 3 s of cool-down, a 60 s cap on a call that never returns
	a destination is a whole place id from 1 up, as a number or as digits
	anything else is not a destination: 0, negatives, fractions, NaN, too large, text, other types
	an idle player may go, and is then busy: one request at a time
	while the engine call has not returned the player stays going, past 15 s, up to the 60 s cap
	once the engine call has returned, nothing happening for 15 s is a failure
	an answer that comes late never pushes the timeout past the cap
	an answer for a player who is not going changes nothing
	a reported failure cools down for 3 s from the report, then the player may go again
	a failure nobody asked for changes nothing
	a late failure changes nothing: it does not extend a cool-down or cool down a newer request
	a go comes with a ticket that names the request; the other answers have none
	a report for an older request changes nothing: the newer one keeps its own wait
	a report with no ticket (the engine's init failure carries none) applies to the current request
	a player who leaves is forgotten
	players are separate
	takes its times from the config it is given
	digits beyond the largest place id are not a destination, even where tonumber rounds them
	a timed-out request cools down from the moment the timeout is seen
