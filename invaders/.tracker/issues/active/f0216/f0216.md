# f0216: Invaders classic rules

## Problem
The user, 2026-10-31: "I want to branch out into multiple games, they will each have their own directory in the project: first person snake, first person asteroids. I also want a new project for a landing portal where you can scroll between the games and see the highscores for each, the same cost model remains but it shoudl show 5 robux for each game witha slash through showing it is currently on sale for 1 robux. highscores shoudl show how many revives were used. I want you to design a full fun playable game for both of those two new game ideas. I also want both snake and asteroids to be a two player game, snakes can even be n-player up to a high number just make the wold bigger. Snakes should have maps that once you have reached a certain length the game ends and we move to a new map, the first is empty, then one wall somewhere in the middle, then two, then three and so on. The walls can be vertical and horizontal but do not connect. for asteroids we could have a two player version where each player is attacking the aliens from opposing sides. You can shoot each other's barriers. the aliens would then come in from the side and and split into two groups that move toward either player". Then: "Add a third game first person asteroids." Decided with the user (2026-10-31): the 2-player aliens game is pure Space Invaders (`invaders`); Asteroids is its own game (`asteroids`); one Roblox experience with the portal as start place, replacing pickman's lobby; every board shows revives used; one free game per hour per game.

The approved design is `invaders/.tracker/game-design.md`, section **Game design: Invaders (`invaders/`)** (c0183). This issue owns these parts of it, as they are said there: **The field and view** ("The rules are 2-D") and **Solo (the classic)** (the revive's restart of the wave as a rule; not the offer). The rest of it belongs to other issues (c0190's ownership table), listed under Non-goals.

What should happen:
- Solo Space Invaders in pure Logic: the 5 × 11 formation and its march, shots (one for the player, up to three for the aliens), four erodible barriers, the mystery ship, lives with the extra at 1,500, invasion as game over, and the waves.

## Non-goals
- Two players: f0217.
- The view, input, the place, and how the rules look and sound (the march's beat): f0218.
- Boards, the revive offer and the portal return: f0219.
- The rest of `invaders/.tracker/game-design.md`: f0208, f0217, f0218, f0219.
- Not asked for by the capture, and left out (prep): shots never collide with each other (the arcade's shot-on-bomb explosions); no high-score table in Logic (f0219's boards); no pause or "ready" countdown at the start of a run (the place's arrival is f0218's).

## Solution
Four pure Logic features in `invaders/src/shared/Logic/`: `Formation` (the 5 × 11 aliens and their march), `Barriers` (the four 8 × 6 bunkers), `Mystery` (the ship across the far end) and `Classic`, one player's run as a state machine that composes the three and owns the cannon, the shots, lives, score, waves, invasion and the revive's restart. A run is plain data, stepped once per tick at 60 ticks a second with the player's input (`{ move, fire }`), and returns the events of that tick; the field is 2-D in whole field units (`x` across, `d` the depth from the base line). Every number is a frozen data table. f0218's server adapter steps it and replicates it, f0219 makes the revive offer and calls `Classic.revive`, and f0217 builds the two-player game from the same three leaves.

## Design

### Product decisions this relies on
Quoted from `invaders/.tracker/game-design.md`, **Game design: Invaders (`invaders/`)**:
- Pillar (2): "Deterministic Logic."
- The field and view: "The rules are 2-D. The classic vertical axis becomes depth: you stand on the ground at your base line looking down a long field, and the formation hovers ahead and advances toward you."
- Solo: "**Formation:** 5 rows × 11 aliens. The back row is worth 30, the middle two 20, the front two 10."
- Solo: "**March:** the formation steps sideways. When it touches a side it advances one row toward you and reverses. It speeds up as it thins, and the beat sound speeds up with it." (The beat's sound is f0218's; the march emits the event it plays from.)
- Solo: "**Shots:** you have one shot on the field at a time. Aliens have up to 3 at a time: one aimed at your column, the others from random columns."
- Solo: "**Barriers:** 4 bunkers between you and the formation. Each is a grid of blocks (8×6) that any shot erodes from either side, and that aliens marching through erase."
- Solo: "**Mystery ship:** crosses the far end about every 25 s, worth 50, 100, 150 or 300."
- Solo: "**Lives:** 3, with one extra at 1,500 points. A hit costs a life."
- Solo: "**Invasion:** aliens reaching your base line is **game over at once** (as in the arcade). A revive gives 3 lives and restarts that wave from its starting depth." (This issue owns the restart as a rule, `Classic.revive`; the offer is f0219's.)
- Solo: "**Waves:** each cleared wave starts the next formation one row closer, as in the arcade, cycling after 8."

Prep choices (marked "(prep)" where they appear below; each is a tuning or a reading the capture leaves open, none changes a decision above, and each costs one data-table edit to change): the field and box sizes; "one row" (each advance, and each wave's start one row closer) read as the arcade's drop of 8 units (`rowStep`), half the 16-unit pitch between alien rows; 60 ticks a second; the march's wait of one tick per living alien and its 2-unit step (the arcade's own rate); the alien reload table and the mystery value table (the arcade's); the mystery ship needs 8 aliens alive; the erosion pattern; 2 s pauses after a hit and a cleared wave; the cannon respawning at the centre; one extra life a run, which a revive does not re-arm; "restarts that wave" read as the wave's own start (a full formation at its starting depth, fresh barriers), not the survivors moved back up; and every wave start, the revive's included, resetting the same list (The wave's start, below).

### The field (prep)
Whole field units, so every rule is exact integer arithmetic and deterministic:
- `x` across, `0 ≤ x < 224`; `d` depth from the base line (`d = 0`) to the far end (`d = 232`). Boxes are half-open, `[x0, x1) × [d0, d1)`. Shots are points.
- The cannon: centre `x` in `[6, 217]` (half-width 6, so 13 units wide), depth `[0, 8)`, 1 unit a tick (the field's width in about 3.5 s). It starts, and respawns, at `x = 112`.
- The formation: aliens 12 × 8, 16 apart across and 16 apart in depth; 5 rows × 11 columns, 172 units wide, centred (`x = 26`). Row 1 is the front row (nearest the cannon), column 1 the leftmost. Wave 1's front row starts at `d = 120`.
- The bunkers: four, 8 × 6 blocks of 3 × 3, left edges `x = 28, 76, 124, 172`, near edge `d = 24` (depth `[24, 42)`). Rows count from the near side.
- The mystery ship: 16 × 7, its lane at `d = [216, 223)`, beyond the formation's back row at any wave start (184 + 8 = 192).
- The view (f0218) maps `x` and `d` to the world; Logic knows nothing of studs.

### `Formation` (`src/shared/Logic/Formation/init.luau`), a leaf
```lua
export type Config = { rows: number, cols: number, colSpacing: number, rowSpacing: number, width: number, depth: number,
	stepX: number, rowStep: number, margin: number, fieldWidth: number, ticksPerAlien: number, minInterval: number, points: { number } }
export type Formation = { config: Config, x: number, d: number, dir: number, elapsed: number, alive: { boolean }, live: number }
export type Move = "step" | "advance"
Formation.CLASSIC: Config -- rows 5, cols 11, colSpacing 16, rowSpacing 16, width 12, depth 8, stepX 2, rowStep 8, margin 8,
	-- fieldWidth 224, ticksPerAlien 1, minInterval 1, points { 10, 10, 20, 20, 30 } (front row first)
function Formation.new(config: Config, d: number): Formation -- full, centred across, front row's near edge at d, marching right
function Formation.tick(f: Formation): Move? -- one tick of the march
function Formation.interval(f: Formation): number -- max(minInterval, live * ticksPerAlien)
function Formation.isAlive(f: Formation, row: number, col: number): boolean -- false outside the grid
function Formation.box(f: Formation, row: number, col: number): (number, number, number, number) -- x0, d0, x1, d1
function Formation.extent(f: Formation): (number?, number?, number?) -- living left, right (exclusive), front near edge; nil when empty
function Formation.hitAt(f: Formation, x: number, d: number): (number?, number?) -- the living alien whose box holds the point
function Formation.kill(f: Formation, row: number, col: number): number -- its points, 0 when not alive (and nothing changes)
function Formation.front(f: Formation, col: number): number? -- the front-most living row of a column
function Formation.columns(f: Formation): { number } -- the columns with a living alien, left to right
```
- `alive[(row - 1) * cols + col]`; `live` is the count. `x` is column 1's left edge and `d` row 1's near edge, whether or not they are alive.
- **The march.** `tick` adds one to `elapsed` and does nothing until `elapsed ≥ interval(f)`. The wait is read from the live count on every tick, never fixed when it starts, so a kill shortens the wait already running ("It speeds up as it thins"). With 55 alive a step comes every 55 ticks (0.92 s), with 1 alive every tick. When it is due, `elapsed` goes back to 0 and:
  - if one more step of `stepX` in `dir` would take the **living** extent past a margin (`left < margin` or `right > fieldWidth - margin`), the formation **advances**: `d -= rowStep` and `dir = -dir`, with no sideways move that tick, and `tick` returns `"advance"`;
  - otherwise `x += dir * stepX` and it returns `"step"`.
  - The full wave-1 formation makes 9 steps right before its first advance; with its two right columns dead it makes 25.
- An empty formation never moves (`tick` returns nil) and has no extent.

### `Barriers` (`src/shared/Logic/Barriers/init.luau`), a leaf
```lua
export type Layout = { lefts: { number }, near: number, cols: number, rows: number, block: number }
export type Barriers = { layout: Layout, blocks: { { boolean } } } -- blocks[bunker][(row - 1) * cols + col]
Barriers.CLASSIC: Layout -- lefts { 28, 76, 124, 172 }, near 24, cols 8, rows 6, block 3
Barriers.EROSION -- { { 0, 0 }, { -1, 0 }, { 1, 0 }, { 0, 1 } }: { column offset, row offset along the shot's travel } (prep)
function Barriers.new(layout: Layout): Barriers -- every block present
function Barriers.present(b: Barriers, bunker: number, col: number, row: number): boolean -- false outside the grid
function Barriers.blockAt(b: Barriers, x: number, d: number): (number?, number?, number?) -- bunker, col, row of the present block holding the point
function Barriers.erode(b: Barriers, bunker: number, col: number, row: number, travel: number): number -- blocks removed
function Barriers.eraseBox(b: Barriers, x0: number, d0: number, x1: number, d1: number): number -- blocks removed
function Barriers.count(b: Barriers, bunker: number?): number -- present blocks, in one bunker or all
```
- **Erosion from either side.** A shot that reaches a present block removes the `EROSION` pattern around it: the block, its neighbours left and right, and the block one further along the shot's travel (`travel = 1` for the cannon's shot, which moves away from the base line, so "further" is the next row back; `-1` for an alien's, so it is the next row forward). Blocks already gone, or outside the bunker, are skipped. A cannon shot therefore digs in from the near side and an alien's from the far side.
- **Aliens erase.** `eraseBox` removes every present block whose square overlaps the box (a box that only touches a block's edge does not).

### `Mystery` (`src/shared/Logic/Mystery/init.luau`), a leaf
```lua
export type Config = { every: number, speed: number, width: number, depth: number, lane: number, fieldWidth: number, minAliens: number, values: { number } }
export type Mystery = { config: Config, x: number?, dir: number, nextDir: number, elapsed: number }
export type Change = "appear" | "leave"
Mystery.CLASSIC: Config -- every 1500 (25 s), speed 1, width 16, depth 7, lane 216, fieldWidth 224, minAliens 8,
	-- values { 100, 50, 50, 100, 150, 100, 100, 50, 300, 100, 100, 100, 50, 150, 100 }
function Mystery.new(config: Config): Mystery -- no ship; the first comes from the left
function Mystery.tick(m: Mystery, live: number): Change? -- one playing tick with `live` aliens alive
function Mystery.hitAt(m: Mystery, x: number, d: number): boolean
function Mystery.value(m: Mystery, shots: number): number -- values[shots % #values + 1]
function Mystery.remove(m: Mystery) -- hit: no ship, the wait starts again
```
- With no ship, each tick adds one to `elapsed`; at `elapsed ≥ every` with `live ≥ minAliens` (prep) a ship appears just off the side it comes from (`x = -width` heading right, or `x = fieldWidth` heading left), the sides alternate starting from the left, and `elapsed` goes back to 0. Fewer than 8 alive: it waits, and appears on the first tick 8 are alive again. A ship moves `speed` a tick and leaves once wholly off the far side (240 ticks for a crossing).
- **Its value** (prep, the arcade's table): indexed by the player's shot count including the shot that hits it, so the 23rd shot, and every 15th after it, scores 300; every value is 50, 100, 150 or 300.
- The 25 s counts **playing** ticks only (Classic ticks it only while playing), from the tick after the ship left or was hit (a hit's tick does not count, as a leave's does not), and from the start of the wave. Each wave's start makes a new `Mystery` (prep): no ship, the wait at 0, the first ship from the left again.

### `Classic` (`src/shared/Logic/Classic/init.luau`, `Classic/Rng.luau`)
```lua
export type Phase = "playing" | "dying" | "waveclear" | "gameover"
export type Reason = "lives" | "invasion"
export type Shot = { x: number, d: number }
export type Bomb = { x: number, d: number, slot: number } -- slot 1 is the aimed shot, 2 and 3 the random ones
export type Input = { move: number?, fire: boolean? }
export type Event = { kind: string, points: number?, row: number?, col: number?, slot: number?, lives: number?, wave: number?, reason: Reason?, interval: number? }
export type Config = { tickRate, fieldWidth, fieldDepth, lives, reviveLives, extraLifeScore, cannonHalf, cannonDepth, cannonSpeed, cannonStart,
	shotSpeed, bombSpeed, reload: { { score: number, ticks: number } }, firstDepth, waveCycle, invasionDepth, dyingTicks, waveClearTicks,
	formation: Formation.Config, barriers: Barriers.Layout, mystery: Mystery.Config } -- all numbers unless named
export type Game = { config: Config, phase: Phase, timer: number, reason: Reason?, wave: number, score: number, lives: number, revives: number,
	extraLifeGiven: boolean, shotsFired: number, cannon: number, shot: Shot?, bombs: { Bomb }, reloadElapsed: number, nextSlot: number, rng: number,
	formation: Formation.Formation, barriers: Barriers.Barriers, mystery: Mystery.Mystery }
Classic.TICK_RATE = 60
Classic.CONFIG: Config -- tickRate 60, fieldWidth 224, fieldDepth 232, lives 3, reviveLives 3, extraLifeScore 1500, cannonHalf 6,
	-- cannonDepth 8, cannonSpeed 1, cannonStart 112, shotSpeed 4, bombSpeed 1,
	-- reload { {0, 48}, {200, 16}, {1000, 11}, {2000, 8}, {3000, 7} }, firstDepth 120, waveCycle 8, invasionDepth 8,
	-- dyingTicks 120, waveClearTicks 120, and the three leaves' CLASSIC tables
function Classic.new(seed: number): Game -- wave 1, 3 lives, playing
function Classic.step(g: Game, input: Input?): { Event } -- one tick; the events in the order they happened
function Classic.revive(g: Game): boolean -- false unless at game over
function Classic.waveDepth(wave: number): number -- firstDepth - rowStep * ((wave - 1) % waveCycle)
function Classic.reloadTicks(score: number): number -- the last reload row whose score the score has reached
```
- `Rng.luau` is a Park-Miller generator (as pickman's `Ghosts/Rng.luau`), its state one number in `Game.rng`, seeded from `seed` floored; a seed that is NaN or infinite counts as 0. The arcade library's Rng (arcade/.tracker/architecture.md, step 4) does not exist yet; when it does, an adapter can pass a seed from it, and Logic still never requires arcade Logic (invaders-arch, change 1).
- **Input** is sanitized in Logic, so a client can't lie through the adapter: `move` counts only when it is exactly `-1` or `1` (anything else is 0); `fire` only when it is exactly `true`. A missing input is no move and no fire.
- **One tick while playing, in this order** (the order is a rule; each case below pins its part):
  1. The cannon moves `move * cannonSpeed`, clamped to `[cannonHalf, fieldWidth - 1 - cannonHalf]`.
  2. A fire with no shot on the field starts one at the cannon's `x`, `d = cannonDepth - 1`, and counts it (`shotsFired`, event `PlayerFire`). A fire while the shot flies is ignored (not queued).
  3. The shot moves `shotSpeed` units, **one unit at a time**, and at each unit: past the far end (`d ≥ fieldDepth`) it is gone; on a present block it erodes it (`travel = 1`, event `BarrierHit` with no `slot`) and is gone; in a living alien's box it kills it (event `AlienHit` with row, col and points, then the score) and is gone; in the ship's box it scores the ship's value (event `MysteryHit`), the ship is gone and the shot too. So no shot ever jumps a 3-unit block, however fast.
     - The last alien killed clears the wave: phase `waveclear` for `waveClearTicks`, every shot and the ship removed, event `WaveClear`; the rest of the tick is skipped.
  4. Each alien shot moves `bombSpeed` units, one at a time, and at each unit: below the base line (`d < 0`) it is gone; on a present block it erodes it (`travel = -1`, event `BarrierHit` with the bomb's `slot`) and is gone; inside the cannon (`d < cannonDepth` and `|x - cannon| ≤ cannonHalf`, judged where the cannon stands after step 1) it **hits**.
     - A hit costs a life (event `CannonHit` with the lives left), removes every shot, and pauses the field: phase `dying` for `dyingTicks`. The last life lost is game over instead (`reason = "lives"`, event `GameOver`). The rest of the tick is skipped: no other bomb is moved, the formation does not move (so it cannot invade that tick) and no alien fires. So two bombs reaching the cannon on one tick cost one life.
  5. The formation ticks. A step or an advance erases every block a living alien's box overlaps, and emits `March` or `Advance` (with the new interval, for f0218's beat). After an advance, if the living front row's near edge is at or below `invasionDepth` (the cannon's far edge), it is **game over at once** whatever the lives (`reason = "invasion"`, event `GameOver`).
  6. The aliens fire: `reloadElapsed` counts up, and once it reaches `reloadTicks(score)` (read from the live score) and a slot is free, the next slot in turn fires (the turn goes 1, 2, 3, 1, …, and a slot whose shot is still out gives its turn to the next free one): slot 1 from the living column whose centre is nearest the cannon (the lower column on a tie), slots 2 and 3 from a living column the generator picks. The shot starts at that column's front-most living alien's near edge, at its centre `x`; event `AlienFire` with the slot and column; `reloadElapsed` goes back to 0. With all three out it waits.
  7. The ship ticks (`MysteryAppear`, `MysteryLeave`), unless it was hit this tick.
- **Pauses.** In `dying` and `waveclear` nothing moves and input is ignored; each tick takes one from `timer`, and the tick it reaches 0 ends the pause and does nothing else. `dying` ends with the cannon back at `cannonStart` (event `Respawn` with the lives left); `waveclear` ends with the next wave: `wave + 1`, then the wave's start (below), event `WaveStart` with the new wave.
- **The wave's start** (one function, used by the next wave and by the revive alike): `formation = Formation.new(formation config, waveDepth(wave))` (full, centred, marching right, its wait at 0); `barriers = Barriers.new(...)` (every block); `mystery = Mystery.new(...)` (no ship, the wait at 0, the next from the left); `shot = nil`; `bombs = {}`; `reloadElapsed = 0`; `nextSlot = 1` (the aimed slot's turn); `cannon = cannonStart`; `phase = "playing"`; `timer = 0`. Nothing else changes: `wave`, `score`, `lives`, `revives`, `extraLifeGiven`, `shotsFired`, `rng` and `reason` are each set, or kept, by the caller.
- **Score and the extra life.** Points are added as they are won; the first time the score reaches 1,500 the player gains a life (event `ExtraLife`, after the hit that won it), once a run.
- **Waves.** `waveDepth`: 120, 112, …, 64 for waves 1 to 8, then 120 again for wave 9 and so on; every start is more than one advance above the base line.
- **Game over** is final until a revive: `step` returns no events and changes nothing.
- **The revive** (`Classic.revive`): only in `gameover` (otherwise false and nothing changes). It sets 3 lives, counts the revive (`revives`, for f0219's boards), clears `reason`, and runs the wave's start for the current wave. Score, wave, `shotsFired` and `extraLifeGiven` stay. It works the same after either reason.
- **The events** (the one channel f0218 renders and sounds from; every field not listed is nil):

  | kind | when | fields |
  |---|---|---|
  | `PlayerFire` | step 2, a shot starts | none |
  | `BarrierHit` | step 3 or 4, a shot erodes a bunker | `slot`: nil for the cannon's shot, 1 to 3 for an alien's |
  | `AlienHit` | step 3, the cannon's shot kills an alien | `row`, `col`, `points` |
  | `MysteryHit` | step 3, the cannon's shot hits the ship | `points` |
  | `ExtraLife` | after the `AlienHit` or `MysteryHit` that reached 1,500 | `lives` (after the gain) |
  | `WaveClear` | step 3, the last alien killed | `wave` (the cleared one) |
  | `CannonHit` | step 4 | `lives` (left) |
  | `GameOver` | step 4 on the last life, or step 5 on an invasion | `reason` |
  | `March` | step 5, a sideways step | `interval` (the wait now, for the beat) |
  | `Advance` | step 5, an advance | `interval` |
  | `AlienFire` | step 6 | `slot`, `col` |
  | `MysteryAppear` | step 7 | none |
  | `MysteryLeave` | step 7 | none |
  | `Respawn` | the tick `dying` ends | `lives` |
  | `WaveStart` | the tick `waveclear` ends | `wave` (the new one) |

### The split and server authority
- Everything above is pure Logic (pickman-arch rule 2): no engine words (the meta spec also bans the word `game` in Logic files, so comments say "run"; `gameover` and `Game` are allowed), time is the tick count, randomness is the seeded state, input is the `Input` value.
- f0218's server adapter owns a `Game` per player, steps it at `TICK_RATE` from an accumulator (fixed ticks, so the rules don't depend on the frame rate), feeds it the player's latest held direction and a fire on the tick a fire intent arrived, and replicates it. The client only sends intents and renders. Score, lives, revives and every collision are the server's (rule 7).
- f0219 calls `Classic.revive(g)` after the purchase, and reads `score`, `wave` and `revives` for the boards.

### Edge cases
- Simultaneous events in one tick follow the order above: a shot that clears the wave ends the tick before any alien shot can hit (the clear wins, so the field can never be left with no aliens and no clear); a hit on the cannon ends the tick before the formation moves or an alien fires (no invasion that tick, no new shot after "removes every shot"); two bombs on the cannon in one tick cost one life; an extra life comes after the kill (or the ship) that earned it.
- Bounds: the cannon is clamped; a shot is gone past either end; `Formation.kill`, `isAlive` and `Barriers.present` are false outside their grids; `hitAt` is nil in the gaps between aliens.
- Empty input: nil, a non-table, or junk values are no move and no fire.
- An empty formation (the moment of the clear) never ticks or fires.
- A player who leaves: f0218 drops their `Game`; Logic holds nothing else. A respawn is the `dying` pause.
- Frame-rate independence: Logic counts ticks only (`TICK_RATE` 60); the adapter's accumulator turns seconds into ticks.
- Determinism: the same seed and inputs give the same run tick for tick; the state and events are JSON-safe (dense arrays, finite numbers) so f0218 can replicate them and a failure can be replayed.

### Scalability
- Per tick: the cannon O(1); the shot ≤ 4 unit checks, each O(bunkers) for blocks plus O(1) for the alien cell; ≤ 3 bombs, 1 unit each; the formation O(1) on a waiting tick and, on a step, O(55) for the extent plus O(55 × 4 × 48) for the erase (only on step ticks, at most one tick in 55 at full strength). Prep's bench (E3): 0.8 µs a step on average, 0.005 % of one core per run at 60 Hz; 2 players (f0217) or a full server of solo runs stay far inside the budget.
- The state is about 55 + 192 booleans, ≤ 4 shots and a dozen numbers: f0218 decides what to replicate (the full grids on change, positions per tick).

### Maintainability
- Every number is a data table: `Formation.CLASSIC`, `Barriers.CLASSIC` and `EROSION`, `Mystery.CLASSIC` (with its value table), `Classic.CONFIG` (with its reload table and wave depths); a new wave pattern, a bunker layout or a tuning is a table edit.
- f0217 builds the two-player rules on the three leaves without changing the solo tables. The leaves know one heading (a formation advances toward `d = 0`, a cannon shot travels `+d`), so f0217 has two ways to use them, its choice: run each group in its own mirrored frame (`d' = fieldDepth - d` for the north group, the erosion `travel` flipped for the far bunkers), or add a heading to `Formation` and `Barriers` whose default is today's. The promoted specs accept the second: E2's correct variants include a new field on each leaf and a second parameter to `Classic.new`, and every spec stayed green (CV2 only proves the specs tolerate a new field; a heading that is used is f0217's to prove).
- The events are the one channel f0218 renders and sounds from, so a new effect needs no new state.

### Usability
- For the player: the arcade's rules and pace (the march's 2 units per living alien tick, the reload table), readable at a glance: one shot of yours, at most three of theirs, one of them always coming at your column; nothing in Logic hides a rule the view can't show.
- For the developer: four small modules, typed, each with one `CLASSIC` table; `Classic.step(g, input)` is the whole loop and returns what happened; the state can be set directly in a spec. Invalid input can't move or fire the cannon.
