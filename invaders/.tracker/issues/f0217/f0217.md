# f0217: Invaders two players

## Problem
The user, 2026-10-31: "I want to branch out into multiple games, they will each have their own directory in the project: first person snake, first person asteroids. I also want a new project for a landing portal where you can scroll between the games and see the highscores for each, the same cost model remains but it shoudl show 5 robux for each game witha slash through showing it is currently on sale for 1 robux. highscores shoudl show how many revives were used. I want you to design a full fun playable game for both of those two new game ideas. I also want both snake and asteroids to be a two player game, snakes can even be n-player up to a high number just make the wold bigger. Snakes should have maps that once you have reached a certain length the game ends and we move to a new map, the first is empty, then one wall somewhere in the middle, then two, then three and so on. The walls can be vertical and horizontal but do not connect. for asteroids we could have a two player version where each player is attacking the aliens from opposing sides. You can shoot each other's barriers. the aliens would then come in from the side and and split into two groups that move toward either player". Then: "Add a third game first person asteroids." Decided with the user (2026-10-31): the 2-player aliens game is pure Space Invaders (`invaders`); Asteroids is its own game (`asteroids`); one Roblox experience with the portal as start place, replacing pickman's lobby; every board shows revives used; one free game per hour per game.

The approved design is `invaders/.tracker/game-design.md`, section **Game design: Invaders (`invaders/`)** (c0183). This issue owns these parts of it, as they are said there: **2 players (the user's design)**, as rules (not "Pairing"). The rest of it belongs to other issues (c0190's ownership table), listed under Non-goals.

What should happen:
- Two players at opposite ends of a field twice as deep; **each wave flies in from the side and splits into two groups**, one toward each player; **a shot crosses the whole field and can hit the other player's barriers** (not the player, by default); own scores, lives and revives; a lone survivor plays on.
- Pure Logic: the place that runs it, and the party's arrival, are f0218's.

## Non-goals
- The solo rules: f0216.
- The place, the party's arrival and E5: f0218.
- The portal's waiting card and teleport: f0208.
- The rest of `invaders/.tracker/game-design.md`: f0208, f0216, f0218, f0219.
- Left to their owners, as the capture splits them: the fly-off's animation and every look and sound of the fly-in (f0218's view, from the state and events below); the revive offer and when it ends (f0219, which calls `TwoPlayer.revive`, and `TwoPlayer.retire` when the offer ends unbought); a player leaving the server (f0218's place calls `TwoPlayer.retire`).
- Not asked for by the capture, and left out (prep): shots never collide with each other (as in f0216); no pause or countdown before a wave's fly-in beyond the wave pause; no choice of who plays which end (player 1 is the first of the pair f0218 seats).

## Solution
A new pure Logic feature, `invaders/src/shared/Logic/TwoPlayer/`, runs the two-player match on the solo rules. Each player's half of the match is a `Classic` run (f0216) in that player's own frame, so their cannon, their group's march, invasion and shots, their barriers, lives, score, extra life and revive are the solo rules through Classic's own steps, which this issue makes public by splitting `Classic.step` into the functions it already calls (no change in what Classic does). Player 2's frame is the field turned half a turn. TwoPlayer owns only what crosses the field or joins the two halves: the cannon shots (which cross the whole field), the fly-in from the east side along the midline, the mystery ship at the midline, the wave's end, the lone survivor and the match's end. A match is plain data stepped once per tick at Classic's 60 a second with both players' inputs; it returns the events of that tick. Every number is a data table; the user's open switch (whether a shot can hurt the other player) is one flag, `shotsHitPlayers`, false by default.

## Design

### Product decisions this relies on
Quoted from `invaders/.tracker/game-design.md`, **Game design: Invaders (`invaders/`)**, **2 players (the user's design)**:
- "The field is twice as deep. Player 1 is at the south end, player 2 at the north end, facing each other, each with 4 barriers in front."
- "Each wave **flies in from the east side at the midline** in single file and **peels off alternately into two groups**: one forms up toward player 1, the other toward player 2 (two formations of 5 × 8). The fly-in takes about 3 s and aliens can be shot during it."
- "Each group marches and advances toward its own player by the solo rules."
- "**A shot crosses the whole field.** It can hit either group, the mystery ship, and **the other player's barriers**. Default: it can't hurt the other player (it stops at their base line). The user may change this."
- "Each player has their own score, lives and revives. A wave ends when both groups are gone."
- "If one player's game ends with no revive, their group flies off and later waves come to the remaining player as one solo formation."
- Pillar (2): "Deterministic Logic." And the solo rules this builds on, as f0216 designs them (its Design, Solo): the formation, march, shots, barriers, mystery ship, lives, invasion, revive and waves.
- Not this issue's: "**Pairing:**" (f0208, f0218).

Prep choices (marked "(prep)" where they appear below; each is a reading or a tuning the capture leaves open, none changes a decision above, and each costs a data-table edit or one rule to change):
1. **Frames** (prep): each player's half lives in their own frame, and player 2's input is read from their own seat (their left is their left), so the adapter passes keys straight through.
2. **The fly-in's path and pace** (prep): one flyer every 2 ticks at 6 units a tick, west along the midline to its place's column and then straight toward its own player; places fill column by column from the field's west, front row first. Every wave forms in 174 to 185 ticks (2.9 to 3.1 s). A lone survivor's 55 come 3 ticks apart, so their fly-in also takes about 3 s.
3. **Nothing marches, fires or crosses the far end during the fly-in** (prep): the groups form up first; the cannons move and fire.
4. **The ship** (prep): one ship for the match, crossing at the midline (its lane is the midline's 8 units, the same from either end), after the solo 25 s of playing ticks once both groups have formed, needing 8 aliens alive in the two groups together, worth the solo table by the shooter's own shot count.
5. **A hit pauses only the player hit** (prep): the solo 2 s pause, their cannon and group still; the other plays on.
6. **A game over waits for the revive** (prep): until the player is revived or retires, their group holds still (no march, no fire) and stays a target for the other player, and the wave can't end. Its cost: the match depends on the caller ending every wait. An offer that ends with neither `revive` nor `retire` stalls the other player's wave without limit, so f0219 must call one on every way its offer ends, and f0218 must call `retire` when a player leaves. Both calls are written into f0218's and f0219's documents as dated lines (2026-11-15). A wait bounded in the rules (Logic retiring the player itself after some ticks) would overlap f0219's ownership of when the offer ends, so it is not this issue's.
7. **A revive restarts that player's part of the wave** (prep), as solo's "restarts that wave from its starting depth": their group full at the wave's depth, already in place (no second fly-in), fresh barriers, cannon at its start; the other player is untouched.
8. **The fly-off takes no time in the rules** (prep): `retire` takes the group out of play at once; f0218's view animates it.
9. **Player 1's shot moves first** (prep): when both shots reach the same alien on one tick, player 1 scores it.
10. **Seeds** (prep): player 2's run is seeded from the match's seed plus one.
11. **A dying pause across a wave's clear** (prep): solo never has both at once, but here the other player can clear the wave while one is dying. The dying pause does not count during `waveclear`. The wave's start ends it: that player plays again at the cannon's start, and event `Respawn` `{ player, lives }` comes right after `WaveStart`. The pause still lasts at least the solo 2 s, because the clear (step 4) comes after the hit's tick and the wave pause is 120 ticks, the same as `dyingTicks`.

### The field
- One field `W = 224` across and `D = 464` deep (`2 × Classic.CONFIG.fieldDepth`), in whole units. The **field's frame** is player 1's: `x` across, `d` the depth from player 1's base line (`d = 0`) to player 2's (`d = D`). The midline is `d = 232`.
- **Each player's frame** is the solo frame seen from their own seat: `x` across as they see it (their left is low `x`), `d` from their own base line. Player 2's frame is the field turned half a turn: a point `(x, d)` of one player's frame is `(W − 1 − x, D − 1 − d)` of the other's, and a half-open box `[x0, x1) × [d0, d1)` is `[W − x1, W − x0) × [D − d1, D − d0)`. Points are whole units, so the turn is exact and its own inverse.
- In their own frame each player has exactly the solo layout: the cannon at `x = 112`, depth `[0, 8)`; four bunkers of 8 × 6 blocks at `Barriers.CLASSIC`; their group's front row at `Classic.waveDepth(wave)`. So in the field, player 2's cannon is at the far end and their bunkers' near edge at `D − 24`.

### Classic, split into its steps (`src/shared/Logic/Classic/init.luau`, a change)
`Classic.step` keeps its order and behaviour (f0216's specs stay green: E1 runs them), and calls these public functions, which TwoPlayer calls for each player:
```lua
function Classic.new(seed: number, config: Config?): Game -- config defaults to Classic.CONFIG (f0216's E2 CV4)
function Classic.sanitize(input: Input?): (number, boolean) -- the move (-1, 0, 1) and the fire, as step reads them
function Classic.moveCannon(g: Game, move: number) -- step 1
function Classic.fire(g: Game, fire: boolean, events: { Event }) -- step 2: a shot only when none flies; counted
function Classic.addScore(g: Game, points: number, events: { Event }) -- points, and the extra life at 1,500
function Classic.moveBombs(g: Game, events: { Event }): boolean -- step 4: true when one hit the cannon
function Classic.hit(g: Game, events: { Event }) -- a hit's consequences: a life, every shot, dying or game over
function Classic.march(g: Game, events: { Event }): boolean -- step 5: true when the run ended (invasion)
function Classic.alienFire(g: Game, events: { Event }) -- step 6: reload, slot turn, aimed and random columns
function Classic.startWave(g: Game) -- the wave's start, as f0216 defines it, from g.config and g.wave
```
Nothing else in Classic changes; TwoPlayer copies no solo rule (no second cannon clamp, reload table, aim or erosion).

### `TwoPlayer` (`src/shared/Logic/TwoPlayer/init.luau`)
```lua
export type Phase = "flyin" | "playing" | "waveclear" | "over"
export type SidePhase = "playing" | "dying" | "gameover" | "out"
export type Side = { run: Classic.Game, out: boolean, waiting: { boolean } } -- waiting[(row - 1) * cols + col]: still flying in
export type Flyer = { player: number, row: number, col: number, start: number }
export type FlyIn = { speed: number, gap: number, loneGap: number, lane: number }
export type Config = { fieldDepth: number, shotsHitPlayers: boolean, pair: Classic.Config, lone: Classic.Config,
	mystery: Mystery.Config, flyIn: FlyIn }
export type Event = { kind: string, player: number?, shooter: number?, points: number?, row: number?, col: number?,
	slot: number?, lives: number?, wave: number?, reason: Classic.Reason?, interval: number? }
export type Match = { config: Config, phase: Phase, timer: number, wave: number, flyTick: number,
	sides: { Side }, flyers: { Flyer }, mystery: Mystery.Mystery }
TwoPlayer.PAIR: Formation.Config -- Formation.CLASSIC with cols = 8 (5 x 8), every other number the same
TwoPlayer.CONFIG: Config -- fieldDepth 464, shotsHitPlayers false,
	-- pair = Classic.CONFIG with fieldDepth 464 and formation PAIR, lone = Classic.CONFIG with fieldDepth 464,
	-- mystery = Mystery.CLASSIC with lane 228 and depth 8, flyIn { speed 6, gap 2, loneGap 3, lane 228 }
function TwoPlayer.new(seed: number, config: Config?): Match -- wave 1, flying in, both players playing
function TwoPlayer.step(g: Match, inputs: { Classic.Input? }?): { Event } -- one tick; inputs[1], inputs[2]
function TwoPlayer.revive(g: Match, p: number): boolean -- false unless player p is at game over (and not out)
function TwoPlayer.retire(g: Match, p: number): boolean -- false if already out
function TwoPlayer.phaseOf(g: Match, p: number): SidePhase
function TwoPlayer.toField(g: Match, p: number, x: number, d: number): (number, number) -- p's frame <-> the field's
function TwoPlayer.fieldBox(g: Match, p: number, x0: number, d0: number, x1: number, d1: number): (number, number, number, number)
function TwoPlayer.flyerBox(g: Match, fl: Flyer): (number, number, number, number, boolean) -- field box; arrived
```
- `sides[p].run` is player p's `Classic.Game`, in their own frame: `run.cannon`, `run.shot`, `run.bombs` (their group's shots, toward them), `run.formation` (their group), `run.barriers` (theirs), `run.score`, `run.lives`, `run.revives`, `run.shotsFired`, `run.phase` (`playing`, `dying` or `gameover`) and `run.wave` (the match's). `run.mystery` is unused (the match's ship is `g.mystery`).
- **Input** is player p's `inputs[p]`, sanitized by `Classic.sanitize` (exactly −1 or 1, exactly `true`); a missing or non-table `inputs` is no input for either. Player 2's move is read in their own frame.

**The wave's start** (the match's, for the first wave and each next one): for each player not out, `run.config` = `lone` when only one player is not out, else `pair`; `run.wave = g.wave`; `Classic.startWave(run)` (full group at `waveDepth(wave)`, centred, fresh barriers, cannon at its start, no shots, reload and slot turn from the start); every place `waiting`. Then a new `Mystery` from `config.mystery`, `phase = "flyin"`, `timer = 0`, `flyTick = 0`, and the flyers.

**The fly-in.**
- The flyers: each player's places in order, columns from the field's west (`x` low) to its east, each column front row first (player 1's columns 1 → cols; player 2's, turned, cols → 1). With both players, flyer `k` goes to player 1's next place when `k` is odd and player 2's when even ("peels off alternately"); a lone survivor gets every flyer. Flyer `k` starts at `(k − 1) × gap` (`gap` 2, or `loneGap` 3 for a lone survivor).
- Its box (the alien's 12 × 8) in the field's frame, at distance `s = clamp(speed × (flyTick − start), 0, across + down)` along its path, where its place's field box is `[tx0, tx1) × [td0, td1)`, `across = W − tx0` and `down = |td0 − lane|`: while `s ≤ across` it is at `x0 = W − s`, `d0 = lane` (from just off the east side, west along the midline lane `[228, 236)`); after, at `x0 = tx0`, `d0 = lane ∓ (s − across)` toward its own player. It has arrived when `s = across + down`, and is then exactly in its place.
- Each fly-in tick first adds one to `flyTick`; an arrived flyer leaves `flyers` and its place stops `waiting`. A waiting place can't be hit (a shot passes through it); a flyer can, at its box.
- The fly-in ends at the end of the first tick with no flyers left (all arrived or shot): `phase = "playing"`, event `Formed`. No group marches or fires, and the ship does not count, until then.
- At a turn the flyer behind on the midline can touch the corner of the turning one for a tick (2 units); no rule reads flyer overlaps (a shot takes the first flyer in order).

**One tick** (in `flyin` and `playing`; the order is a rule):
1. Fly-in only: the flyers move (above).
2. For each player, 1 then 2, not out: `dying` → `Classic.step(run)` (the solo countdown; its last tick respawns them at the start, event `Respawn`); `playing` → they act this tick: `Classic.moveCannon` with their move, then `Classic.fire` with their fire.
3. The shots, player 1's then player 2's (only a player who acts): `shotSpeed` units, one at a time, and at each unit, in player p's frame `(x, d)` and the other player o's `(x', d') = (W − 1 − x, D − 1 − d)`:
   - `d ≥ D`: gone.
   - `d' < cannonDepth` (the other player's base line): with `shotsHitPlayers` false it is gone; with it true, if o acts this tick and `|x' − o's cannon| ≤ cannonHalf`, it is gone and o is hit (`Classic.hit` on o's run: event `CannonHit` with `player = o`, `shooter = p`; the shooter scores nothing; o acts no more this tick); else it goes on.
   - a present block of p's own bunkers at `(x, d)`: eroded with `travel = 1` (from their near side), gone, event `BarrierHit` `{ player = p, shooter = p }`;
   - a present block of o's bunkers at `(x', d')`: eroded with `travel = −1` (from their far side), gone, event `BarrierHit` `{ player = o, shooter = p }`;
   - a living, not waiting alien of p's group at `(x, d)`, then of o's at `(x', d')`: killed, gone (below);
   - a flyer whose box holds the field point `toField(p, x, d)`: killed (below) and out of `flyers`, gone;
   - the ship at the field point: scores `Mystery.value(ship, p's shotsFired)` for p, gone, event `MysteryHit` `{ player = p, points }`, the ship gone (`Mystery.remove`), and it does not tick this tick.
   - **A kill**: `Formation.kill` on the group's run (its points by row), its place stops waiting, event `AlienHit` `{ player = the group's player, shooter = p, row, col, points }`, `Classic.addScore` on the shooter's run (its `ExtraLife` tagged with the shooter). A kill that leaves its group empty adds `GroupClear` `{ player = the group's player }`.
4. **The wave's end**: when no alien is left in any group (formed or flying) and no player who is not out is at game over: `phase = "waveclear"`, `timer = waveClearTicks` (120), every shot and bomb removed, no flyers, the ship removed, event `WaveClear` `{ wave }`; the rest of the tick is skipped. While a player waits on a revive the wave goes on (step 4 is reached every tick, so it ends on the first tick after that player retires).
5. Each acting player's group's bombs: `Classic.moveBombs` on their run (toward them, eroding their own bunkers from the far side, `BarrierHit` with the slot); a hit is `Classic.hit` (`CannonHit`, and `GameOver` `reason = "lives"` on the last life) and that player acts no more this tick (their group does not march or fire). The other player is untouched.
6. Fly-in only: if no flyers are left, `phase = "playing"` and event `Formed` `{ wave }`; the tick ends here either way.
7. Each acting player's group: `Classic.march` (`March`/`Advance` with the interval; an advance to their base line is `GameOver` `reason = "invasion"` at once, and they act no more); then `Classic.alienFire` (`AlienFire` with slot and column, aimed at their own cannon).
8. The ship: unless it was hit this tick, `Mystery.tick(ship, live)` with `live` the two groups' living aliens together (`MysteryAppear`, `MysteryLeave`).
- Every event from a player's run carries `player = p` (and `shooter` where named above).
- **`waveclear`**: nothing moves and input is ignored, and no player's dying pause counts. Each tick takes one from `timer`. The tick it reaches 0 is `wave + 1` and the wave's start, then event `WaveStart` `{ wave }`, then `Respawn` `{ player, lives }` for each player who was dying (the wave's start set them playing at the cannon's start; prep choice 11), and nothing else. **`over`**: `step` returns no events and changes nothing.
- **A player at game over** (`run.phase == "gameover"`, not out): acts no more; their group holds still and stays a target; their score and wave stay for f0219's boards. **A player out**: does nothing; their bunkers stay on the field (still a target, untouched by the wave's start).

**`TwoPlayer.revive(g, p)`**: only for a player at game over who is not out (else false, nothing changes). `Classic.revive(run)`: 3 lives, the revive counted, `reason` cleared, `Classic.startWave(run)` with the run's own config (the group's size is the wave's). Then every place of theirs is in place (`waiting` all false) and their flyers, if the match is flying in, are dropped. Score, `shotsFired`, the extra life already given and the other player stay.

**`TwoPlayer.retire(g, p)`**: false if p is out or unknown. Else p is out: every alien of their group is gone from play (killed, no points; the group's `x` and `d` stay), their places stop waiting, and their shot, bombs and flyers are gone. Their score, revives, lives and `reason` stay for the boards. Their bunkers stay on the field as they are. When both are out, `phase = "over"`. Later waves come to the other player alone, as one formation of 5 × 11 (`lone`), flying in.

**The events** (the one channel f0218 renders and sounds from; every field not listed is nil):

| kind | when | fields |
|---|---|---|
| `PlayerFire` | step 2 | `player` |
| `BarrierHit` | step 3 (a cannon shot) or 5 (a bomb) | `player` (whose bunker), `shooter` (a cannon shot's player) or `slot` (a bomb's) |
| `AlienHit` | step 3 | `player` (whose group), `shooter`, `row`, `col`, `points` |
| `GroupClear` | step 3, a kill that empties a group | `player` (whose group) |
| `MysteryHit` | step 3 | `player` (the shooter), `points` |
| `ExtraLife` | after the hit that reached 1,500 | `player`, `lives` |
| `WaveClear` | step 4 | `wave` (the cleared one) |
| `CannonHit` | step 5, or step 3 with `shotsHitPlayers` | `player` (hit), `lives` (left), `shooter` (step 3 only) |
| `GameOver` | step 5 on the last life, step 3 likewise, or step 7 on an invasion | `player`, `reason` |
| `Formed` | step 6 | `wave` |
| `March`, `Advance` | step 7 | `player`, `interval` |
| `AlienFire` | step 7 | `player`, `slot`, `col` |
| `MysteryAppear`, `MysteryLeave` | step 8 | none |

### The split and server authority
- Everything above is pure Logic (pickman-arch rule 2): no engine words (no lowercase `game` in any Logic file, comments included), time is the tick count, randomness is each run's seeded state, input is the `Input` values. TwoPlayer requires `Classic`, `Formation`, `Barriers` and `Mystery` through their `init`s (`require("./Classic")`), the four edges this issue decides for `invaders/.tracker/architecture.md` (the executor writes them there, Implementation step 3).
- f0218's server adapter owns a `Match` per pair, steps it at `Classic.TICK_RATE` from an accumulator, feeds each player's latest held direction and a fire on the tick a fire intent arrived as `inputs[1]`/`inputs[2]`, calls `TwoPlayer.retire` when a player leaves, and replicates the state. `retire` emits no event: the view draws the fly-off from the group's grid as it last replicated it before the side turned `out`. `retire` leaves the group's `formation.x` and `d` as they were, so the view knows where it stood (mapping each frame to the world with `toField`/`fieldBox`; each client views from its own seat). f0219 calls `TwoPlayer.revive(g, p)` after the purchase and `TwoPlayer.retire(g, p)` when the offer ends unbought, and reads `sides[p].run.score`, `revives` and `g.wave` for the boards. Clients only send intents (rule 7): every collision, score, life and revive is the server's.

### Edge cases
- Simultaneous events follow the tick order: the clear (step 4) comes before any bomb moves, so a tick that kills the last alien can't also cost a life; a hit on a player ends only that player's tick; two bombs on one cannon in a tick cost one life (Classic's `moveBombs` stops at the hit); both shots reaching one alien in a tick: player 1's (prep choice 9); a shot with `shotsHitPlayers` that hits the other player removes that player's own shot (player 2's before it moves that tick, player 1's after).
- Bounds: the cannon is clamped (Classic); a shot is gone past `D` or at the other base line; waiting places pass shots; `revive` and `retire` refuse an unknown player (`sides[p] == nil`).
- Empty input: nil, a non-table `inputs`, or junk in either entry is no move and no fire for that player.
- A player who leaves or respawns: `retire` (above); a respawn is the dying pause (Classic's), or the wave's start when the clear came while they were dying (prep choice 11).
- A game over during the fly-in (only with `shotsHitPlayers`, as no bomb flies then): the revive puts that group in place at once and the other's flyers go on.
- Frame-rate independence: Logic counts ticks only; f0218's accumulator turns seconds into ticks.
- Determinism and replication: the same seed and inputs give the same match tick for tick; the state and events are JSON-safe (dense arrays, finite numbers, string keys), checked on every tick by AC6.

### Scalability
- Per tick: the fly-in O(flyers) (≤ 80) box computations; each shot ≤ 4 unit checks, each O(bunkers) for each player's blocks, O(1) per group cell, O(flyers) during the fly-in; each player's bombs, march and fire as solo (f0216: O(55 × 4 × 48) only on a step tick). Prep's bench (E3): 3.8 µs a tick on average, 0.023 % of one core per match at 60 Hz; a server full of matches stays far inside the budget.
- The state is two solo runs (40 or 55 aliens, 192 blocks each), ≤ 80 flyers of four numbers, and the ship: f0218 decides what to replicate (grids on change, positions per tick; a flyer's box is a function of `flyTick`, so the client can draw the fly-in from the flyer list and the tick).

### Maintainability
- Every number is a data table: `TwoPlayer.PAIR` (the groups' size), `TwoPlayer.CONFIG` (the depth, the switch `shotsHitPlayers`, the pair's and lone survivor's run configs, the ship's lane, the fly-in's speed, gaps and lane); the solo numbers are Classic's, read through `pair`/`lone`, so a solo tuning changes both games at once.
- The solo rules live once, in Classic; TwoPlayer calls them. A third player or another layout would be another frame and another `Side`; the user's switch is one flag with both branches pinned (AC3).
- The events are the one channel f0218 renders and sounds from; a new effect needs no new state.

### Usability
- For the players: each sees the solo game from their own seat (their keys, their left, their group coming at them) with the other player's group and shots beyond; the fly-in is readable (one line from the east, peeling left and right) and short (about 3 s); a hit or a game over never stops the other player; a lone survivor gets the full solo formation.
- For the developer: one small API (`new`, `step`, `revive`, `retire`, `phaseOf`, the two frame maps and `flyerBox`), typed, with each half a plain `Classic.Game`, so f0219's boards read the same fields as solo; invalid input can't move or fire a cannon; a spec can set any state directly.
