# R0144: Invaders classic rules

## Theme
Feature raft delivering Invaders' complete pure Logic rules: `f0216` (Invaders classic rules) and `f0217` (Invaders two players). Both share `invaders/src/shared/Logic/Classic`, with `f0217` directly depending on `f0216`.

Decisions relied on (quoted from `f0216` and `f0217`):
- `invaders/.tracker/game-design.md`: 2-D rules; 5×11 formation (30/20/10 pts), accelerating march, 1 player shot / up to 3 alien shots, 4 erodible 8×6 bunkers, ~25 s mystery ship (50/100/150/300), 3 lives + extra at 1,500, invasion = game over at once, waves cycling after 8; 2-player opposing ends on double-depth field, side fly-in splitting into two 5×8 groups, shots crossing whole field and hitting opponent barriers (`shotsHitPlayers = false` default), own scores/lives/revives, lone survivor plays on.
- Deterministic Logic, no engine dependencies.

## Whole-raft design
Shared modules and interfaces between `f0216` and `f0217`:
- `invaders/src/shared/Logic/Formation`, `Barriers`, `Mystery` (created by `f0216`, reused by `f0217`).
- `invaders/src/shared/Logic/Classic`:
  - `f0216` creates `Classic` (`Classic.new`, `Classic.step`, `Classic.revive`, `Classic.CONFIG`, `Classic.waveDepth`).
  - `f0217` splits `Classic.step` into public helpers (`Classic.sanitize`, `Classic.moveCannon`, `Classic.fire`, `Classic.addScore`, `Classic.moveBombs`, `Classic.hit`, `Classic.march`, etc.) with `Classic.new(seed, config?)` defaulting to `Classic.CONFIG` and preserving `Classic.step` behavior (`f0216` specs remain green).
- `invaders/src/shared/Logic/TwoPlayer` (created by `f0217`): runs two `Classic` halves in each player's own frame, plus cross-field shots, midline fly-in, midline mystery ship, `revive`, and `retire`.
- Both designs agree on every shared type and function signature.

## Order & dependencies
1. `f0216` — solo classic rules (`Formation`, `Barriers`, `Mystery`, `Classic`).
2. `f0217` — two-player match (`TwoPlayer`, splits `Classic` steps; depends on layer 1).
