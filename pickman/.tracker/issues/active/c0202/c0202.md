# c0202: Switch to arcade paid play

## Problem
The user, 2026-10-31: "I want to branch out into multiple games, they will each have their own directory in the project: first person snake, first person asteroids. I also want a new project for a landing portal where you can scroll between the games and see the highscores for each, the same cost model remains but it shoudl show 5 robux for each game witha slash through showing it is currently on sale for 1 robux. highscores shoudl show how many revives were used. I want you to design a full fun playable game for both of those two new game ideas. I also want both snake and asteroids to be a two player game, snakes can even be n-player up to a high number just make the wold bigger. Snakes should have maps that once you have reached a certain length the game ends and we move to a new map, the first is empty, then one wall somewhere in the middle, then two, then three and so on. The walls can be vertical and horizontal but do not connect. for asteroids we could have a two player version where each player is attacking the aliens from opposing sides. You can shoot each other's barriers. the aliens would then come in from the side and and split into two groups that move toward either player". Then: "Add a third game first person asteroids." Decided with the user (2026-10-31): the 2-player aliens game is pure Space Invaders (`invaders`); Asteroids is its own game (`asteroids`); one Roblox experience with the portal as start place, replacing pickman's lobby; every board shows revives used; one free game per hour per game.

The approved design is `arcade/.tracker/architecture.md` (c0183). This issue owns these parts of it, as they are said there: **Technical skeleton**, "Pickman migrates one feature at a time", for PaidPlay and Receipts. The rest of it belongs to other issues (c0190's ownership table), listed under Non-goals.

## Solution
Pickman becomes the arcade library's title `pickman`: its two paid-play adapters, the game place's `Continues` and the lobby's `Paid`, call the library's `Wallet` (arrival, revives, refunds, reads, passes) and `Receipts` (ProcessReceipt, prompts, the test screen), and pickman's own copy (`Logic/Pay`, `Shared/Purchases`, `Shared/TestPurchase`, `tests/unit/Pay`, `Lobby.PRICE`) is deleted. Every rule the player sees stays (f0145's cost model, offer, holds and sending back; f0186's revives and sale price), and the records stay where and as they are (`PaidPlay`, `PaidPlayTest`, f0145's four fields), so nothing migrates. The one behaviour that changes is the one the capture asks for: the game place's arrival takes whatever the player holds whoever sold it, the portal's purchases and passes included, so a player who paid or took the free game on the portal's Pickman card plays at once. The lobby keeps selling Pickman, on the library too, until f0209 and c0188 replace and retire it.

What f0145 landed with after this design was first written stays too (the user's rulings of 2026-11-17, Problem): the game place tells the client its mode on every arrival that plays (with no status after a claim that failed) and what the player holds after every record it writes or hears; a test confirm whose grant write fails, and a granted test confirm whose revive write fails, release the countdown at once; a Studio play with nothing to pay with plays ("Play in Studio only"), an exception that stays in pickman's `Continues` because the library makes none. R0118's `tests/place/paid-play-status.spec.luau`, which loads the deleted copy, moves onto the library's stub as this issue's `status.spec`.

### Authority and what a client could lie about
A client sends `StartGame` (lobby), `Continue` (`"ask"` or `"decline"`, honoured only during that player's own offer), and the library's screen's `"confirm"` or `"cancel"`, which Receipts honours only for a screen it opened for that player, in test mode (f0201 §5). Nothing a client sends names a product, a price, a title or a receipt; every credit is a server write (Wallet); an arrival reads the store, never TeleportData (f0201: "What a teleport carries is never read"). The status Pickman tells is the server's read of the record.

### Edge cases
- A live revive bought within the library's `USE_SECONDS` of a use (the first ask of an offer writes when the arrival's claim failed or timed out, so nothing was known): used `USE_SECONDS` after the grant, while the receipt wait holds the countdown (AC2). If the store still fails then, the revive stays held for the next offer (G9), and the receipt wait ends the hold.
- A test revive confirmed within that span: the use answers `"unknown"`, the countdown runs again at once (as for a failed write: AC9), and the revive stays held; an ask within the rest of the span gets `nil` from the library too and opens the test screen again (a second test purchase, charging nothing, kept as a credit: G9), and an ask after it uses the revive. Main's `ASK_SECONDS` (2 s) had the same window, shorter.
- A play bought during the offer (a receipt retried here, or bought for later): held, revives nothing, tells nothing (AC2).
- A receipt of another title processed in Pickman's place: granted into that title's own record, nothing handed to Pickman (AC1).
- A test confirm whose grant write fails: the screen closes not bought and the countdown runs again (Receipts' `closed(…, false)`; main's R0118 code review 001 F3, AC9). A granted test confirm whose revive write fails: the countdown runs again at once and the revive stays held for the next ask (main's R0118 final review 001 F2, AC9). A confirm with no screen open: dropped by Receipts, every hold kept (AC9).
- An arrival whose claim fails: it plays and is told the mode with no status, with no store read (AC1, AC9); a later write or grant tells what is held.
- A tell whose read answers "busy" or "down" (the library forgot the record seen and its read is under way or failing): nothing is sent, so the line keeps what it last showed; the next write or grant tells again.
- A Studio play with nothing to pay with: it plays with the offer on, nothing charged, never sent back or kicked; `IsStudio` false, missing or erroring: sent back as G10 says (AC9).
- A player who leaves during the arrival's claim: nothing sent back, teleported or kicked (AC1); during a revive's write: the revive is given back and the run ends (AC2).
- A press while the library's test screen is open in the lobby: no second screen, nothing starts (AC3).
- A play granted in the lobby while the read on joining is under way: the grant's own press goes without the read and the game starts once (AC3); the read's older record is forgotten by the landed library, so a later press reads the store (§3).
- A press after `READ_SECONDS`: it reads again and tells the record it read (AC3).
- A controller in the lobby with the test screen open: whichever of the screen and Roblox's selection takes ButtonA, nothing starts or is charged that the player did not buy (AC3; E5 for the rest).
- Old servers of f0145's build after the publish: §6.

### Scalability
Per step nothing new. Store calls per player go down or stay: the arrival is one `UpdateAsync` plus one MemoryStore read (as before); a revive used is one write (plus a refund when it cannot be applied); the lobby reads at most once a minute while the store answers (f0145: once per 2 s of presses), and once per 6 s while it fails; revive writes at most one per 6 s and none while none is known held (f0145: one per 2 s while unknown). Telling the player costs no store call while the library holds the record seen (it does after every lone write or read of the slot), and at most one `GetAsync` when it forgot it (an overlap or a failed write), bounded by the library's read guards; a tell follows only a write or a grant, so it adds at most one read per write. A failed claim's tell reads nothing. `inStudio` is one engine call per arrival the library would send back. Replicated: the library's folder and remote in each place (`ReplicatedStorage.ArcadePaidPlay.TestPurchase`), one message per test screen; `PayStatus` as before. The lobby place now builds `ReplicatedStorage.Arcade` (a few small ModuleScripts, once per client join).

Pickman's paid play has one home, the library; a change to the cost model, the receipts or the test screen is the library's and reaches every place.
