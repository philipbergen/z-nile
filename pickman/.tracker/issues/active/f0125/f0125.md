# f0125: Player outruns ghosts more

## Problem
The player should move another 10% faster relative to the ghosts than today.

User, 2026-10-21: "We want to move another 10% faster than ghosts." Every player is affected: the ghosts catch up too easily. Today the speeds are the classic Pac-Man tables (f0021, `Levels.GHOST_TUNING = 1`). With base speed 30, level 1's player covers 30 units per tick and a hunting ghost 28, about 7% faster; levels 2 to 4 are 34 against 32, and level 5 on 38 against 36. What should happen: on every level, the player's speed relative to a hunting ghost is 10% higher than today (for example level 1 from 30:28 to about 30:25.5).

## Non-goals
- The eyes returning to the house: they keep `Levels.EYES_FACTOR`, as f0021 fixed.
- The overall game pace (`Session.GAME_SPEED`).
- Other difficulty changes (the scatter and chase timetable, ghost release, frightened time).

## Solution
- The ratio can come from slower ghosts (`Levels.GHOST_TUNING = 1 / 1.1`, the knob f0021 built for "slower ghosts", which also scales the tunnel and frightened speeds) or from a faster player (base speed × 1.1, within `checkLevelSpeeds`' one-tile-per-tick limit). Which one, and whether frightened ghosts slow too, is for prep to ask the user.
- Speeds are whole units per tick after rounding, so the 10% is checked per level against the rounded numbers.

## Design
Decisions relied on (game-design.md): **Slower ghosts** (2026-10-01): "Ghosts may be slower than the classic tables, through **one tuning factor** (`Levels.GHOST_TUNING`) on the hunting and frightened ghost speeds, not on eyes. It ships at the classic 1 until the user gives the factor, after a Beta playtest (R0010, f0021)." **Movement** (R0009): the player's speeds per level are the Dossier's Table A.1 (30, 34, 38 units a tick; 34 from level 21). **Authority**: the server simulates; speeds are server-side only.

**The factor (G1, G2, decided with the user 2026-10-25).** The capture leaves two routes open: slower ghosts through the slower-ghosts factor, or a faster player. Prep measured both on prototypes (Unknowns, E1, E2): both reach "10% more" only within a unit's rounding, and each turns one promoted spec red. The recommended route is **slower ghosts by 1/1.1**: it is the knob f0021 built for exactly this, the player's pace, the eating slowdown, the turn buffer and the spy hop stay as game-design.md has them, and its one red spec is a test artifact (c0153), not a behaviour pin. The factor applies to hunting, tunnel and frightened ghosts alike, never to eyes, as the Slower-ghosts decision says (G3, decided: frightened ghosts are slowed too).

**Where the factor lives (G2, decided).** The factor reaches the ghosts as `Ghosts.Set.tuning` through `Session.join`'s `Ghosts.new(…, { tuning = … })`, which f0126 (wall picks) also reads for its pick length; keep it there. `Levels.GHOST_TUNING` stays the classic 1 (the tables' meaning, and f0021's promoted spec `levels.spec` pins it); the shipped config carries the factor: `Session.GAME_CONFIG.ghostTuning = 1 / 1.1`. `Session.DEFAULT_CONFIG` (specs, the headless default) keeps the classic tables. game-design.md's Slower-ghosts row and its open question get the user's factor and where it lives.

**One function for the speeds.** `Session.speeds(c: Config, level: number, frightened: boolean?): Speeds`, with `export type Speeds = { player: number, ghost: number, tunnel: number, frightened: number, eyes: number }`, pure, in `pickman/src/shared/Logic/Session/init.luau`:
- `player = Levels.playerSpeed(c.movement.speed, level, frightened)`;
- `ghost`, `tunnel`, `frightened` = `Levels.ghostSpeed(c.movement.speed, level, kind, k)` with `k = c.ghostTuning or Levels.GHOST_TUNING`; `eyes` the same call with `"eyes"` (which ignores `k`).
`Session.speeds` is the step's source for the player's speed (the step calls `Session.speeds(...).player`; today it calls `Levels.playerSpeed` directly) and the specs' oracle for the ghosts. The ghosts compute their own speeds in `Ghosts.speedOf` from `set.tuning`, which `Session.join` sets with `Ghosts.new(s.maze, s.seed, { tuning = s.config.ghostTuning })` (the `tuning` option exists, f0021); f0126 reads the same `set.tuning` for its pick's length. The two are tied by AC4's motion case, which measures a running session's ghosts against `Session.speeds` (design review 001 F1, F2).

**Config and validation.** `Session.Config` gains `ghostTuning: number?` ("the ghosts' speed factor; nil is Levels.GHOST_TUNING"). `Session.new`, next to `checkLevelSpeeds`, refuses a `ghostTuning` that is not a positive finite number (`session: ghostTuning must be a positive finite number, got …`), and one for which any level's tuned ghost (normal, tunnel, frightened) would cover more than a tile a tick, with the factor resolved as `Session.speeds` resolves it (`c.ghostTuning or Levels.GHOST_TUNING`, so untuned configs are bounded too; design review 002 F2) (`session: ghostTuning {k} is too fast: level {l} ghosts would be {v} > 120`), as `checkLevelSpeeds` does for the player: `Ghosts.contacts` sees only shared or swapped tiles (design review 001 F3). Eyes are untuned (2 × base, as today).

**Pure and adapter.** All of it is Logic (Session, read by the server's `Sessions.luau`, which already passes `Session.GAME_CONFIG`); no client or adapter change: the client renders replicated positions.

### Scalability
One table lookup and four roundings per call; `Session.speeds` is called once per member per step (as `playerSpeed` is today) and the ghosts keep their own per-ghost lookup. No allocation beyond the returned table per call (the same order as today's numbers; the step may cache it per level if the gate's timing shows a cost, Implementation step 4).

### Maintainability
`Session.speeds` decides the player's speed for the step and is the oracle the specs measure the ghosts against (they keep their own `speedOf` over `set.tuning`, which f0126 also reads); the factor is one config field with one default; changing it later is one number. `Levels` is unchanged.

### Usability
Players escape a closing ghost more easily: at level 1 a hunting ghost runs 26 units a tick instead of 28 against the player's 30. Nothing new to learn; the HUD is unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

### Checked
- AC1: in the shipped config, on every level, the player's speed over a hunting ghost's is the classic ratio times 1.07 to 1.13, and 1.09 to 1.13 averaged over the table's levels.
- AC2: the default config keeps the classic tables exactly (player, hunting, tunnel and frightened ghost speeds).
- AC3: eyes keep twice level 1's player speed, and the player stays within one tile a tick on every level.
- AC4: a shipped session's ghosts run at the speeds `Session.speeds` gives (hunting, tunnel, frightened) on every level, and a running session's ghosts move exactly those units a step, hunting, frightened and as eyes, on levels 1 and 5.
- AC5: a `ghostTuning` that is not a positive finite number, or that would move a ghost more than a tile a tick on any level, is refused by `Session.new`; exactly a tile is allowed (base 96, k = 120/114) and a unit more refused (121/114); faster ghosts within a tile are allowed (base 30, k = 2); the shipped and default configs are accepted.
- AC6: game-design.md's Slower-ghosts row records the user's factor and where it lives (`Session.GAME_CONFIG.ghostTuning`), and its Open questions no longer ask for the factor.
