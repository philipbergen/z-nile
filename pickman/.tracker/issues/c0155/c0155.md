# c0155: Narrow f0110's and f0146's button-table pins

## Problem
f0126 (risky wall picks) adds its pick to every input, by the user's rule (2026-10-25: "We support keyboard, cosole, and touch for every feature"): a touch PICK button beside f0110's FIRE and JUMP, and the controller's Y / triangle in f0146's button map. The user decided that f0126 lands after f0110 ("PICK button after f0110") and after f0146 ("f0126 after f0146"). Two of those issues' acceptance specs, promoted when they are done, compare their tables exactly, so f0126's additions would turn `main` red and an executor may not edit a promoted spec:
- f0110's `buttons.spec` case "Touch.buttons is the snap, fire and hop rectangles together" checks `Touch.buttons(box)` with `toEqual` against exactly the snap, fire and hop rectangles; f0126 adds `pick`.
- f0146's `pad.spec` case "the constants and the button map" checks `Pad.BUTTONS` exactly; f0126 adds `ButtonY = "Pick"`.

What should happen: each case is narrowed to pin its own entries one by one, so that added buttons (f0126's now, d0149's later) do not fail it, while every entry it pins today stays pinned. If f0110's or f0146's prep makes that change itself before landing (f0126's prep asked both on 2026-10-25), this chore checks that and has nothing left to narrow for that spec.

## Non-goals
- f0126's PICK button and Y binding themselves.
- Any other case of those specs.

## Solution
- After f0110 and f0146 are done: in each promoted spec, replace the whole-table comparison with one assertion per pinned entry, keep the case's name meaning, and pin the narrowed files whole (as c0150 does), with a header line saying what is no longer pinned and why.
