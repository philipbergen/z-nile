# c0177: Narrow f0125 speed pins for ramp

## Problem
f0125's promoted speeds.spec compares the shipped config against the classic tables (edge per level, eyes at 60, ghost speeds without the ramp, clones of GAME_CONFIG); with f0147's ramp in GAME_CONFIG they must compare against the same config untuned and pass the ramp

Found by f0147's prep (2026-10-31): on its prototype, f0125's speeds.spec fails 4 of 9 cases once `Session.speeds` follows the ramp: the edge per level against the classic tables (level 21 gains 1.26), the eyes at 2 × 30, the ghosts' speeds checked without the set's ramp, and the too-fast bound's clones of GAME_CONFIG (base 96 on the ramp's level 34 is refused). Against the same config untuned the ramp keeps f0125's 10%: 1.083 to 1.120 per level, 1.101 on average (levels 1 to 35). f0147 depends on this chore (its Overrides line).

## Non-goals
- f0125's own rule (the ghosts 10% slower relative to the player) is kept: only the baseline the spec compares against changes.
- c0176's four files.

## Solution
- The edge cases compare `GAME_CONFIG` with the same config untuned (`ghostTuning = nil`, the ramp kept) instead of `DEFAULT_CONFIG`; their bands stay 1.07–1.13 per level and 1.09–1.13 on average, over the ramp's levels when the config has one.
- The eyes case: the shipped eyes equal the untuned config's (eyes are never tuned), not a fixed 60.
- The ghost-speed case passes the set's ramp (`Levels.ghostSpeed(..., set.tuning, set.ramp)`).
- The bound case's clones drop the ramp (`ramp = nil`), so they keep testing the bound on the classic tables.
- Added by f0147's design review 001 (the user's "Carry fractions", 2026-10-31): with the ramp, `Session.speeds`' tunnel and frightened values are rates the ghosts carry fractions of (`Levels.ghostRate`), so the ghost-speed case compares them with `Levels.ghostRate(..., set.tuning, set.ramp)`, and the motion case accepts a step of the rate's floor or ceiling, within a tenth of a unit on average (a whole speed still exactly). f0147's `experiments/narrowings.patch` has the prototype's version; it passes 9/9 there.
- The eyes case's title no longer says "twice level 1's player speed" (the ramp's eyes are twice the level's ramped speed, capped at a tile): it says the shipped eyes are the untuned config's (f0147 design review 002 F7).

## Design
Product decision relied on (game-design.md, Decisions): "Slower ghosts (2026-10-01) | Ghosts run at **1 / 1.1** of the classic hunting, tunnel and frightened speeds, never changing the eyes, through `Session.GAME_CONFIG.ghostTuning` (f0125)." That rule is kept; only the baseline f0125's spec measures it against changes, from the classic tables to the same config untuned, so it holds when f0147 puts a ramp in the shipped config. No file in `src/` changes.

**One file, valid on both sides of f0147.** The narrowed `pickman/tests/acceptance/f0125/speeds.spec.luau` must pass on main today (no `ramp` field, no `Levels.ghostRate`, `Levels.ghostSpeed` takes four arguments) and on f0147's code. f0147's `experiments/narrowings.patch` version calls `Levels.ghostRate` and reads `set.ramp` directly, which neither type-checks nor runs before f0147, so this chore's patch reads f0147's additions loosely (`:: any`) and falls back where they are absent. (prep) choices are marked.

**The narrowing** (`experiments/narrow.patch`, cut from origin/main 9bdd8def; base blob in Implementation step 1), case by case:
- Three helpers after `LAST`: `untunedOf(c)` (a clone with `ghostTuning = nil`, the ramp kept), `classicOf(c)` (a clone with `ramp = nil`) and `levelsOf(c)` (the ramp's `top` when the config has one, else `Levels.COUNT`).
- The file's header gains two lines: the 10% is measured against the same config without its tuning (the classic tables before f0147's ramp, the ramped speeds with it), and its first line says "than without f0125's tuning" where it said "than in the classic tables" (design review 001 F2).
- The per-level case, retitled "on every level the shipped config's edge over a hunting ghost is the untuned config's edge times 1.07 to 1.13": the gain is `edge(GAME_CONFIG) / edge(untunedOf(GAME_CONFIG))`, for levels 1 to `levelsOf(GAME_CONFIG) + 1` (one past the last, as before). Band unchanged.
- The average, retitled "averaged over the config's levels the gain is 9% to 13%": the same baseline, averaged over `levelsOf(GAME_CONFIG)` levels. Band unchanged.
- "the default config keeps the classic tables exactly": unchanged.
- The eyes case is retitled "the eyes returning home are the untuned config's: f0125's tuning never changes them" (the capture's last bullet): the shipped eyes equal `untunedOf(GAME_CONFIG)`'s on every level of the config. **(prep)** It also keeps the old number where it stays true: on `classicOf(GAME_CONFIG)` the eyes are `2 * movement.speed` on every table level. Without that second loop the narrowing would stop catching eyes at any other multiple (code mutant C2; lesson zz-test/narrowing-keeps-each-members-check).
- "the player's speed … within one tile a tick": unchanged.
- "a shipped session's ghosts run at the speeds Session.speeds gives": the set's ramp is passed (`(set :: any).ramp`, nil before f0147) and asserted to be the shipped config's own; hunting speed is `Levels.ghostSpeed(base, level, "normal", set.tuning, ramp)`; tunnel and frightened are `Levels.ghostRate(…, set.tuning, ramp)` when `Levels.ghostRate` exists, else `Levels.ghostSpeed` (before f0147 the two are the same whole speeds). Levels 1 to `levelsOf(GAME_CONFIG) + 1`.
- The motion case's `allEqual`: a step covers the rate's floor or ceiling, and the mean is within a tenth of a unit of it. A whole speed's floor and ceiling are itself, so today it is still exact.
- The bound case: `fast` and `double` drop the ramp (`ramp = nil`), so the bound is tested on the classic tables; `untuned`, `at` and `over` are clones of `fast`. **(prep, design review 001 F1)** It also tests the bound where the shipped config's levels end: when `GAME_CONFIG` has a ramp, a clone with `ghostTuning = 1.25` has a hunting ghost within a tile on `Levels.COUNT` and over a tile on the ramp's top (65 and 123 on f0147's prototype), and `Session.new` refuses it; one with 1.2 stays within a tile on the top (118) and is accepted. The two speed assertions state the reason, so a retuned ramp fails loudly instead of passing for nothing. Before f0147 the branch does not run (the classic tables accept 1.25). Without it nothing in the whole suite caught a ghost bound that still stops at `Levels.COUNT` (code mutant C8; the reviewer ran it against every spec that loads Session, 1371 passed).
- "a ghost tuning that is not a positive finite number is refused": unchanged.
The old spellings are found by search over the base file (lesson z-design/narrowing-substitutions-found-by-search): `grep -c 'edge(Session.DEFAULT_CONFIG'` 2, `'set.tuning)'` 3, `'table.clone(Session.GAME_CONFIG)'` 3 (fast, double, and the bad-tuning loop's `c`, which needs no change: a bad tuning is refused with or without a ramp, and it passes on the ramp, E2), `'the classic edge'` 1. The acceptance asserts the new counts, that no old spelling is left, and that the ramp-top branch is there once.
