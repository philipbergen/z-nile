# c0177: Narrow f0125 speed pins for ramp

## Problem
f0125's promoted speeds.spec compares the shipped config against the classic tables (edge per level, eyes at 60, ghost speeds without the ramp, clones of GAME_CONFIG); with f0147's ramp in GAME_CONFIG they must compare against the same config untuned and pass the ramp

Found by f0147's prep (2026-10-31): on its prototype, f0125's speeds.spec fails 4 of 9 cases once `Session.speeds` follows the ramp: the edge per level against the classic tables (level 21 gains 1.26), the eyes at 2 × 30, the ghosts' speeds checked without the set's ramp, and the too-fast bound's clones of GAME_CONFIG (base 96 on the ramp's level 34 is refused). Against the same config untuned the ramp keeps f0125's 10%: 1.083 to 1.120 per level, 1.101 on average (levels 1 to 35). f0147 depends on this chore (its Overrides line).

## Non-goals
- f0125's own rule (the ghosts 10% slower relative to the player) is kept: only the baseline the spec compares against changes.
- c0176's four files.

## Solution
- The edge cases compare `GAME_CONFIG` with the same config untuned (`ghostTuning = nil`, the ramp kept) instead of `DEFAULT_CONFIG`; their bands stay 1.07–1.13 per level and 1.09–1.13 on average, over the ramp's levels when the config has one.
- The eyes case: the shipped eyes equal the untuned config's (eyes are never tuned), not a fixed 60.
- The ghost-speed case passes the set's ramp (`Levels.ghostSpeed(..., set.tuning, set.ramp)`).
- The bound case's clones drop the ramp (`ramp = nil`), so they keep testing the bound on the classic tables.
