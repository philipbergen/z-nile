# c0182: Narrow the touch pins d0149's circles replace

## Problem
d0149 (touch circle controls, decided with the user 2026-10-25 and 2026-10-31) replaces the phone's touch controls: the left-half swipes, the right-half drag look, the snap control and the JUMP button go, and two fixed circles (steer, look; a tap on the look circle hops) with FIRE and PICK discs beside the look circle take their place. Promoted acceptance specs pin the old controls, and an executor may never edit a promoted spec, so d0149 cannot land while they stand (z-design, Override check). Found in d0149's prep (its prototype `proto-d0149`, 2026-10-31), the specs whose cases fail on the new controls:
- f0027 (swipe turns): `place.spec`, `screen.spec`, `swipe.spec`, `track.spec`.
- f0028 (touch look): `hit.spec`, `look.spec`, `place.spec`.
- f0029 (mobile HUD): `button.spec` (the snap control), `jump.spec` (the case that places the snap button against the jump buttons), `phone.spec`, `place.spec`.
- f0110 (touch FIRE and JUMP): `adapters.spec`, `buttons.spec`, `fold.spec`, `hunt.spec`.
- Once promoted: f0126's `touch.spec`, the touch cases of its `controls.spec` and the clearance case of its `parts.spec` (it reads `Touch.snapRect`); f0143's swipe-up boost case in `controls.spec`, and the phrase "two quick swipes up" its `place.spec` requires of game-design.md (d0149 rewrites that sentence to "two quick pulls up on the steer circle", and d0149's docs.spec requires the old words gone; d0149's design review 002 F3); f0158's `clear.spec` (it reads `Touch.snapRect` and the JUMP button); c0155's `narrowed.spec` (in its prep, `prep-narrow-button-table-pins`: it runs f0110's `buttons.spec` case through `Touch.snapRect`, `fireRect` and `hopRect`; c0155.md says it must be narrowed if d0149 renames or removes that case; d0149's design review 001 F1).
- f0158's `check.sh` runs `clear.spec` with `F0158_EXACT=1`, which requires each accepted overlap to still happen: its ACCEPTED entries for the snap control, JUMP (`jump2` is Roblox's own button, which stays measured), FIRE and PICK go stale once d0149 moves or removes those controls, so they are removed (d0149's own `clear.spec` owns the touch controls' clearance of the ring from then on). That is an edit to a finished item's `check.sh` or spec, so it lands in a tracker PR before d0149 is readied (z-design/finished-overrides-land-before-ready; d0149's design review 001 F3).

Prep's choice (d0149's prep, 2026-10-31, which owns this chore by the user's 2026-10-25 line; the user may veto): **narrow where a rule survives, remove where it cannot**: a case narrows to the rules d0149 keeps; a case that cannot pass on both sides of d0149 (every touch case that runs Controls through the old `Controls.start` arguments, or pins a removed function) is removed, its file's header naming the d0149 case that takes it over; a file left with no case is removed together with its line in the owner's `check.sh`, in a tracker PR before d0149 is readied (z-design/finished-overrides-land-before-ready; d0149's design reviews 001 F4, 003 F4, 004 F3).

What should happen: each of those cases is removed or narrowed so that it no longer pins the replaced controls, while every rule it pins that d0149 keeps and that can be pinned on both sides of d0149 stays pinned (the screen-pixel conversion, the HUD blocks, the radar's place, the ring's clearance of the HUD; the touch FIRE shot, PICK and boost go to d0149's own cases, as the choice above says). `main` stays green after this chore alone: removing or narrowing a pin never fails it. d0149 depends on this chore.

## Non-goals
- d0149's circles themselves, and its own new specs.
- `tests/unit/Touch` and `tests/place/touch-adapters.spec.luau`: not promoted, d0149 rewrites them.

## Solution
- After f0126, f0143, f0158 and c0155 are done: per spec, narrow each case to the rules d0149 keeps; a case that cannot pass on both sides of d0149 (every touch FIRE, PICK and swipe-boost case that runs Controls through the old `Controls.start` arguments, design review 003 F4) is removed, and the header names the d0149 case that takes it over (adapters.spec "FIRE sends the aimed shot…", "two quick pulls up boost…", fold.spec "FIRE and PICK act once…"); with a header line saying what is no longer pinned and why (as c0150 does). A file left with no case is removed with its owner's `check.sh` line, in a tracker PR before d0149 is readied (the prep's choice above).
- Rerun the list against d0149's prototype (or its landed branch) before closing: every failing case there is accounted for.
