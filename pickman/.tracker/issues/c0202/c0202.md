# c0202: Switch to arcade paid play

## Problem
The user, 2026-10-31: "I want to branch out into multiple games, they will each have their own directory in the project: first person snake, first person asteroids. I also want a new project for a landing portal where you can scroll between the games and see the highscores for each, the same cost model remains but it shoudl show 5 robux for each game witha slash through showing it is currently on sale for 1 robux. highscores shoudl show how many revives were used. I want you to design a full fun playable game for both of those two new game ideas. I also want both snake and asteroids to be a two player game, snakes can even be n-player up to a high number just make the wold bigger. Snakes should have maps that once you have reached a certain length the game ends and we move to a new map, the first is empty, then one wall somewhere in the middle, then two, then three and so on. The walls can be vertical and horizontal but do not connect. for asteroids we could have a two player version where each player is attacking the aliens from opposing sides. You can shoot each other's barriers. the aliens would then come in from the side and and split into two groups that move toward either player". Then: "Add a third game first person asteroids." Decided with the user (2026-10-31): the 2-player aliens game is pure Space Invaders (`invaders`); Asteroids is its own game (`asteroids`); one Roblox experience with the portal as start place, replacing pickman's lobby; every board shows revives used; one free game per hour per game.

The approved design is `arcade/.tracker/architecture.md` (c0183). This issue owns these parts of it, as they are said there: **Technical skeleton**, "Pickman migrates one feature at a time", for PaidPlay and Receipts. The rest of it belongs to other issues (c0190's ownership table), listed under Non-goals.

What should happen:
- Pickman's paid play (f0145, with f0186's revives and per-game free hour) runs on arcade's PaidPlay and Receipts, with no pickman-only copy.
- Pickman's game place consumes the portal's entitlement on arrival (a player who paid, or took the free game, on the portal's Pickman card plays at once), as every game place does, so the portal can replace the lobby (f0209, c0188).

Decided with the user (2026-11-16), design review 001 F1 part (1): option (a), keep the struck price through an arcade change (the user's reply "a" to "Which do you want: (a) new arcade issue, (a) reopen f0201, or (b)?"; they named no route, and the lead took the recommended one, a new small arcade issue after f0201: f0395 "Test purchase screen strikes the price", `depends_on: [f0201]`). Guess (pending user, 2026-11-16): a revive's price line on the library's test screen is f0395's own, which f0395 records as a guess following f0186's G3 (a new library value, for example `Prices.revive = "1 R$"`, nothing struck); c0202 pins only that a revive's lines strike nothing and that its price line is not a play's, and follows whatever words f0395 settles.

Decided with the user (2026-11-16), design review 001 F1 part (2): option (i), accept the window as designed (the user's reply "1" to "Which do you want: (i) or (ii)?", read by the lead as (i)): the pass of a lobby that could not read a player's record changes key with the switch, from f0145's `<user id>` to the library's `pickman/<user id>` in the same `PaidPlayPass` map, and nothing bridges the two while servers of both builds run after the publish. ## Solution
Pickman becomes the arcade library's title `pickman`: its two paid-play adapters, the game place's `Continues` and the lobby's `Paid`, call the library's `Wallet` (arrival, revives, refunds, reads, passes) and `Receipts` (ProcessReceipt, prompts, the test screen), and pickman's own copy (`Logic/Pay`, `Shared/Purchases`, `Shared/TestPurchase`, `tests/unit/Pay`, `Lobby.PRICE`) is deleted. Every rule the player sees stays (f0145's cost model, offer, holds and sending back; f0186's revives and sale price), and the records stay where and as they are (`PaidPlay`, `PaidPlayTest`, f0145's four fields), so nothing migrates. The one behaviour that changes is the one the capture asks for: the game place's arrival takes whatever the player holds whoever sold it, the portal's purchases and passes included, so a player who paid or took the free game on the portal's Pickman card plays at once. The lobby keeps selling Pickman, on the library too, until f0209 and c0188 replace and retire it.

Continues.BACK_SECONDS = 10        -- unchanged
Continues.BACK_MESSAGE = "Start a game from the lobby."  -- unchanged (f0187 rewords the way back)

- **The arrival** (once per arrival: `charged[player]`, as before, so `Sessions.made` calling twice charges and tells once): in its own thread, `local verdict, start = Wallet.arrive(player, "pickman")`; a player gone by then gets nothing (f0145 design review 003 F5). `"play"` switches the offer on (`Session.allowOffer`) and, when the claim was written (`start ~= nil`), tells the player what they hold (below); `"back"` refuses the run and sends the player to `LobbyPlaceId`, kicked on every failure, exactly as f0145 (§5 quoted above). So the arrival takes, in Wallet's order: the free start due, else one held play, else the pass `pickman/<user id>` (written by the lobby's `Paid` or by the portal's `Plays` through `Wallet.pass`), and a claim that failed or timed out plays (G10); "Let all of them play" holds (a pass map that cannot be read counts as a pass, f0201 §4).
- **What the player holds** (`tell`): `PayStatus:FireClient(player, Wallet.mode("pickman"), PaidPlay.status(record, os.time()))` with `record = Wallet.read(player, "pickman")`, called only right after a write of this server for that player (the arrival's claim, a revive used), when the library answers from the record it saw with no store call. The status is now the library's (`revives` where f0145 sent `continues`); OfferView reads it (§5).
- **The offer** (f0145's holds, flight and refund, on the library):
  - `"ask"` during the offer and not while held (as before): `useOne` holds the countdown for the write (judged at the ask), calls `Wallet.useRevive(player, "pickman")`, and then: `true` and the player still here, in the same session, offering and not declined → `Session.continue` and tell; `true` otherwise → `Wallet.refund(player, "pickman", "revive")` (one more revive, outside the receipts), then the noted decline; `false` (none held, known from the record seen, no write) or `nil` (the library cannot tell: its `USE_SECONDS` since the last use, the store failing) → the flight's hold is released and the revive product is offered: `if Receipts.ask(player, "pickman", "revive") then Session.hold(s, id, true) end`, so a closed title (Receipts opens nothing and answers false) sells nothing and holds nothing ("Refuse paid play").
  - f0145's own bounds (`known`, `asked`, `Continues.ASK_SECONDS`) go: the library's bounds replace them (no write while none is known held; at most one revive write per `Wallet.USE_SECONDS` = 6 s; one at a time). The flight (`flying`) stays: it notes a decline during the write.
  - `"decline"` ends the offer at the next step, or is noted during a flight and applied after it (unchanged).
  - **Receipts' handlers** (`Receipts.start({ granted, closed })`, once, here): `granted(player, "pickman", "revive")` → `heard`: the revive is used at once during the offer (`useOne`), and when the library answers `nil` (the grant arrived within `USE_SECONDS` of the last use's write, which the first ask of an offer makes when nothing was known) it is tried once more `Wallet.USE_SECONDS` later, while the receipt wait holds the countdown; a grant of any other title or kind (a play bought for later, another game's receipt retried here) is credited by the library and starts nothing. `closed(player, "pickman", "revive", bought)` during the offer: bought → `Session.awaitReceipt` (Roblox does not order the close and the receipt; lesson pickman-arch/purchase-close-before-receipt), not bought → the countdown runs again; another title's or kind's screen changes nothing. A test confirm whose write fails closes not bought (Receipts reports `closed(…, false)`), so the countdown runs again: f0145 left it held.
  - `TeleportInitFailed` and `PlayerRemoving` as before.
- **Revives on the boards**: f0186's count (`Game.revives`, the sim) is unchanged and is what the boards show; `Wallet.revives` is counted by the library and not read here (f0186's interface, quoted).

### 3. The lobby: `pickman/src/lobby/server/Paid.luau` (rewritten on the library)
The lobby keeps selling Pickman until f0209 makes the portal the start place and c0188 deletes it, so its gate moves onto the library too: the capture's "no pickman-only copy" covers both places, and a lobby still on `Purchases` would install a second, different ProcessReceipt and write f0145's pass key, which the new game place does not take.
```luau
Paid.TITLE = "pickman"
function Paid.allow(player: Player): boolean   -- may yield (the read)
function Paid.start(press: (Player) -> ())      -- unchanged signature; Main is unchanged
```
- `allow`: `local record, problem = Wallet.read(player, "pickman")`; a player gone by then → false; `"down"` → `Wallet.pass(player, "pickman")` and true (the play place lets that one game go free, "Let them play free"); `"busy"` (the read on joining, or another press's, still under way) → false (the press is dropped); a record → tell, then true when `PaidPlay.status(record, os.time())` says free or plays > 0 (the lobby charges nothing; the arrival does), else `Receipts.ask(player, "pickman", "play")` (nothing on a closed server) and false.
- `start`: makes `PayStatus`, starts `Receipts` with `granted(player, "pickman", "play")` → tell (from the grant's record, which the library saw) and press START for them; tells each joining player what they hold (`Players.PlayerAdded` → `Wallet.read` → tell when it answered a record).
- f0145's own bounds go (`asking`, `kept`, `Paid.PRESS_SECONDS`): the library's read bounds replace them (one read at a time; within `READ_SECONDS` = 60 s of the record seen, no store call; after a failed read, "down" with no store call for `RETRY_SECONDS` = 6 s). Consequences: the read on joining now answers the presses after it for a minute (f0145 read again after 2 s); the free hour is judged at each press with `os.time()`, and a grant on this server refreshes the record seen (f0201 §4), so the only writes the minute can miss are another server's, which a lobby player's record does not get while they are here (a purchase is processed where the buyer is). A press made while the read on joining is still under way is dropped, where f0145 read again for it: the player sees START do nothing once and presses again (listed under "No behaviour change" as a change a player can see).
- **A play granted while a read is under way** (design review 001 F4): a receipt Roblox retries as the player joins, or a purchase finished then, writes the record while the read on joining is in flight. That read then answers "busy" to the grant's press, and when it ends it stores the record it began with, from before the grant, for `READ_SECONDS` (the library's `read` sees its result after `grant` saw the newer one). The bought game starts once (Starts' Travel refuses a second start while one is under way), the play stays held for the arrival, and nothing is prompted again (AC3, lobby :: "a play receipt granted while the read on joining is under way starts the game once").
