# c0342: Speeds spec ramp cleanup

## Problem
- After f0147 is done, `pickman/tests/acceptance/f0125/speeds.spec.luau` still reads the config's ramp and `Levels.ghostRate` through `:: any`, still has the fallback `levels.ghostRate or levels.ghostSpeed`, and still wraps the ramp-top bound case in `if (Session.GAME_CONFIG :: any).ramp then`.
- Its case "the player's speed in the shipped config stays within one tile a tick on every level, frightened or not" loops to `Levels.COUNT + 1` (22 levels) while the other loops follow `levelsOf(Session.GAME_CONFIG)` (34), so its title is untrue for levels 23 to 34.

## Non-goals
- Nothing under `pickman/src` changes.
- In c0177's `narrowed.spec.luau`, only the digest and the counts whose needles the cleanup alters change; every other case and count stays as it is.
- No case is removed from `speeds.spec.luau` and no bound in it is loosened.
- The change does not start before f0147 and c0177 are done.

## Solution
- `speeds.spec.luau` contains no `:: any` read of the ramp or of `Levels.ghostRate`, no `levels.ghostRate or levels.ghostSpeed`, and no `if (Session.GAME_CONFIG :: any).ramp then`; the ramp-top bound case runs unconditionally.
- The player case loops to `levelsOf(Session.GAME_CONFIG) + 1`, and every other case keeps its assertions unchanged or stricter.
- In the same change, c0177's `pickman/tests/acceptance/c0177/narrowed.spec.luau` moves its whole-file digest to the new text, and each of its text counts that the cleanup changes is updated to the new text's true count: the guard count at line 76 goes from 1 to 0, and the `set.tuning, ramp)` count at line 71 is restated against whatever spelling the cleaned file uses.
- The issue's Regression guard lists both `pickman/tests/acceptance/f0125/speeds.spec.luau` and `pickman/tests/acceptance/c0177/narrowed.spec.luau` as Overrides, and both specs pass after the change.
