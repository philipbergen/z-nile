# d0149: Touch circle controls

## Problem
The user, 2026-10-25: "the touch controls do not work. Show two circles on screen that users pull on to steer (left circle) and view (right circle), tapping right circle is jump. add circle-buttons for shoot and pick on the right".

Touch play shipped in R0012 (f0027 swipe turns, f0028 the look drag, f0029 the mobile HUD), but it doesn't work on a phone. This issue redesigns the touch controls:
- **Left circle steers:** an on-screen circle on the left that the player pulls on (drags from its centre) to steer.
- **Right circle looks:** a circle on the right that the player pulls on to turn the view.
- **Tap the right circle to jump** (the spy hop, f0101).
- **Circle-buttons on the right to shoot and to pick:** shoot fires the blaster (f0100); pick starts a wall pick (f0126, being prepped; the server takes `Picks.INTENT`, "Pick", on the Intent remote).

Decided with the user (2026-10-25): finding why today's touch input reaches nothing is split out as d0151, done first; f0110 (Touch fire and hop, R0051) lands before this as a stopgap with FIRE and JUMP buttons on the same input path; d0149 keeps the circle redesign.

Decided with the user (2026-10-25), answered while R0051 (f0110) was in design, for the circles:
- Steering with the left circle: "Same as keys awsd is left-down-up-right, same as the cursor keys": a pull left, down, up or right is A, S, W or D (`Left`, `Back`, `Forward`, `Right`, relative to the heading, through the turn buffer), the same four directions as the keys.
- The circles are fixed in place (lower left and lower right, in the safe area, at least 44 pt), not where the thumb lands.
- No pick button until f0126 (wall picks) is built; then it sends `Picks.INTENT`. (The order the user set is d0151, f0110, f0126, d0149, so f0126 should be done by the time this is.)
- No snap-look button: releasing the right circle springs the view back to the heading, as releasing an arrow key does (f0104).
- This issue's prep owns a chore that narrows or removes the promoted `tests/acceptance/f0110/` specs (R0051's FIRE and JUMP stopgap) where the circles change what they pin.

Coordination (2026-10-26, recorded at capture by session pickman-ac): f0143 (speed boost) owns every boost trigger on every device (the user's decisions: a W double tap, a D-pad Up double tap, L3, two quick swipes up on touch, a left-stick double push up). This issue replaces the swipes with the left circle, so a quick double pull up on the left circle must also boost through f0143's double-tap fold, and d0149 lands after f0143. The circles must also clear f0158's danger radar ring (centred, 60% of the smaller side) and f0126's pick bar and boon banner.

Decided with the user (2026-10-31), how a pull on the right circle looks: "Points the view (Recommended)": the pull's direction is where the player looks, relative to the heading, as the arrow keys do but in any direction (up ahead, left a quarter turn left, down behind), eased at the arrows' turn rate with the pitch level; aiming follows it as it follows the arrows.
Decided with the user (2026-10-31), how a tap on the right circle is told from a pull: "Short tap, jump on lift (Recommended)": a finger that lifts within 0.25 s and has moved less than 12 px is a tap and hops when it lifts; anything else only looks.
Decided with the user (2026-10-31), f0158 (the danger radar ring) and this issue: "f0158 first (Recommended)": d0149 lands after f0158 and keeps its circles and buttons clear of the ring; a chore narrows f0158's clearance pins on the buttons this issue moves or removes.
Decided with the user (2026-10-31), a finger that lands outside the circles and buttons: "Nothing (Recommended)": only the controls respond; the free drag look and the swipe halves go, and touch keeps the view level (no pitch).
Decided with the user (2026-10-31), the circles' size: "30% of short side (Recommended)": each circle's diameter is 30% of the screen's shorter side, between 96 and 160 px.
Decided with the user (2026-10-31), the one overlap left: "Accept it (Recommended)": on the 480 x 320 screen under a 58 px top bar, PICK may draw over a few of the ring's dots (as f0158's G10 lets f0126's PICK there); any other overlap fails.

## Design
Decisions this relies on (`pickman/.tracker/game-design.md`): every input produces the same intents (*Platforms*, f0146: "We support keyboard, cosole, and touch for every feature"); the server is authoritative and clients send intents (*Authority*); the arrow look turns at the camera's turn rate and springs back the same way, two arrows that tie aim the way of travel (*Ghost hunter tools*, Keys, f0104); touch targets are at least 44 pt (*Mobile*, HUD); PICK is the touch pick (*Wall picks*, f0126); the boost's touch trigger is two quick swipes up (f0143's G5, in its *Speed boost* section once it lands), which this issue turns into two quick pulls up, as the capture's coordination line asks.

**Checklist (defect).**
- *Reproduction*: on `main` a phone has no circles: `Touch.buttons` returns `snap`, `fire` and `hop` rectangles and no `steer` or `look` circle, a finger on the lower left is a swipe zone that turns only on its lift, and a drag on the right half looks freely. `acceptance/fold.spec.luau` and `adapters.spec.luau` fail on `main` for those reasons (`Touch.buttons(...).look` is nil; Controls never calls `point`).
- *Expected behaviour*: the user's words (2026-10-25): "Show two circles on screen that users pull on to steer (left circle) and view (right circle), tapping right circle is jump. add circle-buttons for shoot and pick on the right". It reworks R0012 (f0027, f0028, f0029) and R0051 (f0110).
- *Root cause*: R0012's gesture design (swipe on lift, free drag) is what the user rejects; the input path itself works since d0151. No spec caught it because the specs pinned the gestures as designed.
- *Siblings*: every touch control is in `Touch` and drawn by `HudView`; the gamepad (f0146) and keys are not touch and stay. f0126's PICK and f0143's swipe boost are the only other touch rules, both handled here.
- *Regression guard*: see below; the replaced pins go through c0182.
- *Smallest change*: Touch's fold is rewritten (its gestures are the defect); View gains one optional field; the adapters change only their touch wiring.

**Touch (pure, `pickman/src/shared/Logic/Touch/init.luau`; the full prototype is `experiments/prototype.patch`).** Every point is in screen pixels, as today.
- Types: `Zone = "steer" | "look" | "fire" | "pick"`; `Rect` as today (half-open); `Circle = { x, y, r: number }` (centre and radius; open: a point at distance `r` is outside); `Point = { x, y }`; `Event = { id, phase, x, y: number, t: number? }` (`t` seconds on any one clock, nil counts as 0); `Finger = { zone, x0, y0, t0, x, y, far: boolean, looking: boolean, steer: Pad.Steer }`; `Effect = { kind: "turn", intent: Turn } | { kind: "point", quarters: number? } | { kind: "hop" } | { kind: "fire" } | { kind: "pick" }`; `Buttons = { steer: Circle, look: Circle, fire: Rect, pick: Rect }`; `Context = { steer: Circle?, look: Circle?, fire: Rect?, pick: Rect? }`; `Knobs = { steer: Point?, look: Point? }`; `Area`, `Box` as today.
- Constants: `MIN_TARGET = 44`, `CIRCLE_SHARE = 0.3`, `CIRCLE_MIN = 96`, `CIRCLE_MAX = 160` (the user's), `BUTTON_SHARE = 0.45`, `TAP_SECONDS = 0.25`, `TAP_SLOP = 12` (the user's), `LOOK_DEAD = 0.25` (prep's: a pull under a quarter of the radius points at the heading once the finger is looking).
- `Touch.zone(x, y, ctx): Zone?`: FIRE, then PICK (rectangles), then the steer and the look circle (discs); nil anywhere else and for a non-finite point.
- `Touch.pull(c, x, y): (number, number)`: the finger as a stick tilt of `c`, x right, y up, divided by the radius and cut to length 1; a bad point or circle is 0, 0.
- `Touch.quarters(x, y): number?`: where a tilt points, in `View.arrowQuarters`' quarter turns (up 0, left 1, right -1, down 2), in (-2, 2] (straight down is 2 whatever the sign of x's zero); nil inside `LOOK_DEAD` or for a non-finite tilt.
- `Touch.track(state, event, ctx): (State, { Effect })`, never changing `state`:
  - *begin*: the zone of the point (none: the finger is not recorded and does nothing for its life); a zone another finger holds is ignored (one finger a control). FIRE emits `fire`, PICK `pick`, at once. A steer finger folds its pull through `Pad.steer` from `Pad.steerStart()` (so a finger that lands already pulled steers at once). A look finger emits nothing yet: it may be a tap, and the user's rule is that a tap only hops (design review 001 F2: pointing at once swung the view a quarter turn or more on nearly every tap that missed the exact centre).
  - *move*: the finger keeps its zone (sliding off its control changes nothing); `far` becomes true once it is `TAP_SLOP` or more from where it went down, and stays true. A steer finger folds its pull through `Pad.steer` and emits each `turn` the fold emits (`Pad.STEER_ON` 0.6 engages, `STEER_OFF` 0.4 releases, `STEER_MARGIN` keeps a wobble on the diagonal from flipping: one turn a push, as the keys). A look finger becomes `looking` (decided to be a pull) on the move where it is `far` or `t - t0 >= TAP_SECONDS`, and stays so; while looking, every move emits `point` with `quarters` of its pull. FIRE and PICK fingers do nothing.
  - *end*: a look finger that was looking emits `point` nil and never hops (it was no tap); one that was not looking emits `hop` if it never went far, lifts under `TAP_SLOP` from its start, and `t - t0 < TAP_SECONDS`, so a tap's whole output is one `hop`. Nothing else emits on a lift.
- `Touch.hold(state, id, ctx): (State, { Effect })`, never changing `state`: a tap's time has passed for finger `id` (Controls' timer). If that finger is still down on the look circle and not yet looking, it becomes looking and emits `point` from where it rests (a thumb held still off the centre sends no moves); anything else (lifted, let go, already looking, another control, no look circle) does nothing. The timer is the witness that the time passed, so no clock is compared (design review 003 F3: a one-shot timer judged against `os.clock()` could fire a hair early and never point).
- `Touch.same(a: Context?, b: Context?): boolean`: the same places (every centre, radius and rectangle equal), whatever the tables; a missing control (or a missing set) only matches a missing one, so a hook that hands partial controls never crashes it (design review 003 F4).
- `Touch.knobs(state, ctx): Knobs`: each held circle's finger, cut to the circle's rim (`pull` scaled back to pixels).
- `Touch.buttons(box): Buttons`: diameter `d = clamp(round(CIRCLE_SHARE × min(safeW, safeH)), 96, 160)` (the safe rectangle's shorter side: the screen's less the notch and the top bar; the samples' values equal the screen's except under a top bar, where both clamp to 96), `r = d / 2`, margin `m`. Look circle: right edge `safeX + safeW - m`, bottom `safeY + safeH - m - barHeight` (on the power bar's top: one margin higher meets the radar by 2 px on 480 × 320 and 568 × 320 under a top bar, E3). The row's bottom `safeY + safeH - 2m - max(iconSize, barHeight)` (one margin above the lives row, so PICK clears f0143's boost chevron there on portrait phones, E3): the steer circle's left edge `safeX + m` on it; FIRE a square of `max(MIN_TARGET, round(BUTTON_SHARE × d))`, one margin left of the look circle, bottom on the row; PICK the same size, one margin left of FIRE.
- `Touch.toScreen`, `Touch.blocks` and `Touch.screenSize` stay as they are. `Touch.swipe`, `Touch.snapRect`, `Touch.hopRect`, `Touch.fireRect`, `Touch.pickRect` and the `MIN_SWIPE` and `AXIS_DEGREES` constants go (their promoted pins are c0182's). Touch requires `Pad` (new edge `Touch -> Pad`, declared now in `architecture.md`) and no longer `Hud` or `View`, whose edges the executor removes (AC5).

**View (pure).** `View.Arrows` gains `point: number?`: the look circle's target in quarter turns. `View.arrowQuarters(a)` returns it when no arrow is held and it is finite; held arrows win over it; without it every rule is f0104's. So `View.steer` turns the look toward it at `View.turnRate()` with the pitch going level, and springs back to the heading when it becomes nil, and `View.aim` aims by it with f0104's tie rule; neither changes. (Carrying it in the arrows, not as a new `View.steer` argument, keeps Camera's `View.steer(steer, look, arrows, dt)` call, which f0104's promoted `place.spec` pins word for word: E4.)

**Adapters.**
- `Controls` (`src/client/Controls.luau`): `Keys` gains `point: ((quarters: number?) -> ())?`. `Controls.start(onSnap: () -> (), onTouch: ((held: boolean) -> ())?, buttons: (() -> Touch.Buttons?)?, onKnobs: ((Touch.Knobs) -> ())?)` (the look, snap-rectangle hooks go). Each touch event is folded with `t = os.clock()` against `buttons()` (nil: an empty context, so nothing responds). Effects: `turn` sends the intent, and a `Forward` also folds f0143's `touchTaps` (`Boost.tap(touchTaps, os.clock())`) and sends `Boost.INTENT` on a pair, unchanged from f0143's code; `point` calls `keys.point`; `hop` sends `Hop.INTENT`; `fire` the aimed shot (`Blaster.intent(keys.aim())`); `pick` `View.PICK_INTENT`. After each event it calls `onKnobs(Touch.knobs(...))`. Every path into the fold goes through one local `step(fold)`: it reads the controls first (`controlsNow()`, which lets every finger go when they moved), then folds, applies the effects and reports the knobs; a touch event's step folds `Touch.track`, and the timer's step folds `Touch.hold` (design review 005 F1: a timer that read the fold before the controls brought back a thumb a re-layout had let go, a quarter turn from a thumb that never moved). When a look finger goes down it schedules `task.delay(Touch.TAP_SECONDS, …)`, whose step folds `Touch.hold(state, id, …)` on the fold as it is when the timer fires (not as it was at the press: design review 003 F1; ids are never reused, so a finger lifted, let go on focus loss or let go on a re-layout meanwhile is simply not there) and applies the effects (one timer a look finger, no per-frame loop: a `RunService.Heartbeat` connection turned all of f0126's Controls cases red, its stub having no RunService, design review 002 F2). When `buttons()` returns controls in other places than the ones the held fingers were measured against (`Touch.same`, by value: HudView builds a new table on every HUD refresh, a shot or a life, so comparing tables dropped both thumbs on every shot, design review 002 F1; a real move is a turn or the top bar shown or hidden), it first lets every finger go, as on `WindowFocusReleased`: the fold is emptied, `keys.point(nil)` and `onKnobs({})` are called, and the fingers still down stay unheard until they lift (design review 001 F7: a held steer finger's pull was measured from the moved centre, a phantom turn; lesson folds-reset-when-their-source-changes). On `WindowFocusReleased` it does the same and forgets the ids. `GuiService.TouchControlsEnabled = false` stays (Roblox's thumbstick and jump button stay hidden). The `held` hook (Camera.holdMouse) is as today.
- `Camera` (`src/client/Camera.luau`): `Camera.point(quarters)` stores it as the arrows' `point`; `Camera.arrows(a)` keeps the stored point. `Camera.lookBy` goes. Nothing else changes: the look, the aim and the spring-back already run through `View.steer` and `View.aim` on `arrows`.
- `HudView` (`src/client/HudView.luau`): on touch screens only (`Visible = UserInputService.TouchEnabled`), non-active frames under its `Hud` ScreenGui: `Steer` and `Look` (white, 0.8 transparent, a white `UIStroke`, `UICorner` 0.5) on each circle's bounding square, `SteerKnob` and `LookKnob` (0.5 transparent, 0.4 of the diameter) centred at each knob point or at the circle's centre, and f0110's `Fire` (cyan) and f0126's `Pick` (gold) discs at their rectangles. The `Snap` frame, the `Hop` (JUMP) button and `HudView.snapRect` go. `HudView.buttons()` returns `Touch.buttons(l)` of the last layout; `HudView.knobs(k)` places the knobs and remembers them; a layout whose controls moved (`Touch.same` false: a turn, the top bar) centres the knobs, and a refresh in the same places (a shot, a life: HudView lays the HUD out again on those) redraws the remembered knobs where the thumbs are (design review 003 F2).
- `Main.client.luau`: `Controls.keys({ arrows = Camera.arrows, aim = Camera.aim, point = Camera.point })`, `Controls.start(Camera.snap, Camera.holdMouse, HudView.buttons, HudView.knobs)`.
- Server: nothing. Every touch sends an intent the server already takes (the turns, `Hop`, the fire intents, `Pick`, `Boost`), and looking is the client's.

### Scalability
Per touch event one fold over at most four fingers, one `Pad.steer` call and one `Touch.knobs`; one `task.delay` per look finger going down; per frame nothing new (HudView's existing re-lays, on a screen change and on a shot or life refresh, add one `Touch.same` and four frame placements; Camera reads the point it already holds). No new replication: the intents are the existing ones, at most one per pull or tap, under the server's 4-intents-a-step limit.

### Maintainability
All geometry and gesture rules are in Touch (pure, unit-tested); the steer rule is the stick's (`Pad.steer`), so a change to steering changes both at once; the look target is one optional field the camera code already reads. A new touch button is one `Rect` in `Buttons`, one zone and one effect.

### Usability
Two thumbs, two fixed circles where the thumbs rest; the left steers exactly as the stick and the keys (one turn a pull, through the turn buffer), the right points the view like the arrows and springs back, so a player always knows where "ahead" is; knobs show each pull. FIRE and PICK are big (0.45 of a circle, at least 44 px) and act on the press. Nothing outside the controls reacts, so a resting palm does nothing. The cost the user chose: no pitch on touch.
