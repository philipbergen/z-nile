# d0149: Touch circle controls

## Problem
The user, 2026-10-25: "the touch controls do not work. Show two circles on screen that users pull on to steer (left circle) and view (right circle), tapping right circle is jump. add circle-buttons for shoot and pick on the right".

Touch play shipped in R0012 (f0027 touch turn controls, f0028 touch look, f0029 the mobile HUD layout), but on a phone the controls do not work: players on touch devices can't steer, look or play. What should happen:
- **Left circle steers:** an on-screen circle on the left that the player pulls on (drags from its centre) to steer.
- **Right circle looks:** a circle on the right that the player pulls on to turn the view.
- **Tap the right circle to jump** (the spy hop, f0101).
- **Circle-buttons on the right to shoot and to pick:** shoot fires the blaster (f0100); pick starts a wall pick (f0126).

Scope decision (user, 2026-10-25): "Fold f0110 into the new defect": f0110 ("Touch fire and hop": touch had no way to fire the blaster or spy hop, and the shot bars pile up unused) is folded into this issue and removed.

## Non-goals
- The wall pick itself (f0126): the pick button needs it; until f0126 is done the button is absent or inert (prep asks which).
- Gamepad controls (f0146).

## Solution
- Prep finds why today's touch controls fail (f0027 / f0028 on a real phone: Roblox's default touch thumbstick and jump button, our input handlers, the layout from f0029) before redesigning them; the user's two-circle layout replaces them.
- Open for prep to put to the user: whether the left circle maps a pull to the maze's four headings (with the turn buffer) or steers continuously; the circles' size and placement (fixed, or where the thumb first lands), and the 44 pt minimum from game-design.md's Mobile rules; how a tap on the right circle is told from a pull (time and distance thresholds); where the shoot and pick buttons sit relative to the right circle and the HUD (radar, power bar, snap control); whether the snap-look button stays.
- Keyboard and mouse controls stay as they are; the circles produce the same intents.
