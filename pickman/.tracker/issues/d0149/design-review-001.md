### F2 [high] A tap on the look circle anywhere outside its inner quarter swings the view (up to 180°) before it hops
Where: Design, Touch `track` *begin* ("a look finger emits `point` with `quarters` of its pull"); fold.spec's look and tap cases; Rules table (no row).
Evidence: probe on the prototype, look circle r 50 at (500, 300), a finger down at (530, 300) at t 0 and up there at t 0.1 (a textbook tap: 0.1 s, 0 px): effects `point:-1 point:nil hop:nil`. `View.TURN_SECONDS = 0.12` a quarter turn, so in the 0.1–0.25 s of a tap the view turns a full quarter right, or, for a tap a quarter-radius below the centre, toward behind (quarters 2), then springs back while the hop runs. `LOOK_DEAD` is 0.25 of r, 12–20 px on a 96–160 px circle, so a thumb resting on a fixed circle almost never lands inside it. Every fold.spec tap and look case puts the finger down on the exact centre, so nothing pins what an off-centre tap does, and a mutant that ignores the pull on *begin* (emits `point nil`) also survives (each look case begins on the centre).
Critique: the user's decision is a partition: "a finger that lifts within 0.25 s and has moved less than 12 px is a tap and hops when it lifts; anything else only looks". The design makes nearly every tap both look and hop, so the captured "tapping right circle is jump" is delivered with a view flick on every jump, and a tap that is meant to hop also re-aims a FIRE pressed during it. The tempting fix is to enlarge `LOOK_DEAD`, which only shrinks the look circle's usable area and still flicks on a tap near the rim.
Consequences: holding the look until the gesture is decided means the fold must decide without a move event: a thumb that lands off-centre and stays still has to start looking once `TAP_SECONDS` passes, so Touch needs a time input (a `tick` phase, or Controls re-folding the held look finger each frame with the clock) and Controls a per-frame hook; a pull starts looking only once it is `TAP_SLOP` from where it went down (12 px, under a frame at a thumb's speed), and a held still finger 0.25 s late.
Recommendation: a look finger emits no `point` until it is decided to be a pull: on the move (or tick) where it becomes `far`, or where `t - t0 >= TAP_SECONDS`; from then on as now. Add a `tick` event (or say how Controls feeds time) to the Design, with fold cases: an off-centre tap (down and up at 0.6 r, 0.1 s) emits only `hop`; an off-centre finger held still emits its `point` on the first tick at or after `TAP_SECONDS`; a pull emits its `point` on the move that makes it far; and an adapters case that Controls ticks the fold. Rules rows for each, with mutants (point on begin; no tick). If the author prefers the flick for responsiveness, that trade-off goes to the user (it changes how their decided tap behaves); don't keep it silently.
