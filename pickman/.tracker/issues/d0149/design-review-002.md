### F1 [high] Every shot fired or gained, and every life lost, lets go of the held steer and look fingers (the review-001 F7 fix compares tables by identity)
Where: prototype `src/client/Controls.luau:161-170` (`controlsNow`: `if placed ~= laidOut then … forget()`); `src/client/HudView.luau:136-137` (`lay` builds a new `Touch.buttons(l)` table on every call) and its callers in `HudView.shots` (any change in the shot count), `HudView.show` (any change in lives) and `HudView.pick` (the first pick frame); d0149.md Design, Controls ("When `buttons()` returns a different table … HudView makes a new one on every re-layout: a turn, the top bar shown or hidden").
Evidence: probe on the prototype (a copy of adapters.spec with one more case): the real HudView (`hudView(true)`), Controls fed `h.hud.buttons()`, a steer finger down on the circle's centre, then `h.hud.shots(2)` (the server reports one shot fewer: the player pressed FIRE), then a pull left to 0.9 r. Output: `same table: false same geometry: true`, `fired after a pull left: 0`, and the case fails. The finger is dropped and stays unheard until it lifts. In play, pressing FIRE spends a shot, the next snapshot calls `HudView.shots`, and the next touch event or Heartbeat drops both thumbs. Steering stops after every shot until the thumb is lifted and put back. A look in progress springs back, and a tap on the look circle that spans the snapshot loses its hop. Every adapters case stubs `buttons()` to return one fixed table (`current`), so the spec can't tell identity from equal geometry, and the Design's claim that HudView makes a new table "on a turn, the top bar shown or hidden" is not what HudView does.
Critique: the tempting fix is to have `lay` reuse the old table when nothing moved, which keeps identity as the signal and leaves the next caller of `lay` (f0147 adds HUD blocks to HudView, CV2) one edit away from the same break. The question is what changed, not which table it is: the fold measures fingers against the four shapes.
Consequences: a value comparison needs one definition of "the same controls" (the four shapes' numbers, exact). A re-layout that moves only FIRE or PICK also lets go, which is harmless because those fingers act on the press. No new user decision.
Recommendation: Controls lets every finger go only when the controls' geometry changed. Make this a pure `Touch.same(a: Buttons?, b: Buttons?): boolean` (each circle's x, y, r and each rectangle's x, y, w, h equal; nil equals only nil), with a fold.spec case (an equal deep copy is the same; each of the 14 numbers changed by 1 is not; nil against a layout is not), and use it in `controlsNow`. Add adapters.spec cases: (a) `w.relayout(deepCopy(PLACED))` while the steer circle is held, then a pull left, which expects `{ "Left" }` and no `point nil`; (b) the real-HudView wiring above (`hud.shots`, `hud.show` with a life fewer) keeps a held finger. Add Rules rows with the mutants "identity comparison" (killed by (a)) and "never lets go" (C9, still killed). Correct the Design sentence on when HudView re-lays.
Lesson: zz-test/seed-every-enumerated-case
