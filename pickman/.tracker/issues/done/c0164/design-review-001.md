### F1 [high] The case never moves from edible straight into a non-playing phase, so a Main that freezes the edible state outside play passes, though the text pin kills it
Where: `experiments/views.patch` (the `steps` list: playing 0, 6, 6, 0, 0, then levelclear with frightened 0); Design "The case"; Problem "must kill at least what the text pin kills".
Evidence: mutant S2 adds `local lastEdible = false` above the handler and replaces the walls block with `if frame.play.phase == "playing" then lastEdible = View.edible(frame.play) end; if model then MazeView.edible(model, lastEdible) end`. In the clone, the new case and every views.spec case pass, while `main-walls.spec` fails both its cases. Mutant S2r, the same edit on the radar call (`DangerHud.wallState(overlay.flash, lastEdible)`), behaves the same way: views.spec is green and main-walls.spec is red. In the game, eating the last pellet while the power runs goes straight from (playing, frightened > 0) to levelclear with the power zeroed (View.edible's own comment: "0 on a death, at level clear"). Under S2 the walls and the radar stay green through the level-clear flash's dark beats and through a death's pause. The case's level-clear frames come only after two frames that are already back at 0, so the stale value happens to be right.
Critique: this is the real producer's transition, which zz-test/seed-every-enumerated-case says to walk ("which input combinations does the real producer hand it"). Without it, the Problem's "kills at least what the text pin kills" is false for a plausible "only update during play" Main. The tempting fix is to add an S2 row marked reasoned. That leaves the mutant alive.
Consequences: views.patch changes, so the SHA-256 in `walls.spec` must be recomputed. The Problem's sequence (0, > 0, 0 with the flash unchanged) stays as frames 1–5, and the new frame only adds a transition. E2 and the Rules table gain rows. Every existing mutant must still die and P4, C1 and C2 must stay green. In my clone, inserting one `playing(6)` step (edible = true) just before `levelclear(Flow.FLASH_TICKS)` kills S2 and S2r, and `mutants.py` still prints every N, P and A row killed (N3 OTHER) and P4, C1 and C2 green; views.spec gives 11 passed.
Recommendation: in views.patch, put a `playing(6)` step immediately before the first level-clear frame. Also add, after a further `playing(6)`, a frame with `phase = "dying"` and frightened 0 (Flow.overlay has a `dying` branch; prove it runs with the stub's nil trip on the prototype, and expect flash false and edible false). Add S2 and S2r to `mutants.py` and as `run` rows in the Rules table ("the edible state is handed over in every phase, not frozen outside play"). Recompute the digest and re-record E2 and E3.
Lesson: zz-test/seed-every-enumerated-case

Candidate lessons (for the lead to place):
