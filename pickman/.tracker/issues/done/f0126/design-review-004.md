### F1 [high] The adapter checks are source-text pins that a wrong adapter passes: no gold walls at all, a pick bar that is never placed, and walls rebuilt every frame all keep the 10 files and the whole suite green (partly a repeat of design-review-003 F3's adapter pin)
Where: `acceptance/adapters.spec.luau` (all cases are `has`/`matches` over comment-stripped source); AC6; Behaviour 9; Rules A1 to A8; the user's scope decision "only interior walls with special color" and "Gold"
Evidence: mutants of the prototype's adapters, each run against all 10 sequestered files (`109 passed, 0 failed` for every one):
- W6, `MazeView`: `for _, r in View.walls(m, nil, nil) do` in place of `View.walls(m, nil, marked)`. No wall is ever gold. The pin `View%.walls%(%s*[%w_]+%s*,%s*[%w_]+%s*,%s*[%w_%.]+%s*%)` matches `nil`, and the gold literal, `"MarkedWall"` and `.marked` are all still in the file.
- W3, `HudView.lay`: `if false and pickBar and banner then`. The bar and the banner are never positioned or sized: a Roblox `Frame` keeps its default 100×100 box at the top left, over the score. The pin only asks that `Hud.pickBox(` and `Hud.boonBox(` appear somewhere and that `UDim2.fromScale(0.5` does not.
- W1: `pickBar.Position = UDim2.new(0.5, -barBox.w / 2, 0.93, 0)`. The box is computed and then ignored, and the bar sits in the bottom row over the lives and the power bar.
- W2: `Hud.pickBox(Hud.layout(sw, sh))`. The safe inset is dropped, so on a notched phone the bar is off-centre and the banner can sit under the notch.
- W4, `Main`: drop `wallsKey = key`. About 150 wall parts are destroyed and rebuilt 15 times a second (Scalability says "only when `View.wallsKey` changes").

With W3 and W6 applied together, the whole pickman suite plus the 10 files gives `1514 passed, 0 failed, 217 spec files`. The parts.spec geometry case (R3e, R3f) and the `View.walls` cases (P7, P8) pin the pure functions. Nothing checks that the adapters use what those functions return.
Critique: AC6 says marked walls are gold and the HUD places the bar and the banner at `Hud.pickBox` and `Hud.boonBox`, and a wrong implementation passes it. Gold walls are the only way a player can tell which walls to pick: without them the feature can't be played. Round 2 (F5) and round 3 (F3) each fixed one pin, and each fix was a better spelling of the same text match, so this is a mechanism problem (zz-review: question the mechanism). The tempting fix is more patterns, such as `View.walls(m, nil, marked)` spelled out or `fromOffset(barBox.x`. That pins variable names the Design doesn't name (zz-test/source-pins-name-what-the-design-names), and the next mutant renames a local or ignores a result. The repository already runs these adapters' real source on a stub engine: `tests/place/hud-shots.spec.luau` loads HudView with `luau.load` and a fake `workspace.CurrentCamera.ViewportSize` and `GuiService:GetInsetArea`, and reads where each frame ends up. f0014, f0031, f0065 and f0101's place specs do the same for other client scripts.
Consequences: behavioural place cases load HudView and MazeView with module tables. f0110 also changes HudView (and Controls and Main), and its sequestered `adapters.spec` loads them with closed tables. So the new f0126 cases must fall back to the real module for any require they don't list (pickman-test/stub-loaders-fall-back), or they turn red when f0110 lands first. They must also not depend on HudView's other frames: f0110 adds fire and hop buttons. Main is the hardest to load (it connects remotes at the top level). If a stub-engine run of Main is too costly, its rebuild-once rule can move into a pure function the case calls, for example `View.wallsChanged(lastKey, frame)` or a small `WallsSync` step in View that Main calls once. That is one more interface to name in the Design.
Recommendation: question the mechanism. Pin each adapter rule the Design states by what the adapter builds, on the stub engine, and keep the source pins only for "no new require" and "nothing new fires the server". Add these cases (each a Rules row, run with the mutants above):
- (a) MazeView: `MazeView.build(layout)`, then `rewall(model, View.frameMaze(frame), marks, false)` with two holes and three marks. Exactly three `MarkedWall` parts, coloured (255, 191, 0), at `View.world` of the marked tiles. No part over a hole. The pellet children survive. `flash(model, true)` lights all of them, and `flash(model, false)` gives the marked ones back their gold. This kills W6.
- (b) HudView: at a phone and a desktop viewport, with and without an inset (the hud-shots stub's `GetInsetArea`), `HudView.pick(pick, boon, tick)`. The `Pick` frame's Position and Size equal `Hud.pickBox(Hud.layout(w, h, inset))`, the `Boon` label's equal `Hud.boonBox(...)`, and a viewport change re-lays both. The fill's scale is `Hud.pickProgress`. With no pick and no boon, both are hidden. This kills W1, W2 and W3.
- (c) Main: frames with the same walls key twice, then a changed key. `MazeView.rewall` runs once per key change and is handed `View.frameMaze(frame)` and the frame's marks. This kills W4 and A8.
