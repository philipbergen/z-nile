# f0101: Spy hop: lower walls and a jump

## Problem
The player, in first person, cannot see ghosts behind the maze walls. The radar (R0100, f0033) shows ghosts a short distance around the player and stays as it is. The user wants a second way to find ghosts: slightly lower walls and a jump, so the player can "spy hop" and look over the walls ahead to see where the ghosts are.

(Revised 2026-10-17: this issue was first captured as an explored-maze minimap, and then as the sensor. Both are dropped. The slug is the first capture's.)

## Non-goals
- The radar, which keeps working as it does today (`Awareness.RADAR_RADIUS` 7 tiles).
- The eight-direction ghost glows (f0102), the blaster (f0100), and the key bindings (f0104 binds Space to this jump).
- Air steering, double jumps, or jumping to cross the maze. The jump is a look, not movement: the rail movement continues underneath.
- Invulnerability: the player can still be caught while jumping.
- A jump on touch: f0110 (captured in R0035's prep).

## Solution
_Captured from the request (user, 2026-10-17); the design below follows it._
- Lower the walls slightly (`View.SCALE.wallHeight` 10 today, `eyeHeight` 4) so a standing eye cannot see over them. Ghosts keep the height the walls had before (10), so they stand above the lowered walls.
- A jump raises the camera above the wall tops for a short time, so ghosts beyond the walls can be seen.
- A short cooldown between jumps (a tuning constant).
- The jump height and duration are pure numbers in Logic (View) and the vertical motion is a camera offset only; collisions, tiles and the rail stay in the grid rules.

## Design
### Decisions made in prep (author's choices; the user may veto any)
- **Walls 9 studs** (from 10: "slightly"), **ghosts 10 tall and 6 wide** (the walls' old height, as asked; 7 today), **eye 4**. Doors are as tall as walls (MazeView already uses `wallHeight` for both).
- Measured on the classic maze (E3, below): **standing**, a ghost the walls would hide shows any sliver of itself over 9-stud walls for 0.08% of the (player tile, ghost tile) pairs within 10 tiles, and never at 9.5; at 8 it would be 3.2%. The design accepts the 0.08% (a one-stud sliver of a ghost's top, at a few grazing spots).
- **The jump**: the eye rises **14 studs** (to 18, almost two tiles up) and lands again in **0.8 s** of real time, on a parabola (`rise · 4u(1−u)`, `u` the share of the jump done); it is above the wall tops for about the middle 80% of it (0.64 s). At the top, **85.5%** of the hidden ghosts within 10 tiles show at least their top two studs over the walls (E3; 96% at a rise of 16, 56% at 10). The next jump may start **0.5 s** after one lands. **Real time**, like the camera's turn smoothing and mouse look (game-design, Game speed).
- **Client only**: the jump never reaches the server (it changes nothing the server decides: the client already receives every ghost's position). It works in every phase, like the mouse look.

### Logic: `src/shared/Logic/View/init.luau` (changed)
```lua
local SCALE = { tileStuds = 8, wallHeight = 9, eyeHeight = 4, fieldOfView = 80 }   -- wallHeight was 10
View.GHOST = frozen { width = 6, height = 10 }                -- a ghost's body box
View.JUMP = frozen { rise = 14, seconds = 0.8, cooldown = 0.5 } -- studs, real seconds
-- May a jump start at `now`, the last having started at `last` (nil: never)? false for a now that is not finite;
-- a last that is nil or not finite never blocks; else now >= last + seconds + cooldown.
function View.canJump(last: number?, now: number): boolean
-- Studs above the standing eye: rise * 4u(1-u) for u = (now - last) / seconds strictly inside (0, 1), else 0;
-- 0 when last is nil, or last or now is not finite.
```

### Adapters (`src/client`)
- `Camera.luau`: `local jumped: number? = nil`; `function Camera.jump()` sets `jumped = os.clock()` only if `View.canJump(jumped, os.clock())`; the one `camera.CFrame` is at `View.SCALE.eyeHeight + View.jumpRise(jumped, os.clock())`. Nothing else changes here for this issue (f0104 changes the look). `Camera.pose()` is unchanged (it is tile coordinates and yaw; the radar and the glows don't care about height).
- `GhostView.luau`: `BODY = Vector3.new(View.GHOST.width, View.GHOST.height, View.GHOST.width)`; the body's centre is `BODY.Y / 2` as today, so ghosts stand on the floor. The eyes keep their offsets from the body's centre.
- `MazeView.luau`: unchanged (walls and doors are `s.wallHeight` tall already, so they come down with `SCALE`).
- Space is bound to the jump by f0104 (`Controls.keys`'s `jump = Camera.jump`).

### Pure/adapter split and authority
The rule (may a jump start, how high is the eye) is pure View, tested in Lune; Camera only reads the clock and applies the offset. No server change, no new remote.

### Scalability
Two arithmetic expressions a frame. Ghosts are 3 studs taller: the same part count.

### Maintainability
The heights live in `View.SCALE`, `View.GHOST` and `View.JUMP`: a different maze or a taller ghost changes one table, and the acceptance spec `sight.spec` re-measures the effect with those very numbers, so a change that breaks the spy hop fails the gate.

### Usability
- **Player**: standing, the maze still hides ghosts (a one-stud sliver at a few grazing spots); a hop shows nearly every nearby ghost over the walls for about two thirds of a second, often enough to plan a route (every 1.3 s at most). The camera keeps its yaw and pitch, so the hop is a look up and over, not a disorienting jump.
- **Developer**: two pure functions and three constants.

## Unknowns & experiments
- **E3 (run in prep: PASS)** `R0035/experiments/E3-spyhop.luau` (about 6 s): an exact grid ray walk (Amanatides–Woo) from the eye on each of the classic maze's 300 floor tile centres to points on a 6 × 10 × 6 ghost box on each ghost tile within 10 tiles (34970 pairs; 30862 have the ghost hidden by walls of unlimited height). Standing (eye 4), share of hidden ghosts showing any part over walls of height H: 8 → 3.175%, 8.5 → 2.709%, **9 → 0.078%**, 9.5 → 0. At the jump's top over 9-stud walls, share showing their top 2 studs: eye 16 → 56.5%, **18 → 85.5%**, 20 → 96.0%; top 3 studs at 18: 54.4%. Above the wall top every point shows however far away it is, so "top 2 studs" (one stud below the wall top) is the measure. `sight.spec` re-runs the two chosen rows with View's numbers (1.3 s). ## Acceptance criteria

### Checked
- AC1: The jump's timing: 14 studs up and down in 0.8 s on a parabola, then 0.5 s before the next; nothing for times that are not finite.
- AC2: Walls 9, ghosts 10 × 6, eye 4; a ghost over the walls, the eye under them, the jump's top over both; pellets below the eye and the walls.
- AC3: On the classic maze with View's numbers: standing, at most 0.1% of the wall-hidden ghosts within 10 tiles show over the walls; at the jump's top, at least 80% show their top 2 studs.
- AC4: Camera jumps only when `View.canJump` allows and lifts the eye by `View.jumpRise`; the jump sends nothing; GhostView sizes ghosts from `View.GHOST`; MazeView uses the scale's wall height.
- AC5: Logic stays engine-free and every dependency is declared (`tests/meta/architecture.spec.luau`).

None.
