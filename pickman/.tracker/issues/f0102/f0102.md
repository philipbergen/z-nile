# f0102: Eight-direction ghost glows

## Problem
The player gets edge arrows (R0100, f0033) for ghosts that are out of view: larger and brighter the closer the ghost is along the maze. The user wants glows instead: one in each of eight places on the screen (top, bottom, left, right and the four corners), coloured as the ghost is, in the direction of the ghost relative to where the camera looks (not where the player is moving), and glowing more as the ghost gets nearer.

(Revised 2026-10-17: this issue was first captured as a droppable sensor, which is dropped. The slug is the first capture's.)

## Non-goals
- The radar, which stays as it is (its blips keep their look colours).
- Blaster shots (f0100), the spy hop (f0101), and the key bindings (f0104).
- Changing the maze-distance or bearing maths in `Awareness`; the glows use what it already returns (bearing, distance, size, brightness).
- Inky's colour and green walls while the ghosts are edible (f0109, not in this raft): f0109 will change one entry of the colour table this issue creates.
- The phone's safe-area layout of the HUD itself (f0029, R0012).

## Solution
_Captured from the request (user, 2026-10-17); the design below follows it._
- A pure function maps a ghost's bearing (relative to the camera look, positive left) to one of eight screen sectors: top is straight ahead, bottom is behind, left and right are 90 degrees, the corners are the diagonals.
- The view draws one glow per sector at the screen edge, and replaces the edge arrows.
- Glows show for every ghost within `maxDistance` (12 tiles along the maze), including ghosts straight ahead (one may be behind a wall around a corner); the nearest ghost in a sector sets its colour and its brightness.
- Colour: the four classic ghost colours (red, pink, cyan, orange). The snapshot already names each ghost (`GhostSnapshot.name`, R0010), so no replication change is needed; frightened, blinking and eyes override with today's colours.
- The glows stay off the HUD and in the safe area, and do not cover the view ahead.

## Design
### Decisions made in prep (author's choices; the user may veto any)
- **Every ghost within 12 tiles along the maze glows, in view or not** (the capture's default), eyes excepted (as the arrows). One glow per sector, lit by the **nearest** ghost there (its colour, size and brightness; a tie in distance goes to the earlier ghost in `Ghosts.NAMES` order).
- **Sectors are equal eighths**, centred on 0°, 45°, … from where the camera looks. **Exactly on a boundary, the edge sector (ahead, behind, a side) wins over the corner.**
- **Where a glow is**: the middle of an edge, or a corner; it is a soft disc (a `UICorner` circle with a `UIGradient` fading out) of **12% of the screen's smaller side** at full size, scaled by the indicator's size (0.25 at 12 tiles, 1 next to the player), moved in until it is whole on the screen. So it never reaches more than 12% in from an edge, and the middle of the screen stays clear.
- **Brightness**: opacity `0.85 × brightness` (brightness is f0033's 0.3 at 12 tiles to 1 next to the player).
- **Under everything else, in the safe area**: the glows have their own `ScreenGui` "Glows" with `ScreenInsets = DeviceSafeInsets` (clear of notches and the home bar) and `DisplayOrder = -1`: under the radar (the Danger gui, default 0), Flow's overlay (0: the dying fade dims the glows too) and the HUD (1). The bottom-right corner glow overlaps the radar on common screens (1280×720: radar 1144–1264 × 555–675, glow 1194–1280 × 634–720), so the radar is drawn over it (design review 001 F5).
- **A glow carries its ghost's name and flash** through Awareness (optional fields), so the adapter colours it with no index lookup (design review 001 F6).
- **One colour table**: `View.GHOST_COLORS` and `View.ghostColor`, used by GhostView's bodies and the glows, with GhostView's current values.

### Logic: `src/shared/Logic/Awareness/init.luau` (changed)
```lua
export type Ghost = { x: number, y: number, look: Look, name: string?, flash: boolean? }   -- name and flash: optional, carried through
export type Options = { …, inView: boolean? }   -- also list the ghosts inside the horizontal field of view
export type Indicator = { …, inView: boolean, name: string?, flash: boolean? }  -- inView: inside the horizontal field of view (only ever true with inView); name, flash: the ghost's
export type Glow = { sector: number, bearing: number, index: number, distance: number, size: number, brightness: number, look: Look, name: string?, flash: boolean? }  -- the nearest ghost's
export type Box = { x: number, y: number, w: number, h: number }
Awareness.SECTORS = 8
Awareness.GLOW = frozen { side = 0.12 }
function Awareness.sector(bearing: number): number                  -- nearest eighth, ties to the even (edge) sector; not finite → 0
function Awareness.sectorBearing(sector: number, aspect: number): number  -- 0, atan(a), π/2, π − atan(a), π, −(π − atan(a)), −π/2, −atan(a); a bad aspect counts as 1
function Awareness.glows(list: { Indicator }, aspect: number): { Glow }   -- one per sector with an indicator, in sector order
function Awareness.glowBox(u: number, v: number, size: number, width: number, height: number): Box
  -- side = GLOW.side · min(w, h) · clamp(size, 0, 1); centred at ((u/2 + 1/2)·w, (v/2 + 1/2)·h), clamped into the screen;
  -- u, v clamped to [-1, 1]; NaN u, v → 0, NaN size → 0; a side length that is not positive and finite → 0
```
`Awareness.indicators` keeps its default exactly (in-view ghosts are left out unless `inView`); with `inView` it lists them too, with `inView = true`. The field-of-view test is today's (`unseen` or `|bearing| > fov/2` is out of view). `sectorBearing` is what `Awareness.edgePoint` maps to that sector's place: the corner for a diagonal on any aspect (`edgePoint(atan(a), a) = (−1, −1)`).

### Logic: `src/shared/Logic/View/init.luau` (added)
```lua
export type Rgb = { r: number, g: number, b: number }   -- 0..255
View.GHOST_COLORS = frozen { blinky = {255, 0, 0}, pinky = {255, 184, 255}, inky = {0, 255, 255}, clyde = {255, 184, 82} } -- as r/g/b fields, each frozen
View.FRIGHTENED_COLOR = frozen {33, 33, 255}
View.WHITE = frozen {255, 255, 255}
-- frightened → FRIGHTENED; blinking → WHITE on the flash, else FRIGHTENED; eyes → WHITE; normal → the ghost's own, WHITE for an unknown name.
-- Always a fresh, unfrozen table.
function View.ghostColor(name: string, look: Session.Look, flash: boolean): Rgb
```

### Adapters (`src/client`)
- `DangerHud.luau`: the arrows go (`arrow()`, `ARROW_MAX`, `▲`, the rotation). A second `ScreenGui` `Glows` (`ResetOnSpawn = false`, `ScreenInsets = Enum.ScreenInsets.DeviceSafeInsets`, `DisplayOrder = -1`), beside the Danger gui (which sets no DisplayOrder). The ghost list given to Awareness carries each snapshot ghost's `name` and `flash`. Each render step, after the radar (whose lines `d0105/adapter` pins verbatim, unchanged): `area = glows.AbsoluteSize`, `aspect = area.X / max(area.Y, 1)`; `Awareness.indicators(m, pose, ghosts, { verticalFieldOfView = math.rad(workspace.CurrentCamera.FieldOfView), aspect = aspect, inView = true })`; `Awareness.glows(list, aspect)`; for each glow, `Awareness.edgePoint(g.bearing, aspect)`, then `Awareness.glowBox(u, v, g.size, area.X, area.Y)`, the colour `View.ghostColor(g.name or "", g.look, g.flash == true)` (never `snap.ghosts[...]`), `BackgroundTransparency = 1 − 0.85 · g.brightness`. Eight glow frames are made once and reused; unlit ones are hidden. It still does no geometry itself (`f0033/adapters` bans `atan2`, `math.atan`, `math.sqrt`, path search; this issue's spec also bans `math.sin`, `math.cos`).
- `GhostView.luau`: its colour table and `paint` branches go; `paint` sets `body.Color` from `View.ghostColor(name, look, flash)` (eyes stay invisible bodies).

### Pure/adapter split and authority
All geometry and choice (which sectors, which ghost lights each, where the box is, which colour) is Awareness and View, in Lune. No server change: it draws replicated state, like the arrows did.

### Scalability
Per frame: today's `indicators` (one bounded breadth-first search per ghost, 12 tiles), then O(ghosts) for the sectors and eight boxes. Eight frames made once. Nothing replicated.

### Maintainability
A fifth ghost gets a colour by one row in `View.GHOST_COLORS` and glows with no other change. A different glow size is `Awareness.GLOW.side`. f0109 changes Inky's colour in one place, for its body and its glow.

### Usability
- **Player**: eight fixed places are quicker to read than arrows that slide along the edge; the colour says which ghost, the size and brightness how near along the maze; a ghost ahead behind a wall glows at the top. Under the HUD, the score stays readable; the middle of the screen is never covered.
- **Developer**: `indicators` gains one option and one field, so existing callers are untouched; the new functions are small and pure.

- **E6 (engine, not provable offline: Observed)**: `ScreenGui.ScreenInsets = Enum.ScreenInsets.DeviceSafeInsets` keeps a GUI out of a phone's notch and home bar, and its `AbsoluteSize` is the safe area's size; a `ScreenGui` with `DisplayOrder` -1 draws under ones with 0 (Danger, Flow) and 1 (the HUD), negative orders being allowed. - **E7 (engine, Observed)**: a `UIGradient` with a transparency sequence (1, 0, 1) on a circular frame reads as a soft glow. Fallback: a plain translucent disc (the box, the colour and the brightness are unchanged).

## Acceptance criteria

### Checked
- AC1: Eight sectors from the bearing, ties to the edge sector, and each sector's bearing landing on its edge middle or corner on any screen shape.
- AC2: With `inView`, in-view ghosts are listed and marked; without it nothing changes; one glow per sector lit by the nearest ghost (ties to the earlier), carrying its name and flash; eyes and far ghosts excluded.
- AC3: A glow's box: 12% of the smaller side at full size, scaled by its size, whole on the screen, clear of the middle on every screen tried; nonsense in gives a valid empty box.
- AC4: One ghost colour table: Blinky red, Pinky pink, Clyde orange pinned, Inky (cyan today) only its own (f0109 will change it), blue when frightened, white on a blink's flash and for eyes, fresh tables.
- AC5: DangerHud draws glows from `Awareness.indicators` (field of view, aspect, `inView = true`), `Awareness.glows`, `Awareness.edgePoint`, `Awareness.glowBox` and `View.ghostColor`, colouring each glow from its own name and flash, in a safe-area `ScreenGui` under the radar, the Flow overlay and the HUD, with no arrows and no geometry; GhostView uses `View.ghostColor`.
- AC6: Logic stays engine-free and every dependency is declared.
