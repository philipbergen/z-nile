# f0125: Player outruns ghosts more

## Problem
The player should move another 10% faster relative to the ghosts than today.

User, 2026-10-21: "We want to move another 10% faster than ghosts." Every player is affected: the ghosts catch up too easily. Today the speeds are the classic Pac-Man tables (f0021, `Levels.GHOST_TUNING = 1`). With base speed 30, level 1's player covers 30 units per tick and a hunting ghost 28, about 7% faster; levels 2 to 4 are 34 against 32, and level 5 on 38 against 36. What should happen: on every level, the player's speed relative to a hunting ghost is 10% higher than today (for example level 1 from 30:28 to about 30:25.5).

## Non-goals
- The eyes returning to the house: they keep `Levels.EYES_FACTOR`, as f0021 fixed.
- The overall game pace (`Session.GAME_SPEED`).
- Other difficulty changes (the scatter and chase timetable, ghost release, frightened time).

## Solution
- The ratio can come from slower ghosts (`Levels.GHOST_TUNING = 1 / 1.1`, the knob f0021 built for "slower ghosts", which also scales the tunnel and frightened speeds) or from a faster player (base speed × 1.1, within `checkLevelSpeeds`' one-tile-per-tick limit). Which one, and whether frightened ghosts slow too, is for prep to ask the user.
- Speeds are whole units per tick after rounding, so the 10% is checked per level against the rounded numbers.
