# Refute 003: f0126 (answers to design-review-003)

- F1: fixed, by the redesign the review recommended: the member's holes (`opened`) are the one record; the maze the member plays in is derived from it (`mazeOf`, cached while the list is unchanged), every reader asks for it, and the ghosts are handed it in every step's input (`Ghosts.Input.maze`, taken with `useMaze`), so no trigger has to point a copy back. Behaviour 8's table now has one column per thing a trigger resets (holes, marks, long hops, fast picks, the last boon) and each cell names its Rules row and mutant, "kept" cells included. New cases: `closed.spec` "after a death the holes stay open to the ghosts" (R3d: the ghosts handed the plain maze after a death, the review's X1), `pick.spec` "a new run rolls new marks on the fresh maze" (R3c, X16), and new mutants on the death row (R3h fast picks, R3i the last boon, R3j long hops dropped on a respawn); the review's X17 (ghosts on level 2 in the old maze) can no longer be written, since nothing resets the ghosts' maze. consequences: `Ghosts.Input` gains an optional field (no existing caller passes it, so the ghosts behave exactly as before without holes; parts.spec "the step's maze replaces the set's", R3g); `freshMaze` shrinks to clearing the holes and rolling marks on the derived maze; the 97-mutant run passes.
- F2: fixed: `pick.spec` "a Pick holds no place in the per-tick intent limit" (accepted after four movement intents, and the four after a Pick are accepted; R3a), and Behaviour 2 now says so; the prototype took the Pick after the limit check and now takes it before. `boons.spec` "a pick while the ghosts are blue still lasts a hunting ghost's ten tiles" (asserts the ghosts are blue when the pick starts; R3b); Behaviour 4 says so. consequences: none.
