# f0126: Risky wall picks grant random boons

## Problem
The player can walk the maze and shoot ghosts, but a wall is a wall for the whole game: nothing opens a way through, and the only rewards are pellets and what eating a ghost already gives. They want to pick a hole in a wall. Picking means standing there for a moment, which is risky (a ghost can catch them while they wait). When the wall comes down, they are rewarded with one of a random set of boons: longer jumps, a faster wall pick, a ghost-eating pill, a longer-lasting ghost-eating pill, or ammo for the ghost blaster.

## Solution
_Captured from the request (user, 2026-10-21); design happens in ticket-prep._
- The player starts a pick on a wall and must stay there until it comes down. Leaving, or being caught, wastes the pick.
- A finished pick removes that wall (a hole) and rolls one boon: longer jumps, a faster next pick, a ghost-eating pill, a longer-lasting ghost-eating pill, or a blaster shot.
- How long the stand takes, which walls can be picked, and the odds of each boon are tuning for prep to ask about.

Prep adds (2026-10-25):
- The tuning is the user's (the decisions above): ten paces of a hunting ghost, interior walls only, three marked in gold, one tile per pick, six boons at even odds, F to pick.
- A new pure feature, `Picks`, holds the marks, the rolls and the arithmetic; `Session` owns when a pick starts, what wastes it and what each boon does; each member plays in its own copy of the maze with its holes open (`Maze.open`), which its ghosts walk too.
- The frame carries the holes, the marks, the pick under way and the latest boon, so the client rebuilds the walls (gold where marked), shows a pick bar and names the boon.

## Design
### Decisions this relies on (pickman/.tracker/game-design.md)
- Pillars 2, "Deterministic game code": everything that decides an outcome runs in pure Logic. Authority: "The **server** simulates every maze. Clients send intents and render the replicated state."
- Movement: "The player always moves along the current corridor at the level's speed and stops at a wall", "There is no free strafing; position is always on a corridor centreline", "The buffer expires after **0.8 s of game time** (24 ticks; R0004)". Collisions: "pellets are eaten when the player enters a tile; player and ghost collide when they share a tile".
- Desktop keys (f0104): W A S D, Space jumps, E fires, the arrows look. This issue adds F (the user's "A new pick key").
- Spy hop (f0101): "The player is airborne only while the eye is above the wall tops (the middle of the jump)"; "(prep): walls 9 studs; the eye rises 14 studs and lands in 12 steps (800 ms of real time); airborne the 2nd to the 11th step of it"; "the next jump 8 steps (533 ms) after landing"; "The server decides the hop (the `Hop` intent) and the camera shows it".
- Blaster (f0100): "at most three are held and a drop while holding three is wasted"; "The roll is seeded on the server"; "the drops follow the session seed (the player's user id today)".
- Rules: "Levels: clearing all pellets advances to the next level, which restores every pellet and puts the player back at the start"; "A death keeps the pellets already eaten"; "a power pellet eaten on the tile a ghost reaches in the same tick makes it blue before the contact (so it is eaten)".
- Ghosts: "ghosts decide at tile centres and take the exit closest in a straight line to their target"; "Ghosts never reverse, except when the mode changes" (the code already turns a ghost back at a dead end, the one exit left: `exitsOf`).
- The new **Wall picks** section this prep adds to game-design.md records the user's decisions above and marks this prep's own choices **(prep)**.

### Behaviour
1. **Marked walls.** A wall tile can be marked (`Picks.pickable`) when it is not on the maze's outer edge (x in 1..width−2, y in 1..height−2), no door or house tile is among its eight neighbours, and one of its four neighbours is passable for the player. The classic maze has 342 such walls. At any time `Picks.MARKED` = 3 are marked (fewer only if fewer candidates exist); each is a seeded roll among the candidates not yet marked, in row-major order. A member gets its first marks when it joins.
2. **Asking for a pick.** The `Pick` intent (`Picks.INTENT`, sent on the existing Intent remote) arms a request that stays pending for `turnBufferTicks` (24) steps in play, like a buffered turn; a repeat restarts it; one submitted while a pick is under way is ignored. A request holds no queue slot: it neither counts toward the per-tick intent limit nor is refused by it (a Pick after four movement intents in one tick is accepted, and the four after a Pick are too). In a pause (the run not in play at the step's start) a pending request is dropped.
3. **Stopping beside the wall.** In each step in play with a pending request, no pick under way, no hop under way (from its take-off step until it lands: `tick < hopped + hopTicks`), and no movement intent this step, the session finds the next tile centre (`Movement.nextCentre`: the player's own tile at a centre, else the tile ahead) and the marked wall beside it (`Picks.wallBeside`: ahead, then left, right, behind, relative to the heading). If one is there and this step's speed reaches the centre, the step's speed (whatever set it: the level, the frightened run, f0143's speed boost when it lands) is capped to land exactly on it; on arrival the player is halted (`Movement.halt`) and the pick starts: `PickStarted {x, y (the wall), ticks}`. A request with no such centre in reach this step counts down by one.
4. **Standing.** A pick started in step k finishes at the start of the player's play in step k + ticks, before that step's fire, hop and movement intents (so a key in that step does not waste it). `ticks = Picks.ticks(Levels.ghostSpeed(base, level, "normal", tuning), fast)`, where `tuning` is the member's own ghost set's (`member.ghosts.tuning`, `Levels.GHOST_TUNING` = 1 today, f0125's `1/1.1` once it lands, so the pick stays ten paces of the ghosts the player actually meets; design review 009 F2), the hunting ghosts' speed even while they are blue: ceil(PACES × UNITS / speed), PACES = 10, UNITS = 120; 43 steps on level 1 (2.9 s real), 38 on levels 2 to 4, 34 from level 5 at tuning 1 (47, 42 and 38 at f0125's 1/1.1); a fast pick is ceil(ticks / 2) (22 on level 1).
5. **Wasting a pick.** Any movement intent in a step while picking (`W A S D`) wastes it (`PickStopped reason = "moved"`); a hop that starts wastes it (`"hopped"`); a ghost that catches the player wastes it in that step (`"caught"`, beside `Caught` and `LifeLost`); leaving play any other way (`Session.kill`, a cleared level, game over) wastes it at the next step's start (`"pause"`). The ghosts walk on, and meet the player, while the player stands: a blue ghost met while picking is eaten as ever (`GhostEaten`), and the pick goes on. A wasted pick resumes the player (`Movement.resume`: moving again where the way ahead is open), so W alone can set off again, though `Forward` never restarts a stopped player in Movement (f0012's promoted rule stays). The wall stays marked. Firing does not waste a pick.
6. **Finishing.** The wall tile is added to the member's holes (`walls.opened`), so the member's maze has it open, the wall is unmarked and a new one marked (`Picks.mark`), the boon is rolled (`Picks.roll`, 1 in 6 each) and granted, `WallPicked {x, y, item = boon}` is emitted, the frame's `boon` becomes `{tick, kind}`, and the player is resumed: they set off in that same step.
7. **The boons.**
   - `jumps`: every hop that starts from now on is `Hop.LONG_TICKS` = 19 steps (airborne 15 against a hop's 10, the same 14-stud top), until the maze is fresh again (a new level or a new run). The session's airborne rule, the cool-down and the camera all use the hop's own length (`frame.hopTicks`).
   - `fastpick`: one faster pick is banked (`Picks.fast += 1`); the next pick that **finishes** with one banked spends it; a wasted fast pick keeps it. Banked picks last until a new run.
   - `pill`: `Game.power(play)`, exactly as a power pellet (the `PowerStarted` event turns the ghosts blue after they move in that step, as the power pellet does).
   - `longpill`: `Game.power(play, Picks.LONG_PILL_FACTOR)` = twice the level's frightened time. The HUD's power bar is the share of the level's frightened time (`Hud.model`, unchanged), so it stays full for the long pill's first half and then drains as for a power pellet (a **(prep)** choice: showing the true share needs the period's length in the frame; the user may ask for it).
   - `shot`: `Blaster.gain`: one more shot, wasted when three are held, with no roll (the drop stream is untouched); `ShotGained` when gained.
   - `pellets`: `Pellets.reset`: every pellet and power pellet is back (`PelletsBack`). The returned pellets do not count for the ghost house: the house counts `PelletEaten` events (`Ghosts.pelletEaten` in `stepGhosts`), and the boon emits none.
8. **The member's maze and a fresh one.** The member's holes, `opened`, are the one record of them: the maze the member plays in is derived from it (`Maze.opened(s.maze, opened)`, cached for as long as the list is unchanged), every reader (the player's move, the shot, the stop's search, the marks) asks for that derived maze, and the ghosts are handed it in every step's input (`Ghosts.Input.maze`). So no reset has to point a copy back (design review 003 F1). Three triggers, and what they reset:

   | | holes (`opened`; the member's and the ghosts' maze follow) | the marks | long hops | banked fast picks | the frame's last boon |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | a death (`Ready` reason `respawn`) | kept (S12; the ghosts still walk into them: R3d) | kept (S12) | kept (R3j) | kept (R3h) | kept (R3i) |
   | a new level (`Ready` reason `level`) | none (S13) | rolled anew on the fresh maze (R2c, R2d) | ended (F3b) | kept (F3e) | kept (T4a) |
   | a new run (`playAgain`, then `Ready` reason `again`) | none (F3; no ghost on a closed hole: R2b) | rolled anew on the fresh maze (R3c) | ended (B3) | dropped (F3c) | dropped (F3d) |

   Each cell names the Rules row (and its mutant) that pins it; the derived mazes have no cell of their own because nothing resets them. A pick under way and a pending request are dropped by every row (the pause wastes them first).
9. **The client.** F is `View.KEYS.F = "Pick"`; Controls sends `View.PICK_INTENT`, which View sets to `Picks.INTENT` (View requires Picks, a new edge `View -> Picks`). Controls gains no require: the specs that load Controls with a closed table of its modules (`tests/place/touch-adapters`, f0110's sequestered `adapters.spec`, design review 002 F1) keep working (E8); f0146's own `Pad` require is that issue's (E10). Main rebuilds the walls when `View.wallsKey(frame)` changes, from `View.frameMaze(frame)` (the layout with `frame.walls.opened` open, cached for the last key) and `frame.walls.marked`: `MazeView.rewall` replaces a `Walls` model inside the maze model (pellets and doors stay), the marked runs gold (`255, 191, 0`) and named `MarkedWall`; the level-clear flash lights them too and restores their gold. The radar (DangerHud) and the beam's end (BlastView) take `View.frameMaze(frame)`. The camera lifts the eye by `Hop.rise(hopped, shown, hopTicks)` with `Camera.hopTicks(frame.hopTicks)` from Main. The HUD shows a gold bar filling for the pick under way (`Hud.pickProgress(frame.pick, frame.tick)`, placed at `Hud.pickBox`) and the boon's name for `Hud.BOON_TICKS` = 30 steps (2 s real) from the step it came (`Hud.boonBanner`, placed at `Hud.boonBox`).

10. **Every input** (the user, 2026-10-25: "We support keyboard, cosole, and touch for every feature"). The pick is asked for three ways, all sending `View.PICK_INTENT` on the one Intent remote, so the server needs nothing new:
   - **Keyboard:** F (`View.KEYS.F = "Pick"`).
   - **Controller:** Y / triangle (the user's choice): one row of f0146's controller map, `Pad.BUTTONS.ButtonY = "Pick"` (f0146's prep, `.worktree/prep-console-gamepad`, keeps every gamepad button in that table, and Controls binds its key codes beside `View.KEYS`' and reads `View.action(name) or Pad.action(name)`), so a press of Y goes down the same `action == "Pick"` path as F. Implementation step 1 checks that `Pad.BUTTONS` has no `ButtonY` row before adding it; f0146's prep pins `Pad.BUTTONS` entry by entry (E10).
   - **Touch:** a **PICK** button, added to f0110's touch buttons (the user: "PICK button after f0110"): `Touch.pickRect(box)` (in screen pixels, f0110's space for every touch rectangle: origin the device screen's top-left, the space HudView draws in and Controls hit-tests in) is the fire button's size, one margin left of it, level with it, so the row reads PICK, FIRE, JUMP; `Touch.buttons(box)` gains `pick`; a finger going down on it is the `"pick"` zone (checked after snap, fire and hop) and emits one `{ kind = "pick" }` on that event, nothing on its move or lift, one finger at a time (f0110's rule for every zone); Controls hands the rectangle to the fold through f0110's `buttons` hook and sends `View.PICK_INTENT` for the effect; HudView draws a `Pick` frame there (f0110's `button` helper: a disc, caption `PICK`, gold `255, 191, 0` like the walls it picks, `Active = false`, shown only when `TouchEnabled`). The pick's progress bar is renamed `PickBar` so the button can take f0110's naming. On every screen of f0110's samples and this issue's (fifteen screens, four insets, three top bands) the button is at least 44 px, inside the safe area, and clear of every HUD block, both radars, FIRE, JUMP and the pick's bar and banner (E9).
   - d0149 (the touch circles, after this issue) turns the PICK button into its pick circle-button, sending the same intent.

### The split and authority
Everything that decides an outcome is Logic and runs on the server: the marks, the stop, the stand, what wastes it, the hole, the boon and its effect. The client only sends `Pick` and draws the frame: the walls from `frame.walls`, the bar from `frame.pick`, the banner from `frame.boon`, the hop from `frame.hopped` and `frame.hopTicks` (zz-arch lesson show-what-the-server-decided). A client cannot choose a wall, a boon or a duration: `Pick` carries nothing, and repeats only restart a pending request.

### Scalability
- Per step per member: with a request pending, one `nextCentre` and at most four `isMarked` scans of three tiles; nothing otherwise. Finishing a pick (at most once per 22 steps) clones the 868-tile grid once and scans the candidates once (868 tiles, a few hundred `pickable` checks): well under a millisecond, and not per step.
- Replication: `walls` adds the opened tiles (one per finished pick; until f0147 caps a level at 10 picks, a long level could hold a few dozen) and three marks, `{x, y}` each, plus `pick`, `boon` and `hopTicks`: well under 1 KB per frame at 15 frames a second. The client rebuilds the wall parts only when `View.wallsKey` changes (a pick finished, a fresh maze), about 150 parts.
- N players per maze later: holes, marks and boons are per member, like the pellets and the ghosts today.

### Maintainability
- The next boon is one entry in `Picks.BOONS`, one branch in `Session`'s `finishPick` and one label in `Hud.BOON_LABELS` (a spec checks every boon has a label). Its odds stay even by construction.
- The tuning is data: `Picks.MARKED`, `Picks.PACES`, `Picks.LONG_PILL_FACTOR`, `Hop.LONG_TICKS`, `Hud.BOON_TICKS`, the gold colour.
- A changed maze is a value (`Maze.open`), not a mutable overlay: every existing reader (Movement, Ghosts, Blaster, Awareness, View) works on it unchanged, and the ghost data and pellet slots, keyed by layout id and by the layout itself, still apply.
- f0147 plugs in at `WallPicked` (count ten per level) and at the fresh-maze reset.

### Usability
- Every input (the user's rule): F on a keyboard, Y / triangle on a controller, the PICK button on a touch screen; each acts on the press (a finger going down, a key going down) and asks for the same pick, so the wait-and-stop rule needs no timing on any of them. On touch the PICK button sits in the row PICK, FIRE, JUMP, gold like the walls it picks; on narrow portrait phones it falls in the swipe-to-turn half, so a swipe that starts on it picks instead of turning (the user's accepted cost, "Left of FIRE anyway").
- For the player: the marked walls are gold, unlike every other wall and pellet (f0109's lemon Inky, 230, 255, 0, is the nearest; its prep was asked on 2026-10-25 to measure against the gold, and the user picks Inky's colour there); the stand is shown as a gold bar filling up; the boon is named on the screen for 2 s; picking needs no exact timing (the request waits like a buffered turn and stops the player on the right centre); leaving is any movement key, the natural escape; the player sets off in the step the pick ends. The risk is real: a hunting ghost 10 tiles away reaches the wall before the pick ends.
- Fairness: a pick in progress is never silently cancelled except by the player's own key, a hop, or leaving play; the pill boon makes ghosts blue before the contact in the same step, as a power pellet does.
- For developers: one new intent string, one new feature with a small typed API, and additive optional parameters elsewhere; no existing signature changes meaning.

### Edge cases
- Two marked walls beside one centre: ahead, then left, right, behind.
- A mark beside the spawn: a request at the start (after Ready) picks it at once (distance 0).
- A request while stopped at a wall with no mark beside: it lapses after 24 steps.
- A request in a pause, or during a pick: dropped / ignored.
- Hop and pick in one step: the hop wins; no pick starts until it lands.
- A key in the finishing step: the pick finishes first, then the key acts.
- A ghost caught while picking: the pick is wasted in that step (`caught`), and the holes stay. A blue ghost met while picking is eaten and the pick goes on.
- The pill boon on a level without frightened time (17, 19, 20, 21+): `Game.power` reports 0 ticks, as a power pellet does there; f0147 makes every level use level 1's frightened time.
- Pellets back with the player standing on a pellet tile: it is there again, eaten when next entered; the level does not end.
- Fewer than three candidates (a tiny maze): as many as there are; none: no marks, and a request does nothing.
- A hole beside a hole: the next marks may sit deeper, so picks can dig through a thick wall over time; holes never reach the edge, so no tile wraps into a wall.
- Ghosts in a one-tile nook: they turn back, the one exit left (existing `exitsOf`).
- Frame-rate independence: everything counts steps; the client's bar and banner read the frame's tick.
