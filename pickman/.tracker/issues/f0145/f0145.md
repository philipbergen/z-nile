# f0145: Paid play and continues

## Problem
The user, 2026-10-25: "Unless in playtest (it will show the screen and accept controls, but not actually deduct) we want to allow a free game per hour but after that we charge 1 robux per game and also you can continue with three new lives for 1 robux, we should show in highscore how many lives were spent".

Today every game is free and a game ends when the last life is lost (game-design.md: 3 lives, one extra at 10,000 points). What should happen:
- **A free game per hour:** each player gets one free game per hour.
- **After that, 1 Robux per game:** starting another game within the hour costs 1 Robux.
- **Continue for 1 Robux:** when the last life is lost, the player can pay 1 Robux to continue the same game with three new lives.
- **Hiscores show lives spent:** a hiscore entry shows how many lives the run used, so a score bought with continues is visible as such.
- **Playtest never charges:** in a playtest the purchase screens show and accept their controls exactly as in a live game, but nothing is deducted.

## Non-goals
- The hiscore boards' own rules (all-time, daily, weekly, the top ten): d0124 / R0044; this issue only adds the lives-spent figure to their entries.
- Other purchases (passes, cosmetics, boosts).

## Solution
- Open for prep to put to the user: what "playtest" covers (Roblox Studio play only, or also the Beta place); whether the hour is a rolling hour from the last free game or a clock hour, and whether it is per player across servers (a DataStore) or per server; where the buy prompt appears (the lobby's START GAME, the game place, or both); whether a continue keeps the score, level and pellets (presumably yes); whether continues are unlimited; how lives spent are counted (lives lost, or lives granted including bought and the 10,000-point bonus) and shown on the boards and in the lobby.
- Roblox purchases go through a Developer Product (`MarketplaceService:PromptProductPurchase` and a server `ProcessReceipt` that grants exactly once); the server decides every grant, never the client.
- The hiscore entry gains a lives-spent field; existing entries without it show as unknown (or the base 3).
