### F1 [medium] A death on the level after a bonanza can start the long ghost pill again, and AC2 stays green
Where: f0147.md Edge cases ("A death on the level after a bonanza: the long hops stay (f0126's death row), the long pill has been taken already (it starts the level's play)"); Behaviour 6; levels.spec "the next level has long hops from its start, kept over a death, until the following fresh maze" (kills on level 2, then checks only `hopTicks`); Rules and mutants (no row)
Evidence: mutant Y1 adds, in Session's `Ready` handler before `member.bonanza = false`, `if e.reason == "respawn" and member.longHops then member.bonanzaPill = true end`. Every death on a level reached by a bonanza then turns the ghosts blue for 12 s of game time as play resumes. `Y1 SURVIVED levels` (18 passed, 0 failed). Nothing reads `PowerStarted` or `play.frightened` after a respawn on a bonanza level.
Critique: the Design states this outcome, so it is a rule without a row (zz-review design mode, item 0). An executor is likely to build it this way: the long hops are "kept over a death", so treating the bonanza as a per-level state and re-arming both its parts on every `Ready` of that level looks consistent. In play it gives a free power period after every death from level 2 on.
Consequences: the case has to tell a long pill that is still running from one that restarted. `Session.kill` early on level 2 happens while the first long pill is still running. If the respawn does not clear the frightened time (Game's or the ghost set's), the case must not expect 0. It should expect no `PowerStarted` in the respawn's steps, and the set's `frightened` no higher than it was before the kill.
Recommendation: extend the long-hops case, or add one. On level 2 after a bonanza, let the long pill's `PowerStarted` pass, then `Session.kill` and step through READY into play and 20 steps beyond. Expect no `PowerStarted` event in those steps, and `p.ghosts.frightened` (and `play.frightened`) not above its value at the kill. Add a row: "the long pill is taken once a level, not again after a death", mutant Y1, with its runner entry.
Lesson: z-design/spec-design-correspondence

### F3 [low] What happens to an eaten ghost's carry is unstated: is a ghost that comes back from the house "placed"?
Where: f0147.md Behaviour 2 ("is ½ whenever the ghosts are placed (a new level, a new run, a death), [...] kept across a change of kind (tunnel, frightened, hunting and back) and across a power pellet"); Interfaces `Ghost.carry`
Evidence: in the prototype, `Ghosts.eat` and the eyes' path never touch `carry` (`grep -n carry src/shared/Logic/Ghosts/init.luau`: lines 26, 268, 398 and 401 only; the eyes return from `speedOf` before the carry is read). So an eaten ghost comes out of the house with the fraction it had when it was eaten. The Design's kind list leaves out the eyes, and "placed" could also be read as the ghost re-entering play from the house. One executor resets the carry to ½ on revival, another keeps it, and no case tells them apart.
Critique: it's harmless either way (each step is still whole units, and it's deterministic), but it's one more thing the executor has to guess about the mechanism earlier rounds kept finding holes in.
Consequences: none beyond one sentence and one sub-case.
Recommendation: state it in Behaviour 2: "an eaten ghost keeps its carry through the eyes and the house; only `Ghosts.reset` places it again" (the prototype's behaviour). Pin it the way the "hunted" sub-case does: one frightened step (carry 0.03), the ghost turned to eyes for a step and back to frightened by hand, then the next step is 12. Add a row (mutant: the carry set to ½ when a ghost is eaten).
