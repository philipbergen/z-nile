# f0147: Levels by picked walls

## Problem
The user, 2026-10-25, while setting f0126's tuning: "After 10 picked walls new level (looks the same but is a few percent faster than before, and the level 1 speed should come down 30% to help players into the game). [...] Scores should go up for each level making it mostly a rounding error to start on level 1 if you can start on level 2. Maybe 2x, 3x etc each level being the multiplier."

Every player is affected. Today a level ends when the last pellet is eaten, and each level follows the arcade tables (game-design.md, Scoring and rules: speeds per Table A.1, frightened time, the scatter and chase timetable and the house release vary per level). Level 1 is 30 units a tick for the player and 28 for a hunting ghost, which new players find too fast, and a point is worth the same on every level. What should happen:
- **Ten picked walls start the next level.** A finished wall pick (f0126) counts toward the level; the tenth starts the next one.
- **The next level looks the same but is faster:** every level is 5% faster than the one before, for the player and the ghosts alike.
- **Level 1 is 30% slower than today**, for the player and the ghosts alike, to help players into the game.
- **Points go up with the level:** every point scored on level n is worth n times as much, so a run started on level 2 makes a run started on level 1 mostly a rounding error.

Decided with the user (2026-10-25), the split: "3 rafts in order (Recommended)": f0126 (picks and boons), then this issue (levels, speeds, multiplier), then f0148 (start on any level up to your best). The work lands as sequenced rafts.
Decided with the user (2026-10-25), the pellets: "Only picks; maze stays empty". Clearing every pellet no longer ends a level; the pellets only score, and an emptied maze stays empty until the next level (or f0126's pellets-back boon).
Decided with the user (2026-10-25), the ramp: "5% per level (Recommended)", applied to player and ghosts alike, replacing the arcade's per-level speed jumps; level 1 is 70% of today's speed for both.
Decided with the user (2026-10-25), the new level: "Fresh maze (Recommended)". Picked holes close, all pellets come back, new marked walls, the player back at the start.
Decided with the user (2026-10-25), the other tables: "Level 1 rules on every level (Recommended)". Only the speed ramp changes per level; the frightened time, the scatter and chase timetable and the house release stay level 1's on every level.
Decided with the user (2026-10-25), the multiplier: "× level, extra life unscaled". All points on level n are worth n times; the extra life comes at 10,000 × the run's starting level.
Decided with the user (2026-10-31), the ramp's top (a 5% ramp from level 1's 21 units passes the one-tile-a-step limit at level 35): "Stop at level 34 (Recommended)". Every level up to 34 is 5% faster than the one before; later levels keep level 34's speeds.
Decided with the user (2026-10-31), the eyes: "Follow the ramp (Recommended)". The eyes are twice the level's player speed, so 42 on level 1, rising 5% a level, never past a tile a step.
Decided with the user (2026-10-31), a counter: "Yes, show picks/10 (Recommended)". The HUD shows the level's finished picks out of ten.
Decided with the user (2026-10-31), the tenth pick: "Tenth boon should be the bonanza, you get all the boons at once (except the restore pills since that's a given on level up)", then "Carry into next level (Recommended)": as it finishes, one more banked faster pick and one more blaster shot (wasted at three); on the next level, longer jumps for all of it and, when its play starts after READY, a long ghost pill (twice the power time).
Decided with the user (2026-11-01), the longer-jumps boon from level 2 (design review 003 F2: every level after the first starts with the bonanza's long hops, so a rolled longer-jumps boon adds nothing there): "Keep it, record it". The roll stays even among the six boons; from level 2 a longer-jumps roll shows its banner only, as game-design.md records.
Decided with the user (2026-10-31), the slow ghosts (design review 001 F1: whole units left the tunnel ghost no faster on levels 2, 4, 6, 8 and 12, and the frightened ghost on 3, 6 and 11): "Carry fractions (Recommended)". The tunnel and frightened ghosts move the ramp's exact speed on average, in whole-unit steps that carry the fraction; the player and the hunting ghosts stay whole units, which are strictly faster on every level.

## Non-goals
- The wall pick and its boons themselves: f0126.
- Choosing the starting level and remembering the best level: f0148.
- The player's speed relative to the ghosts: f0125 (this issue scales both alike).
- A short speed burst: f0143.

## Solution
- The level counter moves from "all pellets eaten" to "ten finished picks"; `Levels` gives every level the level-1 row except the speeds, which are the level-1 speeds × 0.7 × 1.05^(n−1), in whole units per tick and within `checkLevelSpeeds`' one-tile-per-tick limit.
- The score multiplier applies to every point (pellets, power pellets, ghosts); the extra-life threshold is 10,000 × the starting level (1 until f0148).
- Open for prep: how the frightened player speed-up and the tunnel and eyes speeds follow the ramp, and how the rounding keeps each level strictly faster than the one before.

Prep adds (2026-10-31):
- The ramp is one value, `Levels.RAMP` (ten picks, 0.7, 1.05, top 34), carried by the shipped config (`Session.GAME_CONFIG.ramp`); every per-level reader takes it, and without it (the default config the specs use) the classic tables and the last pellet's clear stay exactly as they are.
- The frightened player speed-up, the tunnel and frightened ghosts and the eyes all ramp by the same factor; the player's, the hunting ghosts' and the eyes' speeds round to the nearest whole unit, which keeps them strictly faster on each level up to 34; the tunnel and frightened ghosts carry the fraction (the user's decision above).
- The tenth pick's bonanza (the user's), the HUD counter under the score, and the multiplied points through `Game.points`.

## Design

### Behaviour
1. **The ramp.** `Levels.RAMP = { picks = 10, first = 0.7, step = 1.05, top = 34 }`. With a ramp, `Levels.row(level, ramp)` is level 1's row with every speed factor (the player's, the frightened player's, the hunting, tunnel and frightened ghosts') multiplied by `Levels.rampFactor(ramp, level) = first × step^(min(level, top) − 1)`; every other field (frightened seconds, flashes, dot limits, idle seconds, schedule) is level 1's. Rows are frozen and made once per ramp and level. Speeds stay `round(base × factor)` (`round` is floor(x + 0.5)), so on the shipped config: player 21, 22, 23, 24, 26 … 105 at level 34 (frightened 24 … 118); hunting ghost 18 … 90 (f0125's tuning); eyes `round(base × EYES_FACTOR × rampFactor)`, 42 on level 1, capped at a tile (`Movement.UNITS`, 120) from level 23 by `Ghosts.speedOf` and `Session.speeds`. Levels past `top` keep `top`'s speeds. Without a ramp every function returns exactly what it does today.
2. **Faster on every level.** Rounding to whole units keeps the player (frightened or not) and the hunting ghosts (tuned or not) strictly faster on each level up to 34, and the eyes up to level 22 (from 23 they are capped at a tile) (E2). The slow tunnel and frightened ghosts (10 to 13 units on level 1) would round to the same unit on some neighbouring levels, so with a ramp they move the ramp's exact rate on average (the user's "Carry fractions"): `Levels.ghostRate` is the unrounded `base × factor × tuning`, and each step a ghost covers `floor(carry + rate)` units and keeps the rest as its `carry` (0 ≤ carry < 1). The carry is each ghost's own (a field of its record, so two sets, or two players' ghosts, never share it), is ½ whenever the ghosts are placed (a new level, a new run, a death), so a ghost's first fractional step is the rate rounded, the speed whole units gave (design review 002 F1: a carry of 0 made Blinky's first tunnel step 9 instead of 10 and kept it from wrapping in f0032's promoted frame case), and is kept across a change of kind (tunnel, frightened, hunting and back) and across a power pellet (`Ghosts.power` does not touch it): it is a fraction of a unit already earned. An eaten ghost keeps its record, so its carry rides through the eyes' run home and the house (where the eyes and the hunting ghost move whole units and leave it alone) to its next tunnel or blue step. So a frightened ghost on the shipped level 5 (14.5 units) alternates 14 and 15; each step is still whole units, so positions, contacts and the frame are as before. Without a ramp, every ghost speed is `Levels.ghostSpeed`'s whole units, as today.
3. **Level 1's rules on every level.** The ghosts' timetable, house limits, idle release and flashes, the power period (`Game.power`) and the HUD's power share all read `Levels.row(level, ramp)`; so a ramped level 17 has 6 s of frightened time (the classic 0), and every level's first scatter is 7 s.
4. **Ten picks a level.** With a ramp, `finishPick` counts `member.levelPicks` (a finished pick only: a wasted one counts nothing); the pick that brings it to `ramp.picks` is the level's last: it grants the bonanza (6) and calls `Game.cleared`, so the level-clear pause, the flash and the next `Ready reason = "level"` follow as today. `eat` no longer clears a level when a ramp is set: the last pellet only scores, and the emptied maze stays empty. `freshMaze` (a new level, a new run) resets `levelPicks` to 0; a death keeps it.
5. **The fresh maze** is f0126's (`freshMaze` plus `Pellets.reset` on a `Ready` that is not a respawn): holes closed, every pellet back, new marks rolled, the player teleported to the start.
6. **The bonanza** (the user's). The level's last pick rolls no boon (`Picks.roll` is not called): it adds one banked faster pick (`picks.fast += 1`) and one blaster shot (`Blaster.gain`, `ShotGained` when gained, wasted at three), sets the frame's `boon = { tick, kind = "bonanza" }` (banner `BONANZA!`, f0126's 2 s), emits `WallPicked { item = "bonanza" }`, and marks `member.bonanza`. On the next `Ready reason = "level"` (after `freshMaze`, which ends long hops) a pending bonanza sets `longHops` (every hop on that level is f0126's long hop, until its fresh maze; kept over a death) and `bonanzaPill`; the first step in play then calls `Game.power(play, Picks.LONG_PILL_FACTOR)` before anything else, so `PowerStarted` reaches `stepGhosts` and the ghosts turn blue in that step, after READY, for twice level 1's frightened time. Pellets back is not granted: the fresh maze brings every pellet. A run cannot end between the tenth pick and the next level's `Ready` (no death in a pause), so nothing clears a pending bonanza on a new run (M24, Rules and mutants). Since every level ends with a bonanza, every level from level 2 on has long hops (recorded in game-design.md).
7. **Points × level.** `Game.points(g, points)` is `points × g.level` with a ramp, else `points`. Session scores pellets, power pellets, ghosts eaten on contact and ghosts shot through it, and the events carry the multiplied points (`PelletEaten.points`, `GhostEaten.points`, `Shot.points`), so the events still sum to the score. `Game.addScore` adds what it is given; the extra life comes when the score reaches `extraLifeScore × g.start`, where `g.start` (new) is the level the run started on: 1 here, set by f0148. `Game.playAgain` starts the new run on `g.start` (1 until f0148).
8. **The pick's length** stays f0126's ten paces of the member's own hunting ghosts, now with the ramp: `Picks.ticks(Levels.ghostSpeed(base, level, "normal", tuning, ramp), fast)`: 60 steps on level 1 untuned, 67 with f0125's tuning (4.5 s of real time, against 47 before), shorter as the levels speed up.
9. **The counter** (the user's; its place is a **(prep)** choice). The frame's play record gains `picks` and `picksGoal` with a ramp (nil without), and `power` (the level's frightened ticks under the session's rules). `Hud.model` gains `picks = "PICKS n/10"` (nil without), and its power share divides by `play.power` when present. `Hud.picksBox(score)` is the row under the score block (`Touch.blocks(l).score`), its size, left-aligned like the score; HudView draws a `Picks` label there, hidden when the model has no picks. Not under the level: on short touch screens (320 px under a 58 px top band) `Hud.radarBox` lifts the radar to one text row and two margins under the top band, on the right, which a second row under the level would meet; the left column has nothing below the score until the lives row.
10. **Validation.** `Levels.validateRamp` refuses a ramp whose `picks` is not a whole number ≥ 1, whose `top` is not a whole number in 1 to `Levels.MAX_TOP` (1000: `Session.new` checks every level up to it, design review 002 F6), whose `first` is not positive and finite, or whose `step` is not finite and ≥ 1; `Game.new` and `Session.new` call it. `Session.new`'s player-speed bound (`checkLevelSpeeds`) and f0125's ghost-tuning bound run over levels 1 to `ramp.top` with the ramp (the classic `Levels.COUNT` without), so a ramp whose top would move the player or a non-eyes ghost more than a tile a step is refused; the eyes are capped instead (the user's "never past a tile").

### Interfaces
- `Levels` (`src/shared/Logic/Levels/init.luau`): `export type Ramp = { picks: number, first: number, step: number, top: number }`; `Levels.RAMP: Ramp` (frozen); `Levels.MAX_TOP = 1000`; `Levels.validateRamp(r: Ramp)`; `Levels.rampFactor(r: Ramp, level: number): number`; optional trailing `ramp: Ramp?` on `Levels.row(level, ramp?)`, `Levels.playerSpeed(base, level, frightened?, ramp?)`, `Levels.frightenedTicks(level, tickRate, ramp?)`, `Levels.ghostSpeed(base, level, kind, tuning?, ramp?)`, `Levels.schedule(level, tickRate, ramp?)`. Every existing call keeps its meaning.
- `Game`: `Game.new(config?, ramp: Levels.Ramp?)`; `Game.Game` gains `ramp: Levels.Ramp?` and `start: number`; `Game.points(g, points): number`; `Game.power` reads the ramp; the extra life is `extraLifeScore × start`; `playAgain` starts on `start`.
- `Levels.ghostRate(base, level, kind, tuning?, ramp?): number`: with a ramp, the unrounded tunnel and frightened rates; otherwise `Levels.ghostSpeed`'s whole units.
- `Ghosts`: `Options.ramp: Levels.Ramp?` and `Set.ramp`; `Ghost.carry: number?` (the fraction carried, ½ when placed, f0147); every `Levels` read passes `set.ramp`; with a ramp the tunnel and frightened ghosts move `floor(carry + Levels.ghostRate(...))` a step; the eyes are `min(…, Movement.UNITS)`.
- `Picks`: `export type Grant = Boon | "bonanza"`; `Picks.BONANZA = "bonanza"` (never in `Picks.BOONS`, never rolled).
- `Session`: `Config.ramp: Levels.Ramp?`; `GAME_CONFIG.ramp = Levels.RAMP` (`DEFAULT_CONFIG` has none); `Session.speeds` (f0125's) passes `c.ramp`, caps the eyes and gives the tunnel and frightened ghosts' rates (`Levels.ghostRate`, fractional with a ramp); `PlayFrame` gains `power: number?`, `picks: number?`, `picksGoal: number?`; `BoonGrant.kind: Picks.Grant`; the member gains `levelPicks`, `bonanza`, `bonanzaPill` (private). Events: `WallPicked.item` may be `"bonanza"`; points in events are multiplied.
- `Hud`: `Model.picks: string?`; `Hud.picksBox(score: Box): Box`; `Hud.BOON_LABELS.bonanza = "BONANZA!"`.
- Adapters: `HudView` (a `Picks` label, placed by `Hud.picksBox(blocks.score)`, shown from `m.picks`). The server's `Sessions.luau` already passes `Session.GAME_CONFIG`; nothing else changes.

### The split and authority
Everything that decides an outcome is Logic on the server: the ramp's speeds, when a level ends, the bonanza, the multiplier, the extra life. The client only draws the frame's `picks`, `picksGoal`, `power` and `boon`. The client sends nothing new.

### Scalability
- Per step per member: one more comparison in `finishPick` (at most once per pick) and one in `eat`; the ramped rows are made once per level and cached (frozen), so `Levels.row` with a ramp is a table lookup after the first call; `rampFactor` is one `^` per call (`Session.speeds` and the ghosts' `speedOf`, as many calls as today).
- Replication: three numbers more in the play record per frame.
- N players per maze: the count and the bonanza are per member, as f0126's picks are.

### Maintainability
- The level rules are one value (`Levels.RAMP`) in one config field (`GAME_CONFIG.ramp`): a later tuning (a different top, step or pick count) is one number; f0148's start level plugs into `Game.start` and `Game.new`'s callers.
- Without a ramp the code paths are today's, so the classic tables the specs use stay as they are, and the shipped game differs from them by one field (as f0125's `ghostTuning` does).
- The next "level reward" is one line in `finishPick`'s bonanza branch.

### Usability
- Players: level 1 is 30% slower (5.25 tiles/s of game time, 2.6 in real time), each level a little faster, and the counter says how close the next level is. The multiplier makes later levels worth playing. A pick on level 1 now takes 4.5 s of real time (the ghosts are slower too, so the risk is the same ten paces). The bonanza is named on the screen.
- Developers: one optional `ramp` argument everywhere a level is read; the classic call sites are untouched.

### Edge cases
- The tenth pick finishing while a ghost meets the player in the same step: f0126's order runs the finish first (start of the step's play), so `Game.cleared` moves the run to `levelclear` before `stepGhosts`, and `Game.died` refuses outside play: a cleared level beats a kill, as today's "a cleared level beats a kill".
- A pick wasted on the tenth (moved, hopped, caught): no count; the level goes on.
- Pellets back as a boon on an emptied maze: the pellets return; eating them all again ends nothing.
- A death on the level after a bonanza: the long hops stay (f0126's death row), the long pill has been taken already (it starts the level's play).
- A bonanza when three shots are held: the shot is wasted (no `ShotGained`), the fast pick still banks.
- A pick on level 2 or later, with the bonanza's long hops on: the roll is the same one it would be with them off (a longer-jumps roll shows its banner and grants nothing more: the user's "Keep it, record it").
- Levels past 34: same speeds as 34; the multiplier keeps growing with the level.
- A maze with fewer than ten pickable walls over a level (test mazes): marks run out and the level never ends; the classic maze has 342 candidates.
- Frame-rate independence: everything counts steps.
