To see what it draws, I put the same mistake in clear.spec's model (`Awareness.ring({}, s.width, s.height)` shifted by the core frame, which is what the mutant draws). Output: desktop `notched phone, landscape, under a 58 px top bar (844x390): lives; old phone, landscape, under a 58 px top bar (480x320): lives; small phone, landscape, under a 58 px top bar (568x320): lives; small phone, landscape 667, under a 58 px top bar (667x375): lives`; touch adds `snap` on 844×390 and 480×320 and `jump2` on 320×568.
