# f0158: Danger radar ring

## Problem
The capture's own text (commit 68907d65, 2026-10-25; its `./zn land` did not reach main, which holds only the one-line stub, so prep restores it here word for word):

The user, 2026-10-25, answering f0109's radar question ("should the danger radar's walls turn green along with the maze?"): "Yes, and the danger radar needs to be more visible, make it more of circle segments with a circle of translucent indicators, keep it at a size roughly 60% of the smallest dimension (normally Y)".

Today the danger radar (d0105, `DangerHud`) is a small square map in the bottom-right corner (f0029's mobile layout keeps it clear of the touch jump button), and it's hard to see. What should happen:
- **More visible:** the radar is redrawn as circle segments with a circle (ring) of translucent indicators showing where danger is.
- **Size:** about 60% of the screen's smallest dimension (normally its height).
- **Walls follow the maze colour** while ghosts are edible (decided in f0109, which makes the radar walls use the maze's wall-colour rule).

## Non-goals
- The wall-colour rule itself: f0109.
- The touch controls' layout (f0110, d0149, d0151), beyond keeping clear of them.

## Solution
- Open for prep to put to the user: what each segment and indicator shows (direction and distance of each ghost? only dangerous ghosts? edible ghosts in another style?), where the ring sits (centred on the screen around the player's view, or in a corner), its translucency, and how it stays clear of the HUD (score, lives, shot bars, power bar, f0126's pick bar and boon banner, the touch buttons) at ~60% of the screen's smaller side.
- Desktop, touch and console all show it (the user's rule, 2026-10-25: "We support keyboard, console, and touch for every feature").

## Design
**What the user asked.** A more visible danger radar: "circle segments with a circle of translucent indicators", about 60% of the screen's smaller side. Today DangerHud draws two things: a square map of the maze around the player in the bottom-right corner (d0105, `Hud.radarBox`), and eight glows on the screen's edge (f0102), one per direction a ghost is in, coloured as the nearest ghost there and brighter the closer it is. Prep's default (G1, pending the user) replaces the eight edge glows with the **danger ring**: one circle of translucent dots around the middle of the screen, 60% of its smaller side across, cut into the same eight sectors; the square map stays in its corner, its walls following the maze colour rule (f0109).

**The ring (pure, `pickman/src/shared/Logic/Awareness/init.luau`).**
- `Awareness.RING = { share = 0.6, dot = 0.035, dots = 6, lit = { min = 0.35, max = 0.85 }, idle = 0.15 }`: the ring's outer diameter as a share of the screen's smaller side, a dot's side as a share of it, dots per sector, a lit dot's opacity from `min` (farthest) to `max` (nearest), an unlit dot's opacity.
- `export type RingDot = { x, y, w, h: number, sector: number, lit: boolean, brightness: number, look: Look?, name: string?, flash: boolean? }`.
- `Awareness.ring(glows: { Glow }, width: number, height: number): { RingDot }`: every dot of every sector (8 × `dots`), sector 0 (ahead) at the top and the sectors round to the left as `Awareness.sector` counts them, so a ghost to the left lights the left of the ring. Each sector spans an eighth of a turn as `dots` dots and one dot's gap; the dots' outer edge is on the circle of diameter `share` × the smaller side, centred on the screen. A sector is lit when `glows` (from `Awareness.glows`) has it, with that glow's brightness clamped to 0..1, look, name and flash. A side that is not a positive finite number counts as 0. It reuses `Awareness.glows` (f0102), so which ghost lights a sector (the nearest along the maze, ties to the earlier; eyes never; none beyond `maxDistance`) is unchanged.

**The adapter (`pickman/src/client/DangerHud.luau`).** The edge glows' screen becomes the ring's (`Ring`, `ScreenInsets = DeviceSafeInsets`, `DisplayOrder = -1`, under the radar, the Flow overlay and the HUD): each render step it asks `Awareness.indicators` (inView) as today, `Awareness.glows` of them, then `Awareness.ring(lit, area.X, area.Y)`, and draws each dot as a round frame at its box: a lit dot in `View.ghostColor(d.name, d.look, d.flash)` at opacity `lit.min + (lit.max - lit.min) × brightness`, an unlit one white at `idle`. It does no geometry (no sin, cos, atan, sqrt; f0033's rule). `Awareness.edgePoint`, `Awareness.glowBox` and `Awareness.GLOW` stay in Awareness (f0102's pure specs pin them) but DangerHud no longer calls them. The square map (`Awareness.radar`, `Hud.radarBox`) is unchanged; its wall dots take f0109's `View.wallColor` (f0158 depends on f0109).

**Clearance.** The ring is centred, so it never meets the HUD's edge blocks (score, level, lives and shots, power bar), the corner radar, the touch snap control or Roblox's jump buttons, on desktop and touch, landscape and portrait (AC2 checks six screens). HUD overlays drawn mid-screen (f0126's pick bar at 0.4 of the safe height and boon banner at 0.3, not on main yet) may cross the ring on short screens (an 844×390 phone: the pick bar is 211 px wide where the ring's chord is about 206 px); the ring is drawn under the HUD, so those draw over it (G6).

**Every input (the user's rule, 2026-10-25).** The ring is display only: desktop, touch and console show the same ring; nothing is bound.

### Scalability
48 dots, one frame each, reused every render step; `Awareness.ring` is O(48) with no allocation beyond its result list; the indicators and glows are computed once per step as today. No server cost.

### Maintainability
All geometry and numbers live in `Awareness` (`RING`), colours in `View`; DangerHud only draws. The ring reuses `Awareness.glows`, so a later change to which ghost lights a direction changes the ring and its tests at once.

### Usability
A ghost's direction is read at the middle of the screen, where the player already looks, instead of at the edge; the ring is thin and translucent so the maze ahead stays visible; idle dots outline the ring so a lit sector reads as a direction.

## Guesses (pending user)
1. **G1** (pending user): what the ring replaces. Prep's default (a): the ring replaces the eight edge glows; the square map stays in its corner, its walls in the maze colour. (b) the ring is added and the edge glows stay too (no promoted spec changes). (c) the map itself becomes round, 60% across, centred and translucent, with the ring of indicators on its rim, replacing both the corner square and the edge glows (closest to the capture's "the radar is redrawn as circle segments"; it covers more of the forward view). Recommended: (a).
2. **G2** (pending user): what a lit sector shows: the nearest ghost in that direction, in its colour, more opaque the closer it is along the maze (today's glow rule, reused). Alternatives: one dot per ghost placed by distance along the sector; only dangerous ghosts (edible ones not shown). Recommended: the glow rule, edible ghosts shown in their frightened colour, eyes and ghosts beyond 12 tiles not shown, as today.
3. **G3** (pending user): eight sectors of six dots each, one dot's gap between sectors. Alternatives: 12 or 16 sectors (finer direction); a solid arc look (more dots). Recommended: 8 × 6.
4. **G4** (pending user): placement: centred on the screen (around the view). Alternative: centred on the safe area's middle, or on a corner (then far smaller than 60%). Recommended: centred.
5. **G5** (pending user): translucency: lit dots 35%–85% opaque by closeness, unlit 15%. Recommended as stated.
6. **G6** (pending user): mid-screen HUD overlays (f0126's pick bar and boon banner) draw over the ring where they cross it on short screens, since the ring is drawn under the HUD. Alternative: the ring shrinks or gaps there. Recommended: draw over.
7. **G7** (pending user): ring size: 60% of the smaller side as the outer edge, dots 3.5% of it across. Recommended as stated.
