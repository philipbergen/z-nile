# f0158: Danger radar ring

## Problem
The capture's own text (commit 68907d65, 2026-10-25; its `./zn land` did not reach main, which holds only the one-line stub, so prep restores it here word for word):

The user, 2026-10-25, answering f0109's radar question ("should the danger radar's walls turn green along with the maze?"): "Yes, and the danger radar needs to be more visible, make it more of circle segments with a circle of translucent indicators, keep it at a size roughly 60% of the smallest dimension (normally Y)".

Today the danger radar (d0105, `DangerHud`) is a small square map in the bottom-right corner (f0029's mobile layout keeps it clear of the touch jump button), and it's hard to see. What should happen:
- **More visible:** the radar is redrawn as circle segments with a circle (ring) of translucent indicators showing where danger is.
- **Size:** about 60% of the screen's smallest dimension (normally its height).
- **Walls follow the maze colour** while ghosts are edible (decided in f0109, which makes the radar walls use the maze's wall-colour rule).

## Non-goals
- The wall-colour rule itself: f0109.
- The touch controls' layout (f0110, d0149, d0151), beyond keeping clear of them.

## Solution
- Open for prep to put to the user: what each segment and indicator shows (direction and distance of each ghost? only dangerous ghosts? edible ghosts in another style?), where the ring sits (centred on the screen around the player's view, or in a corner), its translucency, and how it stays clear of the HUD (score, lives, shot bars, power bar, f0126's pick bar and boon banner, the touch buttons) at ~60% of the screen's smaller side.
- Desktop, touch and console all show it (the user's rule, 2026-10-25: "We support keyboard, console, and touch for every feature").

## Design
Decided with the user (2026-10-26): "Replace edge glows (Recommended)" (G1): the ring replaces the eight edge glows; the corner map stays.
Decided with the user (2026-10-26): "Accept all, but just a red or something to indicate, not a color match" (G2–G7): a lit sector is one warning colour, not the ghost's colour, more opaque the closer; 8 × 6 dots; lit 35–85% opaque, unlit 15%; dots 3.5% of the smaller side; f0126's pick bar and boon banner draw over the ring on short screens.
Decided with the user (2026-10-26): "Centre below top bar" (G8, design review 001 F1): keep the ring at 60% of the smaller side, but measure and centre it in the area below Roblox's top bar (the core-UI safe area), not the whole screen.

**What the user asked.** A more visible danger radar: "circle segments with a circle of translucent indicators", about 60% of the screen's smaller side. Today DangerHud draws two things: a square map of the maze around the player in the bottom-right corner (d0105, `Hud.radarBox`), and eight glows on the screen's edge (f0102), one per direction a ghost is in, coloured as the nearest ghost there and brighter the closer it is. The user's G1 replaces the eight edge glows with the **danger ring**: one circle of translucent dots around the middle of the area below Roblox's top bar (G8), 60% of that area's smaller side across, cut into the same eight sectors; the square map stays in its corner, its walls following the maze colour rule (f0109).

**The ring (pure, `pickman/src/shared/Logic/Awareness/init.luau`).**
- `Awareness.RING = { share = 0.6, dot = 0.035, dots = 6, lit = { min = 0.35, max = 0.85 }, idle = 0.15 }`: the ring's outer diameter as a share of the screen's smaller side, a dot's side as a share of it, dots per sector, a lit dot's opacity from `min` (farthest) to `max` (nearest), an unlit dot's opacity.
- `export type RingDot = { x, y, w, h: number, sector: number, lit: boolean, brightness: number }`.
- `Awareness.ring(list: { Indicator }, width: number, height: number): { RingDot }`: every dot of every sector (8 × `dots`), sector 0 (ahead) at the top and the sectors round to the left as `Awareness.sector` counts them, so a ghost to the left lights the left of the ring. Each sector spans an eighth of a turn as `dots` dots and one dot's gap; the dots' outer edge is on the circle of diameter `share` × the smaller side, centred in the `width` × `height` frame it is given, and every box is in that frame's pixels (DangerHud gives it the core-UI safe area, G8). Only a ghost that can kill (look `"normal"`) lights a sector: `Awareness.ring` keeps those indicators and hands them to `Awareness.glows` (f0102), so the nearest dangerous ghost in a sector (ties to the earlier) gives the sector's brightness, clamped to 0..1, and a nearer frightened ghost never hides a farther dangerous one. Frightened and blinking (edible) ghosts and eyes never light the ring (prep's choice under the user's "just a red or something to indicate": the ring means danger; an edible ghost is not one). A side that is not a positive finite number counts as 0.

**The adapter (`pickman/src/client/DangerHud.luau`).** The edge glows' screen becomes the ring's (`Ring`, `ScreenInsets = CoreUISafeInsets`, `DisplayOrder = -1`, under the radar, the Flow overlay and the HUD): its frame is the core-UI safe area, below Roblox's top bar and the HUD's own frame (G8), so `area` (its `AbsoluteSize`) is that area's size. Each render step it asks `Awareness.indicators` (inView) as today and `Awareness.ring(list, area.X, area.Y)`, and draws each dot as a round frame at its box: a lit dot in the one warning colour `View.DANGER_COLOR` (255, 60, 30) at opacity `lit.min + (lit.max - lit.min) × brightness`, an unlit one white at `idle`. It does no geometry (no sin, cos, atan, sqrt; f0033's rule). `Awareness.edgePoint`, `Awareness.glowBox` and `Awareness.GLOW` stay in Awareness (f0102's pure specs pin them) but DangerHud no longer calls them. The square map (`Awareness.radar`, `Hud.radarBox`) is unchanged; its wall dots take f0109's `View.wallColor` (f0158 depends on f0109).

**Coordinate spaces (review 001 F1).** Screen pixels: HudView's blocks (`Hud.layout(w, h, core)`: score, level, lives and shots, power bar), the snap control and Roblox's jump buttons (`Hud.jumpButtons` on the core-UI frame). The device safe area: the corner radar (`Danger` screen, `Hud.radarBox(…, bandTop)`), shifted to the screen by the device inset. The core-UI safe area: the ring (`Ring` screen, G8); its boxes are shifted to the screen by the core-UI inset. clear.spec measures every box in screen pixels after those shifts.

**Clearance.** Centred below the top bar, the ring clears the HUD's edge blocks, the snap control, the corner radar and the jump buttons on every f0029 sample screen (portrait and landscape, notched, under a 58 px top bar, touch desktop), on 480×320, 568×320 and 667×375 under a 58 px bar, and on the desktop sizes, on desktop and touch. On 844×390 under the bar the ring's top is at y 120, below the score and level blocks (y 67–85). **Open (G9, to the user through the coordinator): two portrait short screens under a 58 px top bar still overlap with the ring centred below the bar:** 320×568 on touch, where the ring's lower-right dots meet the corner radar (lifted to y 305 above the jump buttons); and 320×480, where they meet the radar (desktop at y 340, touch at y 217) and, on touch, the higher jump button (184, 344). Both draw over the ring (the radar's screen is DisplayOrder 0, Roblox's buttons are core GUI), so nothing is hidden behind the ring. clear.spec is red on exactly these two screens until the user answers; prep has not shrunk the ring. HUD overlays drawn mid-screen (f0126's pick bar at 0.4 of the safe height and boon banner at 0.3, not on main yet) may cross the ring on short screens; they draw over it (G6).

**f0110's touch buttons (review 001 F5).** f0110 (preparing, `prep-touch-fire-and-hop`) adds FIRE and HOP (`Touch.buttons`); on its prototype FIRE sits under the ring on 480×320 (with the ring in the device area; to be re-measured in the core-UI frame). Neither is on main, so f0158 cannot name them. Rule: **whichever of f0110 and f0158 lands second adds `Touch.buttons`' boxes to clear.spec's touch boxes on every screen**, and any remaining overlap goes to the user as an accepted overlap (like G6), not a smaller ring. The coordinator relays this to f0110's prep.

**The warning colour (`pickman/src/shared/Logic/View/init.luau`).** `View.DANGER_COLOR = { r = 255, g = 60, b = 30 }`, a red-orange: of the reds searched (R ≥ 200, G and B ≤ 90, steps of 5), it has the best worst-case WCAG contrast against every wall colour it can sit over: 2.53 against the maze blue (33, 33, 222) and f0126's gold (255, 215, 0), 3.37 against f0109's edible green (14, 64, 14), 3.55 against the white flash. Pure red (255, 0, 0) gets only 2.25 against the blue.

**Every input (the user's rule, 2026-10-25).** The ring is display only: desktop, touch and console show the same ring; nothing is bound.

### Scalability
48 dots, one frame each, reused every render step; `Awareness.ring` is O(48) with no allocation beyond its result list; the indicators and glows are computed once per step as today. No server cost.

### Maintainability
All geometry and numbers live in `Awareness` (`RING`), colours in `View`; DangerHud only draws. The ring reuses `Awareness.glows`, so a later change to which ghost lights a direction changes the ring and its tests at once.

### Usability
A ghost's direction is read at the middle of the screen, where the player already looks, instead of at the edge; the ring is thin and translucent so the maze ahead stays visible; idle dots outline the ring so a lit sector reads as a direction.
