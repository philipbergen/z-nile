# f0235: Show power pills while jumping

## Problem
Power pills are hard to find from the floor. When the player is jumping (including the elevated wait of f0234), power pill locations should be visible, so the player can plan a route to them.

## Non-goals
- The regular-pill glow: f0236 (must be clearly distinct from this indicator).
- The elevated start/new-life wait itself: f0234.

## Solution
- While airborne, mark each remaining power pill so it is visible from the jump height (e.g. a tall beacon or marker seen over walls).

## Design
The beacon is presentation only. The server already sends every pellet's presence (`frame.pellets`), the hop (`frame.hopped`, `frame.hopTicks`) and the play state, so it sends nothing new and decides nothing new. A beacon shows what the client already draws (where the power pellets are) only while the eye can see over the walls. It reveals nothing the client did not already hold.

**Product decisions relied on** (`pickman/.tracker/game-design.md`):
- Spy hop (f0101): "slightly lower walls, ghosts keep the walls' old height (10) and stand above them, and a jump raises the camera over the walls for a short time"; "The player is airborne only while the eye is above the wall tops (the middle of the jump)"; "pellets passed over are not eaten"; **(prep)** "walls 9 studs; the eye rises 14 studs"; "The server decides the hop (the `Hop` intent) and the camera shows it."
- Boons (f0126): "**longer jumps** (every hop airborne 1.5 times as long ...)" and "**pellets back** (every eaten pellet and power pellet reappears)". A fresh maze each level (f0147): "every pellet is back".
- Pillar 3: "**Fair and readable** in first person."
- The executor adds one **(prep)** bullet, *Power pellet beacons* (f0235), right after *Glows* (f0102). The user may veto any of it. The bullet records the numbers below, and AC6 pins it. Its text is prep's, in `experiments/records.patch`, which also extends architecture.md's View row. Both land with the raft, not the prep, because the dispatcher's prep gate takes only the issue's own folder.

**What the player sees.** While the eye the camera draws is above the wall tops, every power pellet still in the maze has a beacon:
- **Look:** a square pillar of the pellets' own light: their colour `(255, 184, 174)`, Neon, 30% see-through.
- **Size:** 3 studs wide (the power pellet's diameter), on the pellet's tile centre, from the pellet's centre (3 studs) up to 20 studs. That is 11 studs over the walls (9), 10 over the ghosts' heads (10) and 2 over the eye at a jump's top (4 + 14 = 18).
- **Near the eye:** a beacon within 1.5 tiles of the eye (12 studs) is not shown, so a hop down a power pellet's corridor never flies through its pillar (review 001 F4). The player is then over or next to the pellet, which they can see.
- **Standing:** no beacon shows on the ground, or at any eye at or under the wall tops.
- **Eaten pellets:** a beacon goes when its pellet is eaten, and comes back with it (pellets back, a fresh maze).
- **Not shown elsewhere:** beacons do not pulse (the pellets under them still do), and the radar and the HUD do not show them.

**When: the eye the camera draws.** f0234's wait holds the player "at the apex of a jump arc", and that is no hop the server's `Hop.airborne` knows about. The one thing every elevated state shares (a hop, a long hop, f0234's hold, or anything later that lifts the camera) is that the camera draws the eye above the walls.
- **The sum:** the camera's eye is one module-level value, `eye`. The render step computes the eye's height as one sum, stores it in `eye`, and builds its one CFrame write from it. `Camera.eye()` returns it.
- **A hop:** the drawn eye is above the walls exactly when `Hop.rise > Hop.CLEARANCE` (`CLEARANCE` is wall 9 minus eye 4). So for a hop the beacons show over the span the server samples for `Hop.airborne`, off from the drawn eye by at most 0.19 of a step (f0101).
- **Why not read `camera.CFrame` back:** that would need a `Position` in every stub camera, f0234's sequestered one included, and it made a `local` in the render step silently shadow the value (review 001 F1).

**With f0234** (prepared on its own branch, `prep-pickman-jump-arc-on-start` at 40365ef0): `f0234.md` designs the same render-step line as `local eye = View.SCALE.eyeHeight + Hop.rise(hopped, shown, hopTicks) + Hop.held(readyEnd, shown)` and `camera.CFrame = CFrame.new(x, eye, z) * ...`. It offers f0235 "the eye sum that Camera computes".
- **In both landing orders**, the line conflicts in git. The right merge keeps f0235's module-level `eye` and adds `+ Hop.held(readyEnd, shown)` to the one sum (E5).
- **The wrong merge** (f0234's `local eye` declared in the render step) shadows the module value, and `Camera.eye()` stays nil. f0235's `acceptance/camera.spec.luau`, which runs the real Camera, fails it (4 of 6 cases). After f0235 is done that spec is promoted, so it holds f0234's executor to the right merge. If f0234 lands first, f0235's executor makes the merge.
- No `depends_on` is needed: E5 ran both orders green with both issues' acceptance specs.

**Interfaces** (`src/shared/Logic/View/init.luau`, pure; no new dependency edge, since View already requires Pellets and defines `Pose`):
```lua
-- A power pellet's beacon (f0235): a square pillar, its centre and size in world studs; y is up.
export type BeaconPart = { x: number, y: number, z: number, width: number, height: number }
View.BEACON = table.freeze({ width = 3, top = 20, transparency = 0.3, clear = 1.5 }) -- clear: tiles
-- On the slot's tile centre (View.world), from View.PELLET.height up to View.BEACON.top, BEACON.width wide.
function View.beacon(slot: Pellets.Slot): BeaconPart
-- The eye drawn at world height `eye` is strictly above View.SCALE.wallHeight; false for nil, NaN and +-inf.
function View.elevated(eye: number?): boolean
-- Keyed by slot index over every power slot of `slots` and no other slot:
-- `elevated and present[i] == true and not near`, where near is `pose ~= nil` and the squared tile distance from
-- the pose to the slot is strictly under BEACON.clear squared. A nil pose is near nothing.
function View.beacons(slots: { Pellets.Slot }, present: { boolean }, elevated: boolean, pose: Pose?): { [number]: boolean }
```
Callers: `PelletView` only. The distance is plain, not wrapped. Beacons are drawn only at their slots (no copies in the tunnel margins, unlike the walls), so the camera at a margin is far from them.

**Adapters** (thin; the one branch is View's):
- `src/client/Camera.luau`:
  - Add the module-level `local eye: number? = nil` and `function Camera.eye(): number?`, which returns it.
  - In the render step, `local drawn = View.SCALE.eyeHeight + Hop.rise(hopped, shown, hopTicks)`, then `eye = drawn`, then `camera.CFrame = CFrame.new(x, drawn, z) * ...`.
  - The literal `View.SCALE.eyeHeight + Hop.rise(hopped, shown, hopTicks)` that f0101 AC5 and f0126 pin, the `local shown = View.shownTick(...)` line f0143 pins, and the one CFrame write all stay.
  - Before the first snapshot the step returns before drawing, so `eye` stays nil. `Camera.pose()` already exists (the radar's).
- `src/client/PelletView.luau`:
  - The `Handle` gains `slots` (from `Pellets.slots(m)`, taken once), `beacons: { [number]: BasePart }` and `shown: { [number]: boolean }` (both by slot index), and `present: { boolean }` (the last decoded code).
  - `build` makes, for each power slot, a `Part` named `"Beacon"` in the same `Pellets` model, and sets `h.shown[i] = false`. The part is Anchored, with CanCollide, CanQuery, CanTouch and CastShadow false, `Material = Neon`, and `Color = COLOR` (the one `local COLOR = Color3.fromRGB(...)` that f0109's spec reads). It is a block (no `Shape`), with `Size = (width, height, width)` and `Position` from `View.beacon`, and `Transparency = 1`. Beacons are not added to `h.power`, so the pulse never resizes them.
  - `show` also stores the decoded list in `h.present`, and keeps its early return on a repeated code.
  - `start(eye: () -> number?, pose: () -> View.Pose?)`: its existing `RenderStepped` handler (the pulse) also loops over `View.beacons(h.slots, h.present, View.elevated(eye()), pose())`. Where `h.shown[i] ~= on`, it sets `h.shown[i] = on` and the beacon's `Transparency` to `View.BEACON.transparency` or 1. A frame that changes no beacon's state writes nothing.
  - The beacons go with the model (Main destroys it on a new maze), and `current` is the latest built, as today.
- `src/client/Main.client.luau`: `PelletView.start(Camera.eye, Camera.pose)` (was `PelletView.start()`), still before `ready:FireServer()`.

**Edge cases.**
- **The eye:** no eye yet is hidden. An eye exactly at the wall tops is hidden: the rule is strict, as `Hop.airborne` is. A NaN or infinite eye is hidden.
- **The pose:** no pose yet is near nothing, so it hides no beacon. The eye exactly `clear` tiles from a beacon shows it: the rule is strict.
- **Pellet changes:**
  - A power pellet eaten, or brought back by pellets back, while up changes its beacon on the next frame.
  - A pellet hopped over is not eaten (f0101), so its beacon stays. It is hidden only while the eye is within 1.5 tiles.
  - With all four eaten there is no beacon.
- **Landing:** every beacon is hidden on the first frame at or under the walls.
- **Pauses:** in a level-clear flash, the dying pause and game over, the eye is standing (no hop runs then, f0101), so the beacons are hidden.
- **f0234's hold** at a jump's top: shown for as long as it holds. f0234's wait is at the spawn, far from every power pellet.
- **A new maze or level:** new beacons come with the new model, all hidden, and `shown` starts false.
- **A death** keeps the pellets, so it keeps the beacons' set.
- **Players:** mazes are solo, so each client draws only its own. A player who leaves takes their client with them.
- **Frame-rate independence:** the rule reads the eye and place drawn this frame, and integrates nothing.
- **Render order (E2):** whether Roblox runs `RenderStepped` handlers before or after the camera's bound step, the eye read is this frame's or the last one's. That is at most one frame (about 16 ms) against the 667 ms a hop is airborne, the same on both edges.
- **Hysteresis** (pickman-arch lesson hysteresis-on-every-boundary): not needed.
  - The eye crosses the wall tops only on a hop's parabola, once up and once down. f0234 holds it 9 studs clear.
  - The pose crosses the 1.5-tile ring only while moving along a rail. A player at rest sits on a tile centre, and no tile centre is 1.5 tiles from another (their distances are 1, 1.41, 2, ...).
  - No input sits on a boundary.

### Scalability
- **Per frame:** two getter calls, one `View.beacons` walk of the maze's slots (about 244 on the classic maze, one kind test each, and a distance for the 4 power slots), and 4 boolean compares, all in the existing pellet render handler. Beacon writes happen only when a beacon's state changes: twice per hop, once per pellets-code change, and once when the eye enters and leaves a beacon's ring. The `Pellets.decode` work stays in `show`, once per new code.
- **Build:** 4 more parts per maze.
- **Replication:** nothing new. The server, its tick and the frame are untouched, so the cost does not grow with the player count, and the ghost count does not enter.
- **Maze size** enters only through the walk of the slots.

### Maintainability
- The look and the ring are data in `View.BEACON` (width, top, transparency, clear), beside `View.PELLET`.
- The rule is two pure functions, `View.elevated` and `View.beacons`.
- A new raised-eye state (f0234's, or a later one) adds its term to the camera's one eye sum, which `Camera.eye` returns, and needs nothing here.
- Another marker shown from the air (f0236's glow over the regular pellets) plugs in the same way: it takes `View.elevated(Camera.eye())` and its own data table.
- A new maze with more or fewer power pellets gets one beacon per power slot, by data.

### Usability
- **The player:**
  - The beacon looks like the pellet it marks (its colour and width, rising out of it), so it reads as "a power pellet is here".
  - It stands 11 studs over the walls and 2 over the eye at the top, so it shows against the sky across the whole maze. From a jump's top, a power pellet itself shows over the walls from only 4.6% of the (tile, power pellet) pairs on the classic maze, and its beacon from 100% (E1).
  - At the farthest pair (about 290 studs) the beacon is still at least 6 px tall and 1.5 px wide on a 320 px tall screen (AC1).
  - It shows only while the player can see over the walls, so standing views and fairness are unchanged. It is never in the player's face: it hides within 1.5 tiles.
  - It is 30% see-through and 3 studs wide in an 8-stud corridor, so a ghost behind it stays visible. A ghost's body is 6 wide.
- **The developer:**
  - `PelletView.start(eye, pose)` takes two getters, the shape of `DangerHud.start(Camera.pose)`.
  - `View.beacons` returns a map keyed by slot index, so it cannot put a beacon on a dot.
  - `Camera.eye` is the one value the camera draws its eye at.

  - It is about the pellet's width, and at most half a corridor.

  - Over a hop and a long hop it is true exactly where `Hop.rise > Hop.CLEARANCE`, and an eye held at a jump's top stays elevated.
  - `View.beacons` keys every power slot and only those. Each is true exactly when that pellet is present, the eye is elevated, and the eye's place is not strictly within `BEACON.clear` (between 1 and 2) tiles of it. A nil place is near nothing.
- AC3: PelletView, run from its real source, builds a hidden, neon, non-colliding `Beacon` in the pellets' colour for each power pellet, at `View.beacon`. Each frame it shows the beacons `View.beacons` gives for the eye and place its getters return:

  - all the remaining ones at a jump's top, for as long as it is held;
  - a change on the next frame when a power pellet is eaten or brought back;
  - the beacon near the eye hidden along a hop down its corridor, and shown again past it; and
  - none after landing.

  It writes only beacons whose state changed. The beacons keep `View.beacon`'s size and place on every frame. The pellets and their pulse stay as before, and a rebuilt maze lights its own beacons.
