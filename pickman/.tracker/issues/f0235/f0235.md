# f0235: Show power pills while jumping

## Problem
Power pills are hard to find from the floor. When the player is jumping (including the elevated wait of f0234), power pill locations should be visible, so the player can plan a route to them.

## Non-goals
- The regular-pill glow: f0236 (must be clearly distinct from this indicator).
- The elevated start/new-life wait itself: f0234.

## Solution
- While airborne, mark each remaining power pill so it is visible from the jump height (e.g. a tall beacon or marker seen over walls).
