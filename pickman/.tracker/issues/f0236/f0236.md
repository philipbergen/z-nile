# f0236: Glow while jumping shows where regular pills are

## Problem
While jumping, the player should see a glow that indicates where regular pills can be found (e.g. regions with pills remaining), helping route planning. The glow must be clearly distinct from the power-pill indicator of f0235 in colour and shape, so the two are never confused.

## Non-goals
- Showing power pills: f0235.
- The elevated start/new-life wait: f0234.

## Solution
- Airborne-only glow over areas holding regular pills; distinct hue and form from the power-pill markers.
- Idea: dim as pills are eaten so it also shows progress.
