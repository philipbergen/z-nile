# R0051: Touch fire and hop

## Theme
A single feature: touch players get the blaster (f0100) and the spy hop (f0101), which today only E and Space reach. It builds on R0012's touch layout (f0027 to f0029): the fold that classifies each finger, the snap control's hit-test, and the jump corner f0029 keeps clear.

Decisions it relies on (`pickman/.tracker/game-design.md`):
- Platforms: "Desktop (keyboard and mouse) and **mobile (touch)**."
- Mobile: "a swipe left/right/down triggers `Left`/`Right`/`Back`, and a swipe up triggers `Forward`"; "Look: drag on the right half of the screen"; "Touch targets are at least 44 pt"; "Recentring: a 'snap look to heading' button".
- Authority: "The **server** simulates every maze. Clients send intents and render the replicated state."
- Blaster (f0100): "The shot is two-dimensional along the maze grid, the way the player looks, rounded to the nearest heading (the arrows' direction when one is held)."
- Spy hop (f0101): "The server decides the hop (the `Hop` intent) and the camera shows it."
- Desktop keys (f0104): "**Space** jumps (the spy hop, f0101). **E** fires the blaster (f0100)."
- Added by this prep, Mobile: "**Fire and hop** (f0110; a stopgap until d0149's touch circle controls, user, 2026-10-25): touch has a fire control for the blaster, aimed as on desktop (the look's nearest heading), and a jump control for the spy hop (f0101), placed with R0012's touch layout. **(prep, R0051)**: both are buttons (not gestures), shown only on a touch screen: a cyan **FIRE** disc (the shot bars' cyan) and a white **JUMP** disc, told apart by their outlined captions, since the user sees that cyan as white (see Inky's colour). Each acts once, when the finger goes down; a finger on them never looks or turns, so a look drag that starts on FIRE spends a shot (firing on the lift would avoid that but delay every shot). JUMP is Roblox's own touch jump button's place (its legacy placement, the corner R0012 keeps clear); FIRE is the same size, one margin to its left, raised clear of the snap button. On the shortest screens (320 px under a 58 px top bar) FIRE's top 14 px sit under the radar's bottom, as the radar already overlaps Roblox's second jump placement there; on a 320 px wide portrait screen JUMP shares one pixel column with the snap button, and with a 500 px short side its bottom row lies on the power bar."
- This prep also removed the open decision "The shot bars on touch (R0035 prep): shown until f0110 gives touch a fire control, or hidden until then": f0110 is that fire control, so the bars stay shown.

- Decided with the user (2026-10-25): "Land R0051 as a stopgap", in the order d0151 (touch input reaches nothing on a phone: found and fixed first), f0110 (this raft), f0126 (wall picks), d0149 (the circle controls that replace these buttons). Until d0149, touch players can shoot and hop.

## Whole-raft design
One issue, f0110; its Design is the whole design.
```
HudView.lay ── Touch.buttons(layout) ──► Fire / Hop frames (drawn, Active = false, touch screens only)
      │                                   HudView.buttons() ─┐
Main: Controls.start(..., HudView.buttons)                   │
Controls (per touch event) ── Touch.track(state, event, { width, snap, fire, hop }) ◄┘
      ├─ { kind = "fire" } ─► fire(): Blaster.intent(Camera.aim()) ─► Intent remote (as E)
      └─ { kind = "hop" }  ─► Hop.INTENT ─────────────────────────────► Intent remote (as Space)
Server: unchanged (Session.submit, the rate limit, Blaster and Hop rules)
```
- Changed Logic: `Touch` (two zones, two effects, `hopRect`, `fireRect`, `buttons`, the `Buttons` type).
- Changed adapters: `Controls` (a fifth optional hook), `HudView` (two frames, `HudView.buttons`), `Main` (one argument).
- Architecture map (done in this prep): the edge `Touch -> Hud`, and Touch's row names the buttons. The deps spec passes with the edge declared before the require exists.

## Risks & rollback
- **The fire button overlaps the radar on the shortest screens** (14 rows on 480×320 and 568×320 under a 58 px top bar), and **the hop button touches the snap control (320×568) or the power bar (a 500 px short side) by one pixel**. Accepted and pinned, as f0029 accepted the radar over Roblox's second jump row; game-design.md says so, so the user can veto it.
- **Finger-down fire wastes a shot when a look drag starts on FIRE.** Chosen for timing (f0100's side shots must be pressed early already); firing on the lift would cost every shot the tap's length. Marked **(prep)** in game-design.md for the user's veto.
- **FIRE's cyan reads as white to this user** (game-design.md, Inky's colour). The outlined captions tell the buttons apart; the cyan follows the shot bars, and the f0109 entry now says so.
- **Roblox's touch jump button showing after all** would sit under the JUMP button. It can't today (f0029 E7: no Humanoid, `TouchControlsEnabled = false`), and both would jump.
- **A tap on a button also reaching a GUI object**: the frames are `Active = false`, as the snap control is, so input reaches `UserInputService` (OC1 confirms on a phone).
- **Touch reaches nothing on a phone today** (the user, 2026-10-25, E6): the buttons would be dead too. d0151 fixes it first; R0051 depends on it, the readying lead re-proves the specs on the prototype merged with d0151's fix (Order & dependencies), and Phase 1 asks the user to re-check on a phone.
- **f0126's PICK button sits beside FIRE and JUMP** (after this raft). Its prep places it against `Touch.buttons`; c0155 (narrowing button-table pins) has nothing left to narrow in f0110's specs, which pin by field.
- **d0149 replaces this design** (user, 2026-10-25: a stopgap). PR #127, which folded f0110 away, was closed unmerged; d0149 on main depends on d0151 and f0110, and its prep owns a chore that narrows or removes `tests/acceptance/f0110/` (promoted by R0051's `done`) where the circles change what it pins. f0110's folder stays (in `done/`), as every done item's does.
- **f0126 (wall picks), c0150 and f0146 (gamepad) touch `Controls`, `HudView`, `Main` and the key pins too** (a pick key and bar, longer hops, a Pad require). The specs here press only E and Space, never count keys or pin the hop's length, load any further Logic require as itself, and pin `Touch.buttons` by field, so a merge with any of them is textual only; whoever lands second re-runs `./zn check f0110`. f0126's pick bar and boon banner (`Hud.pickBox`, `Hud.boonBox`) come after this raft, so f0126's own specs check them against `Touch.buttons` (its prep reports they clear these buttons today).
- Rollback: revert the PR. No stored data, no wire change, no server change.
- Lessons that changed the design (lessons pass, before round 1): `prove-specs-pass-on-a-prototype` (the prototype, the mutants, the Linux run), `specify-every-named-artifact` (the buttons' colours, captions, transparency and names written down), `restated-rules-move-together` (game-design.md's blaster paragraph and Open decisions, architecture.md's Touch row, the module header comments), `never-narrow-captured-scope` (both outcomes, fire and hop, have a Design and Checked criteria; "a jump gesture or button" is answered with a button), `name-each-coordinate-space` (every rectangle in screen pixels, said so; the inset case added to the adapter spec), `pin-call-site-arguments` (HudView's layout argument pinned by side and bottom insets, after a top-only notch proved vacuous).
