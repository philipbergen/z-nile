# R0053: Risky wall picks

## Theme
One issue, f0126 "Risky wall picks grant random boons", rafted alone: it is `solo: true` (the user split the captured request into three sequenced rafts, f0126, then f0147 levels by picked walls, then f0148 start on any level up to your best, 2026-10-25), so nothing else is folded in.

The decisions it relies on are quoted in f0126's Problem and in `pickman/.tracker/game-design.md`'s "Wall picks" section, among them:
- the tuning: a pick lasts ten paces of a hunting ghost (as tuned), interior walls only, always three marked in gold, one tile per pick, six boons at even odds (longer jumps, a faster next pick, a ghost pill, a long ghost pill, a blaster shot, the pellets back);
- every input: F on the keyboard, Y / triangle on a controller (`Pad.BUTTONS.ButtonY`), a PICK button one margin left of FIRE on touch;
- f0109 lands first, and marked walls stay gold while the walls are green;
- the marks' gold is 255, 215, 0 ("Brighter gold", 2026-10-26), at least 30 CIE76 from every ghost colour and look.
