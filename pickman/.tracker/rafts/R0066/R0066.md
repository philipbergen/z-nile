# R0066: Touch circle controls

## Theme
A single defect, d0149: touch play becomes two circles. The left circle steers (each pull left, down, up or right is one turn, as A, S, W, D; two quick pulls up boost), the right circle points the view (the pull's direction, back ahead on the lift; a quick tap hops), and FIRE and PICK sit in a row beside the look circle. The swipes, the free drag look, the snap button and JUMP go. It is a raft of its own by the user's choice (2026-11-01: "New raft for d0149", rather than folding it into R0044, which is `single: true` and ready to start while d0149 waits on its dependencies).

Decisions this raft relies on are d0149's `Decided with the user` lines (2026-10-25, 2026-10-31, 2026-11-01), quoted in its Problem:
> "Points the view": the pull's direction is where the player looks, relative to the heading, as the arrow keys do but in any direction, eased at the arrows' turn rate with the pitch level.
> "Short tap, jump on lift": a finger that lifts within 0.25 s and has moved less than 12 px is a tap and hops when it lifts; anything else only looks.
> "f0158 first": d0149 lands after f0158 and keeps its circles and buttons clear of the ring.
> "Nothing": only the controls respond; the free drag look and the swipe halves go, and touch keeps the view level.
> "30% of short side": each circle's diameter is 30% of the screen's shorter side, between 96 and 160 px.
> "Accept it": on 480 x 320 under a 58 px top bar, PICK may draw over a few of the ring's dots.
> "Mark ready": design review 005's fix accepted without a sixth round.
