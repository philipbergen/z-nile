# R0094: Speed boost escape

## Theme
Three features that change the player's speed, the level and the radar, grouped by the planner because their Touched paths overlap (`./zn raft-plan`): f0143 (Speed boost escape, 260), f0147 (Levels by picked walls, 450) and f0158 (Danger radar ring, 180). Total size 890 changed lines.
- **f0143 Speed boost escape**: a double tap of forward (W, the D-pad's Up, two swipes up, a quick double push of the stick up; the stick's click sends a lone Boost) asks the server for a burst of `Boost.FACTOR` 1.5 times the player's speed for 22 steps, capped at a tile a step, with a 90-step cooldown; a chevron on the HUD and a wider field of view show it.
- **f0147 Levels by picked walls**: ten finished wall picks start the next level; every level is 5% faster than the one before and level 1 is 30% slower than today, for the player and the ghosts alike; every point is worth the level number; the HUD shows `PICKS n/10`; the tenth pick is a bonanza.
- **f0158 Danger radar ring**: the eight edge glows become a ring of 48 translucent dots (8 sectors of 6) at 60% of the smaller side of the area below Roblox's top bar, lit in one warning red by the nearest dangerous ghost in each sector; the corner map stays.

**Shared surface** (the common Touched paths of the designs):
- `pickman/src/shared/Logic/Session` (f0143 and f0147), `pickman/src/shared/Logic/Hud` and `pickman/src/client/HudView.luau` (f0143 and f0147), `pickman/.tracker/game-design.md` (f0143 and f0147), `pickman/src/shared/Logic/View` (f0143 and f0158).
- Indirect: f0158's `clear.spec` measures every HUD block against the ring (`Hud.layout`, `Touch.blocks`, `Hud.radarBox`, `Hud.jumpButtons`), so the boost chevron and the picks counter (the first inside the lives block, the second under the score block) are on its surface; f0147 and f0143 both read `Session.speeds(...).player` for the step's speed.
- The ring (f0158) and the counter (f0147) are both HUD drawing at the edges and centre of the same screen; the boost (f0143) and the ramp (f0147) both set how fast the player moves.

**Decisions the designs quote** (each in its own Problem or Design):
- f0143: "Trigger the speed boost with a double tap on the forward ('w') key" (2026-10-25); "We support keyboard, console, and touch for every feature"; "Accept all (Recommended)" (G1 to G9); "Yes, stick up boosts too" (2026-10-26); "Same as W; D-pad yes".
- f0147: "3 rafts in order (Recommended)" (f0126, then f0147, then f0148); "Only picks; maze stays empty"; "5% per level (Recommended)"; "Fresh maze (Recommended)"; "Level 1 rules on every level (Recommended)"; "x level, extra life unscaled"; "Stop at level 34 (Recommended)"; "Follow the ramp (Recommended)"; "Yes, show picks/10 (Recommended)"; "Tenth boon should be the bonanza, you get all the boons at once ..." with "Carry into next level (Recommended)"; "Keep it, record it"; "Carry fractions (Recommended)".
- f0158: "Yes, and the danger radar needs to be more visible, make it more of circle segments with a circle of translucent indicators, keep it at a size roughly 60% of the smallest dimension"; "Replace edge glows (Recommended)" (G1); "Accept all, but just a red or something to indicate, not a color match" (G2 to G7); "Centre below top bar" (G8); "Accept those overlaps" (G9); "Accept these too" (G10); "Tell f0158 (Recommended)" (c0159's DisplayOrder rule).

## Experiments phase
None at raft level. The one line the members meet at (the speed) is quoted in f0147's Regression guard, and the margins hold on the ramp. On `GAME_CONFIG` (the ramp plus f0125's 1/1.1 tuning), a boosted player gains the least on levels 1 and 2 (design-review-001 F4): 32 against 18 on level 1 and 33 against 19 on level 2, 14 units a step, 308 units (2.57 tiles) over 22 steps, or about 1.9 tiles in a dotted corridor. That is above f0143's E1 bar of 240 units. The gain grows to about 50 at level 29, where the boost first reaches the cap of a tile a step (`1.5 × 21 × 1.05^28 ≈ 123`), then falls to 30 at level 34 (120 against 90). The members ran their own experiments in prep: f0143 E1 to E7, f0147 E1 to E8 and f0158 E0 to E4. Of their prototypes, only f0147's `experiments/prototype.patch` survives. f0143's `proto-f0143-deps2` and f0158's `proto-f0158` are gone (design-review-001 F2).

## Risks & rollback
- **c0176 and c0177 are unfinished** (see Cross-raft waits, with the fallback): without them f0147's narrowed promoted specs fail. Mitigation: z-ready refuses the raft until both are done (they narrow four promoted files and one speed spec).
- **Three members edit `Session`, `Hud` and `HudView`**: a textual merge on the same files. Each member's prototype was built without the others, so the first full gate on the merged tree is the real test: f0143 lands first, f0147 and f0158 rerun their acceptance specs on top of it (their executors do the full `--full` gate at merge only, as the project rules say).
- **The counter against the ring** is pinned only by c0196 (backlog) after both land; f0147 measured no overlap on f0158's 15 screens. If the executor of either finds an overlap, stop and report: neither may edit the other's spec.
- **The boost on the ramp**: f0143's specs never build `GAME_CONFIG`, so no spec runs a boost under the shipped ramp; the cap at a tile and the E1 margins make it safe by reasoning (f0147 Regression guard), and the code review should confirm the speed line reads `Session.speeds`.
- **Gameplay risk**: a 30% slower level 1 and the ramp change the feel for every player; the boost's 1.5 times factor is on top. The constants are one line each (`Boost`, `Levels.RAMP`).
- **Rollback**: revert the raft's PR. Everything is additive behind new modules and fields except the ramp: `GAME_CONFIG.ramp` is one field, and without it the classic tables return. A revert after d0149 or c0182 has landed on top needs theirs reverted first.
