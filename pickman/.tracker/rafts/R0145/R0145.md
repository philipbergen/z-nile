# R0145: Pickman jump arc on start

## Theme
Feature raft delivering Pickman's elevated-view pair: `f0234` (Wait for the start at a jump's top arc, free to look) and `f0235` (Show power pills while jumping). Both share `pickman/src/shared/Logic/View`, `pickman/src/client/Camera.luau`, `pickman/src/client/Main.client.luau`, `pickman/.tracker/game-design.md`, and `pickman/.tracker/architecture.md`.

Decisions relied on (quoted from `f0234` and `f0235`):
- `pickman/.tracker/game-design.md`: READY pause 60 steps; Spy hop rises 14 studs over 9-stud walls in 12 steps (`Hop.RISE`, `Hop.TICKS`), airborne while eye is above wall tops; free look never changes heading; server is authoritative; boons include longer jumps and pellets back.

## Whole-raft design
Shared modules and interfaces between `f0234` and `f0235`:
- `pickman/src/shared/Logic/Hop/init.luau` (`f0234` only): adds `Hop.held(playAt: number?, t: number): number`.
- `pickman/src/shared/Logic/View/init.luau`:
  - `f0234` adds `View.readyEnd(last: number?, play: Session.PlayFrame, tick: number): number?`.
  - `f0235` adds `export type BeaconPart`, `View.BEACON`, `View.beacon(slot)`, `View.elevated(eye)`, `View.beacons(slots, present, elevated, pose)`.
  - Disjoint additions; no signature conflict.
- `pickman/src/client/Camera.luau` (key shared render-step line):
  - `f0234` adds `local readyEnd: number? = nil`, `Camera.ready(play, tick)`, and `+ Hop.held(readyEnd, shown)` in the eye height expression.
  - `f0235` adds module-level `local eye: number? = nil`, `Camera.eye(): number?`, and computes `local drawn = View.SCALE.eyeHeight + Hop.rise(hopped, shown, hopTicks) + Hop.held(readyEnd, shown)` (preserving the literal `View.SCALE.eyeHeight + Hop.rise(hopped, shown, hopTicks)` pin), stores `eye = drawn`, and writes `camera.CFrame = CFrame.new(x, drawn, z) * ...` — never declaring a `local eye` that would shadow module-level `eye`.
- `pickman/src/client/PelletView.luau` (`f0235` only): adds beacon parts and updates `PelletView.start(eye, pose)`.
- `pickman/src/client/Main.client.luau`: `f0234` adds `Camera.ready(frame.play, frame.tick)` after `Camera.hopTicks(frame.hopTicks)`; `f0235` updates `PelletView.start(Camera.eye, Camera.pose)`. Disjoint lines.
- `pickman/.tracker/game-design.md` & `pickman/.tracker/architecture.md`: disjoint bullets/rows (f0234 updates Hop row and adds Waiting at the top bullet before Glows; f0235 updates View row and adds Power pellet beacons bullet after Glows).

## Order & dependencies
1. `f0234` — elevated READY hold and smooth landing (`Hop.held`, `View.readyEnd`, `Camera.ready`).
2. `f0235` — power-pellet beacons while elevated (`Camera.eye` wrapping the combined eye sum including `Hop.held`, `View.beacon`/`elevated`/`beacons`, `PelletView` beacons).
