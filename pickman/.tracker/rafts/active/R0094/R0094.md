# R0094: Speed boost escape

## Theme
Four features that change the player's speed, the level, the radar and where a run starts. The planner grouped f0143, f0147 and f0158 because their Touched paths overlap (`./zn raft-plan`); f0148 joins them because it `depends_on` f0147, and a dependent issue is stacked in its dependency's raft as a later layer (it had a raft of its own, R0095, now retired into this one). The members: f0143 (Speed boost escape, 260), f0147 (Levels by picked walls, 450), f0158 (Danger radar ring, 180) and f0148 (Start on any level up to your best, 450). Total size 1,340 changed lines (260 + 450 + 180 + 450).

User decision (2026-11-15): "Right now can you re-raft everthing that's open and stack the dependencies in rafts?"
The members quote an earlier decision, "3 rafts in order (Recommended)" (f0126, then f0147, then f0148), and f0147's and f0148's documents say "The work lands as sequenced rafts". The 2026-11-15 request replaces that packaging for f0147 and f0148: they now land as two layers of this one raft, in the same order (f0147, then f0148), with f0148 on top.
- **f0143 Speed boost escape**: a double tap of forward (W, the D-pad's Up, two swipes up, a quick double push of the stick up; the stick's click sends a lone Boost) asks the server for a burst of `Boost.FACTOR` 1.5 times the player's speed for 22 steps, capped at a tile a step, with a 90-step cooldown; a chevron on the HUD and a wider field of view show it.
- **f0147 Levels by picked walls**: ten finished wall picks start the next level; every level is 5% faster than the one before and level 1 is 30% slower than today, for the player and the ghosts alike; every point is worth the level number; the HUD shows `PICKS n/10`; the tenth pick is a bonanza.
- **f0148 Start on any level up to your best**: the server remembers each player's best level reached (a new pure `Logic/Best` and a DataStore `PickmanBest`), the lobby offers the levels 1 to best (arrows either side of START, which says `START LEVEL n`), and the game place claims the choice once from the store and starts the run there (`Game.new(config?, ramp?, start?)`, `Session.join(s, id, start?)`). It builds on f0147's `Game.start`, ramp and `Ready reason = "level"`.
- **f0158 Danger radar ring**: the eight edge glows become a ring of 48 translucent dots (8 sectors of 6) at 60% of the smaller side of the area below Roblox's top bar, lit in one warning red by the nearest dangerous ghost in each sector; the corner map stays.
