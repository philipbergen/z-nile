## Lesson candidates (for the lead; this review writes no lesson file)
- zz-review (or a z-nile chore on `trace.luau`): a spec case whose name stylua wraps onto the line after a bare `it(` cannot be cited by line (the reader only matches `it("…"` on the cited line), and `:: <case>` stops at the first comma or semicolon and applies to every spec reference in the cell, so a case name with a comma cannot be named at all. In this raft about 60 of the four items' acceptance cases are wrapped and nearly all have a comma, so those claims fall back to the check.sh line, which proves the whole spec ran, not the case. Check: does the trace reader take the case name from the line after a bare `it(`, and does `::` take the name up to the next `;` per reference?
- zz-review/not-stacked-is-main-moving (a note): the race here was 18 seconds between the skeleton commit and an unrelated `ready` landing on main. A final review on a busy main will nearly always meet it, so the check could report "main moved by N commits that touch none of the table's files" as one actionable line (with the restack command) instead of one stale line and one line per check.sh reference. Check: does `--check` say which commits main gained and whether they touch any cited path?
