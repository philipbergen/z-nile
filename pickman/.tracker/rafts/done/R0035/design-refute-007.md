# Refute 007: R0035 (answers to design-review-007)

- F1: fixed: the clear still forgets a hop whose last in-play tick is before `hopped + 2`, and it still keeps one that has reached step 2. On a step that begins in play, that tick is `s.tick` (the end call). On a step that begins already out of play, `Session.step` has already incremented the clock and `Session.kill` happened between steps, so that tick is `s.tick - 1` (the start call). `airborne.spec`: `Session.kill` right after take-off publishes nil and stays nil for 12 steps while the cancelled arc's midpoint clears the walls; `Session.kill` after the step after take-off does the same, with that frame still the take-off tick and not airborne, and with `Hop.rise` two steps in over `CLEARANCE` and the midpoint equal to `Hop.RISE`; `Session.kill` after the step whose tick is `hopped + 2` (airborne on that step) keeps the take-off tick, the next frame's rise is over `CLEARANCE`, and `Ready` clears it. The in-step cases from review 006 are unchanged. Mutant P29 (the start call passes `s.tick`) is killed by the kill after the step after take-off. Mutant P30 (every paused step forgets the hop) is killed by the kill after step 2. consequences: a kill after step 2 still shows the descent from above the walls; dropping the start call fails the kill right after take-off; a shared `<= hopped + 2` on the end call would forget a hop that dies at the end of step 2, and that call is unchanged.
