Critique: the tempting fix is one comparison at both call sites, `tick <= started + 2`, or `hopped = nil` whenever the run is not in play. The first, on the end call, forgets a hop that dies at the end of its second step (rise already 7.78, eye over the wall). The second nils the landing death frame and pops the descent from 4.28 studs to 0, and it nils a kill after step 2, popping from above the wall to the standing eye. Review 006's formula `s.tick < hopped + 2` is what the code does. It means "the last in-play step" only on the end call. On the start call the clock has already moved, and the new tick is a pause step, not a hop step.
Consequences: the end call's tick is the step that was in play when the step began, even though the catch or the last pellet ends play during it. The start call's last in-play tick is `s.tick - 1`. `moving` has to stay the value taken before `Game.step`; recomputing `canMove` after a `Ready` teleport sees `ready`, and `teleport` has already nilled `hopped`. A kill after take-off (last in-play tick `== hopped`) must still clear, or removing the start call ships. A kill after step 2 (last in-play tick `== hopped + 2`, rise 7.78) must keep `hopped` until `Ready`, or the eye pops through the wall. The `Hopped` event from the take-off step has already been returned; the kill step's `out` has none to drop, and the camera follows the frame. Clearing the kill-after-step-1 hop drops the eye from the already-published 4.28 (still under the wall) to 0. Keying the camera off the phase would zero the landing death frame too, because that frame is already `dying`. `dyingTicks` of 0 reaches `Ready` or game over on the first paused step, and `teleport` nils on `Ready`; the classic pause is 45, which is the one that plays the arc.
Recommendation: one rule, the last tick that was in play is `< hopped + 2`. On a step that begins in play, that tick is `s.tick`. On a step that begins already out of play, it is `s.tick - 1`. Keep the two sites on that one meaning; a shared `<= started + 2` is a different test on the end call (it forgets tick `== hopped + 2`).
