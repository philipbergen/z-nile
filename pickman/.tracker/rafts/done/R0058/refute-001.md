# Refute 001: R0058 (answers to review-001)
- F1: fixed (this commit): a new executor-owned spec, `tests/place/camera-pad.spec.luau`, runs Camera's real source on a stub engine (the render-step callback, a `Camera.pad` stand-in for `Pads.stick`, a real Session snapshot, a `CFrame.Angles` stand-in that records the pitch). Its five cases: the stick right turns the yaw at `View.look(Pad.look(...))`'s rate and the look stays when released; the stick up raises the pitch; a nil read and a tilt inside `LOOK_DEAD` change nothing; the look applies with the cursor locked and free; after a look right, `Camera.aim()` is the heading to the right. The reviewer's mutants E (look computed and thrown away) and F (only with the cursor free) each fail 4 of 5 cases; consequences: none, the spec loads Logic through `@logic` (stub loaders fall back), so later requires in Camera don't break it.
- F2: fixed (this commit): a new executor-owned spec, `tests/place/lobby-pad.spec.luau`, runs LobbyPad's real source on its own small stub (a settable `GuiService.SelectedObject` whose change signal fires only on a real change, `GetLastInputType`, `LastInputTypeChanged`, a PlayerGui holding `Start`) rather than widening the shared fake engine, whose nodes have no `IsA`, so the existing lobby specs see the same engine. Its six cases are the reviewer's list. Mutants B (and→or) fail 4, C (no selection at start) 1, D (no re-select) 1, H (another object) 3; consequences: none, LobbyPad is unchanged.
- F3: fixed (this commit): `Pads.stick` also returns the driving controller's input type name, and Sticks resets its fold (`Pad.steerStart()`) on a nil read and whenever that controller differs from the one its state came from; a fresh fold engages only at `STEER_ON`, so the reset sends nothing by itself. New spec `tests/place/sticks-pads.spec.luau`: a disconnect mid-push then the other pad pushed the same way turns; two connected pads, Gamepad2 taking over pushed left, turns (at `STEER_ON`); a reconnect at rest sends nothing. The old Sticks fails 2 of them, a reset on nil reads only (the review's tempting fix) fails 1; consequences: Camera takes the extra return value and ignores it (its look keeps no state), and its type follows.
- F4: fixed (this commit): `Pad.track` uses the module's `finite`, with a unit case for `math.huge`, `-math.huge` and NaN (mutant G fails it); and a case in `sticks-pads.spec` that a trigger's `InputChanged` does not take over while its `InputBegan` does (mutant A fails it); consequences: none, Roblox never reports an infinite stick.
- F5: fixed (this commit): the widened touch-adapters case now loops over `Pad.BUTTONS` too, asserting Sink on Begin and End for each, and Pass for `Pad.CONTINUE`; consequences: none.
