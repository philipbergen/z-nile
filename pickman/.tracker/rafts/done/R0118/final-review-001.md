Critique: code review 001 F3's fix covers a confirm that wrote nothing. It does not cover a confirm that wrote its grant whose use then failed. In both cases the hold the ask set has nothing left to end it, which is the "No close at all" and "An answer that grants nothing" notes of the lesson below. Two easy fixes are wrong. Releasing on every confirm would break Q6 and the two guard cases at paid-play-status.spec.luau:536 and :552, which pin that a stray confirm leaves a flight's or a receipt's hold. After the release, `known[player]` is nil (set by `heard`), so the next ask writes and uses it, bounded by `ASK_SECONDS`. Nothing changes in live mode or the lobby.
Recommendation: make that change, and add these cases to `tests/place/paid-play-status.spec.luau`, not the sequestered specs:
