## Solution
Three pure leaves and a config table carry the rules. `Logic/Play` holds the server's decision from a read of the player's record, the client's intents and the server's statuses checked field by field, each card's buttons and note, their slots and the keys. `Logic/Pairing` holds the 2 PLAYERS waiting lines of one server, `Logic/Arena` which shared arena an ARENA player joins, and `src/shared/Modes.luau` each title's buttons. A server adapter, `Plays`, installs the arcade's Receipts and owns each player's one play. It reads their record through the arcade's Wallet. A free start due or a held play goes at once; a failed read writes the portal's pass (`Wallet.pass`) and goes; otherwise it opens the 1 R$ play product (`Receipts.ask`) and goes on that purchase's grant. Going is a teleport through the arcade's Travel to the title's place from the place-id attributes (`Places`). PLAY goes to a public server. ARENA goes to the shared arena's reserved server, recorded in a MemoryStore hash map. 2 PLAYERS goes to the title's waiting line, and both players teleport in one call to a fresh reserved server once a second joins. Nothing is charged in the portal (the game place's `Wallet.arrive` claims the free start or the held play, or takes the pass), and nothing rides in TeleportData. A second server adapter, `Invites`, records a waiting player's invite and sends an invited friend who lands in another portal server to the inviter's. The client adapter, `PlayButtons`, fills each card's `Buttons` slot (f0205's `CarouselView.card`) from the card states the server already sends (`Remotes.Cards`), turns taps, the carousel's PLAY (`CarouselView.onPlay`) and its own keys into requests on a new `Remotes.Play`, draws the player's play on its card, and opens Roblox's invite prompt; the portal's client boots the arcade's test purchase screen, so playtests show the same screens and charge nothing.

### 1. `src/shared/Logic/Play/init.luau` (new, pure; requires `./Card`)

```
- **The decision** (the server's, from `Wallet.read`'s answer, `Wallet.mode` and the seconds `waited` since the check began): no record and `"busy"` before `CHECK_SECONDS`: `busy` (read again); still busy at `CHECK_SECONDS`: `pass` (a read in flight that long is a slow store; design review 001 F1). No record otherwise (`"down"`, or no reason): `pass` (the player goes on the portal's pass; first Guess). A record whose `free` is true, or with `plays >= 1`: `go`, in every mode, closed included (f0145's "free games and credits already held still work"; G11's order is the arrival's, `PaidPlay.start`). Neither, in closed mode: `closed` ("buying shows 'store unavailable'"). Else `buy`.
- **The intent** (a client's message): `"play"` with a name of `modes` and one of that name's modes, else nil; `"cancel"` and `"invite"` carry nothing, whatever else is sent. **The status** (`parse`, the client's read): a table whose `phase` is one of `PHASES`; with no name and no mode, just the phase; else a name of `modes` and a mode of it; anything else nil. Neither raises.
- **The price**: unknown state (or its free state not read yet): `""`; free: `"FREE"`; else `Card.strike(prices.struck) .. " " .. prices.price` (`"<s>5 R$</s> 1 R$"` with the arcade's `Prices`).
- **The card's view**: a title not published shows nothing (its badge says COMING SOON, f0205). When the player's status names this title and is not idle: `checking`, `buying` and `going` show only their note; `waiting` shows `NOTE.waiting` with CANCEL and, for 2 PLAYERS, INVITE FRIEND (both enabled, no price); `closed` and `failed` show their note above the title's buttons, which `failed` shows disabled (its cool-down; the server says `idle` when it ends). - **The keys are a contract**: AC1 and AC5 pin exactly these seven (the eighth Guess puts them to the user), so a later issue that adds a key to this card changes them through its own chore; the place spec pins rules, not sets (design review 001 F8).
- **The slots** (scale, of the Buttons slot): `count` buttons side by side, `GAP` apart, across the slot's full width; full height, or the bottom half when there is a note. `noteBox(count)`: the whole slot with no buttons, else its top half. **Readable text** (lesson pickman-test/readable-text-has-a-number): every text a card's buttons row can show (each label and price line, each note) is at least `Card.MIN_TEXT` px on f0205's seven screens, by `Card.textSize` over the front card's `Carousel.box`, `Card.REGIONS.Buttons`, `Card.INSET` and these slots: a button with a price is two lines (each half its height), one without is one line, a note wraps. - **The write budget** (`spend`): one more write is allowed while fewer than `WRITES` of the given instants lie within the last `WRITE_SPAN` seconds (an instant after `now`, a clock gone back, counts for nothing); it answers whether, and the instants kept (old ones dropped, `now` added only when allowed). It never changes the list it is given.
- **The invite's reads**: `inviter(raw)` is the user id in an invited friend's launch data, whole decimal digits of a positive id (at most 15 digits, no leading zero, no sign or spaces), else nil; `invite(raw, modes)` is an invite's stored record `{ job, name }`, a non-empty job and a title of `modes`, else nil.
- Nothing mutates its input. The word `game` appears nowhere in the file (pickman-arch rule 2); a title is a `name`.

### 5. `src/server/Plays.luau` (new, server adapter)

```
Requires: `@game/ReplicatedStorage/Shared/Logic/{Play,Pairing,Arena}`, `@game/ReplicatedStorage/Shared/{Games,Modes}`, `@game/ReplicatedStorage/Arcade/Logic/{PaidPlay,Travel}`, `@game/ReplicatedStorage/Arcade/Places`, `@game/ServerStorage/Arcade/{Wallet,Receipts}` (a server root may reach ServerStorage, portal-arch change 1), `./Invites`. Services: Players, ReplicatedStorage, ServerScriptService, TeleportService, MemoryStoreService, HttpService, RunService.
- **At start**: `remote = ReplicatedStorage.Remotes.Play` (`WaitForChild`); `ids = Places.read(ServerScriptService:GetAttribute)` once; `destination[entry.name] = ids[entry.place]` for each `Games.LIST` entry that has `Modes`. A title with no destination plays nothing; no place id ever leaves this server. `travel = Travel.new()`; `queue = Pairing.new()`; `Receipts.start({ granted, closed })` (this issue owns the portal's one call: f0205's Guess 7); `Invites.start()`.
- **The slot**, per Player object: `{ phase, name, mode, flight, invited, cool }`, made by each play, dropped on `PlayerRemoving`. `flight` is a serial that marks which play a yielding step belongs to; a step whose player left, or whose slot now belongs to a later play, does nothing more. **Kept across plays**, per Player object and dropped on `PlayerRemoving`: `writes` (the instants of the player's recent MemoryStore writes, for `Play.spend`) and `offered` (an invite on record, not withdrawn yet). **Telling**: every phase change (a phase set to the one it has sends nothing) sends `remote:FireClient(player, "status", { phase, name, mode })` to that player only, while they are present.
- **The write budget** (design review 001 F2): every MemoryStore write the portal makes for a player, an arena seat (`UpdateAsync`), a pass (`Wallet.pass`), an invite set or removed (`Invites.offer`, `Invites.withdraw`), first asks `Play.spend(writes, os.clock())`: at most `WRITES` (6) within `WRITE_SPAN` (60 s). Refused: an arena start fails (below), a pass is not written (the start goes on; the arrival then decides by the map), an invite is not set or not removed (it expires by itself in 600 s). Each such write the player waits for is bounded by `within(WRITE_SECONDS, fn)`: the write runs in a thread of its own; the caller waits at most `WRITE_SECONDS` (5) and then goes on, the write's later answer being its own (design review 001 F3, lesson pickman-arch/open-stores-guarded).
- **An intent** (`remote.OnServerEvent(player, action, name, mode)`): a player who left gets nothing; `Play.intent` checks it (else ignored).
  - `"cancel"` while waiting: leave the line, `idle`. `"invite"` while waiting for 2 PLAYERS: the first invite of that wait, when the budget allows, records the invite (`offered`, and `Invites.offer(player, name)` in its own thread); each answers `remote:FireClient(player, "invite")` (the client prompts). Otherwise both do nothing.
  - `"play"`: a title with no destination does nothing; a play while `checking`, `buying` or `going` does nothing (one play per player); a play while `failed` before its `cool` does nothing (the rest after a failure); a play while waiting leaves the line first. Then **the play**.
- **Leaving the line** (a cancel, another play, a leave): `Pairing.leave`, the counts marked changed, and the player's invite **withdrawn** when one is on record (`Invites.withdraw` in its own thread, when the budget allows; design review 001 F7).
- **The play**: a new flight, phase `checking`. **The check**: `record, problem = Wallet.read(player, name)`; with a record, `read = { free, plays }` from `PaidPlay.status(record, os.time())`; `decision = Play.decide(read, problem, Wallet.mode(name), os.clock() - began)`. `busy` reads again after `task.wait(BUSY_SECONDS)` (a busy read costs no store call: Wallet answers it from its in-flight flag). After `CHECK_SECONDS` (10) of busy answers the decision is `pass` (design review 001 F1): in the portal a read stays busy past Wallet's `WAIT_SECONDS` (5) only when a read in flight (most often f0205's card read) is late, so the store is slow, which is the first Guess's case; that late read has already set Wallet's pass allowance, so `CHECK_SECONDS` must stay above `Wallet.WAIT_SECONDS`. Then: `closed`: phase `closed`; `buy`: phase `buying`, `Receipts.ask(player, name, "play")` (failing to open fails the play), and `task.delay(BUY_SECONDS)`: if that flight is still buying (no close came), `idle` (design review 001 F10); `go` or `pass`: phase `going` first, then for `pass` the pass (`Wallet.pass(player, name)`, within the budget and `WRITE_SECONDS`), then **go on**.
- **The purchase** (Receipts' handlers, which hear only players still on the server, each in its own thread): `granted(player, name, "play")` while that player's phase is `buying` for that name: **go on**, with no new read of the record (a read in flight may still answer the record from before the grant: f0201's Wallet, below). Any other grant is a held play, used at the next PLAY (second Guess). `closed(player, name, "play", false)` while buying it: `idle`. `closed(…, true)` while buying it: keep buying, and `task.delay(RECEIPT_SECONDS)`: if **that flight** is still buying, `idle` (third Guess; a later purchase of another flight stays open). Either order of close and grant works (lesson pickman-arch/purchase-close-before-receipt). A revive's grant or close is not this adapter's (f0215, f0219, f0223 sell revives in their places).
- **Going on**, phase `going` before anything yields (design review 001 F3: a press, or a second grant, during a write does nothing), by mode:
  - `solo`: **start** `{ player }` with no reserved server.
  - `arena`: refused by the budget: fail. Else `fresh = HttpService:GenerateGUID(false)`, and within `WRITE_SECONDS` one `MemoryStoreService:GetHashMap(Arena.MAP):UpdateAsync(name, transform, Arena.OPEN_SECONDS)` whose transform is `Arena.join(raw, fresh, os.time())`, the answer being the transform's **last** call's (UpdateAsync may call it again on another server's newer value; lesson zz-test/store-stubs-race-the-transform). The hash map is opened on first use inside the same protected call. Afterwards: a player who left, or a later flight, does nothing more; an error, or no answer within `WRITE_SECONDS`, fails the play (its late answer may still count the seat, which only closes that arena early, §3); else **start** `{ player }` in the answer's `id`.
  - `pair`: `queue, pair = Pairing.join(queue, name, userId)`, the counts marked changed; with no pair, phase `waiting`; with a pair, both players' invites are withdrawn and the two **start** `{ first, player }` (the first being the player who waited, found with `Players:GetPlayerByUserId`) in `HttpService:GenerateGUID(false)`, a reserved server of their own.
- **The counts** (design review 001 F2): whenever the lines change they are only marked changed; once a second the heartbeat sends `remote:FireAllClients("waiting", { [name] = count })` for each title with 2 PLAYERS when they changed, so a client looping 2 PLAYERS and CANCEL causes at most one broadcast a second. A player who joins gets the current counts at once (`FireClient`).
- **Start** (`go(players, name, reserved)`): if any of them is not `idle` in Travel, they all fail. Else each `Travel.request`s (a ticket each) and is `going`; then **one** `TeleportService:TeleportAsync(destination[name], players, options)` in a `pcall`, `options = Instance.new("TeleportOptions")` with `ReservedServerId = reserved` when there is one, and nothing else (no TeleportData, no fourth argument). Raised: tried **once more** after `RETRY_SECONDS` (1), with the same options, when every one of them is still here and still going (Roblox: "Some failures benefit from retries, particularly those involving reserved servers"; design review 001 F6, tenth Guess). Returned: `Travel.answered` for each. Raised twice (or once, with a player gone): `Travel.failed` for each and each fails. `TeleportService.TeleportInitFailed(player)`: `Travel.failed(…)`, and a `going` player fails.
- **A failure** (a start that raised, TeleportInitFailed, the 15 s timeout, a screen that cannot open, an arena write that failed or came late, the budget): phase `failed` and `cool = os.clock() + COOL_SECONDS` (3 s, Travel's own cool-down). **The heartbeat**, once a second: the counts when they changed; every `going` player whom Travel shows `cooling` (the 15 s timeout after an answer that did not take the player) fails; every `failed` player whose `cool` has passed and whom Travel shows `idle` goes `idle`, so the card's buttons come back (design review 001 F4).
- **A leave** (`PlayerRemoving`): out of the line (the counts marked changed, the invite withdrawn), `Travel.left`, the slot, the writes and the offer dropped; so a read, a grant, an arena or pass write or a delay that returns after the leave sends nothing, asks nothing and teleports no one, and a rejoin (a new Player object) plays at once.
- **f0201's read race** (reported by c0202's review, 2026-11-15): `Wallet.read` stores what its `GetAsync` returned when the call returns, so a read that began before a grant on this server can come back after `Wallet.grant` stored the newer record and keep the older one for `READ_SECONDS` (60 s). This adapter is exposed only where it reads: the grant's own start never reads (above, pinned), but a later PLAY of that title within that minute can find "no play held" and open the product again; that second purchase is a held play, never lost (G9). The race is f0201's to fix, in Wallet.

### The split and server authority
Everything that decides is pure and unit-tested: the decision, the intent and status checks, the view, the slots, the keys, the waiting lines, the arena's transform, the invite's reads and the buttons per title. The adapters only translate: Wallet's answers into a decision, the decision into Receipts, MemoryStore and TeleportService calls, Roblox's handlers back into phases; the client's taps and keys into intents and the server's messages into instances. **The server decides** whether a player may play (their own record, never anything the client says), what they pay (Receipts' grant, never the client), where they go (the attributes the publisher set, never a place id from the client), with whom (the lines it keeps), and into which server (ids it makes). A client can only ask: a play of a title in one of its modes, a cancel, an invite. A forged intent names at most a title and a mode the server checks; a forged status or count only misdraws that client's own screen. **Nothing is charged in the portal**: the entitlement is the player's record (a free start due, or a held play bought here) or the portal's pass, and the game place consumes it on arrival (`Wallet.arrive`), so a teleport that never arrives costs nothing (G10). **TeleportData is never sent, so it can never be trusted**; the invite's launch data only names a record to look up.

### Edge cases (purchase and teleport paths)
- **The store read fails** (down or slow): the pass, then the start (first Guess). **The read is busy** (f0205's card reads under way): read again every 0.25 s; still busy after 10 s (a read in flight that is late, so a slow store): the pass, then the start. Every path that reads the record (PLAY, ARENA, 2 PLAYERS before its line) goes through this one check.
- **The pass write fails, or hangs** (MemoryStore down): the start goes on after at most 5 s; the arrival treats an unreadable pass map as a pass ("Let all of them play"), and a written-but-lost pass sends the player back from the game place at no cost.
- **The purchase write fails** (Receipts answers NotProcessedYet; a test confirm's write fails): Receipts reports `closed(…, false)` for the test screen, so the card goes idle; a live receipt is retried by Roblox and granted later as a held play.
- **The receipt arrives after the player left or teleported**: Receipts grants it by user id and hands nothing to a player not here; it is a held play at that title's next PLAY (f0201: "a buyer not on this server is granted all the same").
- **The receipt arrives before the close, or after it**: both orders start the play; a close bought with no receipt for 30 s goes idle.
- **The teleport fails**: a raise is tried once more 1 s later (both players together, for a pair, while both are still here); a second raise, TeleportInitFailed, or 15 s without leaving: COULD NOT START for that player (both, for a pair), the buttons disabled for 3 s, then back; nothing charged.
- **The arena write fails or hangs**: COULD NOT START after at most 5 s; a late answer may still have counted a seat, which closes that arena a seat early.
- **A purchase that never closes**: it ends after 120 s, and a later grant is a held play.
- **A double press, a key and a tap together, or a flood of intents**: one play per player; every press while checking, buying or starting is dropped, and so is a press during a failure's 3 s rest. A client looping 2 PLAYERS, INVITE and CANCEL causes at most 6 MemoryStore writes a minute and the counts at most once a second (Scalability). A second grant while a start is under way (an older receipt retried) starts nothing: the play is already going.
- **Two portal servers at once**: two ARENA joins race on one MemoryStore key, and the transform's last call decides, so both join the same arena; a player is only ever in one portal server, so their own play never races; a receipt processed on two servers is f0201's (one write per receipt id).
- **A player who leaves** while checked, buying, waiting or starting: out of the line, nothing asked, sent or teleported afterwards; a rejoin is a new Player object and starts afresh.
- **The partner left** between pairing and the start: Pairing never holds a departed player (the leave takes them out first); a player found missing anyway fails the newcomer's start.
- **Nothing published** (every attribute 0, as checked in): every card COMING SOON, no button, no read.
- **Studio**: test mode (f0145 G1), so the test purchase screen charges nothing; Roblox's teleport guide: "You can test TeleportAsync() teleports in Studio's Test and Team Test modes without publishing your experience" (E4); Wallet's stand-in test store keeps test credits for that server (f0201 E8). What each test environment can prove is E9.
- **A clock that differs between portal servers**: an arena record opened up to 30 s "ahead" is still joined.
- **Frame rate**: nothing counts frames; the heartbeat sweep reads the clock once a second.

### Scalability
**Worst case per player per minute** (design review 001 F2), whatever the client sends:
- MemoryStore: at most `WRITES` (6) writes in any 60 s, every kind counted (arena seats at 2 request units each, passes, invites recorded and removed), so at most 12 request units.
- Invite reads: one `GetAsync` per join with an inviter's id in the launch data.
- DataStore: Wallet's bound, at most one `GetAsync` per title per 60 s while the store answers and one per 6 s while it fails; a busy answer costs nothing.
- Teleports: at most one start at a time, and after a failure none for 3 s (so at most about 20 attempts a minute of starts that fail, each tried twice).
- Messages to its own client: its status changes only. Messages to everyone: the waiting counts at most once a second, server-wide, whoever loops.
- A full minute of 2 PLAYERS, INVITE and CANCEL is a case: at most `WRITES` writes and 61 broadcasts.

With the experience's quota at "1000 + 120 × [number of concurrent users] request units per minute" (E4), no single client can take more than 12 units a minute of it, so one looping client cannot drain the pass map that every game place's arrival reads. One key takes "approximately 5,000 write request units per minute": the `snake` arena key, at 2 units per ARENA start, takes 2,500 starts a minute before it throttles. Per press: one check at a time (the read loop yields between reads). Replicated: one RemoteEvent; a status is three short fields, a count table four numbers at most. Server memory: a slot, a list of at most 6 instants and a flag per player, and the lines (at most one waiting player per title, since the second pairs). Client: per card a label and five buttons; drawing is a table walk on each message, nothing per frame.

### Maintainability
A new title is one `Modes` entry (beside its `Games` entry, its Catalog entry and its place in Places, from their own issues); a new mode is one `Mode` value, one label and one branch in `Plays`' going-on. Words are `Play.LABEL` and `Play.NOTE`, the price's words the arcade's `Prices`; tuning is `Play.*_SECONDS`, `Arena.CAP` and `Arena.OPEN_SECONDS` (f0212 sets the real cap after its load experiment E6 and changes `Arena.CAP` with it). The seams the next issues use are named: a game place reads its server kind (Interfaces, below), and f0207 and f0206 add their own remotes and adapters (CV1, CV2, CV5 stay green). The purchase plumbing stays the arcade's: this issue only calls `read`, `mode`, `pass`, `ask` and `start`.

### Usability
- **Player**: every text on the buttons' row is at least 12 px on the smallest phone; every card says what PLAY costs before it is pressed ("FREE", or "5 R$" struck through and "1 R$"); a free game or a held play starts at once; a purchase starts the game as soon as it is granted; a failing store never blocks a player; a 2 PLAYERS wait says so on its card, shows who else waits, and has CANCEL and INVITE FRIEND; every control works by tap, keyboard and gamepad (PLAY by Enter, KeypadEnter or A; the second button by 2 or X; CANCEL by Backspace or B; INVITE by I or Y) and none of them fires while typing in the chat; a start that could not happen says so, with that card's buttons disabled for 3 s, and then they come back by themselves.
- **Developer**: the server's API is one `start`; the client's one `start`; a game place needs nothing from the portal but `Wallet.arrive` and its server kind; the Logic is small typed functions with the titles' buttons as data.

### Interfaces the next issues rely on
- **The invite record** (`PortalInvites`, key the inviter's user id, `{ job, name }`, 600 s): written by a waiting player's first invite and **removed when the wait ends**; only the portal reads it.
- **The entitlement** (every game place: c0202 for Pickman, f0213/f0212 for Snake, f0218 for Invaders, f0222 for Asteroids): the portal writes nothing about a play except, after a failed read, the portal's pass (`PaidPlayPass`, key `<name>/<user id>`, 300 s: f0201's `Wallet.pass`). A play is entitled by the player's own record in that title's store (a free start due, or a held play bought through Receipts here). The game place calls `Wallet.arrive(player, name)` once the player is in: "play" plays (and claims the free start or the held play, or takes the pass); "back" sends them to the portal (`Places.read(...).Portal`). It never reads TeleportData, and the portal sends none.
- **The server kind** (f0212, f0218, f0222; sixth and seventh Guess): a server whose `game.PrivateServerId ~= ""` and `game.PrivateServerOwnerId == 0` is a reserved server, and only the portal opens them. For Snake it is the shared arena: up to `Arena.CAP` players are sent there, and more may come while it is open. For Invaders and Asteroids it is one 2-player match: both players are sent in one `TeleportAsync`, and either may arrive late or alone (E5, f0218's). A public server is solo. **This holds in production (live and Beta) only**: Roblox's teleport guide, Known limitations: "In Test mode, both are always empty, even for private servers" and "In Team Test, every server is private, so PrivateServerId is never empty, even for teleports to public servers", so f0212, f0218 and f0222 must not read the server kind in Studio Test or Team Test, and their arena and match steps are Beta's Observed steps. In production "ReservedServerId vs. PrivateServerId: These values can differ in Team Test, though they match in production", so an arena server can find its own `PortalArenas` record by `game.PrivateServerId`. f0212 may keep the arena record fresh (the same key and record shape), but nothing requires it.
- **Places and attributes** (c0210): the portal reads `PickmanPlaceId`, `SnakePlaceId`, `InvadersPlaceId` and `AsteroidsPlaceId` on its own `ServerScriptService` through `Places.read`, once at start; 0 or unset is coming soon. `PortalPlaceId` is not read: an invited friend is sent to `game.PlaceId`. c0210 must also set the arcade's paid-play attributes (`PaidPlay` and each catalog entry's two product ids, f0201) on the portal, which sells the play product.
- **Remotes and adapters** (f0206, f0207, f0209): `ReplicatedStorage.Remotes.Play` is this issue's; `Plays` and `Invites` are server adapters booted by `Plays.start()`; `PlayButtons` is booted by the client's Main after `CarouselView`. The portal boots `Receipts.start` (inside `Plays.start`, its one call) and `TestPurchase.start()` (the client's Main): no other portal issue may call either.
- **f0209**: no change needed here; once the portal is the start place, its buttons work as they do on Beta before it.
- **c0202** (Pickman's arrival): until c0202, Pickman's place runs f0145's arrival, which reads f0145's pass key (`<user id>`), not f0201's (`pickman/<user id>`); a Pickman PLAY that goes on a pass is then sent back. The record, store and products are shared (f0201 kept f0145's names), so free starts and held plays work across both builds. c0190 orders f0209 after c0202, so no player reaches the portal before c0202.
