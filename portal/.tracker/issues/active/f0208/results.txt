	PLAY with a held play teleports at once; nothing is charged here

	a 2 PLAYERS purchase waits on its grant, then joins the line
	a bought screen whose receipt is not here within RECEIPT_SECONDS ends the purchase; the late grant is held
	a busy read is read again BUSY_SECONDS later, with no second read meanwhile
	a check still busy after CHECK_SECONDS (a slow store) writes the pass, then teleports
	a client looping 2 PLAYERS, INVITE and CANCEL for a minute causes at most WRITES writes and 61 broadcasts
	a full or closed arena makes the next ARENA player open a new one
	a grant of another title, a revive, or one with no purchase open starts nothing; it stays held
	a later purchase stays open when an earlier purchase's receipt wait ends
	a pair whose partner leaves before the second try is not tried again; the one left fails
	a party teleport that errors is tried once more, then fails both; neither is left waiting
	a player who leaves during the arena write is sent nothing; their rejoin plays at once
	a player who leaves during the check, or with a purchase open, is told, asked and sent nothing
	a press from a player who already left reads nothing
	a purchase screen closed not bought ends the purchase: idle, no teleport, and a later grant starts nothing
	a purchase screen that cannot open ends in failed
	a purchase whose screen never reports closing ends BUY_SECONDS after it opened; its grant is then held
	a read that failed lets the player go in closed mode too
	a read that failed writes the portal's pass for that title, then teleports
	a receipt before the screen's close teleports, and the close changes nothing
	a screen closed bought before its receipt keeps the purchase open; the grant then teleports
	a second player's 2 PLAYERS on that title sends both in one teleport to a fresh reserved server
	a start that has not left 15 s after the call fails, told once the heartbeat sees it
	a teleport that errors fails; a press during Travel's cool-down does nothing, one after it plays again
	a teleport that raises is tried once more RETRY_SECONDS later, and a second try that works goes
	a waiting player who leaves leaves the line
	an arena record that cannot be written fails the start; nothing is sent
	an arena write not back within WRITE_SECONDS fails the start; its late answer starts nothing
	an invite is withdrawn when the wait ends: a cancel, a pair, another play or a leave
	another play while waiting leaves the line first, then checks the new play
	during the arena write a second grant and every press do nothing: one write, one teleport
	during the pass write a press does nothing; a pass write not back within WRITE_SECONDS still goes
	every failure rests the title COOL_SECONDS: a press then is dropped; then the card is idle again
	in closed mode nothing is sold: store unavailable, no prompt; a free start or a held play still goes
	installs Receipts once, with a grant and a close handler, and Invites once
	nothing rides in TeleportData: the call takes the place, the players and options only
	one play at a time: a press while checking, buying or going starts nothing more
	players waiting on different titles are not paired
	statuses go to the player they are about
	the arena's last transform call decides: another server's arena written meanwhile is joined
	the destinations are the place-id attributes, read once; an unpublished title plays nothing
	the free state is PaidPlay's at os.time(): a free start taken under an hour ago is not due
	the grant starts its own play with no read of the record (a read in flight may still hold the old one)
	the write budget comes back WRITE_SPAN after the writes
	what the client sends is checked: an unknown title or a mode the title lacks plays nothing
	with nothing held it opens the 1 R$ play product of that title, and teleports on its grant
