# Answers to design review 001: f0205

- F1: refuted: the capture gives its own reason for the one table, "because "permanent sale" pricing is an open decision for the user" (`./zn capture f0205`), and the open item is the sale's wording ("Showing 5 R$ struck through with a 1 R$ sale that never ends may conflict with Roblox's rules"). f0201's prepared design makes `Prices` "the one place the badge's words live" and pins exactly the sale's three words in it (its `config.spec` `toEqual`). portal-arch's Layout row ("config tables (the badge text is one config table)") and its Maintainability line tie it to that same open item. Still, the reading is the user's: put under `## Open to the human` with options (a), (b) and (c), and their costs. The design was made so that (b) is local: `Card.badge(state, prices, words)` takes the two words as a table (`Card.WORDS`), and the view passes it, so moving them out of Logic is one new config file and one changed argument. Ruled (user, 2026-11-15): "c". The Problem's line became `Decided with the user (2026-11-15)`. Consequences: `Card.badge` gains a `words` parameter, with a case that hands it words no card holds, the K19 and V33 rows, and the client stub's Card wrapped with other words.
- F2: fixed: the client stub's `CFrame.lookAt` and `CFrame.new(eye, target)` both return a value holding `Position` and `target`. Consequences: rows V28, V29 (camera), V30, V31 (regions), V35 (badge labels) and CV8 (`CFrame.new`). - F3: fixed: a new pure fold, `Card.answer(read, due, reason, waits)`, decides what each read's answer does. A first busy answer keeps what was known and reads again after `Wallet.WAIT_SECONDS`. Busy a second time, down, or no record is the sale, read again after `Wallet.RETRY_SECONDS`, doubling up to `Wallet.READ_SECONDS`. The server keeps one retry per game (a token), stops it on a leave, and cancels it when a read comes back. Consequences: the Problem's two read Guess lines were reworded to match (they stay guesses); Edge cases and Scalability now state the bound (one store call a minute per player and title in a long outage); the server stub gained a timer queue and a clock, and its Wallet stub gained bounds no real module holds (7, 11, 50 s) to pin where the waits come from. New rows: K20 to K24, K29, S17 to S19, S22. Interfaces section: f0208's PLAY read may answer busy while these reads run.

Lesson pickman-arch/bind-above-the-default-controls.
