	the card's regions run top to bottom inside it, without overlapping
	the client drops a state that makes no sense, title by title, and any title it does not know
	the client keeps no free state for a place that is not published
	the client reads back what the server sent
	the preview and the boards have the most room: the preview at least a fifth, the boards at least a quarter
	the preview camera frames a whole FRAME cube turning about its centre
	the preview turns at SPIN radians a second, from 0 up to a full turn
	the sale's words come from the table it is given, never from the card
	the server's states carry every title given, and only those
	the server's states: each title by name, published or not, and its free state where it is published
	the struck price is rich text, its own markup characters escaped
	the text model: one line fills the height or the width; wrapping finds the best of up to four lines

portal/.tracker/issues/active/f0205/acceptance/server.spec.luau
	a busy read (another read of the record under way) keeps what was known
	a busy read is read again once, after the Wallet's WAIT_SECONDS, and not before
	a failed read is read again after RETRY_SECONDS, doubling up to READ_SECONDS, while it fails
	a player who leaves stops the retries, and a retry's answer goes to the asker only
	a player who leaves while a read is under way hears nothing more, and a rejoin starts afresh
	a read that failed shows the sale, never a free game
	a record whose free start is due shows FREE GAME READY when its read comes back
	a second busy read in a row is a failure: the sale, then the down back-off
	an ask after the reads came back reads again, and the new answer replaces the old
	an ask from a player who already left reads nothing and answers nothing
	an ask is answered at once, to that player, before any read comes back
	an ask reads each published game's record through the Wallet, by player and name, and no other
	an ask while a game's read is under way starts no second read of it, and is answered at once
	answers go to the player who asked, and each player has their own reads and free states
	asks closer than ASK_GAP are answered together: a burst gets one answer at once and one at its end
	each read is answered as it comes back: one more answer per read
	no place id leaves the server: nothing in any answer is an id
	one retry at a time per game: an ask's read that comes back replaces the retry waiting
	start is once per server: a second start adds no second answer
	the attributes are read once, at start: a later change does not count
	the free state is PaidPlay's status at os.time(): due a full hour after the last free start
	what the client sends with its ask is ignored
	which games are published comes from the place-id attributes, read through Places
	with no place-id attribute at all, every card is coming soon and nothing is read
