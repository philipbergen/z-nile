Record the answer as a `Scope decision (user, YYYY-MM-DD)` line. Under (a) or (b), `Card.badge` takes the words table, card.spec and client.spec hand it words no card holds (as `PRICES` does now), new rows kill "FREE/SOON written in" in Logic and at the view's call, and the word `game` leaves Logic.

### F3 [medium] A busy or failed free-state read writes the sale over what was known, and nothing in this issue reads again, so a card can show 1 R$ SALE for a whole visit to a player whose free game is due
Where: §4 ("`slot.free[name] = record ~= nil and PaidPlay.status(record, os.time()).free == true` (a nil record, "busy" or "down", is false: the sale)", "Every ask reads again what is not being read (f0207 asks again when its countdown runs out)"); Problem Guess lines 29-30; Edge cases ("the next ask reads again"); server.spec.luau:345-355.
Evidence: Wallet's in-flight flag is per player and title and shared by every adapter on the server (f0201 §4 Slots; "nil and `"busy"` while a read for that player and title is under way"), and f0208's PLAY calls `Wallet.read` too (f0201 Order & dependencies). A card showing FREE GAME READY, a PLAY whose read is still out (up to `WAIT_SECONDS`), and any ask in that window: Cards' read answers busy, `free` becomes false, the card says SALE, and the client asks only once per screen open. "busy" here is not a failure but another read of the same record in flight. For "down" at screen open, Wallet holds the failure for `RETRY_SECONDS`, but no one asks again: the client asks once, and f0207 re-asks only when a countdown runs out, which needs the record a failed read never gave. On the prototype, keeping the known state on "busy" fails only `server.spec :: a read that failed or was busy shows the sale` (:353, expected false, got nil): the spec pins the behaviour in question.
Critique: c0190 gives this issue the free state ("P1 the badge and free state, A3"); a value that is wrong until the player leaves is a wrong result in it. The tempting fix retries every `RETRY_SECONDS`.
Consequences: a fixed 6 s retry during an outage is four `GetAsync` per player every 6 s (40 a minute, f0201's stated worst case), across a server's players far past the ~60 + 10 x players a minute budget, and it starves f0206's board reads. A retry needs a timer the stubs can drive (`task.delay` is a no-op in both stubs today) and must stop on a leave. Cards' reads at screen open also make f0208's first PLAY read in those seconds answer busy, which f0208 has to handle (F6).
Recommendation: on "busy", keep the slot's known free state (nil stays nil, an empty badge) and read once more after `Wallet.WAIT_SECONDS` (Wallet then answers from the record seen, with no store call); on "down", show the sale (the Guess) and read again after `Wallet.RETRY_SECONDS`, doubling up to `Wallet.READ_SECONDS` (at most one store call a minute per player and game in a long outage), only while the player is present and the slot is the one the retry began with. Update the Guess lines, Edge cases and Scalability. Cases, with a stub delay queue the case runs: busy after a known free keeps FREE GAME READY; busy is retried once, not twice; down is retried after `RETRY_SECONDS` and not before, then backs off; a leave stops the retries; a retry's answer goes to the asker only.

### F4 [medium] PRIORITY 2000 ties the PlayerModule's controls and camera, and the touch and chat cases are unexamined: input assumptions with no experiment or Observed step
Where: §1 `Carousel.PRIORITY = 2000`; carousel.spec.luau:106-114 (pins only `0 < PRIORITY < 3000`); Unknowns E1-E6; OC2.
Evidence: 2000 is `Enum.ContextActionPriority.Default` (= Medium), the priority at which Roblox's PlayerModule binds its movement and jump actions (keyboard A, D, Left, Right; the gamepad's ButtonA) and the camera's MouseWheel. At equal priority ContextActionService asks the most recently bound action first, and the control module binds a controller's actions again when the last input type changes (picking up a gamepad), so they end up bound after the carousel. The keyboard's movement handlers pass the key on; if the gamepad's jump on ButtonA sinks (unproven either way here), "A plays" never reaches the carousel, and this issue cannot see it in Studio, since nothing listens to a play until f0208. The portal sets `CharacterAutoLoads = false`, but the PlayerModule still runs. Pickman binds its own controls at `Enum.ContextActionPriority.High.Value` and turns the platform touch controls off, "the platform thumbstick would take the left half" (pickman/src/client/Controls.luau:103-112). E4 covers swipes over GuiObjects, but not a swipe that starts where the platform's dynamic thumbstick takes the touch, and nothing says what keys typed into a focused chat box do.
Critique: the design states the priority's upper bound (f0201's 3000) but not its lower one, and it leaves Roblox input behaviour without a pass/fail question or a fallback (design mode item 1).
Consequences: a priority above 2000 also sinks the default controls' keys and the camera's wheel zoom. That is harmless in a place with no character, and the test screen at 3000 still wins. Turning off touch controls is client-wide, which is fine in the portal (no character), and it removes the thumbstick under the cards.
Recommendation: set PRIORITY strictly between 2000 and 3000 (for example 2500, "above the default controls, below the test screen"), pinned `> 2000 and < 3000` in carousel.spec (mutant: 2000). `CarouselView.start` sets `GuiService.TouchControlsEnabled = false`, pinned in client.spec (mutant: not set). Record both as an E entry with its pass/fail question and fallback. OC2 gains three checks: with a gamepad connected after the portal loads, the shoulders still move the row; a swipe started on the left half and one on the right half each move one card; with the chat box focused, typing a, d and Enter moves nothing.

### F5 [medium] On a landscape phone the 0.6-aspect front card leaves the tagline about 6.6 px tall, and readability has no measurable proxy

### F12 [low] An ask has no per-player bound: each one spawns up to four reads and sends five messages
Where: §4 "An ask"; Scalability ("A client that floods asks gets five small messages per ask and costs the server a table walk each").
Evidence: per ask there is one `FireClient` at once and, for each published game not under way, a `task.spawn` of `Wallet.read`. Within `READ_SECONDS` that read answers synchronously from the record seen, so each thread fires again at once: four threads and five `FireClient` calls (about 1 KB) per `FireServer`, with no bound on how often a client fires it.
Recommendation: coalesce asks per player. An ask within `ASK_GAP` (for example 1 s) of the last answered ask is answered once, when the gap ends. It is not dropped, so f0207's re-ask is never lost. Cases: a burst of ten asks gets one answer at once and one later, and the reads stay bounded as now. Scalability states the bound.

Verdict: changes
