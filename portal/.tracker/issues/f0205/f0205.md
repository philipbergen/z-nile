# f0205: Game carousel

## Problem
The user, 2026-10-31: "I want to branch out into multiple games, they will each have their own directory in the project: first person snake, first person asteroids. I also want a new project for a landing portal where you can scroll between the games and see the highscores for each, the same cost model remains but it shoudl show 5 robux for each game witha slash through showing it is currently on sale for 1 robux. highscores shoudl show how many revives were used. I want you to design a full fun playable game for both of those two new game ideas. I also want both snake and asteroids to be a two player game, snakes can even be n-player up to a high number just make the wold bigger. Snakes should have maps that once you have reached a certain length the game ends and we move to a new map, the first is empty, then one wall somewhere in the middle, then two, then three and so on. The walls can be vertical and horizontal but do not connect. for asteroids we could have a two player version where each player is attacking the aliens from opposing sides. You can shoot each other's barriers. the aliens would then come in from the side and and split into two groups that move toward either player". Then: "Add a third game first person asteroids." Decided with the user (2026-10-31): the 2-player aliens game is pure Space Invaders (`invaders`); Asteroids is its own game (`asteroids`); one Roblox experience with the portal as start place, replacing pickman's lobby; every board shows revives used; one free game per hour per game.

The approved design is `portal/.tracker/game-design.md`, section **Design: Portal (`portal/`)** (c0183). This issue owns these parts of it, as they are said there: "A carousel with one card per game", and from "Each card shows" the title, tagline and preview and the price badge's sale text; "Open item for the user". The rest of it belongs to other issues (c0190's ownership table), listed under Non-goals.

What should happen:
- The portal shows **a carousel with one card per game** (Pickman, Snake, Invaders, Asteroids), scrolled with A/D, the arrows, the mouse wheel, a swipe or the gamepad's shoulders; Enter or A plays.
- Each card shows the title, a tagline and a live 3-D preview or art, and **the price badge: "5 R$" struck through, then "1 R$ SALE"**, or "FREE GAME READY" when that game's free game is due (f0207 decides when).
- A card whose place is not published (id 0) shows "coming soon".
- The badge text sits in one config table, because "permanent sale" pricing is an open decision for the user.

## Non-goals
- The boards on the card: f0206 (Boards per game).
- The countdown to the next free game: f0207 (Free game timer).
- The play buttons and purchase: f0208 (Play buttons).
- The rest of `portal/.tracker/game-design.md`: c0202, f0206, f0207, f0208, f0209, f0215, f0219, f0223, and f0187 (pickman's game over returning to the portal).
