## Solution
One pure leaf, `Logic/Board`, carries every rule: what a board shows (tabs, header, aligned columns a gutter apart, rows with revives "—" for none, the your-best line under every tab, pending until the player's best is heard, its notes and layout), the client's defensive read of an answer, which boards the client asks for and when, and the server's read policy (`Board.plan`). Each title's boards stay the arcade's (f0197): the portal's server adapter `BoardRelay` opens every card's title with `Boards.open(HiscoreGames[name].prefix)` and serves it on a relay of its own, a table with the two members `serve` uses (`OnServerEvent`, `FireClient`), which no client can reach. In front of the four relays sits one RemoteEvent, `ReplicatedStorage.Remotes.Boards`: a client asks with (title, board id), and `Board.plan` decides per ask whether it may make the arcade read its stores (a **refresh**: `REFRESH` after that board's last one, under a read allowance of the larger of 1.5 a minute per player and 4 a minute, 12 at once, and the engine's request budget above a reserve), merely join the arcade's fresh cache (a **forward**, inside its 15 s cache time), or be answered from the rows this server holds (the **snapshot** per board: the arcade's E3 fallback, which this issue makes the design). Every answer carries the player's all-time best on that title once the arcade's all-time answer has reached them, and says "pending" until then; a player's rank is kept while the held rows are the ones it was heard with. The client adapter `BoardsView` fills the `Boards` slot f0205 leaves on every card (`CarouselView.card(name).Boards`) with the tabs, the header, ten row lines, a note and the your-best line, binds the tab keys, and asks for every card's board on the chosen tab (and a card's all-time board while its best is unknown), each again every 30 s, or every 2 s after a wait or while the best is pending.

### 1. `src/shared/Logic/Board/init.luau` (new, pure, a leaf: requires nothing)
The word `game` is refused in Logic (pickman-arch rule 2): a game here is a *title*. Every table is frozen.
```luau

- **The view** (`view(answer, progress, mine)`): `view(nil | wait)` is LOADING with no lines and no best; otherwise one line per row, `mine` when the answer's rank is that row's; the note is "" with rows, else DOWN for a failed answer and EMPTY for an ok one; `best` is `bestCells(mine.best)` when the player's best is known (dashes for no run), else `pendingCells()`, on whichever board the answer is for (the line is under every tab).
- **The client's read** (`parse(names, name, id, rows, rank, status, best)`): nil unless `names[name]`, a board id and a status of ok, failed or wait. Rows: a non-table is no rows; each entry must be a table with a whole rank 1..N, a string name, a whole score >= 0 and a whole level >= 1, else it is dropped; junk revives read 0; sorted by rank, cut to N. A rank is kept only when it is a kept row's. The best, on every board: `false` is `pending = true` (best nil); a table with a whole score >= 0 and level >= 1 is the best (junk revives 0); anything else is no best, not pending. Never raises. **`learn`**: a pending answer leaves `mine` as it was (or unknown); any other answer makes it known with its best (nil: no run).
- **The columns**: each column's label shows `chars` characters whole and sits in a box `GUTTER` characters wider, inset `GUTTER / 2` on each side, so `x` (its left edge) and `width = chars / CHARS` are shares of the slot's width and neighbouring labels are `GUTTER` characters apart; `CHARS` = 27 + 5 × 0.4 = 29. The rank and every figure align right, the name left. YOUR BEST spans from the rank's left edge to the name's right one (9.4 characters).
- **The layout** in a slot `width` x `height` px: the tab bar is `TAB_PX` (the smallest touch target, pickman's `Lobby.BUTTON_MIN_PX`); `line = clamp((h - TAB_PX) / (N + 2), MIN_LINE_PX, MAX_LINE_PX)` for the header, each row and the best line; `view = max(0, h - TAB_PX - 2 line)`; `canvas = N line`; and every text on the board is one size, `text = floor(min(line, w / (glyph × CHARS)))`: Card's text model for a line of `CHARS` characters. A width or height that is not a positive finite number is 0; a glyph that is not a positive number is 1. `MIN_LINE_PX` (13) is at least `Card.MIN_TEXT` (12). Board takes the glyph width as a parameter rather than requiring Card, so it stays a leaf and the portal's map needs no edge.
- **What the client asks for** (`wants`): every card's board on the chosen tab, in the carousel's order, and, under TODAY or THIS WEEK, that card's all-time board too while the player's own best on it is not known (only the arcade's all-time answer brings it). **When** (`askDue`, per board): never asked, a clock that went back, or `REASK` since its last ask (`WAIT_RETRY` when its answer was a wait or its best pending); a non-finite time never.
- **The read policy** (`plan`), the arcade's E3 (E3 below for the numbers):
  - A non-finite `now` or `ttl`: answer, the allowance untouched.
  - The allowance is refilled first (`fill`): full (`BURST`) when never filled; re-dated only when the clock went back; else `max(RATE × players, FLOOR) / 60` a second, at most `BURST`.
  - `bestRead(last, now, ttl)` (all-time asks only; any other board counts "fresh"): "read" when never read, after a clock that went back, or `BEST_GAP` since `last`; "fresh" inside `ttl - MARGIN` (the arcade still holds that best and reads none); "hold" between.
  - **Forward** when the board's last refresh (`snap.at`) is at most `ttl - MARGIN` seconds ago (`now >= at`, `now - at < ttl - MARGIN`) and the last arcade answer heard for it did not fail (`not snap.failed`): the arcade answers from its cache or adds the asker to the read under way, reading no board. An all-time forward on "hold" is answered instead; on "read" it needs `budget.standard >= RESERVE.standard + 1`, else it is answered; `best = (reads == "read")`.
  - **Refresh** when the board is due (no snapshot, never refreshed, a clock that went back, or `REFRESH` since `snap.at`), the allowance holds `1 + KEEP` tokens for a board that has rows (1 for one with none, so re-reads always leave a tab's four first answers), and the engine's budget holds `RESERVE + COST` of both kinds: one token taken; `best = (reads ~= "fresh")`.
  - Else **answer** from the held rows.
- `status`: wait before any arcade answer was heard, failed after a failed one, else ok. `sameRows`: equal length and each row's rank, name, score, level and revives equal, in order. `rankFor`: the held rank when it was heard with the held rows' version, else nil.

### 2. `src/server/BoardRelay.luau` (new, server adapter) and `src/server/Main.server.luau`

```

- **State**: per title, a `Snap` per board key (`Hiscores.cacheKey(id, os.time())`, so a new UTC day or week is a new board; version 0 at first); per Player object a slot `{ ranks[title|key]: Held, best[title]: Best?, heard[title]: boolean, last[title]: number }`; one `Bucket` for the server (starting empty and never filled, so the first fill makes it `BURST`).
- **The player's best as an answer carries it** (`mineOf(slot, title)`): `slot.best[title]` (nil: no run) once `slot.heard[title]`, else `false` (pending). Only an arcade all-time answer to that player sets `heard` (a forward never does).
- **An ask** (`remote.OnServerEvent`, `(player, rawName, rawId)`): nothing for a player whose `Parent` is nil, a title it does not serve, or a board id that is none. Otherwise it forgets the snapshots of past periods for that title (a sweep), reads the engine's budget (`DataStoreService:GetRequestBudgetForRequestType(Enum.DataStoreRequestType.StandardRead)` and `(…OrderedList)`, E2; inside a `pcall`, a failure being a budget of 0, so nothing is read), and calls `Board.plan({ snap, last = slot.last[name], all = id == "all", bucket, budget, players = #Players:GetPlayers(), now = os.time(), ttl = Hiscores.CACHE_TTL })`, keeping its bucket. **answer**: `remote:FireClient(player, name, id, snap.rows, Board.rankFor(slot.ranks[name|key], snap), Board.status(snap), mineOf(slot, name))`. **refresh** and **forward**: when `plan.best`, `slot.last[name] = now`; a refresh sets `snap.at = now`; then `task.spawn(fn, player, id)` for each of that title's relay handlers (the arcade's `serve` handler), so a read that yields never holds the remote's handler.
- **The arcade's answer** (`relay:FireClient(player, id, rows, rank, failed, best)`, from `serve`): for a board id, at `now = os.time()`, the board's snapshot raises its `version` when it had no answer yet or `Board.sameRows` finds the new rows different, then takes `rows` (a table, else none), `known = true` and `failed = failed == true`; the player's slot, if any, takes `{ rank, version }` for that board and, for "all", the best and `heard = true`; and, while the player's `Parent` is set, `remote:FireClient(player, name, id, snap.rows, rank, Board.status(snap), mineOf(slot, name))`. A player who left hears nothing, but the rows are held for the next asker. (f0197 sends `best` nil when that player's best read failed: read as no run until their next best read.)

### The split and server authority
Everything that decides is pure and unit-tested in Lune: what a board shows, the client's read, the layout, when the client asks, and when the server may read the stores. The adapters only translate: `BoardRelay` turns asks and the engine's budget into `Board.plan` calls and its acts into forwards to the arcade's `serve` and replies; `BoardsView` turns answers, keys, taps, the slot's size and the clock into Board calls and Instances. The server alone reads the stores (through the arcade's `Boards`, in ServerStorage, which never replicates); a client sends only (title, board id), both validated (a known title, `Board.id`); nothing a client sends decides how often a store is read: the server's allowance, its per-board `REFRESH` and the engine's budget do. Replies carry no user id (f0197's `boardRows`).

### Edge cases
- No player has played a title: the arcade's all-time read falls back to its per-player board (f0197); the board says NO SCORES YET; the best line shows YOUR BEST with a dash in each column once the arcade's all-time answer says the player has no run.
- A player whose asks fall outside every forward window (13 to 29 s after another player's refresh, every time): answered from the snapshot with the best pending ("…"), never "no run"; the client asks again every `WAIT_RETRY`, so their next ask inside the next window, or their own refresh at `REFRESH`, fetches it (`budget.spec`: within `REFRESH + WAIT_RETRY`).
- A refresh that brings the same rows: every player's held rank stays (their row stays lit); one that brings other rows drops the ranks heard with the old ones until each player's own answer.
- A store down or busy: the arcade answers failed with the rows it held; the portal holds and shows them (or SCORES UNAVAILABLE without rows), closes its forward window for that board and reads again only after `REFRESH`.
- The engine's budget below the reserve, or unreadable: no read; a first ask is answered wait (LOADING) and the client asks again after `WAIT_RETRY`.
- A flood of asks from one client (or many): reads stay bounded by the allowance, `REFRESH` per board and the budget (E3); answers from the snapshot are one small `FireClient` each and no store call.
- A player who leaves during a read: the arcade does not answer them; the rows still reach the snapshot through any other waiter, and when none, the next ask after `REFRESH` reads again. Residual (accepted, E5): if the refresh's only asker leaves and that read fails, the forward window stays open, and a forward inside it after the arcade's failure wait (2 s, then 4 s) may read once or twice outside the allowance.
- A new UTC day or week: a new board key, so a first ask refreshes it (as the arcade's own cache does); the old key's snapshot is swept.
- A player who rejoins: a new Player object, a new slot; their best is pending again and read afresh (inside the budget).
- The arcade's best read for a player failed: f0197 sends no best, read as no run until that player's next best read (`BEST_GAP`).
- A malformed answer (only from a bug): dropped whole or row by row by `Board.parse`; nothing raises.
- A long name: cut at the cell's end at the board's one text size. A small screen: rows scroll in the board; every text at least 12 px and every line at least 13 px on f0205's seven screens (AC1).
- The player typing in the chat: the tab keys pass to the text box and change nothing. A gamepad in GUI selection never lands on a tab or the rows (`Selectable = false`), so A stays the carousel's PLAY.
- The clock going back: on the server a board is due and the allowance is re-dated; on the client an ask is due.
- Frame rate: nothing counts frames; asks go by `os.clock`, reads by `os.time`.

### Scalability
- **DataStore reads (E3)**: a refresh is at most two sorted reads (`GetSortedAsync`, the day or week page or the all-time page for a title with no list yet, tried once more at another size when rejected) and 12 standard reads (the list `GetAsync`, ten companion rows, the asker's best); `COST` charges that worst case against the live budget. Refreshes per portal server are at most `BURST + max(RATE × players, FLOOR) × minutes`: 12 once at start (one per board), then the larger of 1.5 a minute per player and 4 a minute, against the engine's sorted budget of 5 + 2 per player a minute (one player: 4 of 7), and each board at most once per `REFRESH`. Standard reads: at most 18 a minute per player from refreshes (48 a minute for one or two players, from the floor) plus at most 4 best reads a minute per player (one per title per `BEST_GAP`), with the wallet's 4 (f0205), inside the engine's 60 + 40 per player. Across the experience (300 + 2 per concurrent user a minute for sorted reads, shared with every place), the portal spends 1.5 a minute per portal player, at most 4 a minute for a server of one or two players (the floor costs at most 2.5 a minute more than 1.5 per player there) and 12 once at each server's start. Proven on the real arcade `Boards` by `budget.spec` (1, 4 and 20 players flooding every board every second).
- **First answers (F3)**: one or two players see every card's board the first time they choose its tab within `WAIT_RETRY`, whatever the order and spacing of their tab changes (`BURST` holds a token for each of the 12 boards, and `KEEP` keeps a tab's four out of re-reads); the four ALL-TIME boards answer at the screen's open. `budget.spec` pins it, both budgets checked over the same run.
- **Freshness**: a board kept in front shows a new run within `2 × REFRESH` (60 s, `budget.spec`); a player's own best within `REFRESH + WAIT_RETRY` of their first ask.
- **Replicated**: one RemoteEvent; an answer is ten rows of five small fields (under 1 KB); a client asks for four boards per tab (eight while its bests are unknown) every 30 s, and one board every 2 s while it waits or its best is pending; each is a table walk and one `FireClient` on the server, no store call beyond the allowance.
- **Client**: four slots of about 70 GuiObjects each; per frame one `wants` walk of at most eight boards; drawing only on answers, tab changes and resizes.
- **Server**: per ask a table walk, two budget reads and at most one `FireClient`; per arcade answer one comparison of up to ten rows; memory per player is one slot of at most 12 ranks, 4 bests, 4 flags and 4 times, dropped on leave; snapshots are 12 per server.

### Maintainability
A fifth title is one `Games` entry (f0205) and one `HiscoreGames` entry (f0197): the relay opens it, the view fills its slot and labels its figure, and nothing branches on a title's name. The tuning (tabs, keys, columns, lines, the read policy's numbers) is named constants in `Board`; the read policy is one pure function with its own spec, so a later cross-server cache (a MemoryStore snapshot) replaces the relay's forwards without touching the view. The relay depends on f0197's `serve` contract only through the two members its promoted spec pins (E4).

### Usability
- **Player**: every card shows its board when it comes into view, side cards included (the four ALL-TIME boards at the screen's open, a tab's four as soon as it is chosen, F3); the board is readable on every screen the carousel fits, every text one size and at least 12 px (Card's model): 12 px on 320 x 480 (a 230 x 138 px slot) and 844 x 390 (237 x 112), 13 px on 1280 x 720 (437 x 207), 22 px on 1920 x 1080 and 2560 x 1080; columns aligned (the name left, numbers right) with a gap of at least 0.4 characters between neighbours; all ten rows without scrolling on a 720 px screen and above, scrolling on a phone held sideways; tabs are 44 px touch targets; the chosen tab is lit on every card, so a player comparing games keeps their tab; revives read as a count, "—" for none, under a REVIVES header; the player's own row is lit, and stays lit while the rows do not change; YOUR BEST sits under the rows in the same columns under every tab, "…" while it is on its way, a dash only for a player with no run; LOADING, NO SCORES YET and SCORES UNAVAILABLE say what is happening; keyboard (W/S, arrows), gamepad (triggers) and touch (tap, drag to scroll) all work, typing in the chat never changes a tab, and the gamepad's A is never taken by a selected tab.
- **Developer**: the client API is one `start`, the server's one `start`; the Logic is small typed functions; the protocol is one remote with a 2-value ask and a 6-value answer (its best `false` while pending), read defensively.
