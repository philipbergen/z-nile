## Theme
Three portal features, one type, one project, stacked as three layers of one pull request.
- **f0205** (Game carousel, size 900) builds the portal's one screen: a carousel of the four game cards, their price badges and the server's per-player free state (`Logic/Carousel`, `Logic/Card`, `Games.luau`, `server/Cards.luau`, `client/CarouselView.luau`).
