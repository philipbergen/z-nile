### F1 [high] f0208: with the landed Wallet, a PLAY still busy after CHECK_SECONDS writes no pass, so the "slow store goes on the pass" outcome is false; the executor's note says it was raised to the human, but it was not
Where: layer 2 (f0208). `portal/src/server/Plays.luau:267-288` (`check`), `:313-320` (`run`, the pass). `portal/src/shared/Logic/Play/init.luau:79-82` (`decide`). The landed `arcade/src/server/Wallet.luau:254-256` (busy clears `mayPass`), `:266-290` (a late read keeps `reading = true` until its GetAsync returns), `:308-310` (`pass` refuses without `mayPass`). `arcade/src/shared/Logic/PaidPlay/init.luau:222-227` (`arrive`). f0208.md Verification log, the "A joint with the landed Wallet, for the human" bullet. R0117.md `## Open to the human` and `## Addressed during execution`.
Evidence: here is a concrete sequence on one server.
1. t=0: the screen opens. f0205's `Cards` calls `Wallet.read(p, "snake")`, which sets `slot.reading = true`, and its GetAsync is queued. A queue at a server's start is the window design review 002 F1 already names: board refreshes spend the same live budget.
2. t=0.5: the player presses PLAY on Snake. `Plays.check` gets `nil, "busy"`, and Wallet sets `mayPass = false`. It reads again every 0.25 s.
3. t=5: `within` gives up on Cards' read. Wallet sets `late = true` and `mayPass = true` and answers "down" to Cards, but `slot.reading` stays true while the GetAsync is outstanding.
4. t=5.25: PLAY's next read is "busy" again, and Wallet sets `mayPass = false`.
5. t=10.5: `Play.decide(nil, "busy", mode, 10.x)` answers "pass", so `run` sets the play going and calls `Wallet.pass`. That returns false at Wallet.luau:308 and writes nothing. `proceed` then teleports the player to Snake's place.
6. There, `Wallet.arrive` claims. When the store answers and the player has no free start due and no held play, `PaidPlay.start` gives "buy". `takePass` finds no entry, so it answers false. `PaidPlay.arrive("buy", false)` = "back", and the game place sends the player back to the portal.

So AC3's "a failed read, or one still busy after 10 s, writes the pass then teleports" is not true with the real library. The player is never offered the purchase and pays for two teleports for nothing. The design's Guess ("a read still under way after 10 s … goes on the pass") is not delivered. The sequestered `server.spec.luau` "a check still busy after CHECK_SECONDS (a slow store) writes the pass, then teleports" passes only because its stub `wallet.pass` always returns true (server.spec.luau:266-270). It never models the allowance that busy answers clear.

The executor found this. The f0208 Verification log ends "Raised under R0117's `## Open to the human`", but R0117.md has no such bullet at 3c79541b7. The section holds only the two ruled F1s from the design reviews, and "Addressed during execution" says "None yet". Nothing outside the log records it, so the human will not see it. `done` cannot refuse on it either, because `openlist.pending` reads only bullets.
Critique: this is a wrong result on a path the design promises, hidden by a stub. The tempting fix is to leave the code and add the bullet. That records the gap but leaves the outcome false. A second tempting fix is inside Plays: keep reading past CHECK_SECONDS until the late call ends. That breaks the sequestered `play.spec` ("CHECK_SECONDS … goes on the pass") and `server.spec`'s read count of 39 to 42, and the executor may not edit those (zz-execute stop conditions). A third is to have Plays treat a refused pass as a failure. That turns a slow store into COULD NOT START, against f0145's G10 ("a store that cannot be read … lets the player in").
Consequences: the real fix belongs in the arcade's Wallet, another project, and a raft never spans two projects. One form: a busy answer leaves `mayPass` set while the in-flight call is already late, because that call answered "down" to its first caller. That adds state to Wallet's slot: a late flag set at `within`'s timeout and cleared when the call ends. It also meets f0201's promoted cases, which pin `pass` refusing after a busy read (lesson arcade-arch/free-grants-check-their-own-precondition). The new rule is "busy after a late read is still down", and those cases would need an Override chore or a new case. A late GetAsync that then returns a record leaves a pass already written. The arrival takes it whatever the start, which G10 allows. Until that lands, every slow-store PLAY in the window above is bounced.
Recommendation: escalate to the user. Put a `- F<n>:` bullet under R0117's `## Open to the human` with the sequence above, and correct the Verification log sentence to point at it. The options are:
- (a) An arcade defect against f0201's Wallet, its own issue and raft: a busy answer while the read under way is late keeps, or re-opens, the pass allowance. Its spec drives the real Wallet with a GetAsync that outlives `WAIT_SECONDS` and a second caller's busy reads, then asserts that `pass` writes. It needs an override of f0201's "refuses after busy" case where that case covers the late form. R0117 lands before or after it, and the user rules which.
- (b) Unfold f0208 and redesign the busy path, for example: wait for the late call to end, bounded by a longer limit, then pass. That means new sequestered cases, a design review and a refold.
- (c) Accept it: a store call queued more than 10 s sends a player with nothing held to the game place and back. Record it as known, with the server-start window it overlaps (design review 002 F1).

Whichever is chosen, the layer-3 Beta observation that design review 002 F1's ruling (b) owes should also record whether a PLAY at the screen's open came back to the portal, not only whether it answered "down".
Lesson: pickman-arch/open-stores-guarded
