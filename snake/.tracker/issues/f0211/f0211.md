I also want a new project for a landing portal where you can scroll between the games and see the highscores for each, the same cost model remains but it shoudl show 5 robux for each game witha slash through showing it is currently on sale for 1 robux. highscores shoudl show how many revives were used. What should happen:
- The Snake rules in pure Logic: the grid arena sized from the player count, movement with two buffered turns, apples and growth, death on walls, borders and bodies (heads meeting kill both), lives and respawn.
- **Maps**: map 1 is empty and **map k has k−1 straight walls**, horizontal or vertical, that **never connect** (one empty tile all round, diagonals included); reaching the goal length ends the map and the next one starts.

## Solution
Two pure Logic features in snake's home. **Maps** (`src/shared/Logic/Maps`) answers everything about one map's arena: the side for N players, the map's speed and goal length, the spawns, and the seeded roll of its k−1 walls with the "never connect" rule and the 2-tile growth when they don't fit. **World** (`src/shared/Logic/World`) is the rules of one arena of N snakes: a plain-data state the server owns, `World.turn` for the Left/Right intents (two buffered), and `World.advance(w, dt, draw)`, which cuts time at every step and timer, moves every alive snake one tile at once, resolves apples, growth, deaths (walls, border, bodies, heads meeting), lives, respawn at the safest tile, and the goal length that clears the map and starts the next. Time and randomness come in as parameters (`dt`, and a `Draw` of arcade's `Rng.draw` shape), so every rule runs headless in Lune. There is no adapter here: the server session and the view are f0213's, the shared arena's extra rules f0212's, boost and the other foods f0214's, and the revive f0215's; each builds on this state and these functions.

### Decisions relied on
From `snake/.tracker/game-design.md`, **Game design: Snake (`snake/`)** (binding; quoted):
- Arena and view: "A square grid of tiles with a solid border." "Solo arena: 20×20. With N players, the side is `ceil(sqrt(400 + 250·(N−1)))`: 26 for 2, 34 for 4, 47 for 8, 65 for 16, 79 for 24. The size is fixed when a map starts."
- Movement: "The snake moves one tile per step at the map's speed: 4 tiles/s real time on map 1, +0.25 per map, capped at 7." "Intents are `Left` and `Right`, relative to the heading. There is no `Back`: reversing would be suicide. Up to **two turns are buffered**, so a quick Left, Left makes a U-turn over two tiles."
- Food: "**Apple:** +1 length, 10 × map number points. There are always (players + 1) apples on the board."
- Maps: "Map 1 is empty. **Map k has k−1 walls.** Each wall is one straight segment, horizontal or vertical, 3 to max(4, side/3) tiles long." "**Walls never connect:** there is at least one empty tile all round every wall, diagonals included, and between every wall and the border. Spawn areas stay clear. Map 2's single wall is near the middle." "Placement is a seeded roll with retries. If the walls don't fit, the arena grows by 2 tiles." "**Goal length** for map k is 10 + 2k (12 on map 1). Reaching it ends the map: a 2 s "MAP CLEAR" with a bonus of 100 × k, then the next map, the snake reset to 4 segments, and a little more speed."
- Death, lives and revives: "A snake dies when its head enters a wall, the border, or any snake's body, its own included. Two heads meeting (the same tile, or swapping tiles) kill both." "3 lives. A death waits 1.5 s, then respawns the snake with 4 segments at the free tile farthest from every head, on the same map, after a 2 s "Ready"."
- Pillar (2): "Deterministic Logic, simulated headless, as in Pickman."

From `arcade/.tracker/architecture.md`, Technical skeleton: "**Game Logic never requires arcade Logic.** Arcade features are leaves that adapters wire in, and game Logic gets them as parameters (an RNG, a store, a travel status)." f0203 (prep in flight, `.worktree/prep-input-and-look-cores`, its draft of d5743cb7) designs that parameter: "`Rng.draw(g: Generator): Draw` -- a function drawing Rng.int(g, n) from the same g" and "A game's Logic takes a `Draw` (declaring the same function type locally) or a seed". f0211 declares `Draw = (n: number) -> number` (a whole number in 1..n) locally, as f0203 says, and depends on nothing in f0203: its specs pass their own seeded draw.

**The side for 8 and 16 players.** The formula gives 47 for 8 players (`sqrt(2150)` = 46.4) and 65 for 16 (`sqrt(4150)` = 64.4); game-design.md used to list 48 and 66 for them, while 2, 4 and 24 agreed with the formula. This design follows the formula and pins 47 and 65 (maps.spec). The user decided it on 2026-11-15 ("Formula wins: 47 and 65", the `Decided with the user` line at the top of this Design), and game-design.md now lists 47 and 65.

**Prep choices** (each traceable to no user line, so marked; each cheap to change, a tuning value or one rule):
- (prep) Coordinates: x grows east, y grows south, the playable tiles are 1..side on both axes, and the border is the ring just outside (0 and side + 1). "20×20" is the playable grid.
- (prep) A spawn is a head tile and a heading; the snake's 4 segments trail behind the head and the 3 tiles ahead must be clear. A spawn's **area** is those 7 tiles, and "spawn areas stay clear" means no wall tile within one tile of them, diagonals included (the same rule as wall to wall). The spawns for N players sit on a near-square lattice of `ceil(sqrt N)` columns, one per cell in row-major order, each heading east from 4 tiles inside its cell's left edge on the cell's middle row. f0212 owns how a shared arena spreads them ("respawned at spread-out spawns"); it may replace `Maps.spawns` with a better spread, and every rule here reads spawns only through the layout.
- (prep) "Near the middle": map 2's wall's midpoint is within 2 tiles of the arena's centre on both axes (`Maps.TUNING.middleReach`).
- (prep) "A seeded roll with retries": each wall is rolled up to 100 times (`wallTries`); if none fits, the first fitting 3-tile wall in a row-major scan (H before V) is taken; only when nothing fits does the arena grow by 2 and the whole layout roll again. The scan is what makes the roll end for any draw (a draw that always says 1 included); without it a crowded map could grow for ever.
- (prep) Every placement, a map start or a respawn, is followed by the 2 s Ready, during which the snake is solid but does not move and cannot die (only a moving head dies). Turns given during Ready are buffered and used once it moves.
- (prep) "The free tile farthest from every head": the head tile whose Manhattan distance to the nearest other Ready or alive snake's head is largest, among placements with the 4 body tiles free (no wall, snake or apple) and the 3 tiles ahead free of walls and snakes; ties go to the tile farthest (Chebyshev) from every wall tile and the border, then to any fixed order the implementation picks (the prototype takes the lowest y, x and heading in N, E, S, W order; design review 001 F9: C2, the reverse order, is a correct variant, so the specs do not pin it). With no other snake on the board (solo), the second rule decides: the most open tile.
- (prep) A tail that moves away this step frees its tile for any head entering it this step (classic Snake); a tail that stays (its snake is growing) does not.
- (prep) An out snake's body stays where it died and, like a dying one, is off the board: not solid, and apples may land on it. f0213 draws it as it chooses; f0215's revive places the snake afresh (design review 001 F3).
- (prep) "Players" in "(players + 1) apples" and in the side is the number of snakes in the world, an out one (no lives) included.
- (prep) The third death puts the snake **out** at once (no 1.5 s wait): what follows is f0215's game over. A world whose every snake is out is **over** and stands still.
- (prep) When snakes reach the goal in the same step, each one that did gets the 100 × k bonus; the map clears once.

### Modules and public API
Both features are pure Logic (pickman-arch rule 2): no engine word, no `@game`, time and randomness as parameters. `World` requires `./Maps` (inside its `init.luau`, a sibling feature), the one new edge, `World -> Maps`, declared in `snake/.tracker/architecture.md` in this prep.

**`snake/src/shared/Logic/Maps/init.luau`**
```luau
export type Draw = (n: number) -> number -- a whole number in 1..n (arcade's Rng.draw, f0203)
export type Heading = "N" | "E" | "S" | "W"
export type Dir = "H" | "V"
export type Tile = { x: number, y: number }
export type Wall = { x: number, y: number, dir: Dir, length: number } -- `length` tiles from (x, y) east (H) or south (V)
export type Spawn = { x: number, y: number, heading: Heading } -- the head tile; the body trails behind it
export type Layout = { map: number, side: number, walls: { Wall }, spawns: { Spawn } }

Maps.TUNING -- frozen: baseArea 400, areaPerPlayer 250, speedStart 4, speedStep 0.25, speedCap 7, goalBase 10,
            -- goalStep 2, startLength 4, clearAhead 3, wallMin 3, wallMaxFloor 4, middleReach 2, wallTries 100, grow 2
Maps.HEADINGS: { Heading } -- N, E, S, W
Maps.step(h: Heading): Tile -- the unit step: N (0, -1), E (1, 0), S (0, 1), W (-1, 0)
Maps.turn(h: Heading, side: "Left" | "Right"): Heading
Maps.side(players: number): number -- ceil(sqrt(baseArea + areaPerPlayer * (players - 1)))
Maps.speed(map: number): number -- min(speedCap, speedStart + speedStep * (map - 1)), tiles per second
Maps.goal(map: number): number -- goalBase + goalStep * map
Maps.wallCount(map: number): number -- map - 1
Maps.wallMax(side: number): number -- max(wallMaxFloor, floor(side / 3))
Maps.wallTiles(w: Wall): { Tile }
Maps.spawnTiles(s: Spawn): { Tile } -- the 4 body tiles (head first... tail last) then the 3 tiles ahead
Maps.spawns(side: number, players: number): { Spawn }
Maps.nearMiddle(w: Wall, side: number): boolean
Maps.roll(map: number, players: number, draw: Draw): Layout
```
`side`, `speed`, `goal`, `wallCount`, `spawns` and `roll` raise an error naming the function for a count or map that is not a whole number of at least 1.

`Maps.roll(map, players, draw)`: start from `side = Maps.side(players)`; compute the spawns for that side and mark their areas blocked (each tile and its 8 neighbours); for each of the `map − 1` walls, roll up to `wallTries` candidates (direction `draw(2)`, length `wallMin − 1 + draw(wallMax − wallMin + 1)`, then a start inside 2..side−1; on map 2 the start is rolled inside the middle window) and take the first that fits; else take the scan's first fit; a wall fits when every tile is in 2..side−1, no tile is blocked, and (map 2) it is near the middle; a placed wall marks its tiles and their neighbours blocked. If a wall has no fit, `side += grow` and the layout rolls again from the first wall. Neither feature ever calls `draw` with anything but a whole number of at least 1 (f0203's `Rng.int(g, 0)` is a misuse error; the specs' draw raises the same, design review 001 F8). The draws a roll uses, and their order, are the implementation's: the specs pin the rules, never a layout for a seed, so the executor may draw differently (E5 ran three correct variants).

**`snake/src/shared/Logic/World/init.luau`**
```luau
export type Draw = Maps.Draw
export type Heading = Maps.Heading
export type Tile = Maps.Tile
export type Turn = "Left" | "Right"
export type Phase = "play" | "clear" | "over"
export type SnakeState = "ready" | "alive" | "dying" | "over"
export type Snake = {
	id: number, -- 1..N, its index in `snakes`; spawn i is snake i's
	body: { Tile }, -- head first; while dying or out, the body it died with (not solid)
	heading: Heading,
	turns: { Turn }, -- buffered, oldest first, at most 2
	grow: number, -- steps left in which the tail stays put
	lives: number,
	score: number,
	state: SnakeState,
	timer: number, -- seconds left in ready or dying
}
export type World = {
	map: number,
	layout: Maps.Layout, -- the map's arena; walls are read from it on every step
	snakes: { Snake },
	apples: { Tile },
	phase: Phase,
	timer: number, -- seconds left in clear
	untilStep: number, -- seconds until the next step
}
export type Event =
	{ kind: "map", map: number }
	| { kind: "placed", id: number, x: number, y: number, heading: Heading }
	| { kind: "go", id: number } -- Ready is over
	| { kind: "ate", id: number, x: number, y: number }
	| { kind: "died", id: number, x: number, y: number } -- the tile the head entered
	| { kind: "over", id: number } -- no lives left
	| { kind: "cleared", map: number, ids: { number } } -- the snakes that reached the goal

World.TUNING -- frozen: lives 3, turnBuffer 2, deathSeconds 1.5, readySeconds 2, clearSeconds 2, appleGrow 1,
             -- applePoints 10, clearBonus 100
World.new(players: number, draw: Draw): (World, { Event }) -- map 1, every snake at its spawn and Ready
World.start(w: World, layout: Maps.Layout, draw: Draw): { Event } -- start this layout as the world's map
World.turn(w: World, id: number, turn: Turn): boolean -- buffer a turn; false if refused
World.advance(w: World, dt: number, draw: Draw): { Event } -- what happened, in order
World.safest(w: World, id: number): Maps.Spawn? -- where snake id would respawn now; nil if nowhere (O(side²))
World.goal(w: World): number
World.speed(w: World): number
```
**The state is the API.** Every field of `World` and `Snake` is public, plain data and the whole truth: no cache, no metatable, no function value. `advance` reads walls from `w.layout`, bodies from `snakes`, and apples from `apples` every time, so a spec may set any of them by hand, the server can snapshot or send them (f0213), and f0212, f0214 and f0215 extend the same records. `advance` and `turn` change `w` in place, as pickman's Ghosts set does.

**`World.turn`** keeps a turn only when the snake exists, the world is in `play`, the snake is `ready` or `alive`, the turn is `"Left"` or `"Right"`, and fewer than 2 turns wait. Anything else is refused and changes nothing: a client sending `Back` or a third turn cannot do harm (rule 7: the server decides).

**`World.advance(w, dt, draw)`** loops until `dt` is spent or the world is `over`, each time taking the slice up to the next instant something happens: the next step (`untilStep`), the end of a snake's Ready or death wait, or the end of MAP CLEAR. At an instant, in order: timers run out (a dying snake whose wait is over is placed at `World.safest`, or, with nowhere free, tries again at the next step; a Ready snake whose Ready is over becomes alive, event `go`), then the step if one is due (and `untilStep += 1 / Maps.speed(w.map)`), then `over` if every snake is out. During MAP CLEAR only its timer runs: death waits and Readies pause, and nothing moves (a dying snake is placed by the next map, never during the pause); when it ends, `World.start(w, Maps.roll(w.map + 1, #w.snakes, draw), draw)`. Comparisons use a tolerance of 1e-9 s, so slices that sum to `dt` in floating point land on the same instants: at 1/60 s frames a map 2 step (1/4.25 s) and a 1.5 s wait end on the same instants as in one long call (moves.spec "real frame rates give the same game", design review 001 F1). Timers before the step means a Ready that ends on a step instant moves on that step: on map 1 the first Ready ends with the 8th step, at 2.0 s. Because time is cut at every instant, one call of 6 s and any cutting of it into smaller calls do the same thing, frame rate included.

**A step** (all alive snakes at once; nobody moves first):
1. Each alive snake takes its oldest buffered turn, if any, and its new head is the next tile along its heading.
2. It eats if an apple is on that tile; its `grow` gains `appleGrow` (1) if so. If `grow > 0` the tail stays and `grow` drops by 1, else the tail leaves. So an apple adds a segment on the step it is eaten.
3. The board after the step is every moved body plus every Ready snake's body. A moving snake dies when its new head is off the board, on a wall tile, or on a tile that the board counts twice (another body, its own body, another new head, or a swap: each head is then on the other's neck). Dying and out snakes' bodies are not on the board. All deaths of a step are decided on the same board, so head-on and swap kill both, and order never matters.
4. A death costs a life and clears the buffered turns and `grow`. With lives left the snake is `dying` for 1.5 s, its last body kept for the view and not solid; with none it is `over` (event `over`).
5. Each survivor keeps its new body; one that ate scores `applePoints × map`, its apple leaves the board (event `ate`), and if any apple was eaten the board is topped up to `players + 1` apples.
6. Each survivor now as long as `Maps.goal(map)` or longer scores `clearBonus × map`; if any did, the world turns to `clear` for 2 s (event `cleared` with their ids).

**Apples**: an apple goes on a tile drawn among every free tile (no wall, no Ready or alive snake, no apple) in row-major order; with no free tile, none is placed and nothing fails. A map starts with `#snakes + 1` apples, and whenever an apple is eaten the board is topped up to `#snakes + 1`, so the count stays `players + 1` while there is room. A count left short by a full board is made up at the next apple eaten or the next map start, not before (design review 001 F9). A placement counts the free tiles, draws one and walks to it, building no table per tile.

**`World.safest(w, id)`** builds two distance fields once per call, each a breadth-first pass over the grid 0..side+1 with no obstacles: the Manhattan distance to the nearest other Ready or alive head (4 neighbours, from every such head; none when there is no other snake), and the Chebyshev distance to the nearest wall tile or border tile (8 neighbours, from every wall tile and the border ring). It marks each board tile open for a body (no wall, no Ready or alive snake, no apple) or only for the tiles ahead (an apple). Each (tile, heading) is then 7 lookups: the 4 body tiles open for a body, the 3 tiles ahead open, and the two field values, the best kept by the rule of the prep choice above. O(side²) per call, with no table per candidate (design review 001 F5).

**A map start** (`World.start`): `w.map = layout.map`, the layout, no apples, phase `play`, `untilStep = 1 / Maps.speed(map)`, event `map`; each snake that is not out (alive, Ready or dying) is placed at `layout.spawns[i]` with 4 segments, no turns, no growth, Ready for 2 s (event `placed`); lives and score stay. Every placement clears the snake's buffered turns and growth, so a turn left waiting when the map cleared never steers the next map (design review 001 F4). Then the board is topped up to `#snakes + 1` apples.

### Server authority and the split
Everything here is Logic, run by the server (f0213's session; f0212's arena). The server holds the World, applies a client's turn only through `World.turn` (which refuses anything but Left and Right, and a third turn), and advances it with its own clock and its own `Rng.draw`. A client never sends a position, a length or a score: it renders the World state it is sent. There is no adapter in this issue, so nothing here needs Studio.

### Edge cases
- Empty input: a player count or map below 1 is an error from the function that got it; `World.new(1, draw)` is the solo game.
- Bounds: the border is outside 1..side; walls stay in 2..side−1; a wall is 3..max(4, floor(side/3)) long.
- Simultaneous events: deaths, eating and the goal are decided on one board per step; timers fire before the step at the same instant; several snakes reaching the goal together all score and the map clears once; a snake dying while another clears the map is placed at its spawn on the next map, as is any dying snake; an out snake stays out.
- A player who leaves or joins: f0212's (it changes `snakes` between maps and places a joiner with `World.safest`).
- Respawn: a snake respawns at `World.safest`; with no free placement it waits and tries again at every step. A respawn never lands on a Ready snake or with its body on an apple (deaths.spec "on a crowded board").
- An out snake: its body stays as it died, off the board; play goes on for the rest.
- A full board: no apple is placed and nothing fails; the count is made up at the next apple eaten or the next map.
- Frame-rate independence: `advance` cuts time at every instant (moves.spec "however the time is cut").
- Crowded maps: the roll ends for any draw (the scan), and the arena grows 2 tiles at a time until the walls fit.

### Scalability
- Per step: O(S + N) for S body segments over N snakes (one table of tile counts), plus O(W) wall tiles. An apple placement and a respawn each scan the board once or twice: O(side²), only on an apple eaten, a death or a map start.
- Per event, at the largest arena (E7, Lune, 24 snakes, side 79): one `World.safest` 2.5 to 2.7 ms on maps 1 to 30 (8 snakes, side 47: 1.2 ms; solo 0.1 ms); one apple placement 0.22 to 0.37 ms; a map start with its 25 apples 5.6 to 9.3 ms, once a map. Several respawns in one tick (snakes that died together) cost that much each; f0212's E6 sets the cap with these and the snapshot bytes (design review 001 F5 measured the first prototype's brute-force respawn at 45 to 104 ms, which this design replaced).
- Per tick of random play (E2, 24 snakes, 1,800 ticks of 1/30 s, respawns included): 0.06 ms on average.
- A map roll is O(k · wallTries · wallLength) plus the scan when it fires: under 5 ms for any map up to 40 and any player count up to 24 (E1).
- Replication is f0213's and f0212's (E6 measures the snapshot bytes). The state is plain data, so a snapshot is the World itself or a diff of it; what changes per step is one new head and one dropped tail per snake.

### Maintainability
- Every number of the game design is a row of `Maps.TUNING` or `World.TUNING`; no rule branches on a map number except map 2's middle wall, which is the design's own exception.
- The next things plug in here: **boost** (f0214) gives a snake a second move per step (the world's clock ticks at twice the map speed and a normal snake moves on every other tick); **golden apples and crumbs** (f0214) are more food kinds with their own `grow` and points beside `apples` (`grow` already counts several segments); **the shared arena** (f0212) adds a winner's bonus on the `cleared` event and joins through `World.safest`; **the revive** (f0215) gives an out snake lives and places it with `World.safest`. None of them needs a rule here to change.
- A new map rule (another wall shape) is a new branch in the roll's candidate and fit, with its own spec; the never-connect check is one blocked grid for every kind.

### Usability
- For the player: every rule is readable in first person. Death is only ever the head entering something solid that was there or arrived in the same step; a Ready snake is solid but cannot die, so a respawn can't be killed before it moves; a respawn never starts facing a wall or a snake within 3 tiles; turns are relative to the heading, as on the A/D keys, and two can wait, so a quick Left, Left is a U-turn; the MAP CLEAR pause and the Ready give time to read the new map.
- For the developer: two small typed modules; `World.advance` returns ordered events for the view and the session; the state is plain data that tests set by hand; misuse (a count below 1, an unknown turn) is an error or a refusal, never a NaN later.
