# f0214: Boost, golden apples and crumbs

## Problem
The user, 2026-10-31: "I want to branch out into multiple games, they will each have their own directory in the project: first person snake, first person asteroids. I also want a new project for a landing portal where you can scroll between the games and see the highscores for each, the same cost model remains but it shoudl show 5 robux for each game witha slash through showing it is currently on sale for 1 robux. highscores shoudl show how many revives were used. I want you to design a full fun playable game for both of those two new game ideas. I also want both snake and asteroids to be a two player game, snakes can even be n-player up to a high number just make the wold bigger. Snakes should have maps that once you have reached a certain length the game ends and we move to a new map, the first is empty, then one wall somewhere in the middle, then two, then three and so on. The walls can be vertical and horizontal but do not connect. for asteroids we could have a two player version where each player is attacking the aliens from opposing sides. You can shoot each other's barriers. the aliens would then come in from the side and and split into two groups that move toward either player". Then: "Add a third game first person asteroids." Decided with the user (2026-10-31): the 2-player aliens game is pure Space Invaders (`invaders`); Asteroids is its own game (`asteroids`); one Roblox experience with the portal as start place, replacing pickman's lobby; every board shows revives used; one free game per hour per game.

The approved design is `snake/.tracker/game-design.md`, section **Game design: Snake (`snake/`)** (c0183). This issue owns these parts of it, as they are said there: **Movement** ("Boost") and **Food** ("Golden apple" and "Crumbs"). The rest of it belongs to other issues (c0190's ownership table), listed under Non-goals.

What should happen:
- Boost (double speed, sheds a segment every 0.5 s, never below 4), golden apples (+3, every 20 s for 6 s) and crumbs from a dead snake's body.

## Non-goals
- The rest of the rules: f0211.
- The rest of `snake/.tracker/game-design.md`: f0211, f0212, f0213, f0215.

## Solution
Extend `snake/src/shared/Logic/World` (pure Logic, no new feature or dependency edge) with three mechanics built directly on f0211's plain-data `World` and `Snake` state: (1) **Boost** via `World.boost(w, id, on)` and a half-step world clock (`1 / (2 * speed)` with `w.halfStep`) so boosting snakes move on every tick (double speed) while non-boosting snakes move on every second tick, shedding one tail segment into `w.crumbs` every 0.5 s while above 4 segments; (2) **Golden apples** via `w.golden` and `w.goldenTimer`, spawning on a free tile every 20 s for 6 s (+3 length, `50 × map` points), pausing during MAP CLEAR; and (3) **Crumbs** via `w.crumbs` (+1 length, 5 points flat), dropped on boost shed (always) and on every other segment of a dying snake's body when `w.shared` is true. All timers are sliced in `World.advance` with f0211's 1e-9 s tolerance for full frame-rate independence and 100% compatibility with f0211's existing rules and specs.

## Design

### Decisions relied on
From `snake/.tracker/game-design.md`, **Game design: Snake (`snake/`)** (binding; quoted):
- Movement: "**Boost:** while held, speed doubles and the snake sheds one tail segment every 0.5 s, never below 4 segments. A shed segment becomes a crumb (below). This is the move for cutting someone off."
- Food: "**Golden apple:** every 20 s one appears for 6 s. +3 length, 50 × map number points."
- Food: "**Crumbs:** when a snake dies in a shared arena, every other segment of its body becomes a crumb: +1 length, 5 points."
- Pillar (2): "Deterministic Logic, simulated headless, as in Pickman."

From `snake/.tracker/issues/f0211/f0211.md` (the base `World` feature this issue extends):
- `World` is plain data (no cache, no metatable, no function values), mutated in place by `World.turn`, `World.advance`, and `World.start`, with `Draw = (n: number) -> number` (a whole number in `1..n`, never called with `< 1`).
- During `MAP CLEAR`, only `w.timer` runs; when `w.phase == "over"`, `World.advance` does nothing.
- At any instant, timers fire before the step, with a 1e-9 s tolerance for frame-rate independence.
- `World.safest(w, id)` chooses the free placement (4 body tiles off walls, Ready/alive snakes, and apples; 3 ahead off walls and Ready/alive snakes) farthest from every other Ready/alive head, then farthest from walls/border.

### Prep guesses
Every open choice not fixed by `game-design.md` or `f0211.md` is recorded with `./zn guess f0214 --step design` and marked below:
- **Guess (prep) G1**: A 4-segment snake still moves at double speed while holding boost (shedding simply pauses when `#body <= 4`); releasing boost preserves the remaining `shedTimer` (does not refill to 0.5 s) so tap-boosting cannot avoid shedding; death and `World.start` reset `boosting = false` and `shedTimer = 0.5`.
- **Guess (prep) G2**: The world clock ticks at `2 × Maps.speed(w.map)` (`untilStep = 1 / (2 * speed)`) with `w.halfStep: boolean` (starts `true` on `World.start`). On half-steps (`w.halfStep == true`), only alive snakes with `s.boosting == true` move while non-boosting alive snakes remain solid and stationary; on full-steps (`w.halfStep == false`), all alive snakes move together.
- **Guess (prep) G3**: `World` gains `shared: boolean`, defaulting to `players > 1` in `World.new(players, draw)` (plain data, so f0212 can set `w.shared = true` even for a 1-player shared arena). Death drops crumbs only when `w.shared` is true, taking odd body indices (`1, 3, 5, ...` of the pre-step body) on free tiles. Boost shedding always drops a crumb on the shed tail tile in both solo and shared arenas.
- **Guess (prep) G4**: Golden apples follow a fixed 20 s cadence from map start (`w.goldenTimer` starts at 20 s and resets to 20 s on each spawn instant, regardless of when the previous one was eaten or expired); both `w.goldenTimer` and `w.golden.timer` pause during `MAP CLEAR` and reset on `World.start`. New events use distinct kinds (`ateGolden`, `ateCrumb`, `goldenSpawned`, `goldenExpired`, `shed`, `crumbs`) so f0211's `ate` event stays unchanged.
- **Guess (prep) G5**: All food tiles (`w.apples`, `w.golden`, `w.crumbs`) are mutually disjoint. Crumbs persist until eaten, cleared by a respawn, or cleared on `World.start` (no expiry timer). `World.safest(w, id)` keeps f0211's exact rules (ignoring `w.crumbs` and `w.golden`), and respawn placement clears any crumb or golden apple under the 4 newly placed body tiles without scoring or growing.

### Modules and public API

**`snake/src/shared/Logic/World/init.luau`** (additions to f0211's types, tuning, and functions):
```luau

	y: number,

}

export type Snake = {

	crumbs: { Tile }, -- crumbs from boost sheds and shared-arena deaths
	shared: boolean, -- true in a shared arena (death drops crumbs); World.new sets players > 1
	phase: Phase,
	timer: number,

export type Event =

	| { kind: "go", id: number }

	| { kind: "ateGolden", id: number, x: number, y: number }
	| { kind: "ateCrumb", id: number, x: number, y: number }
	| { kind: "goldenSpawned", x: number, y: number }
	| { kind: "goldenExpired", x: number, y: number }
	| { kind: "shed", id: number, x: number, y: number }
	| { kind: "crumbs", id: number, tiles: { Tile } }
	| { kind: "died", id: number, x: number, y: number }
	| { kind: "over", id: number }
	| { kind: "cleared", map: number, ids: { number } }

-- Added fields on frozen World.TUNING:
-- boostMinLength = 4
-- boostShedSeconds = 0.5
-- goldenIntervalSeconds = 20
-- goldenLifeSeconds = 6
```

**`World.boost(w, id, on)`**:
- Accepts only when `w.phase == "play"`, snake `id` exists, `type(on) == "boolean"`, and `s.state == "ready" or s.state == "alive"`.
- Sets `s.boosting = on` and returns `true`; otherwise changes nothing and returns `false` (pickman-arch rule 7: server validates every client intent).
- Does **not** reset `s.shedTimer` when toggled off or on (**Guess (prep) G1**). While `s.state == "ready"`, holding boost is remembered and takes effect once `go` fires (no movement or shedding during Ready).

**`World.start(w, layout, draw)`** (extensions to f0211):
- Resets `w.golden = nil`, `w.goldenTimer = World.TUNING.goldenIntervalSeconds` (20), `w.crumbs = {}`, `w.halfStep = true`, `w.untilStep = 1 / (2 * Maps.speed(layout.map))`.
- For every non-out snake placed at its spawn: resets `s.boosting = false` and `s.shedTimer = World.TUNING.boostShedSeconds` (0.5) (**Guess (prep) G1**).
- Note: `w.shared` is set by `World.new(players, draw)` to `players > 1` and preserved across `World.start` (**Guess (prep) G3**).

**`World.advance(w, dt, draw)`** (event-slicing extensions):
- When `w.phase == "over"`, returns `{}` immediately.
- When `w.phase == "clear"`, only `w.timer` advances (pausing `goldenTimer`, `golden.timer`, `shedTimer`, Ready/dying timers, and steps) (**Guess (prep) G4**).
- When `w.phase == "play"`, each slice takes `nextDt = min(left, w.untilStep, ...)` considering `w.goldenTimer` (when `> eps`), `w.golden.timer` (when `w.golden ~= nil and w.golden.timer > eps`), each active snake's Ready/dying `s.timer` (when `> eps`), and each active boosting snake's `s.shedTimer` (when `s.state == "alive" and s.boosting and #s.body > World.TUNING.boostMinLength and s.shedTimer > eps`) (**Guess (prep) G1**).
- All active timers subtract `nextDt`; events at the instant fire in deterministic order (using f0211's `eps = 1e-9` s tolerance):
  1. **Respawns**: dying snakes with `s.timer <= eps` call `World.safest(w, s.id)`. If placed: reset `s.boosting = false`, `s.shedTimer = World.TUNING.boostShedSeconds`, and remove any crumb or golden apple on the 4 placed body tiles without scoring or growing (**Guess (prep) G5**).
  2. **Ready end**: Ready snakes with `s.timer <= eps` become `alive` (event `go`).
  3. **Boost shed**: alive boosting snakes (in id order) with `#s.body > World.TUNING.boostMinLength` and `s.shedTimer <= eps` pop their tail segment `tail = table.remove(s.body)`, preserve periodic phase with `s.shedTimer = math.max(0, s.shedTimer) + World.TUNING.boostShedSeconds`, place a crumb at `tail` if no wall/solid snake/apple/golden/crumb occupies it (always true in normal play), and emit `{ kind = "shed", id = s.id, x = tail.x, y = tail.y }` (**Guess (prep) G1**, **G3**).
  4. **Golden apple expiry**: if `w.golden ~= nil` and `w.golden.timer <= eps`, capture `{ x, y }`, set `w.golden = nil`, and emit `{ kind = "goldenExpired", x = x, y = y }` (**Guess (prep) G4**).
  5. **Golden apple spawn**: if `w.goldenTimer <= eps`, preserve periodic phase with `w.goldenTimer = math.max(0, w.goldenTimer) + World.TUNING.goldenIntervalSeconds`; pick a free tile (no wall, no Ready/alive snake, no apple, no crumb, no golden) in row-major order via `draw(freeCount)` (never calling `draw` when `freeCount == 0`); if found, set `w.golden = { x = tile.x, y = tile.y, timer = World.TUNING.goldenLifeSeconds }` and emit `{ kind = "goldenSpawned", x = tile.x, y = tile.y }` (**Guess (prep) G4**, **G5**).
  6. **Step**: if `w.untilStep <= eps`, run one tick for `moving = if w.halfStep then { alive boosting snakes } else { all alive snakes }`, then toggle `w.halfStep = not w.halfStep` and add `1 / (2 * Maps.speed(w.map))` to `w.untilStep` (**Guess (prep) G2**).
  7. **Game over**: if every snake is `over`, set `w.phase = "over"`.

**A tick (step)** (simultaneous resolution for `moving` snakes):
1. Each moving snake pops its oldest buffered turn (if any) and computes its new head.
2. Check food at new head (disjoint sets, so at most one matches): apple (`+appleGrow` = 1), golden apple (`+goldenGrow` = 3), or crumb (`+crumbGrow` = 1). Compute provisional `newGrow = s.grow + foodGrow`; if `newGrow > 0`, tail stays and `newGrow -= 1`, else tail leaves.
3. Build the simultaneous occupancy board from: every moving snake's provisional new body + every stationary alive snake's current body (non-boosting snakes on a half-step) + every Ready snake's body (**Guess (prep) G2**). A moving snake dies if its new head is off-board, on a wall, or on a tile with occupancy > 1.
4. Apply deaths: each dead snake keeps its pre-step `s.body`, loses 1 life, clears `turns = {}`, `grow = 0`, `boosting = false`, `shedTimer = World.TUNING.boostShedSeconds`, and becomes `dying` (1.5 s) or `over` (0 lives). Emit `died` (and `over`).
5. Apply survivors: each surviving moving snake commits its provisional body, heading, and `grow`. If it ate an apple: `score += applePoints * map`, remove apple, emit `ate`. If it ate `w.golden`: `score += goldenPoints * map`, `w.golden = nil`, emit `ateGolden`. If it ate a crumb: `score += crumbPoints` (5 flat), remove that crumb, emit `ateCrumb`.
6. Drop death crumbs (only if `w.shared == true`): for each snake that died this tick (in id order), iterate `i = 1, #s.body, 2` (odd indices of its pre-step body); for each tile that is on-board and free of walls, Ready/alive snakes, apples, `w.golden`, and existing `w.crumbs`, append it to `w.crumbs`. If at least one crumb was dropped, emit `{ kind = "crumbs", id = s.id, tiles = dropped }` (**Guess (prep) G3**, **G5**).
7. If any apple was eaten this tick, top up `w.apples` to `#w.snakes + 1` on free tiles (excluding walls, Ready/alive snakes, apples, `w.golden`, and `w.crumbs`) (**Guess (prep) G5**).
8. Check `#s.body >= Maps.goal(w.map)` unconditionally across every surviving snake (matching f0211); if any reached it, award `clearBonus * map`, set `w.phase = "clear"`, `w.timer = World.TUNING.clearSeconds`, emit `cleared`.

### Server authority and the split
Everything in `f0214` is pure Logic in `snake/src/shared/Logic/World`. The server (f0213's session / f0212's arena) receives a client's boost hold/release intent and passes it to `World.boost(w, id, on)`, which rejects invalid types, wrong phases, and dying/over snakes. The server advances `World` on its own clock and `Rng.draw`; clients never decide boost speed, shedding, golden apple spawns, or crumbs—they only render `w.golden`, `w.crumbs`, and snake bodies/events. No Roblox adapters are added in this issue.

### Edge cases
- **Empty / invalid input**: `World.boost` with a non-boolean `on`, unknown `id`, `dying`/`over` snake, or `clear`/`over` phase returns `false` and mutates nothing.
- **Bounds & minimum length**: a boosting snake at length 4 never sheds below 4; a snake shedding from 5 to 4 resets `shedTimer = 0.5` and stops shedding until it grows above 4 again.
- **Simultaneous events**:
  - Shed timer and step at the exact same instant (`t = 0.5 s`): shed runs first (popping tail and dropping crumb), then the step runs.
  - Golden apple expiry and spawn at the same instant (if hand-configured): expiry runs before spawn.
  - Two snakes colliding head-on on a golden apple or crumb: both die, neither eats it, food stays intact.
  - Snake dying while another snake moves onto the dying snake's old tail tile: survivor's new body is committed in step 5 before death crumbs drop in step 6, so no crumb drops under the survivor.
  - Two snakes dying on overlapping or adjacent tiles on the same tick: each dropped crumb marks its tile occupied, so no duplicate crumbs are created.
  - Snake eating an apple on the same tick another snake dies: eaten apple is removed in step 5, death crumbs drop in step 6, and replacement apples top up in step 7 avoiding the new crumbs.
- **Full board**: when `freeCount == 0` at the 20 s golden apple mark, no golden apple spawns, `draw` is not called (never `draw(0)`), and `goldenTimer` still resets to 20 s.
- **Respawn**: `World.safest` matches f0211's exact rules; respawn placement removes any crumb or golden apple under the 4 newly placed body tiles without scoring or growing.
- **Frame-rate independence**: all timers (`untilStep`, `shedTimer`, `goldenTimer`, `golden.timer`) participate in `World.advance`'s exact instant-slicing loop with `1e-9` s tolerance, so 1/60 s, 1/30 s, 0.25 s, and ragged cuts produce the exact same game.

### Scalability
- Half-step clock doubles the number of ticks per second (up to 14 ticks/s at speed cap 7), but on a half-step when no snake is boosting the tick does O(N) scan with zero body allocations and no occupancy table. When snakes boost, per-tick cost remains O(S + N + W + C) where C is the crumb count (bounded by `side²`, typically small since crumbs are eaten and cleared each map).
- Golden apple placement is O(side²), running at most once every 20 s (~0.2–0.37 ms at side 79, same algorithm as f0211's E7 apple placement).
- Replication (f0213/f0212): adds at most one `GoldenApple?` (3 numbers), a `crumbs` tile list (deltas via `shed`, `crumbs`, `ateCrumb` events), and one `boosting` boolean per snake.

### Maintainability
- All constants live in `World.TUNING` (`boostMinLength`, `boostShedSeconds`, `goldenIntervalSeconds`, `goldenLifeSeconds`, `goldenGrow`, `goldenPoints`, `crumbGrow`, `crumbPoints`); no magic numbers in logic.
- Food placement shares one free-tile helper (`freeTile(w, draw)`) used by both `apples` and `golden` so disjointness is enforced in one place.
- Future food types (if any) plug into the same food-lookup table in step 2 and the same free-tile exclusion set.

### Usability
- **Player feel**: boost gives an immediate 2× speed burst for cutting opponents off, with a clear risk/reward (shedding 1 segment every 0.5 s into crumbs behind you, never dropping below 4); golden apples create a 6 s race every 20 s worth 5× a normal apple and +3 length; death crumbs reward aggressive play in shared arenas without altering solo progression.
- **Developer API**: one new function (`World.boost(w, id, on): boolean`) mirroring `World.turn`, plain-data fields on `World` and `Snake`, and typed events for f0213's view/HUD/SFX.
