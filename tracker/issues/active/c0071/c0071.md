# c0071: Up to five review rounds, prefer three

## Problem
Ticket-prep stops after 3 design review rounds (`.claude/skills/ticket-prep/SKILL.md` step 5.4) and hands the open questions to the user. execute-raft caps the code review at 3 rounds too (Phase 4 step 7), and any high finding in `review-003.md` blocks the merge. Sometimes a raft is still converging at round 3: the reviewer finds real, fixable problems, and the fixes bring smaller ones. R0025 (2026-10-03) had high findings in rounds 1 and 2 of its design review. Stopping at round 3 hands the user questions that an agent could settle with another round.

The user wants, for **both** design and code reviews (their decision, 2026-10-03):
- up to **five** rounds when they are needed;
- **three** still preferred: stop at round 3 (or earlier) when nothing major came up, or when answering the latest feedback took only minor changes;
- rounds 4 and 5 only when something major remains.

## Non-goals
- What a round is, or how reviewers are launched (still one clean Opus reviewer per round).
- Removing the cap. Five is the hard maximum. After it, design questions go to the user and a high finding in code review blocks the merge, as round 3 does today.
- `tracker.sh` code. `done` checks only the latest round, so nothing there counts rounds (verified: `reviewProblem` reads the highest `review-NNN.md`).
- Stream reviews (at most two, review skill, Stream mode).

## Solution
Rewrite the round rules in ticket-prep step 5.4 and execute-raft Phase 4 (steps 4 and 7), and the tracker skill's Merging condition 1, around one shared stop rule. Define "major" and "minor" concretely, so an agent applies the rule without judgment calls.

## Design
**The rule** (the same text in both skills, adapted to design or code):
- **Rounds 1–2**: as today. A new round follows whenever the existing triggers hold:
  - design: a high finding was fixed, or the design changed materially;
  - code: execute-raft step 6's five triggers.
- **After round 3, 4**: run the next round only when the round just finished was **major**, meaning one of these:
  - it had a high finding, or (code) a Judged criterion ruled fail;
  - or answering it was more than minor. Minor means the answers changed only wording, labels, comments or single assertions, and added no rule, interface, state, spec case, Rules and mutants row or public API change. In code review, the fixes must also stay under about 15% of the PR's lines (step 6's threshold).

  Otherwise stop: that round is the final one.
- **Ending the design review** (ticket-prep step 5.5, rewritten): set `design: ready` when the final round has `Verdict: approve`, or when every finding in it is answered `fixed` or `refuted`. A final round before round 5 never holds a high (a high makes the round major). Round 5 with an open high, or an unanswered finding, goes to the user. Step 5.5 keeps `status <R-id> prepared` (RC7 pins it).
- **Round 5 is final.** Design: bring the open questions to the user. Code: a high finding or a failed Judged criterion in `review-005.md` blocks the merge and engages the human, as `review-003.md` does today.
- **Deferral** (code): a medium or low finding left in the **final** round may be deferred to a backlog issue, as now. "Round 3" becomes "the final round" in step 4's deferral bullet.

**Texts to change**
- `ticket-prep` step 5.4: "Stop after 3 rounds…" becomes the rule above.

  - step 4's deferral bullet says "left after the final round";
  - step 7's other round-3 texts: "answer it in `refute-003.md`" becomes "in the final round's `refute-NNN.md`"; "(Rounds 1-2 need no such rule…)" becomes "(Rounds before the final one need no such rule…)"; and "Medium and low findings in `review-003.md` do not block" becomes "…in the final round's `review-NNN.md`…".
- `ticket-prep` step 5.5: the ending rule above.
