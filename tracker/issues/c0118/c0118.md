# c0118: Board per project with review graphs

## Problem
The tracker board is one page of every raft and issue. It cannot show two games without mixing them. It has no picture of the pipeline, and no graph of how many design reviews and code reviews a week of landed rafts took, or which model spent the tokens on those reviews.

The new board lists every project in `projects.toml` as a tab on the left, `z-nile` included. One project is selected. Its columns are ticket, design, raft, ready, run, and done. Each issue is a card. A raft is a band behind its members. Done cards stay 24 hours, adjustable from 1 hour to 30 days. Click a card for the worktree, the PR, the checks, the model, and the spend. The only actions are stop, retry, and open. Two charts use their own range, 90 days by default, open to all history: review rounds by week, and tokens by model for `design-review` and `code-review`.

## Non-goals
- Replacing `tracker.sh board` until this page covers the same navigation.
- Editing the workflow from the canvas. The page reads the tracker, worktrees, and GitHub.
- Starting a model. Stop, retry, and open call the dispatcher from c0117.

## Solution
- Draw the page from that project's tracker documents, `git worktree list`, and `gh`.
- Count `design-review-NNN.md` and `review-NNN.md` on rafts that landed that week.
- Split spend tokens by the model id c0117 records. A row with no model is the unrecorded line.
