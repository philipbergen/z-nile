# c0690: Narrow play again specs

## Problem
R0150's f0300 gave game over a PLAY AGAIN prompt, and its acceptance specs were promoted into `tests/acceptance/f0300/` when R0150 was done (2026-10-02). They pin that behaviour exactly: the `playAgain` field and the `PLAY AGAIN (Enter)` button of `Flow.overlay`, the `PlayAgain` remote and the server handler that calls `Session.playAgain`, `FlowView` firing that remote, and the whole-run flow ending in a new run. f0710 deliberately replaces it (results, then a tap, then a teleport to the lobby). Promoted specs are never edited during execution, so a chore has to narrow them first, or f0710 cannot land.

## Non-goals
- Any behaviour change: this chore only removes or narrows assertions.
- The new game-over flow itself (f0710).

## Solution
_Draft ideas; design happens in ticket-prep._
- Overrides (to confirm against the promoted files in the prep): `tests/acceptance/f0300/overlay.spec.luau`, `adapter.spec.luau`, `place.spec.luau`, `presentation-run.spec.luau`, and the lines of `tests/runtime/structure.luau` that assert the `PlayAgain` remote.
- Prefer narrowing (keep what stays true after f0710: the phases, the fade, the flash, the results text) over deleting; if a spec must go, also drop its line from f0300's `check.sh`.
- Ready to prep now: R0150 is done and the promoted specs exist.
