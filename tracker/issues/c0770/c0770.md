# c0770: Review rounds up to five

## Problem
Ticket-prep stops after 3 design review rounds (`.claude/skills/ticket-prep/SKILL.md`, step 5.4) and then takes the open questions to the user. Sometimes a raft is still converging at round 3: the reviewer finds real, fixable problems and the fixes keep introducing smaller ones. R0250 (2026-10-03) went three rounds with high findings in each of the first two. Stopping there hands the user questions an agent could have settled with another round.

The user wants:
- up to **five** rounds when they're needed;
- **three** still preferred. Stop at round 3 (or earlier) when nothing major came up, or when addressing the latest feedback took only minor changes.
- rounds 4 and 5 only when a round still raised something major, or its fixes changed the design materially.

## Non-goals
- Changing what a round is, or how reviewers are launched (still a clean Opus reviewer per round).
- Removing the cap. Five is the hard maximum, after which the open questions go to the user as today.

## Solution
- Reword ticket-prep step 5.4. Prefer to stop by round 3. Continue to round 4 or 5 only if the latest round had a high finding, or its answers changed the design materially (new rules, state, interfaces or specs, not wording or one-line fixes). Stop after round 5 and bring the open questions to the user.
- Define "minor" so an agent can apply it: for example, the fixes touch only wording, labels or test assertions, and add no new rule, interface, state or Rules and mutants row.
- Open question for prep: should execute-raft's code-review cap (Phase 4 step 7, "Cap: 3 rounds", with its `review-003.md` merge-blocking rule) follow the same 3-preferred/5-max rule? The request was about review cycles in general. The R0250 trigger was a design review.
- Check for promoted specs or done `check.sh` files that pin "3 rounds" or `review-003` wording before changing it (the override check).
