# c0840: Review R0210's unreviewed fixes

## Problem
R0210's round-6 fixes and its post-`done` fix were merged without any review, because the user chose to land after round 6:
- writers renaming into place (`sh.writeReplacing`, `check.sh`'s `build_place` and globalTypes download, `deploy-beta`, the sentinel);
- the post-run unlink of top-level `.cache` symlinks and the outer-sandbox symlink probe;
- the watchdog in the runner's `REAPER` launcher;
- the escape-pattern change in `profile`;
- the gate spec spelling the `gh-none` path in two parts.

Every earlier round found new problems in the previous round's fixes, so these are likely to have some too.

## Non-goals
- New hardening beyond what these fixes claim (c0810, c0820, c0830).

## Solution
- A clean adversarial review of exactly these changes, with the review skill's second-order step: for each fix, what new behaviour it adds and what that could break or open.
- Each finding filed or fixed under this chore.
