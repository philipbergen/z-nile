# f0110: Rail movement

## Problem
The player has to move through the maze as the game design describes: rail movement with buffered turns, stopping at walls and wrapping through tunnels. It must be deterministic and frame-rate independent, so that movement, tile entry (for pellets, raft c) and collisions (raft d) can be proven headless in CI.

## Non-goals
- Look and camera (f0130). Look never changes the heading.
- Per-level speed tables (raft c): speed is a config value, and a2 uses one.
- Ghost movement (raft d). It can reuse `Maze` but not this module's buffered-turn rules.
- Collisions and pellet eating (rafts c and d consume `Entered`).

## Solution
A pure `Movement` feature that steps one player's rail state by one tick from a list of intents. Positions are **integer sub-tile units**, so every tick lands on exact values and specs can pin ticks precisely. Turns are buffered and taken at tile centres. `Back` is immediate. Corners are classic: stop unless a turn is buffered, as the user decided on 2026-09-29. Tunnels wrap. The step reports `Entered`, `Stopped` and `Wrapped` events.

## Design

### Decisions made in prep (quoted in R0040's Theme)
- **Speed**: level 1 is **7.5 tiles/s**, the arcade's 80% of full speed. That's `speed = 30` units per tick at 30 Hz with `UNITS = 120` per tile, so exactly 4 ticks per tile.
- **Turn buffer**: **24 ticks (0.8 s)**. At 7.5 tiles/s that's 6 tiles, about the spacing of classic junctions: long enough to press early for the junction you see. (Round 1 claimed a stale press "rarely fires at the one after". design-review-002 F3 measured 35% of one-tick-late presses diverting at a later junction, which is why cornering grace exists.)
- **Re-pressing** a Left/Right, even the same one, replaces the buffered turn and **restarts** its window (design-review-002 F2).
- **Cornering grace** (decided by the user, 2026-09-29): a Left/Right arriving in the step right after the player moved **straight through** a tile centre, where that exit exists at that centre, is taken there. The player cuts the corner, keeping the distance already travelled past the centre: the heading turns, `progress` is unchanged, and `tile` is unchanged. The press is used up, not buffered, so it never fires at a later junction.
  - It covers the one-tick display lag (f0130 renders a tick behind), like the arcade's cornering.
  - It applies only while `progress * 2 < UNITS`, so the occupied tile never changes by it, and only within one step of leaving the centre by movement.
  - **Only after going straight** (user, 2026-09-29, design-review-003 F2): not after a turn or `Back` taken at that centre, and not after starting from a stop or the spawn, so a double-tapped Right that straddles a centre can't spin the player back the way it came.
- **Missed turns** (user, 2026-09-29, design-review-003 F1, design-review-004 F1): a Left/Right that isn't a grace turn, and isn't taken at the centre the player is on (step 3), is **discarded** if the player is nearer the nearest centre behind it with that exit, among those passed straight since the last turn, `Back` or stop, than the next centre ahead with that exit (`prevExitDistance < nextExitDistance`; a tie goes to the one ahead; none behind, or none ahead before the corridor ends, counts as infinitely far). Otherwise it's buffered as usual. The passed junction can be any number of centres back: a press 4 or 5 ticks late, on or after a centre without that exit, is still missed (design-review-005 F1). So a late press never turns the player into a later corridor, and an early press for the next junction still works.
  - A discarded press also clears any older buffered turn: the latest press replaces an earlier one (AC3), even when it's dropped.
  - The position jumps diagonally by at most one step's distance. The client interpolates across it as a short corner cut, so no `jumpTick`.
- **Classic corners** (user): with no turn buffered, the player stops at a corner.
- **Wall stop** means the centre of the last walkable tile. The player is one tile wide, so its front edge meets the wall's edge there. That's how this raft reads the game design's "stopping at the tile edge". `game-design.md` records the reading.
- **Starting heading**: W, as in the arcade. `moving` starts true if the W exit exists.
- **Stopped player**: a buffered `Left`/`Right` whose exit exists applies in the same step and restarts movement. `Forward` does nothing to a stopped player: the only stops are where forward is blocked.
- **`Back`** is immediate and **clears** the buffered turn. A stale turn from before a reversal never fires behind the player.
- **`Back` is a literal reverse of the current heading** (arcade rule, decided by the user 2026-09-29 after design-review-001 F2). Right after a turn at a corner, `Back` faces the wall the player was about to leave, so the player stops facing it. At a T-junction just turned into, it sends the player down the third corridor. It never "retraces" to the arrival direction.
- **`Forward`** clears the buffered turn, so the next junction is passed straight through.
- **Door and house** tiles are impassable for the player, through `Maze.passable(…, "player")`.

### `src/shared/Logic/Movement/init.luau`
```lua
local Maze = require("./Maze")   -- inside init.luau, "./X" is a sibling feature (architecture rule 5)

export type Intent = "Left" | "Right" | "Back" | "Forward"
export type Config = {
	speed: number,            -- integer sub-tile units per tick, 1..UNITS
	turnBufferTicks: number,  -- integer >= 1: steps a buffered Left/Right stays pending, counting the step it arrives in
}
export type State = {         -- public fields are read-only for callers; only step mutates them
	tile: Maze.Tile,          -- the tile whose centre the player is on or last passed
	heading: Maze.Heading,
	progress: number,         -- integer units 0..UNITS-1 from tile's centre toward neighbor(tile, heading)
	moving: boolean,
	buffered: Intent?,        -- "Left" | "Right" | nil
	bufferLeft: number,       -- steps the buffered turn remains pending (0 when none)
	leftCentre: boolean,      -- the last step moved the player off tile's centre (cornering grace, AC9)
	run: number,              -- centres passed straight since the last turn, Back or stop, tile's included; capped at width + height (AC9)
}
export type Event =
	  { kind: "Entered", x: number, y: number }  -- the occupied tile changed; x, y is the new one
	| { kind: "Stopped", x: number, y: number }  -- moving went true -> false this step; x, y is the tile
	| { kind: "Wrapped" }                        -- position() jumped by a maze width/height this step

Movement.UNITS = 120
Movement.DEFAULT_CONFIG: Config               -- frozen { speed = 30, turnBufferTicks = 24 }
Movement.INTENTS: { Intent }                  -- frozen { "Left", "Right", "Back", "Forward" }

function Movement.validateConfig(c: Config)   -- errors naming "speed" or "turnBufferTicks"
function Movement.isIntent(v: any): boolean   -- exactly the four strings
function Movement.spawn(m: Maze.Maze, heading: Maze.Heading?): State   -- at m.spawn, progress 0, heading or "W"
function Movement.step(m: Maze.Maze, s: State, intents: { Intent }, c: Config): { Event }  -- one tick; mutates s
function Movement.position(s: State): (number, number)   -- tile + delta(heading) * progress / UNITS
function Movement.occupied(m: Maze.Maze, s: State): Maze.Tile  -- tile if progress*2 < UNITS, else neighbor(tile, heading)
```
- `spawn` copies `m.spawn` into a fresh table, because `State.tile` is mutated and the maze is frozen. It starts `buffered = nil`, `bufferLeft = 0`, `leftCentre = false` and `run = 0`: leaving the spawn is not passing it straight.
- `step` assumes a valid config. `Session.new` calls `validateConfig` once (f0120). Specs call `step` directly with valid configs.
- **`position`** is continuous. Mid-tunnel it can lie up to 1 tile outside `0..width-1` or `0..height-1`, because `tile` is on one side of the seam and `progress` points across it. The client uses `Wrapped` to snap (f0130), and f0120 records the tick.

### `step(m, s, intents, c)`: exact order
Let `U = UNITS`. "Ahead(h)" means `Maze.passable(m, (Maze.neighbor(m, s.tile, h)), "player")`. `turn(h, i)` is `Maze.left(h)` for Left and `Maze.right(h)` for Right.
1. **Record** `wasMoving = s.moving` and `before = occupied(m, s)`, and set `wrapped = false`.
2. **Apply intents in order**:
   - `Back`:
     - if `progress > 0`: `tile, w = neighbor(tile, heading)`, `progress = U - progress`, `heading = opposite(heading)`, `moving = true`, and `wrapped = wrapped or w`. The position is unchanged, apart from a seam jump.
     - else: `heading = opposite(heading)` and `moving = Ahead(heading)`.
     - In both cases, clear the buffer (`buffered = nil`, `bufferLeft = 0`) and set `run = 0`. Mid-tile, also set `leftCentre = false`.
   - `Left` / `Right`: let `h = turn(heading, intent)`.
     - Let `exitHere = run > 0 and progress > 0 and Ahead(h)` (`tile`, the centre just passed straight through, has that exit).
     - **Grace**: if `exitHere and leftCentre and progress * 2 < U`, then `heading = h`, `leftCentre = false`, `run = 0`, and clear the buffer. `tile` and `progress` are unchanged: the corner cut.
     - **Missed**: else if `not (progress == 0 and Ahead(h)) and prevExitDistance(h) < nextExitDistance(h)`, clear the buffer and drop the press. `prevExitDistance(h)` walks the last `run` centres back from `tile` (`tile` first, then along `opposite(heading)`), and returns `j·U + progress` for the first one, j tiles back, where `h` is passable. It returns ∞ if none of them has it (so ∞ when `run = 0`). `nextExitDistance(h)` walks centres ahead along `heading` from `tile` (at most width + height of them). It returns `k·U − progress` for the first one, k tiles on, where `h` is passable. It returns ∞ if a non-walkable tile comes first.
     - Otherwise `buffered = intent` and `bufferLeft = c.turnBufferTicks`. It replaces any earlier one and restarts the window, even for the same turn.
   - `Forward`: clear the buffer.
3. **At a centre** (`progress == 0`), **decide**. This runs whether the player is stopped or moving: a turn arriving on the tick the player sits moving on a junction centre is taken here, and nowhere else (design-review-001 F1).
   - if `buffered` and `Ahead(turn(heading, buffered))`: `heading = turn(…)`, `moving = true`, `run = 0`, and clear the buffer;
   - else if `moving` and not `Ahead(heading)`: `moving = false` and `run = 0`.
4. **Advance**: if `moving`, set `remaining = c.speed` and `left = false`. While `remaining > 0` and `moving`:
   - if `progress == 0`, set `left = true` (the player is leaving a centre);
   - `d = min(remaining, U - progress)`, `progress += d`, `remaining -= d`;
   - if `progress == U`: `tile, w = neighbor(tile, heading)`, `progress = 0`, `wrapped = wrapped or w`, `run = min(run + 1, width + height)`, then **decide** as in step 3. A turn or stop taken by the decision sets `run = 0`, as below.
   - The remainder carries past centres, including around a taken turn. On a stop it's discarded, and the player rests exactly on the centre.
   - Afterwards: `leftCentre = left and progress > 0 and moving`.
5. **Age the buffer**: if `buffered`, `bufferLeft -= 1`, and clear it at 0. An intent delivered in step k with window W is therefore pending in steps k … k+W−1.
6. **Events**, at most one of each kind per step, in unspecified order:
   - `Wrapped` if `wrapped`;
   - `Stopped{x, y = tile}` if `wasMoving and not moving`;
   - `Entered{x, y}` if `occupied(m, s)` differs from `before`.
   - Since `speed ≤ U`, the occupied tile changes at most once per step.

**Worked timings** at `speed = 30`, used by the acceptance specs. From a centre, the player is at +0.25 after 1 tick, enters the next tile at tick 2 (+0.5), and reaches its centre at tick 4. A 7-tile run into a wall stops at tick 28.

### Scalability
- **Per step**: O(intents + speed/U), with at most two decisions and a handful of table lookups. A Left/Right press mid-tile also walks at most `run` centres back and width + height ahead: O(width + height) per press, and presses are rate-limited by f0120.
- **Allocations**: one small table per `neighbor` call and one event list per step. A few hundred bytes per player-tick.
- R0040's bench (f0120 AC6) covers 50 players × 1,800 ticks through `Session`, at about 0.9 µs per player-tick in prep.

### Maintainability
- **Data, not branches**:
  - speeds come from `Config`, and raft c adds per-level configs;
  - the turn map is `Maze.left`/`right`;
  - passability comes from `Maze`, so a ghost-only tile type added later needs no change here.
- **Consumers**: `Entered` is the hook for pellets (f0150). `occupied` is the hook for collisions (f0220): "share a tile" is `occupied` equality, and a "swap within a tick" compares the before/after `occupied` of both.

### Usability
- **For the player**:
  - turns are forgiving (a 0.8 s buffer, taken at the first tile where the exit exists);
  - reversing is instant;
  - stopping is predictable: you only stop where there's no forward exit and no turn buffered.
- **For developers**: one `step` function with a tiny state. Integer units make every position exact, so specs compare with `==`.

## Acceptance criteria

### Checked
- **AC1**: The player moves `speed/UNITS` tiles per tick on the centreline, enters a tile at the half-tile boundary with one `Entered` per tile, carries remainders across centres, and stops on the last centre before a wall with one `Stopped` event.
- **AC2**: At a corner the player stops unless a turn into the corner is buffered, in which case it turns without stopping, carrying any remainder.
- **AC3**: A buffered `Left`/`Right` is taken at the first tile centre where that exit exists, relative to the current heading. The latest press replaces an earlier one, same-tick intents apply in order, `Forward` clears the buffer, and a press stays pending for exactly `turnBufferTicks` steps.
- **AC4**: A stopped player turns and restarts in the step a turn arrives if that exit exists. Otherwise it stays put and keeps its heading, and `Forward` doesn't restart it.
- **AC5**: `Back` reverses in the step it arrives, from the current position with no jump, then moves `speed` units the new way in that step (mid-tile, at a centre or from a stop). It clears the buffered turn, and it reverses the current heading literally, even on the step right after a turn.
- **AC6**: The ghost door stops the player. Tunnels wrap horizontally and vertically, and a `Wrapped` event occurs exactly on the ticks where the position jumps, including a reversal on the seam.
- **AC7**: Under seeded random play on the classic maze, the player is always on a centreline between walkable tiles, moves exactly `speed/UNITS` per tick while moving, reports every change of occupied tile once, and runs are deterministic.
- **AC8**: `spawn` places the player at the spawn, facing W by default and moving only if that exit exists, with independent states. `validateConfig` rejects a bad `speed` or `turnBufferTicks`, and `isIntent` accepts exactly the four intents.
- **AC9**: Late turns. A Left/Right arriving one step after the player moved straight through a centre with that exit is taken there, cutting the corner with the distance kept (one step at any speed; never after a turn or `Back` at that centre, or leaving the spawn). A later press for an exit passed straight by, however many centres back since the last turn, `Back` or stop, is discarded while the player is nearer that centre than the next centre ahead with that exit, and buffered otherwise (a tie goes ahead). A grace turn or a discarded press clears any older buffered turn. Without the exit, a press is buffered as usual. A grace turn is used up and never fires again.
