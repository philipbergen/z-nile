## Problem
Ghosts start in the ghost house and leave according to classic rules (pellet counters and timers). After being eaten they return to the house as eyes and come back out.

## Non-goals
- Collision consequences and the points' flow into the score (f0220); drawing the eyes (f0310).
- The bobbing of waiting ghosts (they stand still).

## Solution
House states per ghost: `house` (waiting), `leaving`, `active`, `eyes`. Release rules come from the `Levels` table. The door tile can only be passed by ghosts (Maze already says so), and a hunting ghost never goes through it.

## Design
### Files and API
- `src/shared/Logic/Ghosts/init.luau`: the house, the way out and the way back; the default of `Ghosts.new` becomes `start = "house"`.
- `src/shared/Logic/Ghosts/Classic.luau`: the house data used here: Pinky waits at (13,14), Inky at (12,14), Clyde at (15,14); the door column is 13 (the door tiles are (13,12) and (14,12)) and the door row 12.
- `src/shared/Logic/Levels/init.luau`: `Row.dotLimits: { pinky, inky, clyde }` and `Row.idleSeconds`.
```lua
Row.dotLimits: { pinky: number, inky: number, clyde: number }  -- level 1 {0,30,60}; level 2 {0,0,50}; level 3 on {0,0,0}
Row.idleSeconds: number                                        -- 4 for levels 1 to 4, 3 from level 5
Ghosts.pelletEaten(set): { Event }                             -- the player ate a pellet: GhostReleased{ghost}
Ghosts.eat(set, name): number                                  -- the player ate a blue ghost: it is eyes now; returns 200, 400, 800, 1600 (then 1600) along the chain
Ghosts.reset(set, reason, level)                               -- now also: "respawn" starts the global counter, "level" and "run" clear the counters
-- events from Ghosts.step: GhostReleased{ghost}, GhostLeft{ghost} (active above the door), GhostRevived{ghost} (eyes arrived in the house)
```
- **Release** (Pittman's dossier): the ghosts leave in the order Pinky, Inky, Clyde. A **personal counter** per ghost counts the pellets the player eats *while that ghost is the next in line*; a ghost leaves when its counter reaches the level's limit (a limit of 0 means at the first step, and several limits of 0 release several ghosts at once). After a **death** the house uses one **global counter** instead (starting at 0 on `reset(..., "respawn")`): Pinky leaves at 7 pellets, Inky at 17, Clyde at 32, and with Clyde out (at 32 or earlier) the personal counters resume; the personal counters themselves are kept through a death and cleared by a new level or a new run. An **idle timer** counts ticks since the last pellet (and since the last release): when it reaches `idleSeconds * tickRate` the next ghost in line leaves.
- **The way out** (`status = "leaving"`, `decide` is scripted, no targets): along the row to the door column (`exitX`), then north through the door; on reaching the tile above the door the ghost is `active`, keeps its own blue flag, heads west and `GhostLeft` is emitted. A ghost on the way out moves at the speed `Ghosts.step` gives any ghost in its state: the hunting speed, or the frightened one if it is blue (19 instead of 28 at level 1). **Blue-ness belongs to the ghost** (`frightened`): `power` sets it on every ghost that is not eyes, including those in the house or leaving, who therefore leave blue; `eat` clears it; a ghost that is revived leaves **normal and dangerous** for the rest of that power period (the arcade rule: you can die to a revived ghost during an energizer); the next power pellet turns it blue again; the end of the blue time clears every flag.
- **Eyes** (`Ghosts.eat`): the ghost turns to `eyes`, stops being blue, **loses any pending turn-back** (`reverse` is cleared here and when a ghost is released, so a ghost eaten right after a power pellet does not turn back by itself after it comes out), and `chain` increments (`200 << (chain - 1)`, capped at 1600). Eyes walk with `pickExit` towards the tile above the door, `opts.house = true`; there they turn south, pass the door and go on to the middle row of the house (door row + 2), where they become `leaving` (`GhostRevived`) and take the way out again. A decision on a door or house tile (decided by `Maze.kind`, not by the row number) uses the scripted route. Eyes run at `Levels.ghostSpeed(..., "eyes")` and are untouched by `power`.

### Pure/adapter split and authority
Pure. The server's session calls `pelletEaten` for every pellet and `eat` for a contact (f0220).

### Scalability
No allocation beyond the events; one counter update per pellet, one comparison per tick for the idle timer.

### Maintainability
The release numbers are `Levels` columns; the house geometry is `Classic` data (a second maze brings its own); the route is a few lines of scripted decisions rather than a pathfinder.

### Usability
For the player it is what makes the maze fair: ghosts come out one at a time, eyes are fast and readable as "safe", and idling does not freeze the game (the timer). For the developer: three entry points (`pelletEaten`, `eat`, `reset`).

## Unknowns & experiments
- **E1** How long do eyes take to get home and out again? **Run**: from four far tiles (1,29), (26,1), (6,5), (21,23): 86, 64, 56 and 58 ticks (about 2 to 3 game seconds); the spec allows 700.
- **E2** Does the route stay on legal tiles? **Pass**, after a **bug the specs found in the prototype**: the first version used "row at or below the door" to mean "inside the house", which is true for most of the maze; the route is decided by tile kind.
- **E3** What does an idle run look like? **Run**: Pinky leaves at tick 61 (one after the ready pause), Inky at 208 and Clyde at 328 (the idle timer: 120 ticks after the last pellet and the last release).
- **E4** Do ghosts that leave together overlap? **Not an issue**: they come out one after another by distance; classic ghosts may share a tile anyway.
