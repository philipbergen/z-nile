# f0100: Ghost blaster drop from eaten ghosts

## Problem
The player. Eating a ghost while the ghosts are frightened (f0017, f0021) scores 200/400/800/1600 and sends the ghost's eyes home. It gives the player nothing to keep. Each time the player eats a ghost there should be a chance they are given a ghost blaster shot, to spend later on a ghost.

(Revised 2026-10-17: the first capture made the power pellet grant three shots. The user dropped that: the power pellet stays as it is today.)

## Non-goals
- The power pellet itself: its 50 points, the frightened period, the speed boost, the blue look, eat-on-contact and the 200/400/800/1600 chain all stay as they are today.
- The spy hop (f0101), the eight-direction ghost glows (f0102) and the key rebinding (f0104). This issue only needs a key to fire; f0104 binds it (E).
- Pellet counts, pellet points and fruit.

## Solution
_Captured from the request; design happens in ticket-prep._
- Eating a ghost has a chance (default 50%, a tuning constant to set after a Beta playtest) of granting one blaster shot.
- The player can store up to three shots. A drop while holding three is wasted.
- Firing spends one shot. A hit ghost behaves exactly like an eaten one: it scores the next value in the chain, its eyes run home, and it cannot hunt the player until it has respawned.
- Defaults for prep to confirm: the blaster works any time (not only while frightened); a missed shot is spent; the held count is shown on the HUD. Aiming is decided: the shot is two-dimensional along the maze grid, in the way the player is looking, rounded to the nearest of the four headings (see f0104). The shot's range, its speed or whether it hits instantly along the line, whether walls and doors stop it, and what a miss looks like are for ticket-prep.
- The roll must be deterministic under the existing seeded randomness so the server stays authoritative and specs can pin it.
