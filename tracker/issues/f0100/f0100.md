# f0100: Ghost blaster drop from eaten ghosts

## Problem
The player. Eating a ghost while the ghosts are frightened (f0017, f0021) scores 200/400/800/1600 and sends the ghost's eyes home. It gives the player nothing to keep. Each time the player eats a ghost there should be a chance they are given a ghost blaster shot, to spend later on a ghost.

(Revised 2026-10-17: the first capture made the power pellet grant three shots. The user dropped that: the power pellet stays as it is today.)

## Non-goals
- The power pellet itself: its 50 points, the frightened period, the speed boost, the blue look, eat-on-contact and the 200/400/800/1600 chain all stay as they are today for contact eating.
- The spy hop (f0101), the eight-direction ghost glows (f0102) and the key rebinding (f0104). This issue only needs a key to fire; f0104 binds it (E) and sends the shot.
- Pellet counts, pellet points and fruit.
- A shot with travel time, a sound, or a hit effect on the ghost beyond it turning into eyes.
- Firing on a touch screen: f0110 (captured in this prep). Until it lands the bars show on touch too (prep), so the HUD tells the truth; the user may ask to hide them.
- A separate drop stream per player in a shared maze (mazes are solo today): the generator is per member already, seeded from the session seed.

## Solution
_Captured from the request (user, 2026-10-17); the design below follows it._
- Eating a ghost has a chance (default 50%, a tuning constant to set after a Beta playtest) of granting one blaster shot.
- The player can store up to three shots. A drop while holding three is wasted.
- Firing spends one shot. A hit ghost behaves exactly like an eaten one: it scores the next value in the chain, its eyes run home, and it cannot hunt the player until it has respawned.
- Aiming is decided: the shot is two-dimensional along the maze grid, in the way the player is looking, rounded to the nearest of the four headings (f0104).
- The roll is deterministic under the existing seeded randomness so the server stays authoritative and specs can pin it.

## Design
### Decisions made in prep (author's choices; the user may veto any)
- **The blaster works any time** the run is in play, blue ghosts or not. **A missed shot is spent.** The held count is on the HUD.
- **The shot is instant and unlimited in range along its line**: the player's own tile, then every tile the player could walk to in that heading. A wall, a door or the house stops it; a wrapping row (the side tunnel) carries it round once. The first **active** ghost on the line is hit (nearest first; on one tile the first in `Ghosts.NAMES` order). Ghosts in the house, leaving it, or going home as eyes neither are hit nor stop the shot.
- **A hit is an eating**: `Ghosts.eat` (eyes, the chain's next value, and "an eaten ghost comes out of the house dangerous", as today). **The chain also restarts when the power runs out** (today only a power pellet restarts it), so shots after a power period start their own chain at 200 instead of continuing the last one (which could pay 1600 for every shot). Contact eating is unchanged: it only happens while the power runs.
- **Only a contact eat rolls for a drop**; a shot ghost does not, so the blaster cannot feed itself.
- **The roll is made on every contact eat, full or not**, from the session's own generator (`Blaster.new(seed)`, Park–Miller like the ghosts' but with a seed transform of its own, so the drops and the frightened ghosts' wandering do not move together).
- **The shot starts from the tile the player holds at the start of the step** (`Movement.occupied` before the move), so a fire the server takes while the player holds a junction goes up the side corridor even though the player moves on in the same step (design review 001 F2). There is **no grace for latency** (design review 002 F1): what the player sees is one to three steps behind the server, so at about 100 ms round trip a side shot must be pressed about half a tile before the junction's centre as seen; a grace is an open decision for the user (game-design).
- **The seed** is the session seed (the server passes `player.UserId`, unchanged), so a player's n-th contact eat rolls the same in every game (design review 001 F4). This keeps the server adapter untouched; whether to seed each session freshly (one line in `Sessions.luau`, which would also vary the frightened ghosts' wander) is put to the user.
- **Shots outlive a death and a cleared level, and are gone on a new run.** A fire sent during a pause (READY!, dying, level clear, game over) is dropped and spends nothing; it is not kept for when play starts.
- **What the player sees**: one cyan bar per shot after the life icons; a thin cyan beam along the shot's line for 0.15 s (real time) **when the server took the shot** (`Frame.shot`, design review 001 F3), so a fire the server dropped (a pause, none held, a second fire in a tick) shows nothing; a hit ghost turns into eyes in the same frames.
- **The wire**: a fire intent is the string `Blaster.intent(h)` (`"FireN"`, `"FireE"`, `"FireS"`, `"FireW"`) on the existing `Intent` remote. No new remote and no change to the server adapter: `f0025/sessions.spec` runs the real `Sessions.luau` against a fake that has only today's remotes, so a `Fire` remote would hang it (`WaitForChild`).

### Logic: `src/shared/Logic/Blaster/init.luau` (new feature; edges `Blaster -> Maze`, `Session -> Blaster`)
```lua
export type Blaster = { shots: number, rng: number }      -- 0..MAX_SHOTS; the drop roll's Park-Miller state
export type Target = { tile: Maze.Tile, active: boolean } -- one ghost as a shot sees it
Blaster.MAX_SHOTS = 3
Blaster.DROP_PERCENT = 50                                   -- tuning constant (Open decisions)
Blaster.INTENTS = frozen { N = "FireN", E = "FireE", S = "FireS", W = "FireW" }
function Blaster.new(seed: number): Blaster   -- shots 0; rng = (floor(seed) * 48271 + 11) % (2^31 - 2) + 1 (a NaN or infinite seed counts as 0)
function Blaster.drop(b: Blaster): boolean    -- rng = rng * 16807 % (2^31 - 1); drops iff rng % 100 < DROP_PERCENT; gains only below MAX_SHOTS; true iff a shot was gained
function Blaster.spend(b: Blaster): boolean   -- one shot; false and nothing spent at 0
function Blaster.reset(b: Blaster)            -- shots 0, the generator runs on
function Blaster.intent(h: Maze.Heading): string
function Blaster.heading(raw: any): Maze.Heading?   -- exactly one of the four wire strings, else nil
function Blaster.ray(m: Maze.Maze, from: Maze.Tile, h: Maze.Heading): { Maze.Tile }   -- from first; then while Maze.passable(…, "player"); stops before coming back to from; fresh tables
function Blaster.target(m: Maze.Maze, from: Maze.Tile, h: Maze.Heading, targets: { Target }): number?   -- first active target on the ray, lowest index on a tile
```

### Logic: `src/shared/Logic/Session/init.luau` (changed)
- `Member` gains `blaster: Blaster.Blaster` (`Blaster.new(s.seed)` in `join`) and `fire: Maze.Heading?` (the shot asked for this tick).
- `Session.submit`: `Blaster.heading(raw)` or `Movement.isIntent(raw)`, else `"invalid intent"` (so `f0013/intents`' list, `"Jump"` included, is still refused). The per-tick limit counts the pending fire: `#queue + (fire and 1 or 0) >= maxIntentsPerTick` is `"rate limited"`. A second fire in one tick is `"rate limited"` even below the limit. A fire is stored in `member.fire`, never in the movement queue.
- `Member` also gains `shot: Shot?`, `export type Shot = { tick: number, heading: Maze.Heading, from: Maze.Tile }`: the latest shot taken, and the tile it started from (design review 003 F1: the beam is drawn from the server's tile, not the camera's).
- `Session.step`, per player: `fire` is taken out of `member.fire` (cleared) with the queue. After `Game.step`, only if the run was in play at the start of the tick (`moving`), and **before** `Movement.step`: `shoot`. It does nothing (and spends nothing, and leaves `member.shot`) without ghosts or when `Blaster.spend` fails; else it takes `from = Movement.occupied(...)` (before the move), sets `member.shot = { tick = s.tick, heading, from = copy of from }` (the step's new tick), builds `targets` from `Ghosts.occupiedTiles` and `status == "active"`, finds the hit from `from` along the heading, and on a hit calls `Ghosts.eat` and `Game.addScore`. Event `{ kind = "Shot", player, heading, ghost?, points? }` (ghost and points only on a hit) plus `addScore`'s events. `Event` gains `heading: Maze.Heading?`.
- In `stepGhosts`, after each contact eat's `GhostEaten` and score: `if Blaster.drop(member.blaster) then` event `{ kind = "ShotGained", player, ghost }`.
- `teleport` also clears `member.fire`. `Session.playAgain` (accepted) calls `Blaster.reset`. Deaths and levels keep the shots.
- `Frame` gains `shots: number?` (typed optional so older literal frames still type-check), always set by `Session.frame` to `member.blaster.shots`, and `shot: Shot?`, a fresh copy of `member.shot`, its `from` copied too (nil before the first shot). The v1 `Snapshot` and `GhostSnapshot` are unchanged (`f0013/snapshot`, `f0018/frame`, `f0032/frame` pin them).
- The order contract at the top of the file gains the shot (taken before the player moves).

### Adapters (`src/client`)
- `HudView.luau`: a `Shots` frame laid over the `Lives` frame; `HudView.shots(n: number?)` (nil counts as 0) rebuilds one cyan `Frame` per shot when the count changes, and `lay()` places them with `Hud.shotIcons(l, shownLives, shownShots)` (lives changing re-lays them).
- `BlastView.luau` (new): `BlastView.push(frame)` ignores a frame no newer than the last, and when `frame.shot` exists with `frame.shot.tick == frame.tick` (taken in this very step) draws an anchored, non-colliding neon cyan `Part` from the centre of `frame.shot.from` to `View.beamEnd(maze, from.x, from.y, frame.shot.heading)` at eye height − 0.5, removed after 0.15 s (`Debris`). It never reads the camera's pose (which can lag the server's tile by a step under jitter), sends nothing, decides nothing, and does not read `shots`.
- `Main.client.luau`: per frame `HudView.shots(frame.shots)` and `BlastView.push(frame)` after `HudView.show`.
- `src/server/Sessions.luau`: **unchanged**.

### Pure/adapter split and authority
The client sends a heading and nothing else; which ghost is hit, the points, the spend and the drops are all server Logic, from server state. A cheating client can only choose among four headings, which an honest one can too; one fire a tick, inside the existing per-tick limit, so spamming costs nothing.

### Scalability
A shot walks at most `width + height` tiles and checks four ghosts per tile: about 240 comparisons on the classic maze, at most once a tick per player. A drop roll is two multiplications. One extra number per frame (`shots`). The client builds at most three HUD frames and one short-lived part per shot.

### Maintainability
`MAX_SHOTS` and `DROP_PERCENT` are the tuning constants; `Blaster.ray`/`Blaster.target` take any maze and any ghost list, so a new ghost or maze needs nothing here. A new weapon would be another intent family beside `Blaster.INTENTS` and another `shoot`-like step. The chain lives in Ghosts alone.

### Usability
- **Player**: shots are visible beside the lives; a shot is instant along the corridor the player aims down, so it feels like a hitscan blaster, and the beam shows where it went; a miss costs the shot, so it is a choice. Holding three, a fourth drop is lost (as asked).
- **Developer**: `Blaster` is small and pure; a fire intent is just another string on the same remote; `Session.frame(...).shots` is the one place the count is read.

## Acceptance criteria

### Checked
- AC1: The Blaster feature: 50% seeded drops (per seed, deterministic), at most 3 held, spending, the four fire intents, and the shot's line and target.
- AC2: In a Session, a fire intent shares the per-tick limit (one a tick) and is taken before anyone moves, from the tile the player holds before the move: one shot spent, the first active ghost on the line eaten on the chain (blue or not); misses spend; pauses, empty blasters and ghostless sessions do nothing; the chain restarts when the power runs out; the frame names the latest shot taken.
- AC3: Contact eats roll for drops from the session's seeded Blaster; shot ghosts do not; at most 3; shots outlive deaths and levels and are gone on a new run; every frame carries the count.
- AC4: The HUD bars after the lives (`Hud.shotIcons`) and the beam's end (`View.beamEnd`).
- AC5: The adapters: no new remote, the server adapter unchanged, Main feeds `HudView.shots` and `BlastView`, `BlastView` draws only the server's shot of that step and decides and sends nothing.
- AC6: Blaster is a declared, pure, unit-tested Logic feature (`tests/meta/architecture.spec.luau`).

None.
