# f0101: Spy hop: lower walls and a jump

## Problem
The player, in first person, cannot see ghosts behind the maze walls. The radar (R0100, f0033) shows ghosts a short distance around the player and stays as it is. The user wants a second way to find ghosts: slightly lower walls and a jump, so the player can "spy hop" and look over the walls ahead to see where the ghosts are.

Scope decision (user, 2026-10-17; PR #79, "rework ghost-hunt captures"): this issue was first captured as an explored-maze minimap, and then as the sensor; both are dropped, and it is the spy hop: "Lower the walls slightly … Ghosts keep the height the walls had before (10) … A jump raises the camera above the wall tops for a short time … A short cooldown between jumps". The slug is the first capture's.

Scope decision (user, 2026-10-21): "we want to be able to jump to look around, AND to jump over ghosts", merged into this issue rather than a new one. The jump is both the spy-hop look and a way past a ghost: a ghost the player meets while airborne does not catch them. This replaces the Non-goal "Invulnerability: the player can still be caught while jumping" and the prep decision that the jump is client only.

Decided with the user (2026-10-21):
- The player is airborne, and so cannot be caught, only while the eye is above the wall tops: the middle of the jump (about 0.64 s of the 0.8 s), not the whole jump.
- The cooldown between jumps stays 0.5 s.
- Pellets and power pellets passed over while airborne are not eaten; they stay on the board.
- A frightened ghost met while airborne is passed over, not eaten.

## Non-goals
- The radar, which keeps working as it does today (`Awareness.RADAR_RADIUS` 7 tiles).
- The eight-direction ghost glows (f0102), the blaster (f0100), and the key bindings (f0104 binds Space to this jump).
- Air steering, double jumps, or jumping to cross the maze. The jump does not move the player: the rail movement continues underneath.
- A jump on touch: f0110 (captured in R0035's prep).

## Solution
_Captured from the request (user, 2026-10-17); the design below follows it._
- Lower the walls slightly (`View.SCALE.wallHeight` 10 today, `eyeHeight` 4) so a standing eye cannot see over them. Ghosts keep the height the walls had before (10), so they stand above the lowered walls.
- A jump raises the camera above the wall tops for a short time, so ghosts beyond the walls can be seen.
- A short cooldown between jumps (a tuning constant).
- The jump height and duration are pure numbers in Logic (View) and the vertical motion is a camera offset only; collisions, tiles and the rail stay in the grid rules.
- (2026-10-21) Jumping over ghosts: the server must know the player is airborne, so the jump becomes an intent the server validates (cooldown, timing in ticks), and the catch rule skips an airborne player. The camera rise stays client-side and follows the server's airborne window.

## Design
### Decisions made in prep (author's choices; the user may veto any)
- **Walls 9 studs** (from 10: "slightly"), **ghosts 10 tall and 6 wide** (the walls' old height, as asked; 7 today), **eye 4**. Doors are as tall as walls (MazeView already uses `wallHeight` for both).
- Measured on the classic maze (E3, below): **standing**, a ghost the walls would hide shows any sliver of itself over 9-stud walls for 0.08% of the (player tile, ghost tile) pairs within 10 tiles, and never at 9.5; at 8 it would be 3.2%. The design accepts the 0.08% (a one-stud sliver of a ghost's top, at a few grazing spots).
- **The hop**: the eye rises **14 studs** (to 18, almost two tiles up) and lands again in **12 steps** (0.8 s of real time at `Session.STEP_RATE` 15), on a parabola (`RISE · 4u(1−u)`, `u` the share of the hop done). At the top, **85.5%** of the hidden ghosts within 10 tiles show at least their top two studs over the walls (E3; 96% at a rise of 16, 56% at 10). The next hop may start **8 steps** (0.53 s) after one lands: the user's 0.5 s, rounded up to whole steps.
- **Counted in steps, decided by the server** (since the scope decision of 2026-10-21): a hop now decides catches, so it is a Session rule, not a camera effect. It is an intent, `"Hop"` (`Hop.INTENT`), on the existing `Intent` remote. The wire string is not `"Jump"`: the promoted `f0013/intents` requires `Session.submit` to refuse `"Jump"` and `f0012/config` requires `Movement.isIntent("Jump")` to be false, and f0100's `FireN`/`FireE`/… set the precedent of a feature's own intent strings. A hop counts toward the per-tick intent limit and only one is taken a tick, like a shot.
- **Airborne**: the rise is over `Hop.CLEARANCE` = 5 studs, the eye above the 9-stud wall tops (`wallHeight − eyeHeight`, pinned by `heights.spec`), **sampled in the middle of the step**: step N moves the world from tick N − 1 to tick N, and the camera shows it while its shown tick runs over that span, so `airborne(N)` is `rise(N − 0.5) > CLEARANCE` (design review 005 F2). That is the 2nd to the 11th step after take-off: **10 steps, 0.67 s**. The continuous hop is over the walls from 1.19 to 10.81 steps (0.64 s, the user's "about 0.64 s"); the step rule differs from the drawn eye by at most 0.19 of a step (13 ms), always in the player's favour (author's choice; the user may veto). Take-off and the step after it, and the steps from the 12th, are on the ground.
- **While airborne** (the user's answers, 2026-10-21): the contact check is skipped (no catch, and a blue ghost is passed over: no eat, no points, no drop roll), and a tile entered is not eaten (the pellet or power pellet stays; no eating pause). The rail movement, the ghosts and a shot go on as usual. A ghost met on the ground just before or after is a catch as today.
- **When a hop starts**: at the start of the player's play in the step (after the shot, before moving), only while the run is in play (`Game.canMove`), and only when `Hop.canStart` allows. A hop asked in a pause, or too soon, does nothing and is not kept. A teleport (a respawn, a new level, play again) forgets the hop.
- **The camera shows the server's hop**: `Session.Frame.hopped` is the tick the latest hop started; Camera lifts the eye by `Hop.rise(hopped, shown)`, where `shown = View.shownTick(prev, snap, View.alpha(os.clock() - arrived))` is the tick being drawn: the blend of the two latest snapshots, clamped exactly as `View.interpolate` clamps it (design review 005 F1), so on a late snapshot the eye waits with the ghosts instead of running ahead. So the eye is over the walls while the server says the player is airborne, to within the 0.19 of a step above. The cost is the usual one of every intent: up to a step before the hop shows.

### Logic: `src/shared/Logic/Hop/init.luau` (new feature, requires nothing)
```lua
Hop.INTENT = "Hop"
Hop.RISE = 14        -- studs at the top
Hop.TICKS = 12       -- steps from take-off to landing (0.8 s at STEP_RATE 15)
Hop.COOLDOWN = 8     -- steps after landing before the next (0.53 s)
Hop.CLEARANCE = 5    -- studs of rise that put the eye over the wall tops (View.SCALE: 9 - 4)
function Hop.isIntent(raw: any): boolean                  -- raw == Hop.INTENT
-- false for a tick that is not finite; a last that is nil or not finite never blocks; else tick >= last + TICKS + COOLDOWN
function Hop.canStart(last: number?, tick: number): boolean
-- RISE * 4u(1-u) for u = (t - last) / TICKS strictly inside (0, 1), else 0; 0 when last is nil or either is not finite
function Hop.rise(last: number?, t: number): number
function Hop.airborne(last: number?, tick: number): boolean -- rise(last, tick - 0.5) > CLEARANCE: the middle of step `tick`
```
### Logic: `src/shared/Logic/View/init.luau` (changed)
```lua
local SCALE = { tileStuds = 8, wallHeight = 9, eyeHeight = 4, fieldOfView = 80 }   -- wallHeight was 10
View.GHOST = frozen { width = 6, height = 10 }                -- a ghost's body box
-- The tick on the screen between two snapshots: latest.tick when prev is nil, prev.tick >= latest.tick or alpha is
-- NaN; else prev.tick + (latest.tick - prev.tick) * math.clamp(alpha, 0, 1) (View.interpolate's clamp).
function View.shownTick(prev: Session.Snapshot?, latest: Session.Snapshot, alpha: number): number
```
### Logic: `src/shared/Logic/Session/init.luau` (changed)
- `Member` gains `hop: boolean` (asked this tick) and `hopped: number?` (the tick the latest hop started); `Frame` gains `hopped: number?`.
- `submit`: `Hop.isIntent(raw)` is a valid intent; the limit counts `#queue + (fire and 1) + (hop and 1)`; a second hop in a tick is `"rate limited"`.
- `step`, per player: take `hop` with the queue and the fire; after `if not moving then continue` and the shot, `if hop and Hop.canStart(member.hopped, s.tick)` sets `hopped = s.tick` and emits `{ kind = "Hopped", player = id }`; `local airborne = Hop.airborne(member.hopped, s.tick)`; an `Entered` tile is eaten only when not airborne. `stepGhosts` runs every step as today (the ghosts move, leave the house and count down their frightened time exactly as without a hop); only its contact check returns early when `Hop.airborne(member.hopped, s.tick)` (beside the level-clear return).
- `teleport` clears `hop` and `hopped`.
- New edge `Session -> Hop` in `tracker/architecture.md` (and a Hop row).
### Adapters (`src/client`)
- `Camera.luau`: `local hopped: number? = nil`; `function Camera.hopped(tick: number?)` stores it; in the render step `local shown = View.shownTick(prev, snap, View.alpha(os.clock() - arrived))` beside the one `View.interpolate(prev, snap, View.alpha(os.clock() - arrived))` call (`f0054/adapters` pins that call's text), and the one `camera.CFrame` is at `View.SCALE.eyeHeight + Hop.rise(hopped, shown)`. Camera has no jump of its own and sends nothing.
- `Controls.luau`: not touched here. f0104 adds the `Jump` action (Space) and sends `intent:FireServer(Hop.INTENT)` for it; until then nothing sends a hop, and `airborne.spec` drives the server directly.
- `Main.client.luau`: `Camera.hopped(frame.hopped)` after `Camera.push(frame)` for every frame.
- `GhostView.luau`: `BODY = Vector3.new(View.GHOST.width, View.GHOST.height, View.GHOST.width)`; the body's centre is `BODY.Y / 2` as today, so ghosts stand on the floor. The eyes keep their offsets from the body's centre.
- `MazeView.luau`: unchanged (walls and doors are `s.wallHeight` tall already, so they come down with `SCALE`).
- No server adapter, remote or place file changes: `Sessions.luau` passes any intent to `Session.submit` and sends `Session.frame` as it is.

### Pure/adapter split and authority
Every rule (may a hop start, is the player airborne, what an airborne player meets and eats, how high the eye is) is pure Logic, tested in Lune. The server decides the hop; the client only asks for one and shows it. A client cannot fake an airborne window: the server ignores a hop in a pause or in the cooldown, and a hop's window is fixed in steps.

### Scalability
A few comparisons per player per step and two arithmetic expressions a frame. One number more in each frame. Ghosts are 3 studs taller: the same part count.

### Maintainability
The heights live in `View.SCALE` and `View.GHOST`, the hop's in `Hop`; `heights.spec` ties `Hop.CLEARANCE` to the scale, and `sight.spec` re-measures the spy hop with those very numbers, so a change that breaks it fails the gate. The hop's catch and eat rules sit next to the existing contact and eat code in Session, each behind one `Hop.airborne` check.

### Usability
- **Player**: standing, the maze still hides ghosts (a one-stud sliver at a few grazing spots); a hop shows nearly every nearby ghost over the walls for about two thirds of a second, and while over the walls a ghost can't catch the player, so a hop started up to 9 steps (0.6 s) before a head-on meeting, but not in the very last step, carries the player over the ghost (E9; at most once every 1.3 s). Pellets passed over in the air stay, so hopping is not a free way round the maze. The camera keeps its yaw and pitch.
- **Developer**: one small pure feature, one intent, two `Hop.airborne` checks in Session.

## Acceptance criteria

### Checked
- AC1: The hop's timing in steps: 14 studs up and down in 12 steps (0.8 s) on a parabola, then 8 steps (at least 0.5 s) before the next; airborne while the rise in the middle of the step is over the clearance, the 2nd to the 11th step (0.67 s), which is when the eye is over the walls for most of the time the step is shown; the shown tick is the snapshots' blend clamped like `View.interpolate`'s; nothing for times that are not finite.
