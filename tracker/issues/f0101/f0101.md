# f0101: Explored maze minimap

## Problem
The player, in first person, cannot see the corridors they have already walked. They need a minimap of only the places they have been. It shows the map, and it does not show ghosts.

## Non-goals
- Ghosts, other actors, pellets, scores, and the layout of places the player has not visited.
- The ghost blaster (f0100) and the droppable line-of-sight sensor (f0102). The sensor watches one dropped spot; this is a map of where the player has been.

## Solution
_Captured from the request; design happens in ticket-prep._
- Remember the maze the player has already visited.
- Draw only that on a minimap.
- Draw the map, never a ghost.
