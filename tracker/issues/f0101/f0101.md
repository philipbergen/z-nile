# f0101: Spy hop: lower walls and a jump

## Problem
The player, in first person, cannot see ghosts behind the maze walls. The radar (R0100, f0033) shows ghosts a short distance around the player and stays as it is. The user wants a second way to find ghosts: slightly lower walls and a jump, so the player can "spy hop" and look over the walls ahead to see where the ghosts are.

(Revised 2026-10-17: this issue was first captured as an explored-maze minimap, and then as the sensor. Both are dropped.)

## Non-goals
- The radar, which keeps working as it does today (`Awareness.RADAR_RADIUS` 7 tiles).
- The eight-direction ghost glows (f0102), the blaster (f0100), and the key bindings (f0104; this issue only needs a jump action, bound to Space).
- Air steering, double jumps, or jumping to cross the maze. The jump is a look, not movement: the rail movement continues underneath.
- Invulnerability: the player can still be caught while jumping.

## Solution
_Captured from the request; design happens in ticket-prep._
- Lower the walls slightly (today `View.SCALE.wallHeight` 10 and `eyeHeight` 4) so a standing eye cannot see over them. Ghosts keep the height the walls had before (10), so they stand above the lowered walls.
- A jump raises the camera above the wall tops for a short time, so ghosts beyond the walls can be seen.
- A short cooldown between jumps (default; a tuning constant).
- The jump height and duration are pure numbers in Logic (View) and the vertical motion is a camera offset only; collisions, tiles and the rail stay in the grid rules.
- Open for prep, as an experiment: wall height versus eye height versus ghost height must keep ghosts hidden while standing and visible at the top of the jump. A ghost whose tip clears the new wall top could show even when standing; the numbers have to rule that out, or the design accepts it. Pellets float at height 3 (`View.PELLET`), so they must stay visible below the lowered walls.
