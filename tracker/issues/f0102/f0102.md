# f0102: Droppable ghost sensor

## Problem
The player. Hitting a ghost scores while the ghosts are frightened and sends that ghost home (f0021). It does not give the player anything they can place. A hit should give a device the player can drop. Once dropped, it shows any ghost that passes that spot, in every direction, and only as far as the maze walls allow a line of sight.

## Non-goals
- What a hit does to the ghost, and the blaster that may deliver it (f0100). Today a hit is eating a frightened ghost on contact. If a hit becomes a blaster shot, ticket-prep sets that dependency; this capture does not.
- The explored minimap (f0101). This is a watcher at one dropped spot, not a map of where the player has been.
- Showing the maze, pellets, or the player. It shows ghosts that pass the spot.
- Sight through walls. The maze's line of sight is the limit, in every direction.

## Solution
_Captured from the request; design happens in ticket-prep._
- A successful hit grants one device the player can drop.
- The player chooses where to drop it.
- While it is placed, it shows ghosts that pass that spot, in all directions, clipped to the maze's line of sight.
- How many can be held, whether a drop is permanent, and what "show" looks like in first person are for ticket-prep.
