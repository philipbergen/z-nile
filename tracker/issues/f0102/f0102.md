# f0102: Eight-direction ghost glows

## Problem
The player gets edge arrows (R0100, f0033) for ghosts that are out of view: larger and brighter the closer the ghost is along the maze. The user wants glows instead: one in each of eight places on the screen (top, bottom, left, right and the four corners), coloured as the ghost is, in the direction of the ghost relative to where the camera looks (not where the player is moving), and glowing more as the ghost gets nearer.

(Revised 2026-10-17: this issue was first captured as a droppable sensor, which is dropped.)

## Non-goals
- The radar, which stays as it is.
- Blaster shots (f0100), the spy hop (f0101), and the key bindings (f0104).
- Changing the maze-distance or bearing maths in `Awareness`; the glows use what it already returns (bearing, distance, brightness).

## Solution
_Captured from the request; design happens in ticket-prep._
- A pure function maps a ghost's bearing (relative to the camera look, positive left) to one of eight screen sectors: top is straight ahead, bottom is behind, left and right are 90 degrees, the corners are the diagonals.
- The view draws one glow per sector at the screen edge, and replaces the edge arrows.
- Defaults for prep to confirm: glows show for every ghost within `maxDistance` (12 tiles along the maze), including ghosts straight ahead (one may be behind a wall around a corner); the nearest ghost in a sector sets its colour and its brightness is the maximum of the sector's ghosts.
- Colour: four classic ghost colours (red, pink, cyan, orange). Today the code has no ghost identity (colour comes from the look: normal, frightened, blinking, eyes). Prep adds the identity to the snapshot, and frightened, blinking and eyes override with today's colours.
- The glows must stay off the HUD and the safe areas (f0029) and must not cover the view ahead.
