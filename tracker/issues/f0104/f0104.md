# f0104: New key controls

## Problem
The key map has to change for the new ghost-hunt tools and to separate looking from turning. Today (`View.KEYS`) W and Up are `Forward`, A and Left turn left, D and Right turn right, S and Down are `Back`, and Space snaps the look to the heading. The arrow keys duplicate WASD.

## Non-goals
- The actions themselves: the blaster (f0100) and the jump (f0101) are implemented there; this issue binds the keys.
- Touch controls (f0027, f0028): they need a jump gesture and a fire control of their own.
- Mouse look, which stays free yaw and pitch.

## Solution
_Captured from the request; design happens in ticket-prep._
- W: `Forward` (take the straight exit, cancel a buffered turn), as today. A: turn left. D: turn right. S: reverse (`Back`). All unchanged.
- Space: jump (f0101). It is no longer snap look to heading.
- E: fire the blaster (f0100). The key is a default the user may change. The shot goes the way the player is looking, in two dimensions along the maze grid: the view's yaw is rounded to the nearest of the four headings (N, E, S, W) and the shot travels down that row or column from the player's tile. Pitch is ignored. With an arrow held, that is the arrow's direction relative to the heading (diagonals round to the nearer heading; an exact tie goes to the arrow's first key, e.g. up for up-and-left). With mouse look, it is the cardinal heading nearest the camera's yaw (default for prep to confirm; the alternative is to refuse to fire unless the view is within a tolerance of a heading). The client sends only the heading to the server; the server checks it is one of the four and resolves the hit.
- Arrow keys: look relative to the direction of travel, while held. Up looks ahead, down behind, left and right to the sides, and two arrows together give the diagonals (up and left is 45 degrees to the left). On release the view springs back to the heading. Pressing an arrow also snaps the view out of any mouse look.
- The snap-look action goes away (the arrow springback replaces it), and the Snap path in `Controls` and `View` is removed or retargeted.
- The promoted key-map spec `tests/acceptance/f0014/keys.spec.luau` pins the nine current keys; c0103 narrows it first. This issue then overrides what is left of it.
