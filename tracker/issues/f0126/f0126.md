# f0126: Risky wall picks grant random boons

## Problem
The player can walk the maze and shoot ghosts, but a wall is a wall for the whole game: nothing opens a way through, and the only rewards are pellets and what eating a ghost already gives. They want to pick a hole in a wall. Picking means standing there for a moment, which is risky (a ghost can catch them while they wait). When the wall comes down, they are rewarded with one of a random set of boons: longer jumps, a faster wall pick, a ghost-eating pill, a longer-lasting ghost-eating pill, or ammo for the ghost blaster.

## Non-goals
- Choosing the boon. It is random.
- The blaster itself (f0100) and the jump itself (f0101). This issue only grants ammo and a longer jump as boons; those two features have to exist first (`depends_on`).
- The green walls while the ghosts are edible (f0109), and a touch control for the pick (f0110's area, if a touch pick is wanted later).
- Picking a hole that is not a wall, or standing still for any other reason.

## Solution
_Captured from the request (user, 2026-10-21); design happens in ticket-prep._
- The player starts a pick on a wall and must stay there until it comes down. Leaving, or being caught, wastes the pick.
- A finished pick removes that wall (a hole) and rolls one boon: longer jumps, a faster next pick, a ghost-eating pill, a longer-lasting ghost-eating pill, or a blaster shot.
- How long the stand takes, which walls can be picked, and the odds of each boon are tuning for prep to ask about.
