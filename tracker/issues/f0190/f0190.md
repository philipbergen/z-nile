# f0190: Ghost navigation

## Problem
Ghosts have to move through the maze the classic way:
- At each tile they choose the exit that minimises the straight-line distance to their target tile.
- They never reverse on their own.
- Each of the four has its own target in chase mode: Blinky, Pinky, Inky and Clyde.

## Non-goals
- The mode schedule (f0200), the house (f0210) and collisions (f0220).

## Solution
_Draft ideas; design happens in ticket-prep._
A pure `Ghosts` feature with a targeting function per personality and classic tie-breaking (up, left, down, right). Seeded sims and property specs: never an illegal U-turn, always a legal tile.
