# f0320: Danger awareness

## Problem
Pillar 3 says the player can always tell where the danger is. In first person with free look, that's false: a ghost behind the player, or around the next corner, is invisible until it's too late. Top-down Pac-Man shows the whole board, so its difficulty tables assume full information. Without an aid, the classic ghost AI will likely feel unfair in first person.

**Open product decision (the user's):** which aid or aids to use. Candidates:
- a **minimap/radar** in a HUD corner, showing the local maze, the player and the ghosts;
- **audio** cues: spatial ghost sounds, louder as they get closer;
- **edge indicators**: arrows on the screen edge pointing to ghosts that are near but out of view;
- **proximity vignette**: the screen edge tints toward a nearby ghost;
- making the game **easier instead**: slower ghosts than the classic tables.

## Non-goals
- Ghost rendering (f0310). Changing ghost AI.

## Solution
_Draft ideas; design happens in ticket-prep, once the user has picked the aid(s)._
- Any aid reads only the replicated snapshot, so it adds no new authority.
- Pure layout/selection logic (which ghosts get indicated, radar projection) lives in a testable module, and the adapter draws it.
- Playtest on Beta before tuning the classic speed tables.
