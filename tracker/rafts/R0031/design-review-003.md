### F2 [medium] An approving design round with nothing to answer has no recording point, so the cost-per-round metric drops the last round of nearly every prep
Where: c0084 "The phases" table, `design-review` row ("after each design round is answered"); Skills, ticket-prep: "§5 step 3 ends: after answering a round, `spend …`"; ticket-prep §5 steps 3 to 5; metrics table 5 (`minutes/round` = design-review minutes ÷ the count of `design-review-NNN.md` files)
Evidence: §5 step 3 is "Answer every finding". A final round with `Verdict: approve` and no findings (a valid result, review skill) has nothing to answer, and the next step the agent follows is step 5 (`design: ready`, `status prepared`). The new sentence sits at the end of step 3 and is conditioned on "after answering", so a literal agent records no row for that round. Its reviewer minutes and tokens, often the largest single run (E2: round 1 of this prep took 90 minutes and 225k tokens), are lost. The same happens when only low findings remain and the round is closed without a refute file. Table 5 still counts that round's file, so `minutes/round` and `tokens/round` are biased low for every raft that ends on an approve. That is the comparison the user asked for (R0025's nine rounds). AC6 only greps §5 for `--phase design-review` and `--tokens`, so either wording passes.
Critique: the tempting fix is "record after answering, or after an approve". That still leaves out a round that hits the round cap with open questions for the user (c0071's final round), and a round answered across two sessions.
Consequences: recording per reviewer run, not per answer, splits a round's cost into a reviewer row and an author row. Both carry `--note design-review-NNN`, and the metric sums by phase, so nothing in table 5 changes. The author's answering minutes then need their own row or a fold-in. A second `spend` per round is cheap, since rows are append-only and one publish each.
Recommendation: make ticket-prep §5 step 2 end "when the reviewer returns, record its run at once: `scripts/tracker.sh spend <R-id> --phase design-review --time <reviewer minutes> --tokens <reviewer tokens> --note design-review-NNN`". Make step 3 end "after answering, record your answering minutes the same way, without `--tokens`". Say "every round, whatever its verdict". Update the phases table row to match. In AC6, require the §5 text to name `design-review-NNN` and the word `every` (or "whatever its verdict") next to `--phase design-review`, so that a "only after answering" wording fails.
