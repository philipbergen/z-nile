# R0035: Ghost hunter tools

## Theme
Four features that turn the player from prey into a hunter, and that only make sense together: a **ghost blaster** whose shots drop from eaten ghosts (f0100), a **spy hop** over slightly lower walls (f0101), **eight-direction ghost glows** in place of the edge arrows (f0102), and the **new desktop keys** that fire, hop and look (f0104). They share `View` (the scale, the keys, the look, the colours), `Camera`, the snapshot's ghosts and the danger HUD, and f0104 binds what f0100 and f0101 add. One type: feature.

Not in this raft: **f0109** (White ghost and green walls, `new`). It is adjacent to f0102 (a ghost's colour), but it waits on a user decision (Inky's new colour is "not chosen yet") and adds a second presentation rule (green walls while the ghosts are edible); including it would block this raft. f0102 creates the one colour table (`View.GHOST_COLORS`) that f0109 will change.

Decisions relied on (quoted from `tracker/game-design.md`, or from the user's capture where game-design does not have it yet; this prep records the captures in game-design's "Ghost hunter tools" section):
- **Desktop keys** (user, 2026-10-17; f0104, R0035): "**W** `Forward` (the straight exit, and it cancels a buffered turn), **A** `Left`, **D** `Right`, **S** `Back`, as before. **Space** jumps (the spy hop, f0101). **E** fires the blaster (f0100). The **arrow keys look** relative to the direction of travel while held (up ahead, down behind, left and right to the sides, two together give the diagonals) and the view springs back to the heading on release; pressing an arrow also snaps the view out of any mouse look. The snap-look key goes away."
- **Look**: "the camera's yaw and pitch are free and never change the heading. On desktop the arrow keys look relative to the heading (see *Desktop keys*); on touch a 'snap look to heading' button recentres it."
- **Authority**: "The **server** simulates every maze. Clients send intents and render the replicated state."
- **Scoring**: "ghosts 200/400/800/1600 within one power period"; "**an eaten ghost comes out of the house dangerous (not blue) for the rest of that power period**".
- **Danger awareness** (2026-10-01): "**A minimap/radar and edge indicators** show where ghosts are in first person. The indicators give a sense of distance, not just direction: bigger and brighter as the ghost gets closer." The glows are those indicators in a new form, asked for by the user (f0102): still bigger and brighter as the ghost nears.
- **Game speed**: "Camera turn smoothing (`View.TURN_SECONDS`) and mouse look stay in real time" (the jump and the arrow look follow).
- **The user's captures (2026-10-17)**: f0100 "Eating a ghost has a chance (default 50%, a tuning constant to set after a Beta playtest) of granting one blaster shot. The player can store up to three shots. A drop while holding three is wasted. Firing spends one shot. A hit ghost behaves exactly like an eaten one … the shot is two-dimensional along the maze grid, in the way the player is looking, rounded to the nearest of the four headings", and "the power pellet stays as it is today"; f0101 "Lower the walls slightly … Ghosts keep the height the walls had before (10) … A jump raises the camera above the wall tops for a short time … A short cooldown between jumps"; f0102 "one [glow] in each of eight places on the screen … coloured as the ghost is, in the direction of the ghost relative to where the camera looks … glowing more as the ghost gets nearer".
The author's choices (shot rules, heights and timings, glow sizes, tie rules, the arrow look's motion) are listed in each issue's Design; the user may veto any. One new open decision: the drop chance (`Blaster.DROP_PERCENT`) after a Beta playtest.

## Risks & rollback
- **R0012 lands first, or second**: its sequestered specs pin, among others, `Controls.start(Camera.snap, Camera.lookBy,` in Main, `function Camera.snap(`, and `View.action(`, `View.KEYS` and `BindActionAtPriority` in Controls; its touch swipe spec checks `View.action("A"/"D"/"S"/"W")`. This raft keeps all of them (`Controls.start(onSnap)` unchanged, the camera's key hooks in a separate `Controls.keys`, `Camera.snap` kept, `"Snap"` kept in `View.Action` for the touch button). R0012's `Camera.lookBy` and this raft's `View.steer` both write the look: whoever merges second routes `lookBy`'s deltas into `View.steer`'s `dx, dy` (a merge, not a redesign). If R0012 lands first, this raft's executor merges `origin/main` before `start` and keeps R0012's promoted specs green; if this raft lands first, R0012's executor does.
- **The drop chance is a guess** (50%, the user's default): one constant, `Blaster.DROP_PERCENT`, an open decision after a Beta playtest.
- **The spy hop's numbers**: E3 measures them on the classic maze only; a new maze re-runs `sight.spec`'s method.
- **Engine behaviour not provable offline** (E6–E8): each has an adapter-only fallback.
- **Size**: the prototype is about 730 changed lines of code and unit specs (`src/` about 700), plus about 1500 lines of acceptance specs and experiments written in this prep.
- **Rollback**: revert the PR. Nothing is persisted (no data store, no replication version change): the frame field `shots` is ignored by an older client, and the walls and keys come back with the code.
