# R0035: Ghost hunter tools

## Theme
Four features that turn the player from prey into a hunter, and that only make sense together: a **ghost blaster** whose shots drop from eaten ghosts (f0100), a **spy hop** over slightly lower walls that also carries the player over a ghost (f0101), **eight-direction ghost glows** in place of the edge arrows (f0102), and the **new desktop keys** that fire, hop and look (f0104). They share `View` (the scale, the keys, the look, the colours), `Camera`, the snapshot's ghosts and the danger HUD, and f0104 binds what f0100 and f0101 add. One type: feature.

Not in this raft: **f0109** (White ghost and green walls, `new`). It is adjacent to f0102 (a ghost's colour), but it waits on a user decision (Inky's new colour is "not chosen yet") and adds a second presentation rule (green walls while the ghosts are edible); including it would block this raft. f0102 creates the one colour table (`View.GHOST_COLORS`) that f0109 will change; Inky's new colour is asked of the user now (design review 001 F9), and if it is answered before execution it is one row in that table.
Also not in this raft: **f0110** (touch fire and hop), captured in this prep (design review 001 F7).

Decisions relied on (quoted from `tracker/game-design.md`, or from the user's capture where game-design does not have it yet; this prep records the captures in game-design's "Ghost hunter tools" section):
- **Desktop keys** (user, 2026-10-17; f0104, R0035): "**W** `Forward` (the straight exit, and it cancels a buffered turn), **A** `Left`, **D** `Right`, **S** `Back`, as before. **Space** jumps (the spy hop, f0101). **E** fires the blaster (f0100). The **arrow keys look** relative to the direction of travel while held (up ahead, down behind, left and right to the sides, two together give the diagonals) and the view springs back to the heading on release; pressing an arrow also snaps the view out of any mouse look. The snap-look key goes away."
- **Look**: "the camera's yaw and pitch are free and never change the heading. On desktop the arrow keys look relative to the heading (see *Desktop keys*); on touch a 'snap look to heading' button recentres it."
- **Authority**: "The **server** simulates every maze. Clients send intents and render the replicated state."
- **Scoring**: "ghosts 200/400/800/1600 within one power period"; "**an eaten ghost comes out of the house dangerous (not blue) for the rest of that power period**".
- **Danger awareness** (2026-10-01): "**A minimap/radar and edge indicators** show where ghosts are in first person. The indicators give a sense of distance, not just direction: bigger and brighter as the ghost gets closer." The glows are those indicators in a new form, asked for by the user (f0102): still bigger and brighter as the ghost nears.
- **Game speed**: "Camera turn smoothing (`View.TURN_SECONDS`) and mouse look stay in real time" (the arrow look follows). The hop does not: since the user's 2026-10-21 scope decision it is a server rule (no ghost catches an airborne player), so it is counted in steps (`Session.STEP_RATE`) and the camera shows the server's hop; this prep records that in game-design's "Game speed" note.
- **The user's captures (2026-10-17)**: f0100 "Eating a ghost has a chance (default 50%, a tuning constant to set after a Beta playtest) of granting one blaster shot. The player can store up to three shots. A drop while holding three is wasted. Firing spends one shot. A hit ghost behaves exactly like an eaten one … the shot is two-dimensional along the maze grid, in the way the player is looking, rounded to the nearest of the four headings", and "the power pellet stays as it is today"; f0101 "Lower the walls slightly … Ghosts keep the height the walls had before (10) … A jump raises the camera above the wall tops for a short time … A short cooldown between jumps", and (2026-10-21) "we want to be able to jump to look around, AND to jump over ghosts", with the user's answers: airborne only while above the wall tops, the cooldown stays 0.5 s, pellets passed over are not eaten, a blue ghost passed over is not eaten; f0102 "one [glow] in each of eight places on the screen … coloured as the ghost is, in the direction of the ghost relative to where the camera looks … glowing more as the ghost gets nearer".
The author's choices (shot rules, heights and timings, glow sizes, tie rules, the arrow look's motion) are listed in each issue's Design; the user may veto any. New open decisions (game-design): the drop chance after a Beta playtest, a shot grace at junctions, the drop seed, the shot bars on touch, and Inky's colour (f0109).

- **New feature `Blaster`** (Logic, f0100): shots, the seeded drop, the fire intents, the shot's line and target. **New edges**: `Blaster -> Maze`, `Session -> Blaster`, declared in `tracker/architecture.md` in this prep (rows for Blaster, and the changed Ghosts, Awareness, Session, Hud and View).
- **New feature `Hop`** (Logic, f0101): the hop's numbers in steps, when one may start, the eye's rise, the airborne window, the hop intent. It requires nothing. **New edge**: `Session -> Hop`, declared in `tracker/architecture.md` in the re-prep (a Hop row; Session's and View's rows changed).
- **Changed Logic**: `Session` (fire intents, `shoot`, drops, `Frame.shots`; the hop intent, the airborne player, `Frame.hopped`), `Ghosts` (the chain restarts when the power runs out), `Hud` (`shotIcons`), `Awareness` (`inView`, sectors, glows, glow boxes), `View` (lower walls, `GHOST`, ghost colours, the beam's end, the new keys, the arrow look, the aim).
- **Adapters**: `Controls`, `Camera`, `Main.client`, `HudView`, `DangerHud`, `GhostView`, and a new `BlastView`. **No server adapter or place file changes**: a shot rides the existing `Intent` remote as the string `Blaster.intent(h)`, and a hop as `Hop.INTENT` (`"Hop"`; `"Jump"` stays invalid, as the promoted `f0013/intents` and `f0012/config` require); `f0025/sessions` runs the real `Sessions.luau` against today's four remotes, and `Session.frame`'s new fields reach the client through it unchanged.
- **Interfaces between the issues**:
  - f0104 → f0100: `View.aim(heading: Maze.Heading, s: View.Steer, a: View.Arrows): Maze.Heading` and `Blaster.intent(h: Maze.Heading): string`. The beam needs nothing from f0104: BlastView draws `Frame.shot`.
  - f0104 → f0101: Controls sends `Hop.INTENT` for the `Jump` action; `Session.Frame.hopped: number?` feeds `Camera.hopped(tick: number?)`, called by Main for every frame.
  - f0102 ↔ f0100/f0101: `View.ghostColor(name: string, look: Session.Look, flash: boolean): View.Rgb` (f0102) is used by `GhostView`, whose body size f0101 changes: both edit GhostView, in order.
  - `Controls.keys(k: { arrows: (View.Arrows) -> (), aim: () -> Maze.Heading? })` (f0104).
  - `Session.Frame.shots: number?` feeds `HudView.shots`; `Session.Frame.shot: { tick: number, heading: Maze.Heading, from: Maze.Tile }?` feeds `BlastView` (f0100).

## Risks & rollback
- **R0012 has landed** (it was prepped in parallel; design review 001 F1 planned for either order). Its specs are promoted now, and the prototype keeps what they pin: `Controls.start(Camera.snap, Camera.lookBy,` in Main; `function Camera.snap(`; in Camera `GetMouseDelta()`, `lookedThisFrame = true`, and `not mouseHeld and not lookedThisFrame` before `View.look(look, delta` before `lookedThisFrame = false` before the first `snap == nil` (so `Camera.aim`'s local is `last`, not `snap`); `View.action(`, `View.KEYS` and `BindActionAtPriority` in Controls; `View.action("A"/"D"/"S"/"W")` in its swipe spec. The mouse line `look = View.look(look, delta.X, delta.Y)` stays verbatim inside R0012's guard, with the arrow look on the lines after it. R0012's `tests/place/touch-adapters.spec.luau` stubs Controls' requires: it gains `Blaster`, `Hop`, `Maze` and `Movement` (f0104's touched modules). The executor merges nothing: the branch starts from a main that has R0012.
- **The hop is server-side now** (scope decision, 2026-10-21): a hop costs one intent on the wire and up to a tick of latency before it shows (the camera shows the server's hop, blended like the ghosts, so what the player sees in the air is what the server decided). An airborne player is not caught, does not eat and passes over a blue ghost; a head-on ghost is crossed when the hop starts 2 to 9 steps before the meeting (E9 in f0101, checked by `airborne.spec`), and the airborne steps are the 2nd to the 11th (0.67 s, sampled mid-step to match what the camera shows; design review 005 F2). The numbers are constants in `Hop`, an open tuning decision after a playtest.
- **f0110 (touch fire and hop)** is not in this raft; when it is designed it sends the same `Hop.INTENT` from a touch button.
- **The drop chance is a guess** (50%, the user's default): one constant, `Blaster.DROP_PERCENT`, an open decision after a Beta playtest.
- **The spy hop's numbers**: E3 measures them on the classic maze only; a new maze re-runs `sight.spec`'s method.
- **Engine behaviour not provable offline** (E6–E8): each has an adapter-only fallback.
- **Size**: the prototype is about 920 changed lines of code, unit and place specs (on a main with R0012; about 730 before the server-side hop), plus about 1500 lines of acceptance specs and experiments written in this prep.
- **Lessons pass (re-prep)**: `ticket-prep/spec-design-correspondence` added `airborne.spec`'s shot-in-the-air case (the Design said a shot goes on); `ticket-prep/restated-rules-move-together` cleared every restatement of the client-only, real-time jump (game-design, architecture.md, R0035, f0104); `ticket-prep/prove-specs-pass-on-a-prototype` ran every member's specs on a prototype built on R0012 and found f0100's `hud.spec` type error; `ticket-prep/never-narrow-captured-scope` checked f0101 against its capture.
- **Design review after the re-prep**: the user reset f0101's scope on 2026-10-21 (jumping over ghosts), so the review count restarts: its round 1 is `design-review-005.md` (rounds 001–004 reviewed the client-only jump).
- **Rollback**: revert the PR. Nothing is persisted (no data store, no replication version change): the frame field `shots` is ignored by an older client, and the walls and keys come back with the code.
