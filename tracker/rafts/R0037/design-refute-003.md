# Refute 003

- F1: fixed: the size cache is gone. The patch places the radar on every render step, before the snapshot guard, with no `if` between `RenderStepped:Connect` and `Hud.radarBox(view.X, view.Y, radarSize)`. The decision and Scalability say "place every frame": a condition on the placement cannot be checked offline, and the cost is one `layout` per frame, the same order as the tables the step already builds. The cache Rules row is replaced by "placed on every render step, with no condition". The placement case asserts there is no `%f[%w]if%f[%W]` between the step and the call. Mutants, all killed on that case: `and` instead of `or`, place only on the first frame, a cache seeded from the current size, and the call after the snapshot guard's `return`. consequences: any later condition between the step and the call, including a nil check on the view, fails this spec and needs a new decision. One `layout` and one box table are allocated every frame.
