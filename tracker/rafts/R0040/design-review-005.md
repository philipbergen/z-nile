### F1 [high] (repeat of review-004 F1, the on-centre half) A late press that arrives while the player sits on the next centre is still buffered and diverts the player at a later junction. At speed 30 that's every 4th tick, including the typical 4-ticks-late press, and grace.spec can't tell this apart from the product rule
Where: f0110 Decisions "Missed turns" ("arriving while the player is **between centres**"); step 2 **Missed** (`else if progress > 0 and prevExitDistance(h) < nextExitDistance(h)`); against AC9 ("discarded while the player is nearer that centre than the next centre ahead with that exit"), game-design.md Movement ("a late press never diverts the player at a later junction"), the R0040 Theme quote, and R0040 Risks ("discarded, never a wrong turn").
Evidence:
- **A concrete case, on grace.spec's own LONG maze** (`{ "##########", "###.##.###", "#P.......#", "##########" }`, heading E, speed 30). On the prototype, `Left` at 11 and at 13 is discarded: E at (6.5, 2) at time 22. `Left` at 12 arrives on the (4,2) centre (`progress == 0`, `run = 3`), one tile past (3,2) and two before (6,2), so the product rule says discard. The `progress > 0` guard sends it to the buffer instead. (4,2) has no north exit, so step 3 keeps it, and it fires at (6,2): heading N, at (6, 1.5) at time 22. Result: the press is discarded one tick earlier and one tick later, but diverts the player in between.
- **How often, on the classic maze.** I swept every (junction passed straight, heading, turn with that exit) case, with a press d = 2..16 ticks later wherever the product rule says discard. I compared each run with the no-press run.
  - Presses between centres: 0 diversions, apart from 24 grace corner cuts at a later centre that has the exit, which are intended.
  - Presses on a centre: 142 of 220 divert. For **d = 4 (133 ms late) it's 78 of 104**, the same count review-004 reported for d = 4. d = 8 gives 36 of 60, d = 12 gives 8 of 16, d = 16 gives 20 of 40.
  - So the round-4 fix closed d = 5, 6, 7, … but left the single most common late-press delay (the player one tile past the junction) exactly as it was.
- **Why the design has it.** Refute-004 kept "at `progress == 0` the current centre's exit belongs to the step-3 decision". That's right when the current centre *has* the exit. The guard, though, drops the missed rule for every on-centre press, including ones where the current centre lacks the exit and only an earlier passed centre has it.
- **The spec can't tell the two apart.** Mutant: change the condition to `(progress > 0 or not Ahead(h)) and prevExitDistance(h) < nextExitDistance(h)`. With `run` counting `tile`, and `tile` lacking the exit, the walk back skips it naturally. The mutant passes all nine f0110 checks and all seven f0120 checks. It discards `Left` at 12 on LONG, and the sweep gives 0 on-centre diversions. So the documented design and the product rule both pass `check.sh`, and an executor following f0110 to the letter ships the hole.
Fix:
- In f0110 step 2, make **Missed**: `else if not (progress == 0 and Ahead(h)) and prevExitDistance(h) < nextExitDistance(h)`. On a centre that has the exit, the press goes to the step-3 decision as now. Otherwise the walk back starts at `tile`, which lacks the exit, and it finds the passed junction. A stopped player, a spawn or a `Back` still gives `run = 0`, so ∞, so the press is buffered.
- Drop "between centres" from the Decisions bullet.
- Add to grace.spec's LONG case: `sim.intent(12, "Left")`, then `sim.expect(21, { x = 6.25, y = 2, heading = "E" })`. It passes on the mutant above and fails the current prototype. I ran both.
- Optionally add an on-centre variant of the tie rule: on PAIR, `Left` at 12 at (4,2), which has the exit, is taken there.
