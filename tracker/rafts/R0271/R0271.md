# R0271: Board graphs

## Theme
A single chore, **c0790**: the tracker board computes statistics about the work and draws them as graphs. The user asked for statistics on 2026-10-03, and on 2026-10-05: "Make a single issue raft that puts the graphs in the board"; asked whether that meant charts or moving c0790 out of R0250, they answered "both". c0790 was prepped inside R0250 through four design-review rounds (R0250's `design-review-00{6..9}.md`); it left R0250 in PR #62 and its design was widened here from tables to charts. Tooling: a chore.

User decisions relied on (quoted in c0790's Problem):
- views of a month by day, a quarter by week and a year by month;
- bucket edges rounded down to Monday, the 1st, the quarter start or Jan 1;
- per type: opened, closed and net, time, and lines (added, removed, net);
- defect % of (defects + features) and chore % of all, by count and by time;
- time in each state.

Prep decisions for the user to adjust: the window starts and the calendar periods (c0790, Usability), and the chart forms (c0790, Charts on the page).

## Whole-raft design
One issue. See c0790 Design:
- `stats.luau` (pure numbers);
- `history.luau` (git log, parsed purely);
- `chart.luau` (pure SVG);
- the board model and page (a Stats section beside the tabs, charts plus a Table toggle);
- `board.yml` full history.

No `src/` change, no `tracker/architecture.md` change.
