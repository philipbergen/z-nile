# Refutations 004: R0100 (design mode)

- F1: fixed, and the first fix was wrong: positions are not wrapped (a ghost heading east runs on from 27.0 to 27.99, the player likewise), tiles are, so `x - player.x + wrap * width` was off by a whole map in the last half tile before the edge and the arrow vanished or pointed backwards for a few ticks every time someone used the tunnel. The offset is now taken along the path and is continuous: `dx = (tile.x - here.x) + wrap * width + frac(ghost.x) - frac(player.x)` with `frac(v) = v - floor(v + 0.5)`; a ghost is *unseen* (and then always indicated) when the 3D positions are on opposite sides of the maze from where the path puts it, `|(ghost.x - player.x) - dx| > width / 2` (this also covers a player standing past the edge on the unwrapped x). Cases: a ghost sweep across the edge (27.0 to 27.9 and -0.1 to -0.6, the arrow behind never goes, the distance moves a tile at a time), a player sweep across the edge eastwards and westwards (ghost ahead through the wrap, arrow ahead), and the exactness of the bearing across the edge (the fractions of both positions count). Mutants "fractions ignored" and "a ghost unseen only when the path crosses the wrap" killed; the raw-offset formula of round 3 fails the sweeps.
- F2: fixed: f0320's API declares `Awareness.Radar`; the raft says f0200 creates `Rng` (f0190 owns `Classic`); the Maintainability text says the indicators and the radar share `bearing` and the wrap test, and the indicators use the path search that `pathDistance` exposes.
