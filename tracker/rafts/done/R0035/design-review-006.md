### F1 [high] A hop that has started, then meets a ghost or the last pellet on a ground step, plays its full arc during the death pause or the level-clear flash
Where: f0101 Design, "When a hop starts" ("A teleport (a respawn, a new level, play again) forgets the hop") and "While airborne"; `Session.step` (`if not moving then continue` before any hop update; `teleport` clears `hopped` only from `Ready` and from `playAgain`); `airborne.spec` "a respawn forgets the hop" (asserts `hopped ~= nil` on the catch step) and "each player has their own hop" (`frame(q).hopped == s.tick` on q's take-off catch); `Game.CLASSIC_CONFIG` (`dyingTicks = 45`, `levelClearTicks = 60`); `Flow.overlay` (dying fade starts at 0; level clear does not fade).
Evidence: run on the prototype. Settle, submit `Hop`, put blinky on the player, step: that step is the take-off (not airborne), `Caught` fires, phase is `dying`, `hopped` stays the take-off tick, and a `Hopped` event was emitted. `Ready` is 45 steps later. Over those steps `Hop.rise` at the snapshot tick is 4.28, then 7.78, 10.50, 12.44, 13.61, 14.00 and back down, so the eye is over the 9-stud walls from the second dying step through the tenth. The dying fade is `0.6 * (45 − timer) / 45`: about 0.03 when the eye first clears the walls and about 0.08 at the top, so the maze stays essentially undimmed. The same shape is head-on d=1 (hop starts, the next step catches, `airborne` is false): the death step's own blend is `rise` 0→4.28 and the next snapshot is already at 7.78. Level clear: with only the pellet on tile 12,23 left, the step after take-off eats it, phase becomes `levelclear` (`timer` 60, no fade, walls flash) and `hopped` stays; the next snapshot's rise is 7.78 and the one after that climbs to 14. A last-life take-off catch reaches game over with `hopped` still set and `rise` already 0, because the arc finished during the dying pause; `playAgain` then clears it. `Ready` clears it too, one pause later than the picture. You cannot die or clear during an airborne step (contacts and eating are both skipped). The steps that leave play are the ground ones, and steps 0 and 1 still have the whole arc ahead. The respawn spec requires `hopped` set on the take-off catch, so a fix that clears it on that frame fails the spec as written.
Critique: the tempting fix is to clear `hopped` only inside `teleport`, or only on the step after death. The take-off catch's own snapshot shows no rise (`rise` is 0 until the shown tick passes the take-off), so clearing on the next snapshot happens to hide that one case. The step-after-take-off catch does not: that snapshot is the blend from `rise` 0 to 4.28, and the snapshot after it is over the walls. Clearing "on Ready" is what the design says today, and it is after the arc.
Consequences: a hop that has already had an airborne step and is caught on the landing (step 12) should keep `hopped`. That snapshot's blend is the descent from 4.28 studs to 0; nil there pops the eye. `Session.kill` (no production caller; tests and `f0065` use it) sets `dying` between steps, so the clear has to run on the non-moving path as well as at the end of a step that itself left play. A same-step `Hopped` event must leave with the field, or the step both announces a hop and publishes `hopped == nil`. A hop that started on an earlier step keeps that earlier event. Shots stay through death (`f0100`). `playAgain` and `Ready` still teleport. An airborne step cannot eat the last pellet or be caught, so this rule does not give up a jump that had carried the player. A `dyingTicks` of 0 still publishes the death step's frame; that frame has to be the one with `hopped` nil.
Recommendation: at the end of each player's step, and at the start of a step that is already out of play, if `hopped ~= nil` and `s.tick < hopped + 2`, set `hopped = nil` and drop a `Hopped` event emitted on this step. A hop that has reached step 2 keeps `hopped` so the landing blend can finish; `teleport` still clears it. Specs in `airborne.spec`: (1) take-off catch, and a catch on the next step: that frame's `hopped` is nil, the take-off catch emits no `Hopped`, and the next 12 steps stay nil while `Hop.rise` of the cancelled tick would have passed `CLEARANCE`; (2) one pellet left on the tile entered the step after take-off: `LevelCleared`, and `hopped` nil through the level-clear pause; (3) a catch on step 12: `hopped` is still the take-off tick on the death frame, and nil once `Ready` fires; (4) last life, take-off catch: `hopped` nil at game over with no `Ready`, and nil after `playAgain`. Change the respawn case so it no longer requires `hopped` set on a take-off catch, and split the two-player case: q's hop starts during p's cooldown while q is not caught (`frame(q).hopped` set, p's unchanged); q's take-off catch then leaves q's `hopped` nil and does not clear p's. Mutant: leave `hopped` set when play stops before step 2 of the hop.
Lesson: testing/seed-every-enumerated-case

### F2 [medium] No spec submits a hop and a fire in the same tick, so dropping the hop or counting the two as one intent passes every member spec
Where: f0101 Design, the hop intent ("counts toward the per-tick intent limit and only one is taken a tick, like a shot") and `Session.step` (shoot, then maybe start the hop, both before moving); `Session.submit` (`pending = #queue + (fire and 1) + (hop and 1)`, `maxIntentsPerTick` 4); Rules and mutants table (no row); `airborne.spec` "a shot taken while airborne" (the fire is several steps after the hop) and "a hop counts toward the per-tick limit" (forwards plus a hop, no fire).
Evidence: run on the prototype, then restored. Replacing the pending sum so a fire and a hop add one between them leaves `airborne.spec` and `shots.spec` green (13 and 18 passed). Replacing the hop start with `if hop and fire == nil and Hop.canStart` leaves `airborne.spec`, `shots.spec` and `hunt.spec` green. The unmutated step does both: `Hop` and `FireW` in one tick emit `Hopped` and `Shot`, the ghost on the line becomes eyes, the take-off step is not airborne, and two forwards plus those two intents refuse a fifth with "rate limited".
Critique: the tempting fix is a comment that the shot is first. A later edit that treats "one special intent" as a single slot, or that skips the hop when a shot was asked, still satisfies every case that submits only one of them. Pressing Space and E in the same step is how the hunt is played.
Consequences: the take-off step is not airborne, so the shot must be judged against `hopped` from before this step's start. Checking `airborne` after assigning `hopped` happens to allow the shot today and will not once someone writes `rise(tick) > 0`. The shot's tile is `Movement.occupied` before the move; the hop does not change it. The fifth intent is what distinguishes "each counts" from "together they count as one".
Recommendation: one `airborne.spec` case. In play, submit two movement intents, `Blaster.intent` of a heading with an active ghost on that line, and `Hop.INTENT`, then a fifth intent. The fifth is "rate limited". The step emits one `Shot` and one `Hopped`, the ghost is eyes, the shot was spent, the player is not caught, and `Hop.airborne` is false on that step. Mutants: pending adds one if either flag is set; the hop start requires `fire == nil`.
