### F4 [high] AC4 passes a poll that never waits while checks are pending, and the loop can still end with 124
Where: Design, Waiting (`dispatch.waitCommand`, "the runner's bound sits above the loop so a timeout is 3, never 124"); prototype dispatch.luau `waitCommand`/`waitPrompt`; dispatch-activities-cli.spec "the wait command exits 0 … and 2 …"; Rules and mutants row "z-wait's poll …".
