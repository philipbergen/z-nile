### F1 [medium] `--merges` requires exactly one survivor, which is stricter than the invariant it states: a lesson merge that keeps every source is red whenever another lesson of the skill was already named beside it
Where: acceptance/sources-kept.luau lines 122-136 (`#survivors == 1`); sources-kept.spec.luau lines 122-135 (the case "two survivors hold all of from's sources" asserts red); c0112.md Existing work (b) ("only when exactly one current lesson of the same skill holds every source"), Rules row "a deleted lesson is kept only when exactly one lesson of its skill holds all its sources" (mutant "any number of survivors accepted").
Evidence: on my copy, `z-nile/.tracker/issues/done/c0116/harvest.md` line 11 is `- design-review-002 F2: zz-rules/retire-keeps-what-readers-count, zz-test/prove-state-folders-on-the-real-tree, zz-test/seed-every-enumerated-case`. I merged `zz-test/prove-state-folders-on-the-real-tree` into `zz-test/no-vacuous-pass`, as c0181's `lesson-merge` does: the id was rewritten in the ledger and the `from` file deleted, every source was kept, and `--check` reported nothing new. `sources-kept.luau --merges <tree before> .` printed `lesson zz-test/prove-state-folders-on-the-real-tree is gone, and 2 lessons of zz-test hold all its sources (exactly one may)` and exited 1. The same merge into `zz-test/seed-every-enumerated-case` passes. Over the tree (a scratch script on `lessons.sources`), 5 of 157 lessons already have a same-skill lesson that holds all their sources: `z-design/fallbacks-name-the-modes-they-cover`, `z-design/intent-owner-owns-every-trigger`, `zz-arch/several-writers-check-the-subject`, `zz-test/feed-the-real-producers-extremes` and `zz-test/prove-state-folders-on-the-real-tree`. Merging any of them into a lesson other than its co-cited one is a false red. The backfill makes this common: it adds about 100 lessons with one or two sources, and a line may name two lessons of one skill. Near-duplicates are named together on one line, and those are the ones c0181 merges. The false red reaches c0112's own merges (b, d: "after c0181 arrived"), every later consolidating branch that follows the zz-rules sentence, and the promoted spec, which pins the false red.
Critique: the file's header says "The invariant is over sources, not lesson ids", but "exactly one" is an extra rule over ids. A second lesson that already holds every source loses nothing. The tempting fix is to exclude lessons that held the sources before the merge, keeping only those that gained them. That rule fails the other way: a merge into the co-cited lesson gains nothing and would then be red.
Consequences: "at least one" accepts a deleted lesson whose findings all still name another lesson of its skill, even when no one merged its text anywhere. Neither mode judges a lesson's text, only its sources, so this is no new hole. The text belongs to review and to c0181's own merge rule.
Recommendation: keep a gone lesson when at least one current lesson of its skill holds every source the base gave it, and name all of them (`merged into <a>[, <b>]`). Zero survivors, or survivors only in another skill, stay a loss. In the spec, turn the two-survivors case green with both named, keep the other-skill and missing-source cases red, and add a case where the only survivor held every source before the merge (green). Make the same change in sources-kept.luau's header, the spec header, (b), and the Rules row (its mutant becomes "zero survivors accepted" and "a survivor of another skill accepted"). Rerun the three mutants.
Lesson: zz-test/refusals-tested-on-both-sides
