### F1 [medium] The `zz-review` exemption covers every lesson of the line, so a design round's finding reaches design with a `when: build` lesson filed under its upstream topic skill
Where: Design decision 2 ("other than the reviewer's own mistake (`erred zz-review`)"); `lessons.check` (`designReview … and erredNow ~= "zz-review"`, applied to every lesson of the entry or note); z-design 5.3 ("only `erred zz-review` is exempt"); zz-review item 10 ("unless its `Erred:` is `zz-review`"); check.spec's exemption cases (each names a `zz-review/` lesson only).
Evidence: Probe on the prototype, an item with a `design-review-001.md` whose F1 carries `Erred: zz-review` and `Upstream: zz-test`, harvested as `- design-review-001 F1 (erred zz-review, upstream zz-test): zz-test/build-only` (`when: build`): 0 problems. The same holds for a waiver note, `- note design-review-001 F1 step lines invalid (erred zz-review, upstream zz-test): zz-test/build-only` on a finding with a typo'd line: 0 problems; and for a phase note with the same group. The exemption exists because a reviewer reads its own skill's lessons whatever their `when:` (zz-review's Lessons: `./zn lessons --skill zz-review --full`, no `--when`), which is why `zz-review/run-the-evidence-you-cite` (`when: build`, from a design review) is harmless. That reason does not hold for a lesson filed under the upstream skill: the next design reviewer and author read other skills only through `./zn lessons --when design`, which never shows a `zz-test` lesson that is `when: build`. So a design reviewer's mistake that a `zz-test` rule should have prevented (a miss of a spec hole: `Erred: zz-review`, `Upstream: zz-test`) gets a lesson the next design round never sees, which is the outcome the folded c0140 line forbids ("a design-phase mistake filed under a topic skill still does").
Critique: The exemption is keyed on the line's `erred`, but what makes it safe is where the lesson is filed. The tempting fix is to drop the exemption altogether, which refuses `zz-review/run-the-evidence-you-cite` (the one design-review `build` lesson on main) and every future reviewer lesson that is about build-time reviews.
Consequences: Keying the exemption on the lesson's skill keeps every existing record valid (the only design-phase `build` lesson on main is filed under `zz-review`), and changes nothing for code and stream reviews (their phase is build). The same rule then covers the entry, the disagreement note, the waiver note and the phase note, which all go through the same loop. The skill text that states the exemption (z-design 5.3, zz-review item 10, zz-rules Items) changes with it, and check.sh AC5's item-10 sentence ("unless its `Erred:` is `zz-review`") is the pinned wording that moves.
Recommendation: In `check`, a lesson answering a design-phase finding is exempt only when it is filed under `zz-review` (the line's `erred`); a lesson filed under the upstream skill of an `erred zz-review` line is `design` or `both`. Say so in z-design 5.3, zz-review item 10 and zz-rules Items ("a lesson filed under `zz-review` for the reviewer's own mistake is exempt"), and move the AC5 sentence. check.spec cases: the entry above, the waiver note above, and the existing `zz-review/build-only` cases still accepted; a row and a mutant (exemption keyed on `erred`), run.
Lesson: zz-arch/guard-no-is-explicit
