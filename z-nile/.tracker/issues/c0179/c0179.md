# c0179: Fixture volume without fseventsd

## Problem
The user (2026-10-31), after a full gate pegged fseventsd at a whole CPU core: "This is not acceptable. Find a better solution. Test has to be thorough and complete but also very fast and efficient."

Measured 2026-10-31: one worktree's `z-nile/.cache` changed 10,711 files in 10 s during a full gate (`find -newer`, so files made and removed inside the window are not even counted); fseventsd ran at about 80-100% of a core and `mds_stores` at about 35%. The z-nile suite is 1265 cases in 183 files, about 1,850 s of spec time and 185-230 s of wall time idle (up to 1,800 s under load); `c0116/claims` and `c0120/finished` take about 175 s each idle and set the floor. c0175's capture ran the full gate three times (about 6 min each) and still lost the race to main.

macOS logs every file change on the main disk in fseventsd and Spotlight indexes it, so the specs' throwaway fixtures under each home's `.cache` cost a CPU core even after c0178 cuts how many files they write.

What should happen: on macOS, spec fixtures live on a volume that fseventsd doesn't log and Spotlight doesn't index, made without admin rights; where it is missing (Linux, CI, a new machine) fixtures fall back to `.cache` as today. Every guard (the jail, the fence, `fixture.check`, the sentinel) follows the fixture root and still fails closed.

Decided with the user (2026-10-31): "Yes, and fewer files": the volume and c0178.

## Non-goals
- Cutting files per fixture: c0178.
- Moving run state (the lock, durations, the board) off `.cache`.

## Solution
- `./zn fixtures-volume`: a sparse APFS disk image (`hdiutil create -type SPARSE -fs APFS`, no admin) attached `-nobrowse` at a fixed per-user path, with `.fseventsd/no_log` and `.metadata_never_index`; run by `setup.sh`, re-attached by `./zn` when missing.
- `fixtures.root()`: `<volume>/<checkout id>/<home>/` when attached, else `<home>/.cache/`; specs take fixture folders from `fixtures.dir("<name>")` instead of a literal `.cache/<name>`, and the gate's unique-literal rule becomes a unique fixture name.
- The jail profile, `fixture.check`, the sentinel `.git` and `cachepaths.ALLOWED` take the fixture root by physical path.
- Experiment first: an FSEvents watcher on the volume sees nothing during a fixture build, and `mds_stores` ignores it; fallback a RAM disk with the same markers.
