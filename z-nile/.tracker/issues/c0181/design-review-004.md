### F4 [medium] The pipe-filter row is still marked full but leaves a clause: a pipeline that is itself a check's status (repeat of design-review-003 F3, new evidence)
Where: E2 row `zz-test/pipe-filter-keeps-the-failure` (F); G1 ("a lesson wrongly marked `full` loses its unchecked half"); JC1.
Evidence: The lesson's check reads "(or a filter after a pipe), does a failure of `cmd` itself still turn the check red?". The lint covers three contexts: `|| true`, `$(…)` and `<(…)`. It misses a pipeline that is itself the status of a check or of a helper. Two examples: `check "ACn: …" bash -c 'git diff --name-only origin/main...HEAD | awk "/x/{f=1} END{exit f}"'`, or a function whose last line is `git log … | sed -n …`. With no `pipefail`, the status is the last stage's, and awk, sed, sort, head, tail, wc and tee exit 0 on empty input. A failing `git` therefore passes the check, which is exactly the lesson's defect, and the lint never sees it. Marked `full`, the lesson leaves every `--when` reading, so this half is never asked again.
Recommendation: There is no full floor, so either option costs nothing on AC10b. (a) Mark the row `part`, with the remaining check being "a pipeline that is the status of a check or helper". (b) Widen the lint to every pipeline in a check.sh whose left side can fail, unless the shell set `pipefail` or the left side prints a captured variable. Option (b) needs `bad` fixtures for the `bash -c '… | awk …'` form and the helper form. Record the re-read in E2 for both F rows: what each clause is, and which check decides it.
