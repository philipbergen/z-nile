# c0190: Capture arcade step two

## Problem
The user, 2026-10-31, asked for an arcade: the new projects `snake`, `invaders`, `asteroids`, a `portal` and the shared `arcade` library, with game designs that c0183 moves into each project's `.tracker/game-design.md`. The work in those projects can only be captured once c0183 has registered them, because `./zn new issue --project <name>` needs the project in origin/main's `z-nile/projects.toml`. The list of issues to capture is c0183's Solution, the arcade block's "Tracker work" section, **Step 2**:
- **arcade:** A1 hiscores with revives and per-game stores; A2 travel; A3 paid play and receipts (one free game per hour per game, 1 R$ play shown as 5 R$ struck through, 1 R$ revive, playtests never charge); A4 Rng, Pad, Touch and Look cores. Each has a pickman "switch to arcade X" chore.
- **portal:** P1 the carousel and cards with the price badge; P2 boards per game with revives; P3 the free-game timer per game; P4 play buttons (free or buy, entitlement, teleport, ARENA, 2 PLAYERS); P5 portal is the start place; Z3 the N-place deploy manifest.
- **snake:** S1 arena, maps and growth; S2 the N-player shared arena; S3 first-person view, minimap and input; S4 boost, golden apples and crumbs; S5 hiscores, revives and the portal return.
- **invaders:** I1 the solo classic rules; I2 two players from opposite ends; I3 the cannon view, radar and input; I4 hiscores, revives and the portal return.
- **asteroids:** T1 the solo classic rules; T2 two players in one field; T3 the cockpit view with the wrap drawn tiled, radar, rear-danger glow and input; T4 hiscores, revives and the portal return.

Nothing else owns these captures once c0183 is done (c0183 design review 001 F5). What should happen: every listed issue is captured in its project through z-ticket, each Problem quoting the user's words and pointing at its section of the project's `game-design.md` (or the arcade's `architecture.md`), with `depends_on` as the list implies (the pickman switch chores on their arcade feature; the per-game scores issues on A1 and A3; P5 on Z3).
