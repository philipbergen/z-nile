# c0229: Tmp trees on fixture volume

## Problem
Agents' and runs' temp trees under .cache/.tmp (c0194) still churn fseventsd and Spotlight: route them onto a volume fseventsd ignores, as c0179 does for fixtures, with an owner that mounts and detaches it; c0194's captured wish that .cache/.tmp stay out of fseventsd, which c0179 does not deliver

## Non-goals
- Fixtures under `.cache/fx`: c0179.
- Where temp trees live (`.cache/.tmp`): c0194, decided by the user.

## Solution
Ideas from capture; z-design decides.
- Reuse c0179's volume tooling (`z-nile/lib/fxvol.pl`, the `.fseventsd/ignore` marker c0179 measured to work) for a standing per-checkout volume at `.cache/.tmp`, or route `.cache/.tmp` onto c0179's per-run volume while a run holds it.
- A standing mount needs an owner that mounts it at session start and detaches it at session end (c0191's SessionEnd hook, c0194's SessionStart hook).
- Measure first: how much fseventsd and Spotlight work `.cache/.tmp` causes once c0194 lands.
