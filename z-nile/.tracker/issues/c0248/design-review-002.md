### F3 [high] On a raft readied by a person or z-work (`Lands: approved`), `done --through` waits for the approval and guesses of every member, the blocked one above included, so the green layers below a block do not land
Where: c0248.md outcome 4 ("What it does"), AC6, AC7; prototype `z-nile/engine/main.luau:5561-5584` (with `issues = layergit.orderedMembers(raft)`) and `:7022-7029`
Evidence: `ready` writes the trailer `Lands: approved` on every ready that is not dispatched (main.luau:7024-7025), so it is the default for the primary worker (z-work) and for a person. In `done` that branch loops `for _, m in issues do local gap = f0399.approvalGap(m)` over every member and fails "`<R>` waits for approval: …" when any gap remains. `issues` is every ordered member, not `bindSet`. `f0399.guessedGap(m)` loops over every member the same way. Only the other branch (no trailer) is scoped to `bindSet` (main.luau:5586-5588). `approvalGap` returns "no approval" for an unapproved member (main.luau:1558-1559). So under z-work's guess mode, where members are built unapproved and approved at the end, layer 3 blocks while layers 1 and 2 are green and approved, and `./zn done R --through <layer 2's issue>` refuses, naming layer 3. That defeats Q3 "They merge now" ("the green layers below it land now"), the captured "a red or blocked layer stops the layers above it and not the ones below". The Design says nothing about approvals or guesses on an early landing. No layers-land case readies a raft with an unapproved member above the landing, so AC6 and AC7 pass with this behaviour.
Critique: A captured outcome fails on the default path, and the acceptance criterion meant to prove it can be passed by the wrong implementation. The tempting fix is to loop over `bindSet` in the `Lands: approved` branch too. But f0399's text "nothing lands without an approval that holds in the tree being landed; the whole raft waits" was written when every landing was the whole raft, and the raft's own `guessedGap(raft)` names the raft's first member's guesses, not the landing's.
Consequences: Scoping to the landing means the approval of each landed member is checked once, at its own landing (it is in `done/` afterwards and the last landing skips it). A member above stays unapproved until its own landing, which is what f0399 asks. The raft-level `guessedGap(raft)` must still hold, or be read for the landing's members only. Pick one and say which: a raft-level Guessed line naming only the first member's guess is the first member's, so it is in scope only when the first member is in the landing. If the owner prefers the whole raft to wait (f0399's literal rule), that is a scope decision that narrows Q3 for every z-work raft, and only the user can make it.
Recommendation: In outcome 4, state that an early landing checks approvals and guesses for the landing's members only (`bindSet`, in both branches), with the raft's own Guessed line in scope as above. Add a layers-land case: a raft readied by `./zn ready` (`Lands: approved`), members 1 and 2 approved, member 3 unapproved with layer 3 finished. `done --through <member 2>` lands 1 and 2, and a full `done` refuses, naming member 3. Add the Rules row with its mutant (the approval loop over every member). If the design keeps the whole-raft wait instead, escalate to the user: options (a) scope approvals to the landing (Q3 holds for guess-mode rafts), (b) the whole raft waits (green layers below a block land only after every member is approved, Q3 narrowed).
