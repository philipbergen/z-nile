### F2 [low] The restore refusal still offers "or review again as the next round", a way out that cannot be taken

Recommendation: Drop ", or review again as the next round" from the refusal and from outcome 1. The message becomes "<name> was committed and deleted in <sha>: restore it with git checkout <sha>^ -- <path>". AC4's assertion already pins the restore words.
