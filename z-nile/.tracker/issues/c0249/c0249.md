# c0249: Issues and rafts have a rank

## Problem
Tickets have no order. A worker (or you) cannot say which ticket comes next. Every issue and raft should carry `rank:` (integer, lower first). `depends_on` stays a hard constraint; `rank` orders what is free. The next item is the lowest rank among items whose `depends_on` are all done and whose status is claimable.

What should happen: z-ticket and z-design append by default; `./zn rank <id> top|after <id>|<n>` reorders; a raft takes the best rank of its members; `./zn next` prints the next claimable item; `./zn list` and the board show the stack in order; the engine audit rejects a malformed rank.

Part of the z-nile vision (z-nile/README.md, section How it should work). To be delivered in the raft **R-vision** as one **stacked PR set**, one layer per ticket in this order: c0248, c0249, c0250, c0251, c0252, c0253, c0254.

Added by the user (2026-11-07):
- `rank:` is optional. **Unset is the lowest rank**: below every set rank.
- Equal or unset ranks fall back to the ticket number, so the order is always stable.
- **Lost work first:** anything in progress but lost (a stale heartbeat, c0265) comes before anything not yet started, whatever its rank, even if a human started it without updating the rank.
- `depends_on` (and `blocked`, c0250) let the order work around rank: an item that is blocked, or waits on undone work, is skipped, and its rank is left as it is.
- **z-raft asks for a rank when it makes a raft.** Unset is a valid answer.
- `./zn rank` can also unset a rank.
- The z-nile app in Omarchy (c0257) reflects the order and reorders by drag and drop through `./zn rank`.

## Idea
Tickets have no order, so neither a worker nor the user can say which comes next. Every issue and raft can carry a `rank:`, an integer where lower comes first.

- **Unset is the default and the lowest rank** (Q2): below every set rank. Equal or unset ranks fall back to the ticket number, so the order is always stable. z-ticket and z-design write no rank, and unset does the appending. Only a person sets a rank: with `./zn rank`, in the board app, or when z-raft asks.
- **`./zn rank <id> top|after <id>|<n>`** reorders, and can also unset a rank.
- **A raft's rank** (Q5). z-raft asks for a rank when it makes a raft, and unset is a valid answer. The answer is the raft's own rank and overrides its members' ranks. A raft with no rank of its own takes the best rank of its members.
- **Who set it.** Setting or unsetting a rank (with `./zn rank`, in the board app, or as z-raft's answer) records who set it in c0263's record (`./zn record <id> ranked`). The user's c0263 decision (2026-11-08) and c0264's design give that row to this issue.
- **`./zn next`** prints the next claimable item, in this order:
  - lost work first: anything in progress but lost (a stale heartbeat, c0265) comes before anything not yet started, whatever its rank, even if a human started it without updating the rank;
  - then the claimable raft with the lowest rank;
  - only when no raft is claimable, the issue with the lowest rank whose design a worker may draft (Q1). Which issues qualify is c0253's: a confirmed idea (c0260).
  `depends_on` is a hard constraint, and rank only orders what is free. An item that waits on undone work, or is blocked (c0250), is skipped, and its rank is left as it is.
- **Claimable** (Q1, and the user's 2026-11-08 scope decision): a worker carries a raft to merge, and it may draft the design of an issue, but a person approves it.
- **`./zn list` and the board show the stack in order.** Blocked items come first, because they wait on the person (c0250), then the order `next` uses. The engine's audit rejects a malformed rank.
- **The Omarchy app (c0257) follows the rank and reorders by drag and drop** (Q3). c0249 makes the app order tickets and rafts by rank (unset last, the ticket number as the tiebreak), the rank part of what c0257's capture gave to "the order c0249 defines". Lost work first in the app, and its marks, are c0290's (the user, 2026-11-09). c0249 also sets the app's `rankCommand` to `./zn rank`, so a drag persists as a rank on `origin/main`. The app's hook today calls `./zn rank {id} --before {beforeId}`, so c0249 either gives `./zn rank` that form or changes the hook. c0249 therefore depends on c0257.
- **The README.** Its "lowest rank among items whose status is claimable" says what claimable means above. Its older line that every item "gets a rank" moves to the Rank and lost work sub-section, where unset is a valid rank. Its "z-ticket and z-design append by default" becomes "unset by default" (Q2).

**R-vision stays one raft** (Q4): c0249 is its second layer.

Solution ideas from the capture: an integer in the frontmatter with gap numbering (10, 20, 30), so one move edits one file, and a pure `next` function in the engine over the parsed tracker.

Not here: the worker loop that uses `./zn next` (c0253), auto-prioritising by cost or value, the record itself (c0263), and the `blocked` status (c0250, which makes `next` skip a blocked item).
