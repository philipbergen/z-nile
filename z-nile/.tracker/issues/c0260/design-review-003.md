### F1 [high] An interactive capture whose interview is legitimately `None: <why>` can never be confirmed: the audit fails it
Where: capturecheck.luau ~line 421 (patch line 540: `if isNone and asked == 0 and l.who ~= "unattended" and #lines == 1`); z-ticket step 0b ("no question to ask is `None: <why the ask was complete>`"); review-record.spec "the unattended and user forms cannot be mixed" (tests only `None: unattended`).
Evidence: probe P1, `check` of a record with `## Interview` = `None: the ask named the file and the fix exactly` and `Confirmed (user, 2026-11-07) after round 1: "yes"` → `Confirmed (who …) beside an Interview that is only `None:` needs the user's words and a real question (nobody was asked)`. The rule tests `isNone` (any `None:`), not `unattendedNone`. So the path step 0b explicitly allows ends in an audit error, and the gate (`./zn land` runs the full gate) refuses the capture; the only way out is inventing a question.
Critique: the tempting fix is to key the rule on `unattendedNone`. That reopens design-review-002 F4: a dispatched z-ticket that writes `None: the ask was complete` and `Confirmed (user, …)` then passes everything, and z-design never asks. The shape alone cannot tell who wrote the line.
Consequences: the one fact the tool knows is whether the capture lands from a dispatched step (`ZN_DISPATCH=1` / `ZN_PUBLISH_BROKER` set in `land`); round 2 recommended exactly this and the design did not take it. With it, `None:` stays legal interactively and `Confirmed` is impossible on the unattended path.
Recommendation: (1) the rule becomes "`Confirmed` alone beside `None: unattended…` is an error"; (2) `captureScope` refuses, when `ZN_DISPATCH=1`, a landed document whose `## Confirmation` gains a `Confirmed` line or whose Interview does not begin `None: unattended` (the unattended record is the only one a dispatched capture may land). Promoted spec cases (rules): review-record.spec `None: <complete>` + `Confirmed` passes; `None: unattended` + `Confirmed` alone fails; land.spec, with `ZN_DISPATCH=1` in the env, refuses a `Confirmed` record and lands the `Unconfirmed` one. Rows in Rules and mutants for both, run on the prototype.
