### F1 [medium] A `split` ruling sends the ticket to re-prep, but `finalview.state` keeps it `blocked` whatever the view, so the re-prepped ticket can never be reviewed again
Where: c0262.md, `work.luau` bullet "After the human's `./zn resolve` ... after `split` the new issue is captured and the ticket re-prepped"; `work.finalStep` (92842ff0c, "After split, the new issue is captured and {issue} re-prepped."); zz-execute 4a (same); `finalview.state` (`if off >= 2 and latest.verdict == "off-course" and rule ~= "amend-design" then ... blocked`); guesses.md G3 ("split and abort change nothing"); verdict.spec AC14; z-design's line "A ticket sent back by its final review is re-prepped with the off-course `final-review-NNN.md` files copied from its old layer branch into its issue folder".
Evidence: c0250's decision table: on a raft, `split` is "the same on the `Layer:` member, which leaves the raft as in `amend-design`" (the member is unfolded to `new`, `design: draft`, with a copy of the note in its folder), and `./zn resolve` accepts all five decisions whatever the note's Options list. The round-3 fix now tells the worker that after `split` the ticket is re-prepped. A re-prep copies the off-course reviews (z-design's line), and `finalread.rulings` reads the copied note, so the ruling map is `{ [N] = "split" }`. `finalview.state` treats every decision but `approve`, `retry` and `amend-design` as no ruling: on the spot mutant, `finalview.state(TWICE, "v2", { [2] = "split" })` returns `blocked`, and since the block holds "whatever the view", it stays `blocked` after the re-prep and rebuild. So the re-prepped ticket's next `work next` says `blocked`, the dispatcher refuses a review (`mayFileAgain("blocked")` is false), `done` refuses it, and the worker blocks it again for a second human ruling on a split the human already made. If instead the worker does not copy the reviews (the split sentence does not say to), the count restarts at zero, which drops the "second off-course verdict blocks" history the redo route exists to keep (The redo route and the count).
Critique: the new route text and G3 disagree: G3 was written when only the state's own decisions mattered, and the F2 fix added a `split` route without giving it a state. The tempting fix is to delete the `split` sentence, since the note's Options never offer it; but `resolve` accepts `split` anyway, and the human choosing it is exactly the case the sentence is for.
Consequences: `split` becomes a second spelling of `amend-design` in `finalview.state` (c0250's own table says it "goes as `amend-design`"): `redo-design` on the ruled review's view, `review-again` once the re-prepped layer changes the view, and a further off-course verdict after it blocks again, as for `amend-design`. The split-off new issue is a new ticket with no final reviews, marked at its own `status prepared`; nothing reads the old ticket's reviews for it. `abort` stays "changes nothing" (G3): a raft readied again after an abort meets the block once more, which is the human's to rule.
Recommendation: in `finalview.state` read `split` as `amend-design` (one line: `local rule = byReview[latest.n]; if rule == "split" then rule = "amend-design" end`, and the same in the `why`); update G3 ("split is read as amend-design, as c0250's table routes it; abort changes nothing"); add to verdict.spec AC14 the three `split` assertions above (redo-design on `v2`, review-again on `v4`, blocked after a third off-course verdict); add a Rules and mutants row "split sends the ticket to z-design, as amend-design" with mutant "split changes nothing" (run); and make the `split` sentence in the Design, `finalStep` and 4a say the same as `amend-design`'s: the ticket has left the raft, is re-prepped as a redesign with its `final-review-NNN.md` files copied first, and the raft is readied again without it. Optionally list `split` among the decisions the worker's `## Options` may offer (c0250's five).
