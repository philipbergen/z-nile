- **One prep per issue**: before you claim an issue, and again whenever you resume a prep, look for another prep of it, and never design the issue a second time beside one.
  - **Search**: `git fetch --prune origin`, `git branch -r --list '*<id>*' '*<slug>*'` (a prep branch is `prep-<slug>` and need not hold the id), `gh pr list --repo "$(gh repo view --json nameWithOwner -q .nameWithOwner)" --state all --search <id>` (without `--repo`, a checkout whose `origin` names the repository before its rename finds nothing), `git worktree list | grep -e <id> -e <slug>` and `./zn status <id>`.
  - **Yours**: the branch this worktree is on and its PR, and every prototype branch or worktree your issue document names (and a worktree named after one, detached or not). Name each prototype you make in Unknowns & experiments, so that a resume knows it as yours.
  - **The claim**: before you claim, a `preparing` claim is another prep's, and `./zn status <id> preparing` refuses it. At a resume the claim alone proves nothing, because nothing records who made it: under `ZN_DISPATCH=1` it is the chain's, since the dispatcher starts z-design on a `preparing` issue only after its own chain's z-design step; in a terminal it is yours when this prep claimed it. A claim given back and taken again by a session elsewhere that has pushed nothing is not found: Whose claim (zz-rules) forbids giving back a claim you did not make.
  - **Another prep**: any other branch with an open PR or none, or worktree, that holds a design of this issue (its copy of the issue document has a Design section or `acceptance/` files that main's lacks), and any open PR whose head is such a branch or whose title names it as `prep <id>`. Read the rest without counting it: a PR that only mentions the id, a branch or worktree with no design of the issue (a capture), and a closed PR, whose comments say where its work went (follow it). A merged one is history.
  - **Then**: join another prep only with its owner's say (ask the user), or stop and take another issue. Under `ZN_DISPATCH=1`, first read `$ZN_DISPATCH_DIR/answered-*.md` and follow a ruling that names exactly the hits you find now. Otherwise write the hits to `$ZN_DISPATCH_DIR/questions.md` with the three choices that work, and stop: the user ends the other prep (closes its PR, deletes its branch, gives its claim back) and retries; the user keeps it, runs `./zn dispatch stop <id>` and carries it on in a terminal; or the user rules that a hit is not a prep of this issue. (Lesson `z-design/one-prep-per-issue`.)
