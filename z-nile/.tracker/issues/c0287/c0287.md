# c0287: Control cli orphan wait on wall clock

## Problem
c0117/control-cli.spec.luau proves an orphan's death with absentAfter(grandchild-orphan, 5): a file must be gone within 5 s of wall clock, the same load-sensitive shape c0279 found in dispatch-cli.spec.luau (where it failed under load, 4 of 8 runs red at load 47 to 97). control-cli is the slowest spec in the gate (905 s). Found during c0279's prep, outside its scope (the user scoped c0279 to every load-sensitive case in dispatch-cli). What should happen: control-cli proves a process's death by the process (c0279's deadWithin, kill -0 polled on a bounded wall clock), never by a file's absence after a fixed sleep, and stays green on a loaded machine.
