The full gate's format and lint stages read every `.luau` in each project's `.tracker/`, the issue's `acceptance/` and `experiments/` included, and fail on a diff, an error or a warning. Nothing a prep runs earlier does: not `tests/run`, `./zn check` or `./zn promote-check`, and the edit hook formats only a file written through the editor, not one a shell command, a copy or a merge wrote. They read no `.sh` and no `.luau.txt`.

**Why:** c0261's prep PR #259 reached its merge gate on 2026-11-09 with all 12 acceptance specs unformatted, invalid `'\{'` escapes in plain strings and an unused require; the gate failed at format and lint, and it took a fix commit (59b76a99).

**How to apply:** from the repository root, right before the prep commit and again after any later change to a `.luau` in the folder: `d=$(./zn find <id>); stylua "$d"; stylua --check "$d" && selene --quiet "$d"`, until both exit 0 (selene exits 1 on a warning alone). Name the folder: `stylua .` skips `.tracker/` (zz-test/format-tracker-specs-by-path).
