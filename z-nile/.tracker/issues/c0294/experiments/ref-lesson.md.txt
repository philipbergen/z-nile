---
title: An answer that moves a part rewrites the ticket that had it
when: both
tags: [capture, dependencies, scope]
check: For each answer that moves a part or adds an edge, does the other ticket name the new owner, and can each of its outcomes still be built? And is each ticket this capture cannot edit named to the user?
---
An answer that gives ticket A's part to ticket B, or adds a `depends_on` edge from B to A (B waits for A) while A owns that part, changes A as much as B. Unless A's text changes in the same capture, A still reads as the owner, and an outcome of A that needs B's work cannot be built once A must land first.

**Why:** the 2026-11-11 worker idea interview (capture 0e91b4d7), Q3 "c0249 wires it", gave c0257's `rankCommand` wiring to c0249 with the edge c0249 -> c0257. c0257's captured "orders tickets and rafts by the order c0249 defines" was left with no owner able to build it (c0249's idea review round 1, F5): c0249 took its rank order, lost work first stayed c0290's, and c0257 gained a decision line and an Owners line.

**How to apply:** for each such answer, in the same capture, add to the other ticket's Problem a decision line that quotes the option picked, and an Owners line for what you settle beyond it. Walk its outcomes in the new order, move each it cannot build to the ticket that can (both texts say so), and name to the user each ticket whose text it cannot rewrite (past draft, or on a prep branch, as c0276's Non-goal was in draft PR #281).
