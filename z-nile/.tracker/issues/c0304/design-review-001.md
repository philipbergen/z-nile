### F1 [high] narrow.py accepts any line added inside the two narrowed cases, so a change that disables the cases' assertions passes AC1 and AC2
Where: `acceptance/narrow.py` (`kept_in_order`: "each of the two cases keeps every line of its base, in order (lines may only be added)"); Design, The change, item 2 ("The executor may add an assertion …"); Implementation steps 1 ("or make the same two narrowings by hand"); AC2.

This is the defect zz-test/narrowing-pins-the-whole-file records: text probes for the kept lines miss every other way the file can be weakened.
