# c0316: Goals and escapes

## Problem
- z-nile has no written, ordered statement of its goals, so nothing says which objective wins when two conflict.
- Only review findings are forced into lessons: a question to a human at any stage, rework, and a design made stale with work lost leave no tracker record and no lesson unless the agent chooses to write one, as on 2026-11-12 when a question about a check's cost during c0256's prep build left neither.

Scope decision (user, 2026-11-13): the wording of the three goals, of "Only the human shortens a check", of the escape kinds and of the measure is the text of `statement.md` in this folder, word for word (the user approved it as the plan of 2026-11-13, with seven rulings quoted there); the design places that text and does not reword it.

Scope decision (user, 2026-11-13): "Split c0316 in two". The two Solution outcomes about a question to a human (an agent first records the question as a file in the work's folder; a stop for a human answer is only possible through that record), and the parts of the `--uncovered` and zz-review outcomes that are about recorded questions and "not avoidable" verdicts, move to c0321 (Questions are escapes), which depends on this issue and on c0250 and builds them on c0250's `escalation.md`. This issue keeps the statement, the gates, the escapes the tracker sees without a recorded question, the `one-off` challenge, the shortened-check rule, the report and the first lesson.

Scope decision (user, 2026-11-13): "Yes, any type". An issue of any type that corrects something already merged names it in a `reworks: <id>` frontmatter line and is an escape found after the merge; the 20% rework figure keeps its own definition.

Scope decision (user, 2026-11-13): "The reviewer". Each finding in a review names the stage whose work holds the mistake, and the tracker refuses a review with a finding that has no such line.

Scope decision (user, 2026-11-14): "Build on c0136". This issue depends on c0136 (Review lessons go to the step that erred) and reads a finding's stage from c0136's `Erred:` and `Upstream:` lines (z-ticket is the idea stage, z-design and z-raft the design stage, every other step implementation). It adds no `Stage:` line of its own, and the ruling "The reviewer" is met by c0136's lines and its check when a review is filed.

Scope decision (user, 2026-11-14): "Goals first, Intent follows". This issue depends on c0264 (README states z-nile's intent). `## Goals` goes directly above c0264's `## Intent`, and one sentence in Goals says it is the order Intent is read by; c0264 is not changed. "The README opens with" the goals in that sense.

Scope decision (user, 2026-11-14): "The three gates". The outcome "`./zn land` refuses work that has an escape with no harvest entry" names the gates where work lands: `./zn status <id> prepared`, `./zn ready` and `./zn done`. `./zn land` keeps landing captures, and an issue captured with `reworks:` has its escape entry before its design is marked prepared.

## Non-goals
- Rerunning c0256's 30 skipped dispatcher mutants is left to its raft.
- No numeric target is set for escapes between stages before the merge.
- Rebasing a design before its build starts is not counted as an escape.
- A question asked in a session with no tracked work (no issue or raft in hand) is not recorded.

## Solution
- z-nile/README.md opens with the three ordered goals, the rule that only the human shortens a required check, and the definition of an escape, and AGENTS.md, zz-rules and the Lessons section every skill shares point to it.
- An agent that asks a human anything at any stage (idea, design, implementation, verification or merge) first records the question in the tracker as a file in the work's folder, naming its stage and, for a design question, whether the idea could have settled it and why.
- A stop for a human answer (z-work step 5, the dispatcher's question stops) is only possible through that recorded question, so a question asked during implementation, verification or merge always leaves a record.
- `./zn lessons --uncovered` lists every escape kind that has no harvest entry: review findings against an earlier stage, recorded questions, a stop of a started raft, a redesign after approval, a defect issue naming what it reworks, and a design made stale with work lost.
- `./zn land` refuses work that has an escape with no harvest entry, where an entry names the stage that should have caught it and a lesson, or `one-off: <reason>`.
- The stage's adversarial reviewer (zz-review) challenges every "not avoidable" verdict and every `one-off`, and also fails the review when the work's history shows a question to a human or a shortened required check with no recorded question.
- zz-stats reports escapes per stage, with a target of zero after a merge, and keeps the 20% rework figure as history.
- The first lesson is recorded with it: an agent that asked the human does not go on with its own recommendation when the answer decides whether a required check runs.

### 2. The escapes the tracker sees (pure, `z-nile/engine/lessons.luau`)
A stage is `idea`, `design` or `implementation`: the stage whose work held the mistake, which is the stage whose review should have caught it.

| kind | what the tracker reads | where it is found | the harvest entry that covers it |
|---|---|---|---|
| finding | a review finding whose `Stage:` line names a stage earlier than the review's own (a `design-review` is at design; a `review` and a `final-review` are at implementation) | the review's stage | the finding's own entry, as today: `- review-001 F3: <lesson ids>` or `one-off: <reason>` |
| redesign | row `n` of the item's `approval.md`, for every `n` of 2 or more (the contract was approved again, so the approved design changed) | implementation | `- escape redesign <n> (<stage>): <lesson ids \| one-off: reason>` |
| stop | stop `n` of a started raft, `n` from 1 to the raft's `stops:` frontmatter value | implementation | `- escape stop <n> (<stage>): …` in the raft's `harvest.md` |
| rework | an item whose frontmatter has a non-empty `reworks:` | after the merge | `- escape rework 1 (<stage>): …` in that item's `harvest.md` |

A design made stale by a moved `main` with work lost is seen as the stop of its started raft, or as the redesign that follows: the lost work is what the tracker can see, and the entry's lesson says what moved. A rebase before the build starts changes neither `stops:` nor `approval.md` unless the contract changed, and so is not counted, as the user ruled.

New and changed API (all pure over texts; `load` only reads):
- `export type Stage = "idea" | "design" | "implementation"`; `lessons.STAGES`.
- `Entry` gains `escape: { kind: "redesign" | "stop" | "rework", n: number, stage: Stage }?`. `lessons.parseHarvest` reads `- escape <kind> <n> (<stage>): <target>` with the target rules of a finding's entry (lesson ids or `one-off: <reason>`); an unknown kind (`finding` included), an `n` that is not a positive integer written plainly (`^[1-9]%d*$`), an unknown stage and a second entry for the same kind and `n` are problems, named with the line. The entry is stored with `review = "escape"` and `finding = "<kind> <n>"`, so the ledger keeps one key shape, and it is a source of the lessons it names (`<id> escape <kind> <n>`, after the review entries and before the notes).
- `Item` gains `approvals: number?` (rows of `approval.md`), `stops: number?` (the document's `stops:`) and `reworks: string?`; nil is none, so a caller that builds an `Item` by hand (the promoted c0111 specs) still type-checks, and `load` always fills the two numbers. An `approval.md` that `contract.parse` refuses is a problem of the tree (`<id>/approval.md: <why>`), which `--check` and every gate report. `lessons.load` fills them from the item's folder with `contract.parse` (the one reader of `approval.md`) and `store.readFront`-style frontmatter reading already used by the engine (the one reader of frontmatter); it also loads an item that has none of a review, a harvest or these, when `approvals >= 2`, `stops >= 1` or `reworks` is set.
- `lessons.stageOf(review: string, finding: string): Stage?`: the `Stage:` line inside the block of `### <finding>` (from its heading to the next `### ` or `## ` heading or the end). `lessons.reviewStage(stem): Stage` (design for `design-review-…`, implementation otherwise).
- `lessons.escapes(item): { Escape }` with `Escape = { item: string, kind: "finding" | "redesign" | "stop" | "rework", ref: string (`<stem> <F>` for a finding, else `<kind> <n>`), made: Stage?, found: "design" | "implementation" | "merged" }`: every escape of the item, covered or not. `made` comes from the review's line for a finding and from the harvest entry for the others (nil while uncovered).
- `lessons.uncovered(items)` keeps every line it prints today and adds, per item and after its findings, `<id> escape redesign <n>`, `<id> escape stop <n>` and `<id> escape rework 1` for each with no entry.
- `lessons.unstaged(item, exempt: { [string]: boolean }): { string }` (`exempt` is keyed by review stem, `design-review-001`): `<id> <stem> <F>: no Stage line` for every finding of a review not in `exempt` whose block has no valid `Stage:` line.
- `lessons.check` also refuses an escape entry with nothing behind it: `redesign <n>` with `n` below 2 or above the item's approvals, `stop <n>` above its `stops:`, `rework <n>` with `n` other than 1 or on an item with no `reworks:`; and a `reworks:` that names no item `tree.found` holds.
- `lessons.escapeCounts(items): { { made: string, found: string, n: number } }` for the report: one row per pair with a count above zero, `made` in the order idea, design, implementation, `not harvested`, then `found` in the order design, implementation, merged. The report prints the full grid from it. `scripts/tracker/lessons.luau` (the forwarder the specs require) re-exports the new types.

**Which reviews must carry `Stage:` lines.** Reviews written before the rule have none and are never edited. `lessons.STAGED_SINCE` is a date constant (`YYYY-MM-DD`, set by the executor to the day the layer is finished). The adapter (`main.luau`, `stagedExempt(item)`) asks git for the date of the commit that added each review file (`git log --diff-filter=A --format=%cs -1 -- <path>`); a review added before that date is exempt, and a review git does not know yet (written in this worktree, not committed) is not. The pure function takes the exempt set, so the specs need no clock.

### 3. The gates (adapter, `z-nile/engine/main.luau`, `readycheck.luau`, `readyfacts.luau`, `workread.luau`)
One helper, `harvestProblem(ids: { string }): string?`, loads the tree from the working tree, takes the named items, and returns nil or one refusal: `<first line> (and <k> more): harvest each in harvest.md (a lesson id, or one-off: <reason>), then run this again; ./zn lessons --uncovered <ids> lists them`. Its lines are `lessons.uncovered` of those items, then `lessons.unstaged` of each with its exempt set; a `harvest.md` that does not parse is the refusal ("… harvest.md: <problem>").

| gate | what it adds | why there |
|---|---|---|
| `./zn status <id> prepared` (an issue) | `harvestProblem({ id })` on the local item, after `designOpen` | the design lands: its review's findings, a redesign's escape and a rework's escape are harvested in the prep, which carries `harvest.md` |
| `./zn done <R>` | `harvestProblem({ R, members… })` (the raft first, then its members in `issues:` order), after `reviewProblem` and before `runCheck` | the implementation lands: every finding of every review in the raft's and its members' folders, each stop, each redesign, each rework |
| `./zn ready <R>` and `./zn work next` | `readycheck.reason` refuses a raft with a stop that has no entry (`ready` fails with it; `work next` lists it as the reason it skips the raft and offers the next one): `<R> was stopped after it started (stop <n>) and its harvest.md has no entry for it: add "- escape stop <n> (<stage>): …" by pull request, then ready it again` | a stopped raft does not start again before its lesson is written; `readyfacts` and `workread` already walk the raft's files and gain `harvest.md` and the raft's `stops:` |

`./zn ready` does not refuse for a member's unharvested design-review findings: a raft prepared before lessons existed is harvested by its executor (zz-execute Phase 6), and `done` holds it to that. `./zn land` lands a capture, which holds a draft document and no review, approval or stop; the capture's "`./zn land` refuses" names the landing of work, which is these three gates, and `land` itself changes only in that `reworks` joins `CAPTURE_KEYS` so a capture can name what it reworks. The dispatcher's own stop after a review step (`dispatch.luau`, "left unharvested findings") stays, and its `uncovered` fact now carries the new lines too, because it runs the same command.

**The final review's findings are harvested after the trace** (found while the gate specs were written). `trace.md` is sealed before the final review, and `trace.luau`'s `isAllowedAfterTrace` lets only the raft document, `final-review-NNN.md`, `final-refute-NNN.md` and spend rows change after it, so today a `harvest.md` entry for a final-review finding, or the lesson it names, makes `./zn trace --check` call the table stale: the new `done` gate would ask for an entry there is no legal way to commit. Two checks in `trace.staleReason` read every commit after the finish commit: `isTrackerFileOf` first (a file that is not the raft's or a ticket's tracker file is stale at once), then `isAllowedAfterTrace`. Both call one new predicate, `isLedgerFile(path, raftDir, issues)`, which is true for `harvest.md` in the raft's folder and in each member's folder and for a lesson file (a path matching `/skills/<skill>/lessons/<slug>.md`); `isAllowedAfterTrace` gains the member list `isTrackerFileOf` already takes. Neither file changes code, a spec or the contract the table traces.

**`./zn stop <R>`** (`cmd.stop`): when the raft has a layer branch on origin (`refs/heads/<R>/<n>/…`, which `stop` already lists to print them), the commit that moves the raft back also rewrites the raft document's `stops:` to one more (0 when absent), and prints "stop <n> of <R> is an escape: its lesson goes in <R>'s harvest.md before it is readied again (- escape stop <n> (<stage>): …)". A stop of a raft that never started (an un-ready) changes nothing.

### 4. The reviewer (skills only)
The reviewer names the stage of every finding, so the author does not count their own escapes. Design mode's item 10 and Final mode's last item add: challenge every `one-off` in the item's `harvest.md` whose reason does not hold (a finding, at least medium), and raise a high finding when the work's history (the documents, the Verification log, the pull request) shows a check a design or a skill requires was skipped or shortened with no ruling of the user quoted for it. A `one-off` written while answering the design review is read by the next reviewer of the item, which is the code review or the final review; the design reviewer reads those of the reviews before it (a redesign's, a rework's).
