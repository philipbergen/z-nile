Where: c0316.md section 2 (`Stage`), section 1's following sentence, "The texts" (zz-rules)
Evidence: statement.md: "The stages are idea, design, implementation, merge, and after the merge", and the entry "names the stage that should have caught it". The zz-rules text says the stage is "the stage whose work held the mistake". `STAGES` has three values, and nothing says where a verification or merge mistake goes, or whether the final review is the merge stage's reviewer.
Critique: a reader of the README cannot tell how a merge-stage mistake is recorded.
Consequences: adding `merge` changes the grid; a mapping sentence changes nothing.
Recommendation: one sentence, pinned by docs.spec (in zz-rules and the README's following sentence): verification and merge mistakes are recorded as `implementation`, the final review included, and the stage whose work held the mistake is the stage whose review should have caught it. Or add `merge` with its own row and column. Ask the user if unsure.
