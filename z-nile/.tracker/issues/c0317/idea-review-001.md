# Idea review 001

## Findings

- F1: The Solution's last clause, "whichever of the heartbeat and provenance rafts landed first", can't be judged true or false. It could mean "no matter which raft lands first", or it could mean "the record is written by whichever raft landed first". It also ties the claim to the order the rafts land in, which can no longer be checked once both have landed. Without the clause, the sentence is a claim a reader can test: every successful takeover of a lost claim writes exactly one provenance record naming the item and the session that took it over.

## Verdict

revise
