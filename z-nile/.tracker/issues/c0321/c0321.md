# c0321: Questions are escapes

## Problem
- An agent can ask a human a question during design, implementation, verification or merge without leaving any record in the tracker.
- z-work step 5 and the dispatcher's question stops can stop for a human answer without a recorded question.
- c0250's escalation.md has no field for the stage of a question, or for whether the idea could have settled a design question.
- `./zn lessons --uncovered` does not list a question that is an escape under c0316's statement.md and has no harvest entry.
- zz-review neither challenges a "not avoidable" verdict on a design question nor fails a review for a question to a human that has no recorded question.

## Non-goals
- Idea-stage questions are out of scope: the idea interview (c0260) and z-ticket's restatement (c0247) keep recording their answers where those issues put them (the issue body, or c0256's local answer path while a capture has no item folder), and this issue adds no escalation.md entry for them.
- An idea-stage question is never an escape, so `./zn lessons --uncovered` never lists one and zz-review never fails a review for one.
- This issue adds no second question file or format: the stage and verdict are fields of c0250's escalation.md.
- This issue does not change the goals statement, the landing gates, the escape rule or the harvest entry for an escape, which stay in c0316.
- This issue does not change the rules in c0316's statement.md that decide which question is an escape.
- This issue does not build escalation.md, `./zn block` or `./zn resolve`, which stay in c0250.

## Solution
- An agent that asks a human anything during design, implementation, verification or merge first records the question in c0250's escalation.md in the item's folder, whether it asks in chat or through a dispatcher stop.
- Every recorded question names its stage.
- Every recorded design question also carries an avoidable verdict (whether the idea could have settled it) and the reason.
- z-work step 5 and the dispatcher's question stops can stop for a human answer only through that recorded question (`./zn block`).
- `./zn lessons --uncovered` lists every recorded question that is an escape under c0316's statement.md and has no harvest entry.
- A recorded question counts as an escape when its stage is implementation, verification or merge, when it is a design question with the verdict "avoidable", or when it asks about the cost of a required check.
- zz-review challenges every "not avoidable" verdict on a design question.
- zz-review fails the review when the work's history shows a question to a human during design, implementation, verification or merge with no recorded question.
