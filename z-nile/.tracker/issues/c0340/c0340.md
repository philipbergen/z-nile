# c0340: Provenance fixture names main

## Problem
- c0282's sequestered spec `z-nile/.tracker/issues/c0282/acceptance/lifecycle.spec.luau` clones the checkout with `clone.isolated(root, dir)`, removes origin and runs `./zn start` without naming the clone's branch main, so on a layer branch `start` refuses with "no main branch" and c0282's `check.sh` cannot pass.
- Once c0282's done promotes that spec, c0302's promoted scan `tests/acceptance/c0302/fixtures.spec.luau` turns red naming it.
- c0282's frontmatter does not depend on c0302, though its AC7 runs `c0074/done-gate` and `c0001/tracker-lifecycle` on its layer branch, where both fail until c0302 lands.

## Non-goals
- Nothing outside `z-nile/.tracker/issues/c0282/` changes: no promoted spec, no tooling code, and none of c0302's seven specs or its scan.
- Inside c0282's folder, nothing changes beyond the one fixture line, the `depends_on` entry, and the records the re-prep requires (design text, design review, approval row); c0282's acceptance criteria, `check.sh` and behaviour under test stay as they are.

## Solution
- c0282's lifecycle spec runs `git({ "checkout", "-q", "-B", "main" })` right after the origin removal, the line c0302 adds to its seven specs.
- c0282's `depends_on` lists c0302.
- The change lands as a re-prep of c0282: its design records the layer-branch run, a new design review is written, and `approval.md` gains a row pinning the new hashes of the spec and the design, so c0282 is prepared again with a valid approval before it is readied.
