## Non-goals
- Ready still reads approvals only from `approval.md` on origin/main.
- `./zn approve` never merges, or changes the state of, a prep pull request.
- `./zn approve` does not retry a failed publish or push on its own.
- What an approval records and who may approve stay as c0283 left them. (c0283 has not landed, and by the decision of 2026-11-16 it lands after this: until then they stay as today's approve has them, one `approval.md` row by the git identity, refused under `ZN_DISPATCH` and without a terminal; c0283 adds its agent-session refusal and `approved` row on top of this, Design section 7.)

## Solution
- When the design it approves is already on origin/main, one `./zn approve <id>` records the approval and publishes `approval.md` to origin/main (what `--publish` does today), so ready counts it at once with no second command.
- When the design is on an unmerged prep branch, one `./zn approve <id>` commits `approval.md` to that branch, pushes it so the prep pull request carries it, and prints that the approval counts when that pull request merges.
- When the publish or push fails, the approval stays recorded locally, and the command prints that it does not count yet and names the one command that retries it, `./zn approve <id> --publish`; it counts once that retry succeeds.
- `--publish` stays as that retry and `--check` behaves as it does today.
- The c0283 approve specs this changes are listed under Overrides, and the issue depends on c0283 being done. (The dependency clause is replaced by the decision of 2026-11-16, "Re-prep c0369 now": this issue no longer depends on c0283 and is built on today's approve; c0283 is re-prepared on top of it. No c0283 spec is on origin/main, so Overrides lists the specs on main this changes, and c0283's own sequestered specs are its re-prep's, Design section 7.)

## Design
`./zn approve <id>` becomes one command, run from the main checkout or any worktree: it finds the place, says where it acts, records the approval there, and makes it count. It builds on the approve that is on origin/main today (#295's `cmd.approve` in `z-nile/engine/main.luau`, `z-nile/engine/approval.luau`, the `approve` block in `zn`): the wrapper's `ZN_DISPATCH` and terminal refusals, the engine's same two refusals, the recording of one `approval.md` row in a `tracker: approve <id>` commit, and `--publish`, which publishes `approval.md` alone through `publish.commit`. There is no agent-session refusal, no `approved` row and no `provenance.md` on main, so this design has none of them (the first design took them from c0283; section 7 says what c0283's re-prep inherits). Prototype: `proto-c0369-main` (a scratch worktree off origin/main fd8aa9720, local commits 7930270a7..68b8ec2e7 and the specs' final edits, never pushed or merged); `experiments/prototype.patch` is the change and `experiments/mutants.py` the mutation runner.

### 1. The place (`z-nile/engine/approveplace.luau`, new)
A pure rule over what the edge read. A *copy* of the issue is its document, `check.sh`, specs and `approval.md` in one checkout or at one commit: whether it is `design: ready`, its slug, its contract hash (`contract.hash`), and whether its last approval row holds for that contract (`none`, `changed`, `holds`, `bad`). This module is the first design's (it read nothing of c0283's), with two changes: design review 002 F1 (the pushed rule below) and the user's ruling on its F2 (the raft's worktree).

- **Which worktrees are read** (the user's scope decisions of 2026-11-15, "Only prep-<slug>", and 2026-11-17, "Also read the raft worktree"): the linked worktree on the branch `prep-<slug>` (the issue's slug, read from origin/main's copy, else from the copy in the checkout the command runs in; z-design step 1 makes that worktree); for an issue that origin/main holds in `active/`, its raft's own worktree, `<main checkout>/.worktree/<R-id>` with `<R-id>` the `raft:` line of origin/main's copy (the path `./zn worktree <R-id>` always gives; z-work cds into it), where a contract changed on the raft's branch waits for the re-approval `./zn finish` and `./zn done` ask for; the worktree the command is run in; and the command's own (section 2). No other worktree is opened (another raft's worktree, or the raft worktree of an issue that is not in `active/`, included): a prototype, a mutation copy or a raft's worktree that carries a changed copy of the issue is never a place unless the command is run inside it. The main checkout is never a place.
- **A worktree's design is its own** when its copy is `design: ready` and its contract is none of: origin/main's (when main's copy is ready), the contract of a ready copy at the worktree's merge-base with origin/main, the contract of a ready copy origin/main held at any commit since that merge-base that changed the issue's folder. So a worktree that is only behind main, and a prep worktree left behind after its pull request was squashed onto main, hold nobody's design. A draft never counts as "main's design", whatever its text hashes to (the contract leaves the front matter out). This is the capture's "holds a newer one". **(prep)**
- **Waiting**: a read worktree with a design of its own whose approval does not hold. **Held for main**: a read worktree whose approval holds for the very contract origin/main holds ready and unapproved (a prep that merged before the person approved, the command's own worktree after a failed publish). **Held, own**: a design of its own whose approval holds; it is *pushed* when the worktree is on a branch whose `origin/<branch>` is at its `HEAD`. A worktree that holds an approval of any other design (a leftover of a failed publish after main's design was replaced, a merged prep) is nothing: it is never retried, pushed or published.
- `./zn approve <id>`, in order: a waiting worktree is the place and a new approval is recorded there (the one the command runs in first, then the prep worktree, then the raft's); else a worktree held for main, or held, own and unpushed, is the place and what it holds is published or pushed with nothing new recorded (so an approval is never recorded twice for one design); else, while a read worktree holds a design of its own that is approved and pushed, it prints `<id> is approved in <name>: it counts when its pull request merges`, adding `; origin/main's older design stays unapproved: that pull request replaces it` when origin/main holds a ready design with no matching approval: main's older design is never approved behind a worktree's own (the capture's "or `origin/main` when the design has merged and no worktree holds a newer one"; design review 002 F1: asked a second time, the first design approved and published main's older design); else origin/main is the place when its copy is ready and its approval does not hold; else, when main's approval holds, it prints `<id> is already approved on origin/main`; else it refuses: `<id>: nothing to approve: it is not design: ready on origin/main, and the worktree of its prep branch (prep-<slug>) holds no design: ready of its own.` (or `it is not on origin/main, and no worktree of its prep branch holds a design: ready of its own.`).
- At most one worktree is on `prep-<slug>`, so more than one place can be meant only when the command is run inside another worktree that also holds a design of its own, and running it there says which. The capture's "refuses and names each" has no case left and nothing ever tells the person to change directory (the scope decision records this).
- `./zn approve <id> --publish` (the retry) never records: a held worktree (for main, or own and unpushed) is the place; then the two printed lines above; else `<id> has no recorded approval to publish. Run ./zn approve <id>.` It finds its place from anywhere. **(prep)**
- `--check` is unchanged and reads the checkout it runs in.

### 2. Recording, in the place
- The refusals come first, in every run, before anything is read or made: the `zn` wrapper's (`ZN_DISPATCH` set, no terminal on stdin) and then the engine's (`ZN_DISPATCH` set, `stdinIsTerminal()` false), as today. `--check` passes both, as today.
- Before anything is written the command prints `<id>: approving in <place>` (or `publishing from <place>` when it only publishes or pushes); `<place>` is the worktree's path from the main checkout (`.worktree/prep-<slug>`) or `origin/main`.
- **A worktree**: the engine starts itself there (`lune run <this checkout>/z-nile/engine/main -- approve <id> [--publish]`, working directory the place, `ZN_TOP=<the place>`, `ZN_CWD=""`, `ZN_APPROVE_IN=<the place>`, the terminal inherited), and exits with its code. `ZN_APPROVE_IN` equal to the checkout's top makes that run act where it is and skip placement, and nothing else: both refusals come before placement in every run, with the variable set or not, and a run that records still refuses the main checkout (`refuseInMainCheckout("approve")` stays for the run that acts). The code that records is today's, unchanged: the same preconditions (design: ready, open list ruled, `check.sh`, a git identity, a committed contract) and the same `tracker: approve <id>` commit of `approval.md` alone. The engine that runs is the one the person invoked, not the place's own copy. **(prep)**
- **origin/main**: nothing is written in the main checkout or in the worktree the command ran in. The command makes a detached worktree of origin/main at `<main checkout>/.worktree/.approve-<id>` (a name `./zn worktree` cannot make, so no person's worktree is ever in the way), records there, and removes it (`git worktree remove`, no force) once the approval is published. This is what "stays recorded locally" needs when the publish fails. A path taken by anything else (a folder that is no worktree, a worktree on a branch, one with changes) is refused, naming it; a clean detached one left by an earlier failure is moved to origin/main and reused. **(prep)**
- A repository with no origin has no origin/main to compare: `approve` records in the checkout it runs in and publishes nothing, as today (its main-checkout refusal already applies only with an origin).
- The record line: `approved <id> <hash> in <place>`, after the place line and before the count line. `<place>` is the checkout's path, or `a worktree of origin/main` in the command's own worktree.

### 3. Making it count, in the same command
After recording (and alone for a held approval or `--publish`), in the place, the step `count` (a function inside `cmd.approve`):
- **Publishing off** (`PICKMAN_PUBLISH=0`, or inside a check, `publishing()`): nothing leaves the machine. The record stays where it is, and the command fails with the failure line below (`publishing is off (PICKMAN_PUBLISH=0, or inside a check)`), so the command's worktree is kept and the retry finds it.
- **Published** when origin/main's copy is `design: ready` and its contract is the one approved (today's `--publish` contract read from origin/main, unchanged): one `tracker: approve <id>` commit through `publish.commit` that holds origin/main's `approval.md` with this approval's last row appended (never the worktree's file, so a row main has is never lost). The appended text is built inside `publish.commit`'s callback from the file as that attempt read it (`read(path)`), so a row another machine appended between attempts is kept (design review 002 F11); the publish validator's `approval.md` rule is untouched and sees a file that grew by one row. It prints `published <id> to origin/main: it counts now`. When origin/main's last row is already this very row (the same `when`, `who` and hash: a retry of a publish that landed and was reported failed), it prints `<id> is already approved on origin/main` and publishes nothing; whether main's approval *holds* is `choose`'s question, answered before this.
- **Pushed** otherwise (main's copy missing, a draft, or another contract, while the place's own approval holds for its own copy): `git push origin HEAD:refs/heads/<branch>`, never forced (a branch that moved on origin refuses, and the record waits for the retry: design review 002 F10), printing `pushed <branch>: the approval counts when its pull request merges`. When the place's approval does not hold for its own copy either, it refuses with `approval.refusal`'s text. Two branches are not pushed (design review 002 F2 and F5) **(prep)**:
  - a raft's layer branch (`R<n>/<n>/<id>`, the shape `./zn start` makes): the record is the executor's, and `./zn finish` and `./zn done` read it in that checkout; it prints `<id>: approved on the layer branch <branch>: nothing is pushed; it counts for ./zn finish in <place>, and ./zn done once the layer is finished and pushed again` and exits 0 (design review 003 F2: `./zn done` lands only the published top-layer tip of finished layers, and an `approval.md` commit above a finish leaves the layer unfinished; whether approve should finish or push the top layer itself is `## Open to the human`, F1);
  - a branch that is neither `prep-<slug>` nor already on origin (`refs/remotes/origin/<branch>` exists): no branch is created on origin for a prototype; it prints `<id>: approved in <place>; <branch> is not on origin, so nothing is pushed: it counts when this design reaches origin/main` and exits 0. The retry finds the same place and says the same.
- **An `approval.md` on origin/main that does not parse** (design review 003 F3): main's copy is `bad`, so origin/main is the place; in the command's own worktree of origin/main the recording run prints `<id>: origin/main's <path> does not parse: this approval replaces it`, removes the file and records one row, and the publish callback, finding main's file unreadable, starts from no rows, so the commit replaces it (no row can be read, so none is lost; the publish validator allows `approval.md` to be replaced). Anywhere else (a person's worktree) an unreadable `approval.md` is still refused, as today.
- **A placed run on no branch** (design review 003 F6): before it records in a worktree it was placed in (not its own of origin/main, which is detached by design), the run refuses a detached `HEAD` (a rebase or restack in progress leaves one): `<id>: <place> is on no branch (a rebase or another operation may be in progress): nothing recorded`. A merge in progress already refuses the partial commit.
- **A failure** (publishing off, the publish, the push, a place on no branch) leaves the commit, exits 1 and prints `<id>: approved in <place>, but it does not count yet: <reason>. Retry: ./zn approve <id> --publish`. Nothing retries by itself.
