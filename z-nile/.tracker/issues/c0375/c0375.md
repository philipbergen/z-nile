# c0375: Close superseded items

## Problem
- The tracker cannot close an item whose outcome another item delivers: c0141, absorbed by c0371 on 2026-11-15, can only be given back to new, so it stays in the backlog where z-design can claim it and the board, raft-plan and metrics still count it.
- z-ticket's Duplicates step asks whether to merge but does not say what merging does to the item that is merged away.

## Non-goals
- The command does not move the superseded item's design, specs or `check.sh` into the surviving item; that stays a manual step.
- No new way to close an item that nothing delivers (abandoning or won't-do) is added.

## Solution
- One `./zn` command records that an item is superseded by another named item and moves it out of the backlog at once, with its history kept, whatever state the surviving item is in (c0371 still in design is accepted).
- A superseded item is never offered by raft-plan, cannot be claimed by z-design, and is absent from the board's open columns and from the open counts in metrics.
- A superseded item is reported as delivered only once the item it names is done; until then the board and metrics show it as superseded and waiting on that item.
- Audit fails when a superseded item names no item, names an item that does not exist, names itself or a chain that loops back to itself, or names an item that was closed without being delivered.
- Audit does not fail because the named item is not done yet.
- z-ticket's Duplicates step says that merging a duplicate means running this command on it, naming the surviving item, at capture.
