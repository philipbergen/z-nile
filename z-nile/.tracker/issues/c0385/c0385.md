# c0385: Lesson landed raft still owes phase 6

## Problem
- On 2026-11-15, with R0101, a worker read the line `end - <R> landed. Stop. …` from `./zn work next <R>` as "stop now" and left zz-execute Phase 6 undone (the watch-kick, the merge gate and cicd for a game change, `./zn spend <R> --phase deliver`, the final report on the pull request, and the cleanup).

## Non-goals
- The lesson does not change z-work step 6 or the landed line in `z-nile/engine/work.luau`, and it does not settle the disagreement between them for sessions started with no raft named.

## Solution
- A lesson in the z-work skill's `lessons/` folder says that zz-execute Phase 6 is part of landing the raft and must have run before the worker acts on an end line that follows a landing, whichever way z-work step 6 then directs (stop when a raft was named, take the next raft when none was).
- The lesson carries the check "before acting on an end line after a landing, has zz-execute Phase 6 run for that raft?" and does not say what the end line means for taking another raft.
