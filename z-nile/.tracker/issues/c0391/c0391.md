# c0391: Approve pins follow one command

## Problem
- c0369 makes `./zn approve <id>` one command from anywhere, yet on origin/main three prepared tickets pin the old two-command flow in sequestered specs that execution cannot edit (c0250's `work-board.spec` and `block-resolve.spec`, c0267's `waiting.spec`, `docs.spec` and `dispatch.approveLine`, f0339's `approve-page.spec` and its recording path), and c0368, still `design: draft` with no `acceptance/` folder, plans to override the same three c0283 approve specs c0369 overrides (`tests/acceptance/c0283/approve.spec.luau`, `tests/tooling/approve.spec.luau`, `tests/tooling/approve-publish.spec.luau`) without covering c0369's promoted `approve.spec`.

## Non-goals
- No change to c0369 or to what approve does, no change to any of the four tickets' own outcomes, and no designing of c0368 to prepared here beyond its dependency and the specs its draft names.

## Solution
- Each of c0250, c0267, f0339 and c0368 has `depends_on` c0369; the three prepared ones are re-prepped so their design, texts and sequestered specs agree with c0369's approve (one command, the texts c0369's `docs.spec` pins, c0267's printed line the bare command) and pass on their prototype merged with c0369's, with c0369's promoted specs green on that merge; c0368's draft names c0369's versions of the three c0283 specs and c0369's promoted `approve.spec` as what it narrows; and c0250, the only one with an `approval.md`, is re-approved by the person, while the other three get their first approval through the normal flow.
