# c0402: Raft cap 3000 lines

## Problem
- `raftplan.CAP` in `z-nile/engine/raftplan.luau` is 1500, and `z-nile/skills/z-raft/SKILL.md:18` and `z-nile/skills/z-design/SKILL.md:46` both say "about 1,500", while the user asked on 2026-11-16 for a cap of 3,000 changed lines.

## Non-goals
- Features still join a raft only when they share a surface (an overlapping Touched path).
- A `solo: true` issue is still always a raft by itself.
- An open raft marked `single` still never takes another issue.
- An issue estimated over the cap is still a raft by itself and is never split.
- A raft still never mixes types or projects.
- The argument forms (new raft, fold, recombine) still override the cap.

## Solution
- `raftplan.CAP` in `z-nile/engine/raftplan.luau` is 3000.
- `z-nile/skills/z-raft/SKILL.md:18` and `z-nile/skills/z-design/SKILL.md:46` say "about 3,000" where they said "about 1,500".
- `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0116/plan.spec.luau` pins the cap at 3,000 lines and passes.

## Design
**One number and the sentences that restate it.** `raftplan.CAP` (`z-nile/engine/raftplan.luau:46`) becomes `3000`. It is the only place the figure lives as code: `raftplan.plan` reads it as `local limit = cap or raftplan.CAP`, and compares with `b.size + size <= cap` (a raft's sum may reach the cap) and `(i.size :: number) > limit` (an issue over it is single). Neither comparison changes, so the boundary moves with the constant: a sum of 3,000 fits, 3,001 starts another raft; an issue of 3,000 is not over the cap, 3,001 is a raft by itself. Nothing else in `z-nile/engine/` names the figure (searched: `grep -rnE "1,?500" z-nile/engine z-nile/skills z-nile/README.md z-nile/templates z-nile/tests AGENTS.md SETUP.md */skills`, 2026-11-17).

**The restated rule changes with it (checklist: Restated rules).** The search of the engine, skills, README, templates and specs found every live restatement; the tracker was searched separately (see Existing work); each changes from "about 1,500" to "about 3,000":
- `z-nile/skills/z-raft/SKILL.md:18` ("a raft that would pass about 1,500 changed lines (the sum of its members' `size:`) starts another; an issue estimated over that is a raft by itself and is never split").
- `z-nile/skills/z-design/SKILL.md:46` (Size and solo: "z-raft sums them against its cap of about 1,500 lines").
- `z-nile/skills/z-design/lessons/size-is-re-estimated-when-scope-grows.md`: its `check:` line ("the raft cap of about 1,500 changed lines") and its first paragraph ("its cap of about 1,500 lines"). The captured Solution names the two skill lines; the lesson restates the same rule in the same words, and a lesson that kept the old figure would send a designer to split an issue the cap now allows (lesson z-design/restated-rules-move-together). Its other mention, "`size: 1500`" in the f0399 note, is a quoted past value and stays.
- Left alone on purpose, because they record what was true then: raft and issue documents and reviews that quote the old cap or sizes near it (done: R0004, R0010, R0024, R0035; active or backlog: R0071, R0073, R0110, R0128, c0257, and the reviews of c0121, c0256 and f0399), and the spend ledgers.
