# c0418: Narrow one round pins

## Problem
Narrow the promoted specs that pin the one-round review rule and c0260's cap of three, so f0417 (back-and-forth reviews) can land: c0119/scope-docs 'ticket-prep has one review round...' (drop 'There is no second reviewer round.'); once c0260, c0262 and c0250 are promoted, c0260/loop.spec (the four AC12 cases that pin 'of at most 3', 'third challenge' and 'idea: calls 6'), c0260/record.spec 'AC4: a change after the confirmation is the next round...' (idea-review-005 absent), c0260/skills-restated 'AC7: ... the loop and its cap, not one challenge' (three -> five), c0262/work.spec 'AC8: a second off-course verdict...' (questions.md -> ./zn block), c0250/docs.spec 'AC12: zz-rules, the Headless paragraphs...' (review-exhausted at a stage's cap); the exact list is f0417's Overrides line.

Decided with the user (2026-11-20): "Review rounds should be toml config" and "toml should have a setting for easy, normal, risky" (the most rounds a review may run is read from TOML: one number per stage (idea, design, implementation) for each of three risk levels, easy, normal and risky. A change that is low risk and easily fixed or reverted gets just a few; one that is complex, large and not easily reverted is allowed more. The numbers are maximums: the skills say so, and say the reviewer is expected to find everything in the first round. The idea cap is configured the same way: "configurable, ideally just one round". The pins this ticket narrows follow the configured maximums and no fixed number: c0260's cap is already five and no longer three, so "three -> five" above is replaced by "the configured idea maximum".)
