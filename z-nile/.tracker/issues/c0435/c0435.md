# c0435: Slim design for easy tickets

## Problem
- Every ticket pays the same fixed drafting work before its risk is known (every design section, acceptance specs, experiments and a size record), and that minimum is high even for a small change: on 2026-11-21 the design of d0328, a three-line fix, cost about 195,000 tokens, 16 minutes and two review rounds on a cheap model.
- Design review is about 43% and design about 25% of agent time, and the cost comes from what the design skill demands, not from the model.
- A ticket is marked easy (`risk: easy`) only once its draft is complete (`z-nile/skills/z-design/SKILL.md:59`), so no ticket is known to be easy when its design starts and the drafting work cannot scale with its risk.
- What already scales with risk comes after the draft: the review round caps for design and for code (2, 5 or 9, `[rounds]`, `z-nile/skills/zz-rules/SKILL.md:185`), and the light path, where `./zn ready <issue>` makes an easy ticket's single raft with no rafting step, the separate code review is skipped and the final review is its only code review (`z-nile/skills/z-ready/SKILL.md:8`, `z-nile/skills/zz-execute/SKILL.md:62`, `z-nile/skills/zz-review/SKILL.md:137`).
- An easy ticket on the light path therefore still gets two reviews, a separate design review of its full draft and then the final review that covers its code.

## Non-goals
- It does not change the design path for a ticket marked normal or risky.
- It does not change which model runs any activity.
- It does not change the rules for what counts as easy, normal or risky, only when the mark is set.
- It does not change the light path's conditions, its skipped code review or any round cap.
- It does not add a review to any ticket.
- It does not remove the ticket, the full gate, the final review or the owner's approval for any ticket.

## Solution
- The designer sets a ticket's `risk:` as the first step of design, before writing any of it, and the owner can still overrule it by editing the ticket.
- A ticket marked easy at that point gets a short note as its design: the problem, the change, the files, the one test that proves it, and what could go wrong.
- An easy ticket's test is written with the code, still before the code it tests, with no separate acceptance script beforehand.
- An easy ticket's design has no experiments section and no size record.
- An easy ticket with a short note has no separate design review, and takes the existing light path unchanged in its conditions (`z-nile/skills/z-ready/SKILL.md:8`).
- The light path's final review is that ticket's one review: it adds the short note to what it already checks (the trace and the code), so it replaces the design review and adds no review.
- That final review keeps its model (`final-review`) and the code round cap for an easy ticket (2).
- An easy ticket that does not meet the light path's conditions (an unlanded dependency, an `Overrides:` entry, or membership of a raft with other tickets) takes the full design path.
- The final review of an easy ticket may raise its level, which sends the ticket through the full design path, design review included, before it can merge.
- Every ticket keeps the full gate before merge, the final review on the best model, and the owner's approval of every guess.
- Approved by the owner (2026-11-22): after the lead walked through the proposal (a short design note, the test written with the code, no experiments section or size record, one review covering design and code, and a review that may raise the level and send the ticket through the full path), offering "approve", "approve but keep two reviews" or "reject", the owner answered: "I like easy path". The lead reads that as approval of the proposal as walked through, with the one combined review.

## Design
What the light path (f0420 step 3) already did: an easy ticket in no raft is readied by `./zn ready <issue>` into one single raft (`light: true`), skips the separate code review, and its `final-review-NNN.md` is the only code review (`light.reason`, read by `lightready.check`, `lightgit.reason`, the walk and `done`). What it did NOT do: the ticket still needed the full drafted design and its own separate design review (z-design steps 3 to 5, the review's file `design-review-NNN.md`). This chore builds only the rest, by extending that one rule and those readers.

**The easy path, step by step.**
1. The designer sets `risk: easy` FIRST (z-design, Risk), then writes, in the Design section, one `### Short note` with five labelled lines: `Problem:`, `Change:`, `Files:` (backticked paths), `Test:` (the one spec or command, backticked) and `Risk:` (what could go wrong). That is the whole design: no Touched-modules list beyond Files, no experiments, no `size:`, no acceptance folder, no design review.
2. The test is written with the code, still red first: the first commit holds the failing spec under the project's `tests/`, the second the code. `check.sh` has one `check "AC1: ..."` line that runs that spec.
3. `./zn status <id> prepared`, `./zn ready <issue>`, the build, `./zn finish`, as for any light ticket.
4. ONE review, `final-review-NNN.md`, on the activity `final-review` (`./zn model final-review --for <R-id>`, today `claude-fable-5-2`, never the model that built the code). In its Light path item it also judges the short note (is the change small, are the parts true, does the test prove it) and the design (zz-review Design mode's checks) beside the code.
5. The review may say the ticket is not easy by a line `Level: normal` or `Level: risky` (the Verdict line is as before). The engine then reads the ticket as not light: the walk and `done` refuse, naming the sentence below. The author raises `risk:` in the ticket by a tracker pull request, `./zn stop <R-id>`, and the ticket goes through z-design in full (every section, acceptance specs, experiments, size, design review), z-raft and `ready` as a normal or risky ticket. The full path may reuse the code already written.

**Which gates change for an easy ticket (and nothing else).**
- `./zn ready <issue>` (`lightready.check`) and `./zn work next` (the walk, `workread`) used to need no design review file either, but z-design demanded one; now `light.reason` itself says: an easy ticket with NO `design-review-NNN.md` in its folder is light only with a complete short note. An easy ticket WITH design reviews (the old path) is unchanged and needs no note. `check.sh` with a `check "` line, `design: ready`, the owner's approval, `Overrides: none`, the protected files and the 150-line bound all stay.
- z-design step 3 and 4 (acceptance specs, experiments, size, mutation check, promote dry-run) and step 5 (design review) stop being demanded of an easy ticket that has a short note. They are skill text; the engine never required the acceptance folder or the size for a light ticket (`work.luau`'s `fix` for an unsized issue is for rafting only).
- `./zn done` and the walk: the final review still is required (`reviewProblem`, `final-review-NNN.md` with an approve verdict or every finding answered); a missing or `changes` one still refuses. Not changed.
- Not changed: the ticket, tests first, the full gate (`check.sh --full`) before merge, the final review's model, the owner's approval of every guess, every round cap (`[rounds]`), the protected files, no check touching the real repository.

**The short note's check.** `easy.note(doc)` (new pure module `z-nile/engine/easy.luau`) returns `(found, problem)`: found when a heading titled `Short note` (any level) exists; the problem names the first missing or empty part, a `Files:` or `Test:` line with no backticked token, or a part given twice. Parts may carry a list marker and `**` emphasis, as `Overrides:` does.

**Raising is one way.** `easy.raised(reviews)` reads a `Level:` line in any `design-review-NNN.md` and `final-review-NNN.md` of the ticket's folder and the raft's folder; the highest of `normal` and `risky` wins, `Level: easy` raises nothing, a value that is not easy, normal or risky is a problem (fail closed). A raised level makes `light.reason` refuse, so a ticket cannot be lowered to easy after a design review that said normal (the design review's `Level:` line stays in its folder and the reader sees it whatever the ticket's `risk:` says), and an author deleting the line is the same act as editing a review (reviews are append-only by their numbering rule, zz-review).

**Modules.** `light.luau` gains two optional facts (`designReviewed: boolean?`, `reviews: { string }?`; nil means not judged, so every existing caller is unchanged) and calls `easy`. Readers: `lightready.check` reads the design-review files it already collects; `lightgit.reason` lists the ticket's folder at the layer tip and origin/main (`git ls-tree`) and takes `finals` from `lightgit.of` (the raft folder's `final-review-*.md`); `workread` passes the raft's `sides`. `lightgit.Verdict` gains `raised: string?` so `done` can name the full path. `easy.reviewTexts(files)` picks the review texts out of a name-to-text map: the one place that decides which files are reviews. No new top-level local in `main.luau`.

**Code review 2 changes (all built).** (1) A raised raft: the walk answers `release` (never a code review round) naming `./zn stop <R>`; `stop` reads the Level line from the layer's tip and publishes ONE move: the ticket gets `risk: <raised>`, `design: draft`, an empty `raft:` and a `new` stamp, the raft is emptied (`issues: []`) and given back; the publish exception is `publish.raiseEdit` (opt-in `raise`, the document may change only those keys, risk only upward). The raise therefore lives on main, the old branch cannot land (its raft is in the backlog, its ticket risk is not easy), and `work next <id>` answers `draft`. (2) `easy.raised` reads every spelling (list marker, `**`, indent, space before the colon, case, a closing period, no final newline); any other line starting with "level" refuses. (3) `light.PROTECTED` gained `easy.luau`, `rounds.luau`, `models.luau`, `modelcmd.luau` and `z-nile/.models.toml`, and `judgeDiff` now protects a `.models.toml` anywhere, tracker folders included: this edits a reviewed guard list. (4) `readycheck.reason` refuses an easy member with no design review unless the raft is the light path (`light`), and the walk answers `draft` for such a ticket with the reason it is not light. (5) `risk:` is a part of the approval contract only when present and not `normal`: normal tickets and every approval recorded before keep their hash (no open approval belongs to an easy or risky ticket today); an approval of an easy or risky ticket recorded before this goes stale once, which is the point. (6) The ticket's acceptance line for an easy ticket is `AC1: <the note's Test:>`, which the trace maps as any; z-design, zz-execute and AGENTS.md say so. (7) The short note counts only under `## Design`, its `Test:` path must appear in `check.sh`, and a design review counts only when origin/main holds it. f0448 extends the note with a root-cause part for a defect; this ticket does not build it.
- G5 `risk:` joins the approval contract only when not normal (an alternative was a version number on the contract: more machinery for the same effect). Example: c0100 approved at `risk: normal` keeps its hash.
- G6 A raised raft is emptied, not moved to `retired/` (retiring a raft is a rafting pull request, not a published move). Example: R0050 stays in the backlog with `issues: []`, which audit and `ready` already read as dead.
- G7 A line is a Level line when it starts with "level" after markers and emphasis; prose that starts a review line with "Level" must be reworded. Example: "Level of risk: low" refuses.

**Code review 3 changes (all built).** (A) `easy.raised` reads a line as a Level line only when it is `Level:` after heading marks, quote marks, list markers (`-`, `*`, `N.`), backticks and `**` are stripped; the first word after the colon is the value and the rest is ignored; fenced code is skipped; prose such as `Level 2 ghosts...` is no candidate. A candidate whose value is not easy, normal or risky is a problem, and `done`, `stop`, `ready` (design reviews) and the walk all refuse outright on it. (B) `publish.raiseEdit` is public and spec'd on its refusals; it needs the raft's origin copy to say `light: true`; the ticket and its raft must both be raised in one publish; a risk the author already raised on main (equal to the raised level) is accepted, a lower one is not; the light reason now says what the walk says. (C) The emptied raft stays in the backlog (the publish rules move a raft only between backlog and active; retiring is a rafting pull request), so the walk ignores a backlog raft that says `light: true` and lists no ticket. (D) The note's `Test:` must be a `.spec.luau` path that a `check "` line of `check.sh` runs, and the ticket must have an `AC1` line under Acceptance criteria; a spec takes a real easy ticket from note to the trace's claim derivation. (F) `readycheck`, `readyfacts` and `workread` are in `light.PROTECTED` (they hold the demand, the walk's draft answer and the raise); `main.luau`, `work.luau` and `contract.luau`: contract was already listed; `main.luau` and `work.luau` are not, because almost every tooling ticket touches them and protecting them would send those tickets to the separate code review. (G) z-ticket proposes `risk:` at capture as a recorded guess; `./zn model design --for <id>` reads it (spec: `level=easy`); the idea rounds and models are unchanged.
- G8 `risk:` is proposed at capture as a guess with `./zn guess --step design` (there is no capture step). Example: `./zn guess c0500 --step design --open "risk of c0500" --chose easy --why "a three-line fix"`.

### Scalability
One pure parse of one section and one line pattern per review file; no extra git call in the walk beyond one `ls-tree` of one folder for a ticket that is a light candidate.
### Maintainability
The rule stays in `light.reason` (one rule, one reader); `easy.luau` holds only the note and the level words; the skills state the path where they describe the steps (z-design, zz-review, zz-execute, z-work, zz-rules, AGENTS.md).
### Usability
An easy ticket's design is five lines; the refusal says which part is missing, or that the review says it is not easy and what to do next.

**Guesses (recorded, owner approves).**
- G1 The note is five labelled lines under a heading `Short note`. Example: `- **Test:** \`z-nile/tests/tooling/x.spec.luau\` case "refuses a bad id"`. Alternative: free prose checked by a reviewer only.
- G2 The ticket carries no marker for "short"; the absence of any `design-review-NNN.md` is what demands the note, so an old easy ticket with a design review is untouched. Example: c0100 (easy, design-review-001.md) needs no note.
- G3 "Not easy" is a `Level:` line in the review file, not a new verdict word, so `Verdict:` parsing and the round rules do not change. Example: `Verdict: changes` + `Level: normal`.
- G4 The walk and `done` refuse a raised raft by the light rule (so `done`'s existing refusal path), with a message that names the full path steps; no automatic status change of the ticket is made.
