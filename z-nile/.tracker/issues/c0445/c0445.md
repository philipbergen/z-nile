# c0445: Raft adjacent work together

## Problem
- The stages shown for a ticket have no rafting stage between design and code, so nothing shows that a ticket was grouped into a raft or that a light-path ticket skipped it.
- `./zn raft-plan` puts a prepared feature in a raft with another only when it depends on it or their touched paths overlap, so two prepared features with neither link always land as separate rafts, each with its own pull request, final review and full gate.
- `./zn raft-plan` already folds prepared defects into one open defect raft and chores into one open chore raft, linked or not, so this problem is about features only.

Decided with the user (2026-11-21, the bundle stage): the stages are seven (idea, design, bundle, code, review, approval, landed); "bundle" was chosen by the lead as the word recognizable without inside knowledge, for the owner to overrule; "raft" stays as the noun for one bundle and is explained in plain words ("a bundle of tickets delivered together") where a page first uses it; whether the noun is renamed is open (c0436).

## Non-goals
- No fixed test for "functionally adjacent" is added to `raft-plan`; adjacency is the worker's judgement, checked only through its recorded guess.
- Features, defects and chores are never mixed in one raft, by the policy or by an adjacency merge.
- No line cap is added to a worker's merge, and the cap `raft-plan` applies when it builds its own plan is not changed.
- How `raft-plan` folds defects and chores is not changed.
- No guess is required for a grouping a person asks for by argument.

## Solution
- Rafting is a named stage between design and code, shown wherever a ticket's stages are shown, and a ticket on the light path shows it as skipped.
- `./zn raft-plan` stays the fixed policy that needs no judgement: the same prepared tickets give the same plan as today.
- After the plan, the worker, not `raft-plan`, may merge planned rafts of the same type into one raft when it judges their tickets functionally adjacent.
- A worker's adjacency merge is not limited by `raft-plan`'s cap of about 1,500 changed lines, the same as `./zn recombine` with arguments, so two adjacent feature rafts of 1,000 lines each may be merged into one.
- Every merge the worker makes this way is a recorded guess (`./zn guesses`) that names the rafts and tickets joined, the merged raft's total changed lines and the reason they are adjacent, so no worker merge exists without its guess.
- A raft that `raft-plan` made, or that a person grouped with `z-raft <ids>`, `fold` or `recombine`, carries no adjacency guess and needs none.
- The owner can overrule each adjacency guess at approval, and an overruled merge goes back to the rafts `raft-plan` gave.
