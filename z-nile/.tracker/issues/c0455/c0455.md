# c0455: Lessons staging inbox and prune

## Problem
- At `c530e048b` there are 646 lesson files; counted with the engine's `lessons.load` and `lessons.sources`, 231 have no `from:` key and exactly one harvest citation (177 from a review finding, 54 from a `- note`), and another 78 have no `from:` key and 2 or more citations that all come from one item.
- zz-rules (`SKILL.md:221`) lets a lead write a new lesson file for any review finding or note that no existing lesson fits, without requiring that the mistake shipped as a defect or recurred in another item.
- A lesson with a `from:` key needs no harvest citation at all, the engine accepts any non-empty `from:` (`z-nile/engine/lessons.luau:145`), and zz-rules limits it in prose only (`SKILL.md:43`), so a lead can write a lesson for a single finding by adding `from:`.
- A `- note` harvest entry must name lesson ids (`z-nile/engine/lessons.luau:421`), so the only way to record a user correction, failed step or subagent candidate seen once is to cite or write a lesson.
- Deleting a lesson file that any `harvest.md` cites makes `./zn lessons --check` fail, so today no lesson can be pruned or graduated (`c0456`, `c0457`, `f0461`, `c0462`, `c0463`).
- `./zn lessons` prints every lesson with its citation count, most first (`lessons.luau:957`, `:993`), and none of its flags (`--skill`, `--when`, `--tag`, `--check`, `--uncovered`, `--unattributed`, `--check-review`) lists only the lessons at or above the "3-5+ citations" that c0455's Decided line sets for graduation.

## Non-goals
- This idea does not fold lessons into skill checklists (`c0456`) and does not build the mechanical linters (`f0461`, `c0462`, `c0463`).
- This idea does not audit, edit or delete any of the 92 lessons with a `from:` key, including the 4 that also have one harvest citation; listing their ids in `legacy-lessons.txt` changes none of their files, and any audit of them belongs to a later idea.
- This idea does not reword, merge or delete any of the 415 lessons that remain after the prune.
- This idea graduates no lesson: it only lists candidates with `--graduate` and defines the ledger row that a graduation writes.

## Solution
- A new ledger, `z-nile/skills/graduated.tsv`, holds one row per retired lesson: its id, its title, a reason (`pruned` or `graduated: <where>`), and the ids of every item whose `harvest.md` cited it when it was retired.
- `./zn lessons --check` (`z-nile/engine/lessons.luau`) accepts a citation of a ledger id only in the `harvest.md` of an item listed in that id's row, whatever directory the item is in, fails a citation of that id from any other item, and fails any lesson file whose id is in the ledger.
- The harvest rules in zz-rules Items, z-design, z-raft and zz-execute make `one-off: <reason>` the default entry for a review finding and for a note, and the engine accepts `- note <text> (erred <skill>): one-off: <reason>`.
- `--check` fails every lesson, `from:` key or not, unless it passes one of these: harvest entries in at least two different items cite it (review entries and notes count alike); it carries `cause: defect <id>` naming an item folder whose id starts with `d`; it carries a valid `recurs:` key; or its id is listed in `z-nile/skills/legacy-lessons.txt`.
- When a mistake recurs, the harvest rules have the later item's lead write the lesson under a new id, cite it in the later item's own `harvest.md`, and give it `recurs: <item> <review stem> F<n>` or `recurs: <item> note <text>`, naming the earlier entry; no other item's `harvest.md` is edited.
- `--check` fails a `recurs:` key that names an entry that does not exist, an entry in an item whose `harvest.md` also cites the lesson, or an entry that is neither a `one-off:` nor a citation of a ledger id.
- The zz-rules Items sentence on `from` (`SKILL.md:43`) says a `from:` key exempts no lesson from these tests.
- The prune deletes all 231 lesson files that have no `from:` key and exactly one harvest citation, and adds a `pruned` ledger row for each, even when the lesson came from a shipped defect.
- `z-nile/skills/legacy-lessons.txt` lists exactly 170 ids: the 78 kept lessons whose citations all come from one item and the 92 lessons with a `from:` key; `--check` fails a line that names no lesson file, and zz-rules forbids adding a line.
- `./zn lessons --graduate` lists every lesson with 3 or more harvest citations as `lessons.sources` counts them, most first, and a `from:` key does not count; at `c530e048b` that is 240 of the 415 lessons left after the prune.
- The threshold of 3 is a recorded guess, the low end of the Decided line's "3-5+", for the human to rule on; a threshold of 5 would list 142.
- After this change, `./zn lessons --check` passes on main, and no `harvest.md` has been edited.
- That includes the 126 pruned lessons whose only citation is in a backlog or active item (80 in backlog issues, 38 in active issues, 3 in backlog rafts, 5 in an active raft), because each of those items is listed in its lesson's ledger row.
