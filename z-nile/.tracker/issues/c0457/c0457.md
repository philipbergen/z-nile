# c0457: Single source skill boilerplate

## Problem
- The 26 registered skills in `z-nile/skills/` and `<project>/skills/` that are not aliases (all except `z-run` and `z-ticket`) each hold their own copy of the `## Lessons` text, and the 4 dispatch-step skills `z-idea`, `z-design`, `z-raft` and `z-code` each hold their own copy of the `## Headless (under `./zn dispatch`)` section, so changing either rule means making the same edit in up to 26 files.
- Four promoted specs require those copies word for word: `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0111/skill-docs.spec.luau` (the Lessons paragraph in each non-alias skill), `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0115/docs.spec.luau` test "every skill's lessons paragraph names the real folder" (three phrases inside each skill's `## Lessons`), `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0136/docs.spec.luau` test "every skill's Lessons section has the same paragraph naming the step" (the "A lesson goes to the step that erred" line, the same in each non-alias skill and at least 14 of them), and `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0117/docs.spec.luau` (the same Headless section, before Lessons, in each of the 4 dispatch-step skills).

## Non-goals
- No lesson is graduated or deleted, including `z-design/restated-rules-move-together`.
- The copies of the Lessons rule in AGENTS.md, in raft documents and in `check.sh` comments stay as they are.
- The procedural bodies of the pipeline skills are not trimmed (that is `c0467`).
- The dispatcher's code and step-prompt text, the rules for lesson-file frontmatter and `./zn skills --check` do not change.

## Solution
- `z-nile/skills/zz-rules/SKILL.md` keeps the only full copy of the `## Lessons` text, including the "A lesson goes to the step that erred" paragraph and the three phrases c0115 checks for, and gains the only full copy of the `## Headless (under `./zn dispatch`)` section, placed before its `## Lessons` heading.
- Each of the other 25 non-alias skills keeps its `## Lessons` heading, and the only thing under it is one line telling the reader to read the `## Lessons` section of `z-nile/skills/zz-rules/SKILL.md` and follow it.
- `z-idea`, `z-design`, `z-raft` and `z-code` keep their `## Headless (under `./zn dispatch`)` heading in the same place (after `## Guess mode` where that section exists, before `## Lessons`), and the only thing under it is one line telling a step run under `./zn dispatch` to read the `## Headless (under `./zn dispatch`)` section of `z-nile/skills/zz-rules/SKILL.md` and follow it before it does anything else in that step.
- The dispatcher's step prompt ("Read `<skillPath>` and follow it for <id>", `z-nile/engine/dispatch.luau:277`) stays as it is, so a dispatched step reaches the unattended rules (never guess a ruling, write `questions.md` and stop, never push to `main`) through the Headless pointer line in the skill it is told to read.
- No other skill gets any Headless text, the alias skills stay one line that hands over, and `./zn skills --check` does not change.
- `c0111/skill-docs.spec.luau`, the named test in `c0115/docs.spec.luau`, the named test in `c0136/docs.spec.luau` and `c0117/docs.spec.luau` are changed under explicit `Overrides:` lines, and no digest is added or changed for any of them.
- After the change, c0111, c0115 and c0136 each check that zz-rules' `## Lessons` holds the full text they check for now, and that each of the other 25 non-alias skills has a `## Lessons` heading whose only content is the pointer line naming `z-nile/skills/zz-rules/SKILL.md` and `## Lessons`.
- After the change, c0117 checks that zz-rules holds the full Headless section before its `## Lessons` heading, and that each of the 4 dispatch-step skills has the Headless heading before its Lessons heading, with the pointer line naming `z-nile/skills/zz-rules/SKILL.md` and `## Headless (under `./zn dispatch`)` as its only content.
- All four changed specs keep skipping the aliases as they do now, and `z-nile/tests/acceptance/f0399/docs.spec.luau` passes without any change because the Headless and Guess mode headings stay where they are.
