# c0463: Static check sh and sequestration lint

## Problem
- `check.sh` files with non-home-relative paths, broken `./zn find` fallbacks (`d0362`, `c0384`), branch-editable file reads inside sequestered checks, or promotion path mismatches are caught late on `main`'s full gate or in adversarial reviews instead of immediately inside `./zn check` and `./zn promote-check`.

## Non-goals
- Writing a duplicate `check.sh` parser separate from `c0120`/`c0371`'s `checkpaths` reader (`zz-arch` one-reader-per-format rule).

## Solution
- Extract and reuse `checkpaths.luau` as the single `check.sh` reader in `z-nile/engine/` shared by `c0120`/`c0371` specs, `./zn check`, and `./zn promote-check`, statically refusing non-home-relative paths (handling guarded `./zn find` fallbacks cleanly), branch-editable reads in sequestered checks, and promotion dry-run mismatches.
- Graduate `check-paths-from-the-home`, `checks-read-only-what-the-branch-cannot-rewrite`, and `checks-run-as-done-runs-them` into `z-nile/skills/graduated.tsv`.
