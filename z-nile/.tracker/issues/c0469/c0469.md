# c0469: Narrow c0458 rafting pins

## Problem
- `c0458` (Collapse reviews and tighten caps, prepared) ships a sequestered acceptance spec, `z-nile/.tracker/issues/c0458/acceptance/walk.spec.luau`, that `./zn done` promotes to `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0458/walk.spec.luau`. Two of its cases pin the issue-level `rafting` action of `./zn work next`:
  - "AC7: an easy ticket off the light path with no design review is not drafted and ready does not refuse it" expects `rafting` for a prepared, sized ticket in no raft (its line 148) and `fix` for the same ticket with no `size:` (line 151);
  - its AC9 case expects `rafting` for a prepared ticket in no raft once a raise has freed it (line 252).
- `f0464` (Retire raft PR stage) answers `ready` for such a ticket, sized or not, and lands after c0458 (`depends_on: [c0458, …]`), so these expectations are red the day f0464 is built, and a promoted spec is never edited during a feature's execution (z-design, Override check). f0464's design review 001 F1 found them; `c0468` narrows the other promoted pins of the stage and its captured Problem does not name this file.

## Non-goals
- No engine, skill or document change, and nothing of c0458's own behaviour: the cases keep every assertion that is not about the issue-level `rafting` action or the unsized `fix` (an easy ticket off the light path is not drafted, ready does not refuse it, the raise's release words).
- No promoted spec is removed, and no line of c0458's `check.sh` changes.

## Solution
- Once c0458 is done, the three expectations listed in the Problem are narrowed in `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0458/walk.spec.luau` so that each case passes both before and after f0464: the assertion that the action is `rafting` (or `fix` for the unsized ticket) becomes an assertion that the action is neither `draft` nor `none`, and every other assertion of the two cases stays.
- It lands after c0458 (`depends_on`), and before f0464 is readied (f0464 depends on it).
