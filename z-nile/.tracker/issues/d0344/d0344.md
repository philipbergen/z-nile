# d0344: Harvest after final review unfinishes

## Problem
zz-execute Phase 5 has the lead answer the final review and then confirm the harvest (step 4) with ./zn commit, after trace.md is committed. A final review finding needs a harvest.md entry and often a lesson file, and neither the raft's harvest.md nor a skill's lessons file is on a post-finish list (layergit.isPostFinishPath, trace's isTrackerFileOf and isAllowedAfterTrace), so that commit unfinishes the layer and makes the trace table stale. R0102 (2026-11-14) avoided it only by restacking and finishing again so the harvest rode inside the finish commit. Sibling of d0333, which adds only the raft's spend.md. Wanted: a raft whose final review has findings can harvest them as Phase 5 says and still land with ./zn done, with no second finish.
