# d0383: Watch net dead behind leftover worktree

## Problem
- On 2026-11-15 `./zn watch-kick` printed "watch-net: started, log at .cache/watch-net.log" and exited 0, although the net it started exited before creating its worktree, with `git worktree add -q .worktree/watch-net watch-net` failing (128) in `.cache/watch-net.log`.
- A `.worktree/watch-net` left from 2026-11-12 21:01, with no net process running, makes every later net fail at `git worktree add` with "watch-net is already used by worktree".
- The last "watch-net: the suite passed" commit on origin/main is from 2026-11-12 14:54, and nothing reported the three days without a pass, during which main went red in the fast tier (c0120/check-paths) and stayed red in the slow tier (c0084/commit-phase).

## Non-goals
- A net that exits after it has created its worktree is not reported by the kick that started it; only the 24-hour line shows it.
- This issue does not fix the red specs themselves (c0120/check-paths and c0084/commit-phase).
- This issue does not change what the net runs or when it is kicked.

## Solution
- `./zn watch-kick` returns only after the net it started has either created its worktree or exited, and if the net exited first, the kick prints the net's error line on its own stderr and exits non-zero.
- A net that finds `.worktree/watch-net` or the `watch-net` branch held by a worktree with no live net process removes that leftover and starts, and it refuses only when a live net holds it.
- When origin/main has a commit more than 24 hours newer than the last "watch-net: the suite passed" commit, `./zn watch-kick` and the board each print a line that names the date of that last pass.
