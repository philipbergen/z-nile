# d0406: Metrics reads idea spend rows

## Problem
- On main, `./zn metrics` stops with "attempt to index nil with chore" in the spend-by-phase table of `z-nile/engine/main.luau`, because `./zn idea` writes spend rows whose phase is `idea` or `idea-challenge` (for example `portal/.tracker/issues/f0396/spend.d`) and metrics only knows the phases in `ledger.PHASES` and `ledger.EXTRA`.
- Two slow-tier specs are red on origin/main because of it: `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0043/rounds.spec.luau` runs metrics on the real tracker, and `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0116/retired.spec.luau` runs rounds.spec in a clone.
- No spec proves that the other readers of spend rows (the board, spend summaries, lessons, rounds) finish on a tracker that holds `idea` and `idea-challenge` rows.

## Non-goals
- The spend-row format does not change.
- A phase other than the known ones and `idea` and `idea-challenge` stays rejected: `ledger.parse` still returns "unknown phase" and `ledger.append` still raises.
- `./zn idea` keeps writing the rows it writes today.

## Solution
- `./zn metrics` and `./zn metrics <id>` finish on a tracker that holds `idea` and `idea-challenge` spend rows.
- Metrics reports those rows' minutes and tokens under their own phases instead of dropping them.
- Both `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0043/rounds.spec.luau` and `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0116/retired.spec.luau` pass on main's slow tier.
- The board, spend summaries, lessons and rounds each finish without error on a fixture tracker whose spend rows carry the phases `idea` and `idea-challenge`, and a spec proves it for each reader.
