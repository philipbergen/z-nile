### F1 [high] The raft-only stamp leaves a prepared member claimable: a z-design re-claim during a rafting is silently overwritten by the rafting PR, and `ready` then hands the issue out under a live design claim
Where: §1 table, row "`status <R> prepared` (carried)" ("each member of the local `issues:` is `preparing` (stamped locally, as today) or `prepared`…"); prototype `setStatus` (main.luau, `if carried and t ~= id and cur ~= "preparing"`: any `preparing` stamp passes); z-design §1 ("refuses an issue another terminal holds"); z-raft §4 (a member "sends it back to z-design").
Evidence: reproduced (`scratchpad/c0116-review001/clone/tests/scratch/rv1.spec.luau`). Prepared, raftless c0010 and c0020. Terminal A: `new raft chore pair c0010 c0020` → R0010 `preparing-…`, members untouched on origin (as designed). Terminal B: `status c0010 preparing` (z-design re-claiming it, for example after a coherence finding sent it back) succeeds: origin c0010 = `preparing-lucky-quiet-crimson`, because nothing on origin says c0010 is being rafted. A runs `./zn sync` (any later sync does it: `raft-plan --apply`'s next op runs `raftingStart`, which merges `origin/main`), and B's status line merges in cleanly (A changed only `raft:`, nine lines away). A writes Theme, Order, a coherence review and `design: ready`, and runs `status R0010 prepared`: **accepted**, because c0010 is `preparing` on origin and any stamp passes; it rewrites c0010's `status:` to `prepared-…` locally. A's merge of `origin/main` is "Already up to date" and A lands: origin c0010 is now `prepared-heron-amber-raging`, `raft: R0010`, while B still holds `preparing-amber-walrus-amber` and is redesigning it. `./zn ready R0010` in the main checkout then **succeeds** ("readied R0010 … (c0010, c0020)") with c0010's old design. B's work later conflicts on a line whose claim is already gone.
Critique: the old flow stamped members with the raft's claim, so `prepared>preparing` on a member was refused for the window. The new lock takes that stamp away and relies on the `raft:` line, but the `raft:` line only conflicts with another *rafting*; a design claim edits `status:` instead (lesson architecture/lock-reads-live-state: the hold lives only on the rafting branch, so the re-claim cannot see it). The tempting fix is to make `status <id> preparing` refuse an issue a `preparing` raft lists in its origin `issues:` — that reads the stale list the lesson forbids, and misses a `new raft` whose `issues:` is written only after the publish.
Consequences: refusing at the carried move instead means an old-flow member legitimately `preparing` with the raft's own stamp must still pass (`new raft` over `new` issues, `status <R> preparing` over a draft raft both stamp members with R's value), and recombine's members pass only through the other raft's shared stamp (they are `prepared`, so unaffected). A refused member is then a conflict-like event z-raft must explain: the agent must not "take ours" on a `status:` conflict either (today's Conflicts paragraph speaks only of `raft:` and `depends_on:`).
Recommendation: in the carried move, a member that is `preparing` on origin passes only when its origin `status:` equals the raft's origin `status:` (R's stamp); otherwise refuse "<id> is claimed by another terminal (preparing-…): drop it from <R> or release". Add the same sentence to §1's rule and table, zz-rules' **Rafting** bullet and z-raft's Conflicts paragraph ("a conflict on a member's `status:` means it was re-claimed for design: take `main`'s line, drop the member, prepare again"). Spec case in claims.spec: exactly the reproduction above, expecting the carried move refused with the claimant's stamp named and origin's c0010 unchanged; a second case where the carried move ran first and the later sync shows `CONFLICT` on c0010's document. Mutant: "any `preparing` stamp accepted" (today's code). Rerun c0053's specs (they exercise old-flow members) to confirm they still pass.
Lesson: architecture/lock-reads-live-state

### F2 [high] Retiring a raft deletes its design reviews and `harvest.md`: following audit's own advice on today's tree turns `lessons --check` red and drops five design rounds from metrics
Where: §7 Orphans and the audit message ("retire it with ./zn recombine <a raft of its project and type> R0040"); §2 recombine (`git rm -r` of the other raft's folder); §10 ("their design reviews stay in their raft folders and still count"); G8.
Evidence: R0040, the one orphan the warning finds today, holds `design-review-001..005.md`, five refutes and `harvest.md`. In the scratch clone, removing `z-nile/.tracker/rafts/R0040` (what `recombine R0030 R0040` does in its branch) makes `./zn lessons --check` exit 1 with `z-design/no-check-red-between-steps: no source: no harvest entry names it and it has no from`, so the rafting PR fails the gate (AC8's own check). `./zn metrics` before removal lists `| R0040 | 5 | 0 | second-order |`; afterwards the rounds are gone. The same happens for a full move of any legacy prepared raft with review history (R0012 holds five rounds, R0035 four), and for a z-raft raft whose coherence review and harvest are in its folder.
Critique: retirement was designed as deleting a raft document; the folder also holds the review history that lessons (sources) and metrics (rounds, before/after split) read. The tempting fix (skip `lessons --check` sources from deleted folders, or say "don't retire rafts with reviews") either loses the data silently or leaves R0040's warning with no remedy.
Consequences: moving the files into the keeper collides with the keeper's own `design-review-NNN.md` numbers and would count O's rounds as K's; keeping the folder with only its document removed breaks `lessons.load` and audit, which find items by their document.
Recommendation: a retired raft keeps its review history in place: `recombine` deletes only the raft's document and `check.sh` when the folder holds review, refute or harvest files, and leaves them under a marker the readers know (for example a `retired.md` stub naming the keeper), with `lessons.load`, `designRoundsIn`/metrics and audit reading such a folder as a retired raft (rounds still counted, not drawn, no orphan warning); or pick another shape that keeps both readers whole, and say which in §2 and §7. Spec cases: `recombine` retiring a raft that has a `harvest.md` keeps `lessons --check` green and metrics' rounds for it unchanged; run against a copy of R0040's files. Mutant: the whole folder deleted (today's code).

### F3 [medium] G4 counts a member with no `size:` as 0, so every open raft today looks empty and the plan fills it past the captured cap
Where: §3 rule 5 ("a member with none counts 0"); G4; §10 ("Prepared issues without `size:`: none on today's tree is raftless").
Evidence: R0012 (prepared feature raft, `single: false`, members f0027, f0028, f0029) and R0035 (four features) are open rafts, and none of their members has a `size:` line (no `size:` in any issue document on the prototype's tree). A prepared feature of `size: 1500` whose Touched paths overlap R0012's members gets `./zn fold R0012 <id>` (0 + 1500 ≤ 1500), however large R0012 already is. The capture says "A raft that would pass about 1,500 changed lines starts another"; this guess knowingly breaks that for every raft prepared before c0116, and §10 only looked at raftless issues.
Critique: lesson ticket-prep/new-rules-migrate-existing-work: the size rule meets existing prepared rafts on day one. Counting an unknown as 0 is the unsafe direction; the plan already treats an unknown candidate size as a skip (rule 2), and an open raft should get the same treatment.
Consequences: refusing to fold into a sizeless raft means today's open feature rafts never take a new member without an argument, so the plan opens a new raft instead (harmless; z-raft with an argument can still fold). Backfilling `size:` on existing members is a design edit on prepared issues, which needs z-design and a PR per issue.
Recommendation: an open raft with a member that has no `size:` is not open to the no-argument plan (the plan opens another raft and prints `skip-fold <R>: <id> has no size:`, or simply never picks it), stated in §3, G4, §10 and z-raft §1; plan.spec case: an open raft whose member lacks `size:` is not folded into. Mutant: counted as 0 (today's rule).
Lesson: ticket-prep/new-rules-migrate-existing-work
