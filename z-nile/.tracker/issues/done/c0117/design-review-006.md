### F2 [medium] G13's reason ("the step's own skill records its minutes") doesn't hold for a dispatched review: a design or raft review's minutes are now recorded nowhere, while a code or stream review's printed line still carries the dispatcher's wall minutes

A nested review's minutes also overlap its author's wall time (the author waits through it), but the author's skill already records "answering minutes" apart from the review, so the overlap matters only if the author counts its waiting time.
Recommendation: escalate to the user, as a reading of G13: "Dispatcher row 0 min applies to author steps, whose skills record their minutes. A dispatched reviewer records none. With yes, `record` uses `a.minutes` for `zz-review` steps, and §5, G13 and zz-rules Spend say so.
