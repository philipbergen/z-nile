z-nile/.tracker/issues/active/c0156/acceptance/pin.spec.luau
	every line break counts: turning any one into a space changes the text
	a vertical tab or a form feed, which a Luau comment runs through, is kept as it is: no space, no line break
	blank lines are kept: one more or one fewer changes the text
	runs of spaces and tabs within a line are one space, and a line's trailing whitespace goes
	CRLF and a lone CR are line breaks like LF
	item ids are masked, as whole words of f, d, c or R
	a comma before a closing brace goes, across at most one line break
	the blank lines at the start and end of the text go
	nothing else is tolerated: a changed token, a deleted line, a comment prefix, a swapped order
	normalising twice is normalising once
	the digest is the SHA-256 of the normalised text, as lowercase hex
	joined: the line after the comment goes onto it, with one space, and nothing else changes
	joined reads its text and its comment through the normaliser: line endings, ids, indentation
	joined refuses a comment no line ends in, and a comment followed by a blank line
	without: deletes the one occurrence, read through the normaliser, and nothing else changes
	without refuses a piece that is empty, absent or repeated
