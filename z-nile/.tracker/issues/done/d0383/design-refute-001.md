# Design refute 001

- F1: fixed: the ready marker and both bounds are flags (`./zn watch-net --ready`, `--start`; `./zn watch-kick --wait`, `--start`); no variable is read or set (Design §1, guess G12 replaces G4); a case dumps the environment of the net's suite, and E6 runs the promoted `c0278/readers.spec` green inside a net started by the new kick; consequences: the two commands gain flags in their usage text, and `tests/lib/cachepaths.luau`'s usage-line entry changes with it.
- F2: fixed: the reaper skips the net's shell and the tree below it, recognised by the kick's exact script and argv0 and only up to the net's own time plus ten minutes (Design §4, guess G6); six pure cases (AC3), and E5 shows `./zn reap --dry-run` beside a real fixture net lists a planted orphan and not the net, and lists the net when the exemption is removed; consequences: a net stuck past 70 minutes is reaped as before; a spec's fixture net is exempt for the same bounded time; the script text now lives in `reap.luau`, so a change to the kick's shell line is a change to the exemption.
- F3: fixed: one lock per repository under the main checkout's `.cache/watch-net/`, held only by the live process that wrote it; a second net asks for a rerun and exits 0 with "already running", the holder reruns once, at most four rounds (Design §3, AC2); consequences: a kick can now succeed without starting a net of its own; a net that dies by an error drops the request (guess G8); `lib/procstart.luau` is new and duplicates the runner's start-id script (guess G9).
- F4: fixed: the kick's poll, the guard's wait and the rounds are counted `for` loops, `tests/lib/loops.luau` is not edited, and AC5 runs `c0193/tree.spec` and `tests/meta/scanning-loops.spec` by name; consequences: none.
- F5: fixed: guess G1 is withdrawn (G11): the net bounds its own start and the kick fails at its backstop, on a dead shell and on an early exit, so it never reports a start it has not seen; consequences: a kick can now exit 1 while a slow net is still within its own limit of 120 s; the line says where the log is.
- F6: fixed: every kick has its own log, ready, exit and pid file; nothing is cleaned or shared, and the mutant that shares the names again is killed by five cases; consequences: files accumulate, so the kick prunes to the newest 20 kicks.
- F7: fixed: the files live in the main checkout's `.cache/watch-net/`, the kick prints the log's absolute path, a case removes the raft worktree and reads the log, and AGENTS.md's list of ignored files gains the folder; consequences: `tests/tooling/watch-map.spec.luau`'s landing case reads the path from the printed line.
- F8: fixed: escalated to the user, whose answer is the Problem's scope decision of 2026-11-19 ("Only source commits count"); `watch.newestSource` with `trackerstate.under` as the one reader of "tracker file" (Design §5); consequences: a day of tracker-only commits prints no line, and `trackerstate.under` becomes public.
- F9: fixed: the specs hold on marker files with bounded waits and share fixtures; lock 10.5 s, stale 7.4 s, reaper 1.1 s; the kick file is 33.7 s alone, against 35.6 s before, because three cases must wait out a limit; it stays in the fast tier (guess G10, for the user to confirm); consequences: under heavy load that file took 54 to 75 s.
- F10: fixed: the constant's case asserts the board calls `watchcmd.noPassLine` and never the pure rule, and the board's fixtures sit at the limit ± 60 s; consequences: none.
- F11: fixed: zz-test "The net" and zz-execute Phase 6 step 1 are rewritten in the patch (Design §6), and the specs that read skill files pass; consequences: the worker now reports a failed kick in its final report.
