# f0339: Ticket explainer page

## Problem
- A person approving a ticket in z-nile is asked to answer y or n without first being shown a plain-words explanation, with before and after diagrams, of the idea and its design.

## Non-goals
- The page does not walk through the change line by line or replace the ticket's design text: detail beyond the high-level list of changes is left to asking an AI to walk through it.

## Solution
- z-nile records one self-contained HTML page per ticket that explains the idea and design in simple words, with a diagram of the system before, a high-level list of what changes where (which files get which functions and types), a diagram of the system after, and the pros and cons behind each choice, and the approve command opens that page rendered in the system's default app for `.html` files (the default browser, via `open` on macOS) and then waits for a y/n answer at the prompt.
