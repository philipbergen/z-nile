# f0399: z-work works every ticket

## Problem
- z-work takes only rafts that are ready, so a ticket that is captured, designed or prepared but not yet rafted and readied gets no work from a z-work session.
- Nothing states the order in which z-work would pick among tickets at different steps and with different dependencies.

## Non-goals
- z-work never lands a ticket the human has not approved, however green its gates are.
- This ticket does not change what `./zn dispatch` does, which stays with c0267 and c0252.
- This ticket does not define stacked PR delivery or part landing (c0248), work rank beyond the order above (c0249), or how an added issue waits inside its raft (c0394).

## Solution
- With no raft named, z-work picks any ticket that is not landed and does its next step (design it, fix it, raft it, or implement it), using its best judgement where the ticket leaves a choice open.
- z-work picks in two tiers, and the tier always wins: every ticket with no unmet dependency comes before any ticket that has one.
- Inside a tier, z-work picks the ticket furthest along first (ready raft, then rafted, then prepared, then designed, then captured), so a captured ticket with no dependency is taken before a prepared ticket that depends on an unlanded one.
- A ticket has an unmet dependency when a ticket it depends on has not landed on main.
- z-work starts the second tier only when no ticket in the first tier has a step left that it can do without the human.
- z-work branches a ticket with an unmet dependency off that dependency's branch, not off main.
- z-work puts a ticket and the tickets it depends on in one raft.
- z-work records every guess and assumption it makes in the ticket it made it for, each with what it chose and why.
- A ticket picked up before it was approved may go through every step up to landing, and does not land until the human approves it.
- A ticket with a material change, guess or judgement call pauses after all its gates pass, and continues only on the human's approval.
