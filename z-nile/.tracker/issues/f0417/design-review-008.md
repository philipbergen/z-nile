### F1 [high] Nothing runs `workread`, the one place that turns `idea.fact` into a `work next` pick, so a `workread` that never fills `idea`, or fills it only for marked tickets, passes AC4 and leaves S3 and G34 undelivered

Critique: this is the gap zz-test/one-mutant-per-wiring-point exists for. The design threads a decision through a reader at the edge and pins only the decision. The fix the author will be tempted to make is a `reasoned` row. That is not justified for the idea fill: `workread.luau` and the fixture route (`trackerfix` running `./zn work next`, as `f0399/land.spec.luau:428,713` already does) both exist on main, so the case can be written and mutation-run now.

Neither changes a guess.
