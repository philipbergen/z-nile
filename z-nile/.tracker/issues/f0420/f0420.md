# f0420: Named raft works until landed

## Problem
- Today `./zn work <R>` stops the session the first time `./zn work next <R>` says `end` or `wait`, which can be well before the raft has landed (for example when a member still needs design or re-prep, the raft needs rafting or readying, or a layer is unfinished).

Scope decision (user, 2026-11-20): "walk the graph, deliver the other rafts first in the order necessary" and "make branches and branch off of those branches" (a named raft is delivered, not stepped: today `z-work <R>` does "one tiny little part of the work" and stops, and ten runs still do not finish it. With a raft named the session keeps going until that raft has landed. When the raft depends on other rafts, the session does not refuse and name them: it shows the dependency graph, delivers those rafts first in the order the graph needs, from the back and as parallel as it can, and then the named raft. A raft with unapproved guesses is not a stop: its work stays on a branch, and the rafts that depend on it are built on branches made from that branch, so one approval session can approve the whole stack and, when every guess was good, every branch just merges. Nothing unapproved goes to the final gating review or to main. This widens this ticket past its captured Non-goal "The session does not take up any other raft": it takes up the rafts the named one depends on, and no others.)

Decided with the user (2026-11-20): "unblock the most, we want parallelism and progress" (the order among workable units, for a named raft and for `./zn work` with no raft named, is what unblocks the most, then rank, then the lowest id. The Non-goal below that `./zn work` with no raft named does not change holds for its loop and its stops, not for its order: f0419 asked for that order and this ticket delivers it as its last build step, so that step needs no further yes from the owner.)

## Non-goals
- `./zn work` with no raft named does not change.
- The session does not take up any other raft, before or after the named one lands or stops.
- No approval is skipped or guessed past: nothing unapproved lands, and the session does not run `./zn approve` itself.
- The session does not idle or poll for the person's approval or answers; it stops, and the person re-runs `./zn work <R>` to resume.

## Solution
- With a raft named, the session keeps taking that raft's next action, whatever its stage, and stops in exactly two cases: the raft has landed on main, or the only remaining next action is one that only the person can clear.
- A person-only stop is a `wait` for `./zn approve`, or a blocked raft that has written `questions.md` and run `./zn stop <R>`.
- When the session stops before the raft has landed, its last message names the raft, the stage it reached, and the exact command or answer the person owes.
- A `wait` that the session can clear itself (a gate, a review or a merge still running) is never a reason to stop: the session holds and then continues.

## Design
A named raft is a *target*. `./zn work next <R>` stops answering for R alone: it answers with the next step that unblocks the most towards R landing, across R and every open unit R waits on. A unit is what the board's Flow draws: an open raft, or an open issue in no raft. The skill loops on that answer and stops only on `end` (R landed, or R released with `questions.md`) or `wait` (only the person can move anything left). The two action words are kept, not renamed, so that the pinned specs move as little as possible. **Guess (prep) G1**

**The walk (pure, `work.luau`).** `work.decide` with `asked = R` (a raft) does this:
1. R done: `end`. R active with `questions.md`: `release`. Both are unchanged.
2. The units come from `depgraph.units(rafts, issues)`, a new pure function that holds the rule the board uses today (board.luau ~1026; the board then calls it too). A raft waits on the open raft of each outside dependency, or on that dependency itself when it is in no raft. A raftless issue waits the same way. The closure is R plus every unit reachable over `waitsOn`.
3. Each unit in the closure gets one *unit answer*: an action, `waits on <ids>`, or an owed item (something only the person can clear). Rules for a raft: the `forRaft` action when active. When it is a backlog raft: `ready` when claimable; `ready --on <bases>` when its only unmet dependencies sit in 1 to 3 active rafts whose layers are all finished (rule 4 below); `fix` when `work.fixable`; `draft` for the first member that is not `design: ready`. A raftless issue gets its `issuePick` action with the tiers ignored (`draft`, `resume`, `rafting`, `fix`), or `ready` when it is the light path (below). `questions.md`, an idea the person must answer (`draftBlock`), a closed dependency, a changed approved contract, an unapproved member once the final review is approved, and a dependency cycle (`depgraph.bands`' second result) are owed.
4. The closure's workable units are ordered by `depgraph.order(ids, depgraph.unblocks(closure))`: the most dependents first, then the id. The first one's Decision is returned, with `For R: ` in front of its why when the unit is not R.
5. When nothing is workable: if anything is owed, the answer is `wait`, and its why lists every owed item as an exact command or file (`./zn approve f0377 (R0093)`, `answer <path>/questions.md`), separated by `; `. When R is backlog with `questions.md` and nothing else is owed, the answer is `end`, as today. Otherwise it is `wait` naming what R waits on that is outside the facts.

`--one` (`./zn work next <id> --one`) is today's answer for that one unit (`forRaft` or `forIssue`). It is what a subagent runs. Under `ZN_DISPATCH=1` a named raft is always `--one`, because `dispatch.next` keeps its own decision. A named issue, and every unnamed call, also stays `--one`.

| situation (R named, not landed) | today | new |
|---|---|---|
| backlog, an outside dependency not done | `none` | the closure's first workable unit's action |
| backlog, a member not `design: ready` | `none` | `draft` that member (z-design, guess mode) |
| backlog, ready refuses for a repairable reason | `none` | `fix` (the same text as the unnamed `fix`) |
| backlog, dependencies in active rafts whose layers are finished | `none` | `ready R --on A [--on D]` |
| backlog with `questions.md` | `end` | another unit's action while one is workable, else `end` |
| active, built, members unapproved | `wait`, "ask again with no raft" | another unit's action while one is workable, else `wait` listing every `./zn approve` the closure owes |
| active, approved, built on an unlanded raft | `wait` | that base's action (its `done`, or its owed approval) |
| backlog, a changed approved contract | `none` | owed: `./zn approve <id>` |
| done / active with questions / unknown id | `end` / `release` / `none` | unchanged |

**The graph (3).** `./zn work graph <R>` is pure `work.graph(input, R, cap)` over one `workread.collect`. `./zn work <R>` prints the same lines after the skill. There is one line per unit of the closure, band 0 first, and in each band the `depgraph.order` order, then a `now:` line (the first `cap` workable units, in order) and an `owed:` line:
```
R0099 needs 5 units; 2 can be worked now (at most 3 at once)
band 0  R0091  implement     unblocks 2  layer 2 f0388
band 0  c0410  draft         unblocks 2
band 1  R0093  owed          unblocks 1  ./zn approve f0377
band 1  R0095  waits on c0410
band 2  R0099  waits on R0091, R0093, R0095
now: R0091, c0410
owed: ./zn approve f0377 (R0093)
```

**Building on unapproved work (4).** Approval gates only the final gating review and the landing, so a unit that waits on approval does not block the units above it. A raft's *bases* are the unlanded rafts that hold its members' outside dependencies, counting direct dependencies only.
- 0 bases: layer 1 starts from `origin/main`, as today. 1 base: `<R>/base` is that raft's top layer plus the tracker-restore commit (`layergit.buildBase`, f0399, as today).
- 2 or 3 bases: `buildBase` detaches at the lowest-id base's top tip, runs `git merge --no-ff --no-edit` with each other base's top tip in id order, then makes the same tracker-restore commit (`tracker: base R on A D`). The ready commit's trailer becomes `On: A D` (`readyRecord` reads a list, so one id still parses), and `ready` takes `--on` more than once. A merge conflict is refused at `ready`, naming both rafts, and the walk shows R as `waits on` until one of them lands. More than 3 bases also waits. The limit is there because every extra base is one more merge to redo on every restack and one more place for a conflict. **Guess (prep) G2**
- A chain (A unapproved, B on A, C on B and D): C's bases are B and D. A reaches C through B's top. There is no depth limit: restacks are mechanical and the walk orders them.
- A base moves (a fix after an approval session, or its own restack): `workread`'s `stacked` also asks that each base's top tip be an ancestor of R's layer 1. R's answer is then `restack`, and `./zn restack R` already rebuilds `<R>/base` from the moved tops. The walk does these restacks in dependency order, because a lower raft unblocks more. f0423's send-back uses the same restack.
- All approved: each raft runs `done` in band order. `done` already refuses while a base has not landed. After a base lands, the raft above it answers `restack` (`buildBase` drops the landed base, and with none left the base is main), then `done`.

**Parallelism (5).** A raft's units in the same band, and any workable units with no path between them, are independent. The session that was named R is the *lead*. It repeats: run `./zn work graph R`, and for each `now:` unit it has no subagent on, start one plain general-purpose subagent (`model: "sonnet"`, AGENTS.md; reviewers on the review model, as zz-execute says; f0421's model per activity once it lands). The brief is the unit id, z-work in `--one` mode, the worktree its action names, and `./zn run` for every probe. A subagent loops `./zn work next <unit> --one` and returns when the line is a lead action (`ready`, `release`, `done`, or a pull request that is green and ready to merge), `wait`, `end` or `none`. The lead does every lead action itself, one at a time, because each one publishes to `origin/main` or runs from the main checkout. It also never runs two `done`s at once (each runs the affected gate on the done tree). The lead merges pull requests too, so every publish to main goes through one place. **Guess (prep) G3** The cap is `work.PARALLEL = 3` **Guess (prep) G4**, changed with `work graph --parallel <n>` (1 is serial). The runner's eight shared lanes take three `--affected` gates and the lead's one `done`. The lead waits on its subagents' notifications and never polls (that is the Solution's "holds and continues").

**Order among equals (6).** `work.rankKey` goes. `work.order(ids, input)` is `depgraph.order` over the unblocks of the whole open graph (not the scope), and it takes c0249's rank as the second key when that lands. `nextClaimable`, `lowestIssue`, the held, fixable and `--on` picks of the unnamed `decide`, and the walk all use it. Without dependencies it is the id, as today. It is the last build step, because the Non-goal says the unnamed `./zn work` does not change. The brief asks for the change, and the owner said "unblock the most". **Guess (prep) G5**: step 6 needs the owner's yes before it lands.

**Light path for an easy raftless ticket (7).** An issue in no raft that is `design: ready` with `risk: easy` (f0421) goes from designed to done in one pull request, the layer's:
1. `./zn ready <issue>` creates a `single: true` raft (the `new raft` templates) in the same tracker-only claim commit that moves it to `active/`, instead of refusing "has no raft". **Guess (prep) G6**
2. `./zn start`, build, `./zn finish`, `./zn restack` only if main moved.
3. `./zn trace R`, then one final review (Opus, final mode), which also reviews the code. **Guess (prep) G7** Its findings are answered once and harvested.
4. `./zn approve` if it has guesses, then `./zn done`.

What is skipped: z-raft, `raft-plan`, the rafting pull request, and the separate code review (`review-NNN.md`, `refute-NNN.md` and its harvest). `RaftFact.light` (single, and its one member easy) sends `forRaft` from restack straight to trace. What stays: `check.sh`, the raft's RC check, the affected gate at `done`, promotion, `trace --check`, the final review's verdict, approval and `## Open to the human`.

**Stops (8).** The only stops before landing are the walk's `wait` and `end`. The last message is three lines: `R0099 stopped at <R's stage>: <k> of <n> units done`, then each owed item as its exact command or answer (until f0423 lands, `./zn approve <id>` per member; after it, `./zn approve R0099`), then `Then run ./zn work R0099 again.`

**Seams.** f0421: `risk:`, a review whose findings are all answered counts as settled (so the walk never sits on `review`), and the models for the subagents. f0422: owns tributaries and the board, and shares `depgraph.units`; `./zn work` prints nothing of its own. f0423: the approval walkthrough, `./zn approve <scope>` (whose raft scope must include the rafts it is built on), moving `wait` before the final review, the prep-branch base for a dependency still in design, and send-back. It builds on rule 4 here rather than writing its own base rule.

**Prototypes.** These were judged from their logs only. `proto-d0331-r2`'s `stack.baseSubject` (the base commit known by its subject) is what a merge base needs, so take it in step 5. `proto-c0248-r3` is the same base work on top of it: read its `aboveLandingBase` before step 5. `proto-c0249`'s rank fills step 6's second key later. `proto-c0253` is a session-level loop that this skill text replaces, and nothing of its engine is reused.

### Scalability
The walk and the graph are pure over the facts `workread.collect` already reads, O(units + edges) per call. The new git work is one `merge-base --is-ancestor` per base per active raft in `stacked`, and in `buildBase` one merge per extra base, run on ready and restack only.
### Maintainability
There is one unit rule (`depgraph.units`) and one order (`work.order`) for the board, the walk and the unnamed picks. A new kind of unit answer joins step 3 of the walk only.
### Usability
Usage text: `work graph <R> [--parallel <n>]`, `work next ... [--one]`, and `ready <R|issue> [--on <R>]...`. The `work` refusals name the option. `work graph` joins the usage block. It is not in READ_ONLY, because `work` guards the root as today.
