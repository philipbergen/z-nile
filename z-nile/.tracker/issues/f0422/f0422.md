# f0422: Tributaries and banded board

## Problem
- The board has no grouping by theme, so the owner cannot tell from it which of z-nile's roughly 80 open tickets and 20 open rafts belong to which body of work, or how much of each body is done.
- The board shows what a raft depends on and what it blocks only as text lists inside one opened card, so no view shows what can be worked now or how far every other item is from workable.
- The board never draws the dependency links of a raft, so the owner cannot follow one raft's chain upstream and downstream without opening each card in turn.
- A worker can be started on one raft or on everything, but not on one theme.

## Non-goals
- This does not change which rafts the board reports as workable, or what each raft depends on and blocks, for any raft on main today; it only adds the band number and the unblocks count on top of those results.
- This does not print the dependency graph from `./zn work <raft>`, which stays in f0420's scope decision of 2026-11-20.
- This does not change how the worker takes, carries or finishes a unit, which is f0420's; it adds only the tributary filter and its stop rule.
- This does not add or change any status column or lifecycle step.

## Solution
- A tributary is a named group with a one-line purpose, kept in one editable ordered list whose entries are rafts and raftless tickets.
- A ticket in a raft belongs to its raft's tributary and is never listed itself, and a done raft or ticket stays in the tributary that lists it.
- The gate fails, naming the item, when a raft or raftless ticket (open or done) is listed in no tributary or in more than one.
- `./zn list <tributary>` shows only that tributary's rafts and tickets.
- The board shows for each tributary four counts of its units (a unit is a raft or a raftless ticket): done, in flight, waiting for the owner's approval, and not started.
- The board's main view places every open unit in exactly one band, top to bottom: waiting for the owner's approval, then workable now, then band N for N = 1, 2, and so on.
- A unit that is not workable is in band N, where N is one more than the highest band among the units it waits on, counting a workable unit as band 0.
- A raftless ticket is placed by the same rule as a raft, applied to the dependencies recorded on the ticket.
- A unit's "unblocks" count is the number of open units anywhere downstream of it, direct or indirect; each card shows that count and the units it waits on, and the workable band is sorted by it, highest first.
- Selecting a card highlights every unit upstream and downstream of it and no other.
- A focused view of one raft draws that raft and every unit it depends on, direct or indirect, as a graph with a line for each dependency.
- `./zn work <tributary>` passes the worker only the units of that tributary and stops when none of them is workable.
