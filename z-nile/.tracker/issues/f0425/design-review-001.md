### F1 [high] The idea stage rewrote the captured Problem into a smaller one, and no owner line covers what it dropped
Where: f0425.md `## Problem` (line 20 on), `## Non-goals` bullets 2, 3, 4 and 6; idea-refute-001 F3 and F4 and idea-refute-002 F3 answered `revised`

The idea challenge produced these cuts: idea-review-001 F3 (which ticket owns the real-data view) and F4 (guesses.md has no options field) were both answered `revised` by narrowing the outcome, not by an owner's ruling. No `Scope decision (user, …)` or `Decided with the user (…)` line records any of the four moves. f0450's "a" rules how to close (by reading the merged commits), not what f0425's scope is.

The designer saw this and asks the owner in the first open bullet, which is right. But the document as it stands already reads as the narrowed ticket: a reader of the Problem cannot see what was captured, and the trace will still derive P1 from the capture (the first claim is the captured sentence, as the probe shows), so the document and the trace disagree about what f0425 promised. The tempting fix is to leave the rewrite and rely on the open bullet.
Consequences: Restoring the captured sentence makes P1 visibly unmet in part by a mockup on simulated data, so the final review needs the owner's ruling to fill P1 (F7). Once the owner rules, each move that the ruling accepts needs its own line, and any outcome no other ticket owns needs a home (F2 lists two that have none).
Recommendation: Put the captured sentence back word for word as the first paragraph under `## Problem`, keep the three bullets after it as added detail, and mark Non-goals 2, 3, 4 and 6 as pending the owner's ruling on this finding (for example "(pending design-review-001 F1)").
