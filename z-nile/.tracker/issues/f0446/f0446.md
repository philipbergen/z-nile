# f0446: Stats explorer

## Problem
- `./zn metrics` is a fixed report that prints cost per issue (lines, agent minutes, tokens), totals by type, spend by phase, design-review cost per round, review rounds per raft, design rounds per issue, size estimates, and a rework rate by lines, issues and agent time.
- Only defect tickets count as rework, so work redone after a review finding or a send-back is not measured.
- Spend rows and the live layer's events carry the model, tokens, AI seconds and wall seconds, but no dollars, no lines and no mark of first pass or rework.
- The report does not give the time from idea to landed, the time in each stage, or the time waiting for the owner against the time waiting for a worker.
- The report does not give the share of findings found in the first review round or the share of guesses approved unchanged.
- A person cannot pick a measure, what it is divided by, a slice or a time range, cannot keep a view to open again later, and no chart updates when a step forward is recorded.

## Non-goals
- Rafts are not counted as a measure.
- The stats do not report what was actually paid: no subscription, discount, negotiated rate or invoice figure is read or shown.
- The stats do not show a rework figure that merges the three kinds without also showing each kind apart.
- The stats do not define, choose or change a work profile: they only read the name c0433 records, and until c0433 lands every row falls in the "none" slice.

## Solution
- Every recorded step forward carries these base measures as totals that add up: list-price dollars; tokens in, out and cached; AI time, wall-clock time and machine time for gates; lines added, removed and changed.
- Every recorded step forward also carries counts of tickets by type (feature, chore, defect), review rounds, findings, guesses, send-backs, gate runs and landings.
- The dollars of a step are its tokens priced from a per-model list-price table at the price in force on the day of the step, and every dollar figure shown is labelled as list price.
- Every step is marked first pass or rework, and rework is one of three kinds shown apart and summed: work after a review finding, work after a send-back, and a defect ticket charged to the ticket that caused it.
- Every base measure can be sliced by time (hour, day, week, month, year), ticket type, risk level, tributary, project, stage, model and alias, CLI and worker, and first pass against rework.
- Every base measure can also be sliced by work profile, which is the profile name c0433 writes on each spend row (the named set of relaxations a session ran under), with rows that carry no name shown as one slice called "none".
- The stats give any measure per unit of time, any measure per ticket by type, the rework percent of every measure, and cost per landed line.
- The stats give the time from idea to landed, the time in each stage, and the time waiting for the owner against the time waiting for a worker.
- The stats give the share of findings found in the first review round and the share of guesses approved unchanged.
- A person picks a measure, what it is divided by, a slice and a time range and sees the chart.
- A person can keep a view and open it again later.
- Every open chart updates when a step forward is recorded, without a reload.
