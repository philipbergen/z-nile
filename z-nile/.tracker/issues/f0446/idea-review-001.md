# Idea review 001

## Findings

- F1: Non-goals 2 and 3 are not real fences, and they contradict the Solution. Non-goal 2 says the idea does not rule on list price versus what is actually paid. But the Solution sentence "The dollars of a step equal its tokens priced from a per-model price table at the price in force on the day of the step" does rule on it. A dated price table per model is list pricing, and what is actually paid cannot be computed from it: a subscription, a discount or a different cache rate would break it. Non-goal 3 excludes nothing. It is a status note ("not settled until the owner rules"), yet the Solution states the three-kind rework split and the price-table dollars as settled things to build. Each non-goal should either name something the idea will not do, or leave the Solution sentences it contradicts out of the Solution.
