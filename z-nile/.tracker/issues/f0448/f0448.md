# f0448: Defect path proven and root caused

## Problem
- A defect can reach design without a failing reproduction or a written root cause, because `z-nile/skills/z-design/checklists/defect.md` asks for its Reproduction and Root cause items only at design.
- The escape entry c0316 designs for a defect (`- escape rework 1 (<stage>): …` in `harvest.md`) names one stage, so a defect can land with no record of what the other two of idea, design and implementation could have done to stop it.
- No command or gate reads a defect's Design for the answers to the defect checklist, so a design that leaves out the Siblings item is stopped only if the reviewer notices.

## Non-goals
- The path for features and chores does not change.
- No landing gate, gate helper or record outside `harvest.md` is built here: the refusal is c0316's at `status prepared`, `ready` and `done`, and the ticket holds no lesson or per-stage answer.
- The rule of c0316 that an issue correcting merged work names it with a `reworks:` line and is an escape is used as it stands, not rewritten.
- The short design for easy tickets that c0435 proposes is not built here.
- The wording of the defect checklist's Siblings, Expected behaviour, Regression guard and Smallest change items does not change.

## Solution
- A defect's idea stage is complete only when the ticket holds a reproduction that fails, kept as the test the fix must turn green.
- A defect's idea stage is complete only when the ticket states the root cause, naming the change that introduced it when that can be named.
- The defect checklist's Reproduction and Root cause items are rewritten to have the Design confirm and refine the ticket's reproduction and root cause, so each is written in one place and not asked for twice.
- This issue names c0316 in `depends_on`, and c0316's gates refuse a defect until its rework escape in `harvest.md` gives, for each of idea, design and implementation, a lesson id or a `one-off: <reason>`.
- A defect's design is refused as prepared by the engine unless its Design answers the checklist's Siblings item as that item stands, with every other place that makes the same mistake fixed in this issue or captured as its own.

## Design
A feature of the tooling: the engine (`z-nile/engine/`) and the skills, nothing under any game's `src/`. A defect gets its own path: it is proven and root caused before design starts, its design is the fix plus a search for the same cause elsewhere, and it cannot land until each of the three stages that let it through (idea, design, implementation, each with its review) has a lesson or a stated reason. "Code" in the owner's words is c0316's stage `implementation`.

The idea challenge's findings, answered here:
- idea-review-001 F1 (the design sentence answered no Problem): the third Problem sentence now says nothing reads the Siblings answer; section 3 gives the rule that does.
- idea-review-002 F1 (the checklist already asks for Siblings, Reproduction and Root cause): the checklist's Siblings wording stays (Non-goals), and the engine starts reading that answer (section 3). Reproduction and Root cause are written once, in the ticket at the idea stage, and the checklist items become "confirm and refine" (section 1).
- idea-review-003 F1 (c0316 already builds the landing gate): f0448 builds no gate. It adds one coverage rule inside c0316's `lessons.luau`, so c0316's own `harvestProblem` at `status prepared` and `done` refuses a defect with a missing stage (section 2), and it names c0316 in `depends_on`.

### 1. The idea stage of a defect: proven and root caused
Two sections in the ticket, after `## Solution`, written by `./zn idea` like the Problem (z-ticket: that command stays the only writer). The capturer does the work first, in the capture worktree: it reproduces the report and finds the cause, and passes the evidence to `./zn idea <id> --text`. Each part is a labelled line.

```
## Reproduction
- Test: `z-nile/.tracker/issues/d0450/acceptance/repro.spec.luau` fails on main: "expected exit 0, got 1"
  (or) - Ran: `./zn restack R0140`   - Saw: "restack stopped at R0140/2: conflict" and the branch unchanged   - Expected: the rebase left in progress
  (or, when it cannot be reproduced on demand) - Seen: <date, where, the evidence quoted>   - Tried: <each attempt, its conditions and result>
## Root cause
- Mechanism: <cause, then effect, down to a line, rule or setting: not the symptom restated>
- Evidence: <what shows the mechanism: a path and line, a dry run, a log line>
- Introduced by: <item id and commit> | <commit> | unknown: <what was searched: git log -S, git blame>
```
- **Proven** means a failing test (`Test:`, a spec in the item's `acceptance/`, which the fix must turn green and which is promoted at `done`), or a recorded command with its output (`Ran:` and `Saw:`). A crash, a panic or a rare flake that cannot be shown on demand has `Seen:` and `Tried:` (the standing lesson `z-design/reproduce-each-captured-report`, moved to this stage: every attempt under the reporter's conditions is recorded) and goes to the owner as a bullet `- Reproduction: <what was tried>. Decision: may design start?` under `## Open to the human`.
- **Root caused** means the mechanism, the evidence for it, and the introducing change. An unknown introducer needs only the search that failed. An unknown mechanism (`Mechanism: unknown: <what was tried>`) goes to the owner as `- Root cause: ...` with the same question; the owner may rule that design starts with finding the cause as its first experiment. Why no spec caught it is not asked here: it is the implementation stage's escape entry (section 2), so it is written once.
- **The challenge.** For a defect, `idea.challengePrompt` gets the two sections and a defect checklist after its three questions: does the Reproduction show the reported failure, and would it pass once the cause is fixed; if it is `Seen:`, were the attempts made under the reporter's conditions; is the Mechanism a cause leading to the effect and not the symptom; does the Evidence show that chain and not only the symptom; is the introducer named, or the search stated; does the Solution remove the cause and not only the symptom. Its findings are answered like any other (`revised`, or open to the owner).
- **The checklist** (`z-nile/skills/z-design/checklists/defect.md`): Reproduction becomes "the ticket's `## Reproduction`, confirmed: the `Test:` spec fails on main for the reported reason (`./zn check <id>`), or the design adds the spec that does; a `Seen:` report gets the check the owner ruled"; Root cause becomes "the ticket's `## Root cause`, confirmed against the code, refined in place when the design learns more". The other four items are unchanged.

### 2. The escape record: one mechanism, c0316's
The question for each stage, asked of the work that introduced the defect: what could this stage, or its review, have done to stop it? Each answer is one c0316 escape entry in the defect's `harvest.md`, written by the prep's lead (zz-rules, Lessons: a subagent proposes, the lead writes the lesson file):
```
- escape rework 1 idea (erred z-ticket): z-ticket/<slug>
- escape rework 1 design (erred z-design, upstream zz-review): z-design/<slug>, zz-review/<slug>
- escape rework 1 implementation (erred zz-test): one-off: <why that stage could not have>
```
- This is c0316's entry (`- escape rework 1 …: <lesson ids | one-off: <reason>>`) with c0136's step group, plus one word: the stage it answers. c0316 keys an entry by kind and number; f0448 keys it by kind, number and stage. The word is optional for every escape c0316 counts (absent, the stage is read from the group as c0316 does). For an item of `type: defect` with `reworks:` set, the rework escape is covered only when all three stages have an entry.
- What f0448 adds is that rule, in c0316's `lessons.escapes` and `lessons.uncovered`: the lines `<id> escape rework 1 idea|design|implementation` for each stage with no entry. c0316's `harvestProblem` already prints `uncovered`'s lines at `./zn status <id> prepared` and `./zn done`, so its gate refuses with no change. `lessons --check` refuses the stage word on an entry other than a defect's rework, an unknown stage, and two entries for one stage.
- A stage answers for itself as it is today: a lesson when today's skill would still miss it; `one-off` when today's skill already catches it (naming the rule that does) or the stage cannot see it (the zz-review challenge of every `one-off` is c0316's).
- `reworks:` is c0316's rule as it stands. When `Introduced by:` names an item, `reworks:` names the same one, and `lessons --check` refuses a defect where they differ.
- **What f0448 needs from c0316, and the order.** c0316 is not built (parked after its design review, `design: draft`); its blockers c0136 and c0264 are done. f0448 needs from it: the escape entry and its parser, `reworks:` and the gates at `status prepared` and `done`. So c0316 is designed and lands first. f0448 is built after it, all of it, since the Solution names c0316 in `depends_on`. The parts of sections 1 and 3 do not read c0316's code and could land earlier as a ticket of their own (guess G6). c0316's redesign quotes this section (lesson `z-design/quote-other-preps-before-promising`).

### 3. The short design of a defect
- Once proven and root caused, the design is the fix and the Siblings answer: the search for every other place the same cause may have broken, written as the checklist's Siblings item stands. The form the engine reads:
```
- **Siblings**: searched `grep -rn "rebase --abort" z-nile/engine`
  - `z-nile/engine/main.luau:4375`: fixed here
  - `z-nile/engine/stack.luau:88`: d0451
```
  or `- **Siblings**: searched `<command>`: none found`. Each place ends in `fixed here` or an issue id that exists. A sibling captured as its own issue joins this defect's raft (c0394: an added issue in the raft, never a side list).
- **With c0435.** An easy defect's short note keeps c0435's five lines, with `Test:` naming the Reproduction's spec, and adds a sixth, the `Siblings:` answer above. The cause is not repeated: it is in `## Root cause`. The reproduction spec stays in `acceptance/` even on the easy path, because it exists before design and before the risk is known.
- **Risk** applies as for any ticket: the designer sets `risk:` first (c0435), and the owner overrules by editing it. A risky defect gets the full design review (up to 9 rounds) and the separate code review. A defect that touches a file in `light.PROTECTED` is never light.
- At `./zn status <id> prepared` the engine also refuses a defect whose Reproduction names a `Test:` that no `check "AC…"` line of its `check.sh` runs, so the reproduction is kept as the test the fix turns green.

### 4. The steps, end to end
1. Capture (z-ticket): `./zn new issue d …` pushes the id at once. The capturer reproduces the report and finds the cause, writing the repro spec in `acceptance/`. A subagent that does the digging runs on `./zn model idea --for <id>`.
2. `./zn idea <id> --text "<evidence>"`: the author drafts the Problem, Solution, Reproduction and Root cause (`idea`). The challenger checks them against the defect checklist (`idea-challenge`), for up to `[rounds] idea` rounds.
3. **Owner**: rules every bullet under Open to the human, including a report that does not reproduce or a cause that is unknown. The idea PR lands (with the repro spec).
4. `./zn status <id> preparing` refuses a defect without the two sections in their form (section 5).
5. z-design (`design`): risk first, then the fix and the Siblings search, the check.sh line that runs the reproduction, and the three escape entries with their lessons. Reviews: `design-review` and `design-answer`, none for an easy short note (c0435).
6. `./zn status <id> prepared`: refuses a missing Siblings answer or an unrun reproduction (f0448), and a missing stage entry (c0316's gate with f0448's rule). **Owner**: `./zn approve <id>`, with the guesses.
7. z-raft (`raft`, and `raft-review` for several issues), z-ready, the build (`code`, then `code-review` unless the ticket is light, then `final-review`; answers on `code-answer` and `final-answer`; `trace`, `fix`), and `./zn done`: c0316's gate again, and the repro spec promoted. **Owner**: approves the landing.

### 5. What the engine enforces (small) and what is skill text
- New pure module `z-nile/engine/defect.luau` (facts in, one refusal string or nil out):
  - `defect.applies(front: { [string]: string }): boolean`: `type: defect` and `created:` on or after `defect.SINCE` (the day slice 2 lands). A capture cannot change `created:`, and older defects keep their path (G5).
  - `defect.ideaProblem(doc: string, facts: { exists: (path: string) -> boolean, known: (id: string) -> boolean }): string?`: the Reproduction holds a `Test:` with a backticked path that `exists`, or `Ran:` and `Saw:`, or `Seen:` and `Tried:` with a `- Reproduction:` bullet ruled. The Root cause holds `Mechanism:` (or `unknown: …` with a ruled `- Root cause:` bullet), `Evidence:`, and `Introduced by:` (an id that is `known`, a commit, or `unknown: <text>`). Each refusal names the first missing part and its form.
  - `defect.siblingsProblem(design: string, known): string?` and `defect.reproRun(doc: string, checkSh: string): string?` (section 3).
- `z-nile/engine/idea.luau`: `designRefusal(doc, review, id, facts?)` calls `defect.ideaProblem` when `defect.applies`. A nil `facts` refuses a defect that names a `Test:` (fail closed); a feature or chore is unchanged. `parseDraft(text, isDefect?)` reads and `apply` writes the two sections. `authorPrompt`, `answerPrompt` and `challengePrompt` take an `isDefect` flag and are byte for byte as today without it.
- `z-nile/engine/main.luau` (`setStatus`, `cmd.idea`) and `z-nile/engine/workread.luau` pass the facts. `defect` is required inside a function, as `rounds` is: no new top-level local.
- `z-nile/engine/lessons.luau` (after c0316): the stage word and the three-stage rule (section 2).
- Skill text: z-ticket step 1 (a defect is reproduced and root caused before `./zn idea`; a flake follows G1), the checklist's two items, zz-review Design mode (read Reproduction and Root cause as the ticket's, and judge the Siblings search), zz-rules Items (the stage word). The Lessons section that every skill shares does not change.

### Guesses for the owner (recommended answer first)
- G1 A flake is a defect on this path only when the code is wrong (a race in the engine). A flaky spec over correct code stays a chore (z-ticket). Example: "3 of 50 runs fail" is `Ran:` a bounded loop under `./zn run`. The test kept is a spec that forces the interleaving, or a one-time `check.sh`-only proof when none can.
- G2 The introducing change is named when it can be found. `unknown: <the search>` needs no ruling; an unknown mechanism does. Example: d0434 names R0020; code older than the tracker writes `unknown: git log -S shows the bootstrap 6a195ea05`.
- G3 A defect found in unmerged work is not a d ticket and has no escape record: nothing escaped to main. It is a finding of that raft, harvested there with its step group, or an issue added to the raft (c0394). Example: a bug in layer 2 found by layer 3's review.
- G4 A stage answers as it is today. Example: R0079 came before the idea stage existed (#299), and the idea entry is still a z-ticket lesson if today's z-ticket would still miss the problem.
- G5 Defects captured before slice 2 lands (d0434, d0426, d0442, …) are not re-gated at the idea stage. They still meet c0316's gate.
- G6 Keep one ticket after c0316, as captured. The alternative: sections 1 and 3 become their own ticket and land now; the owner decides.
- G7 The Siblings form is the one in section 3 (search command, then places and their fate). Alternative: free text, read only by the reviewer.

### Scalability
Two section parses per gate run on one document, and one `check.sh` read at `prepared`; no git calls are added. The coverage rule adds three keys per defect to c0316's single walk of the lessons tree.
### Maintainability
One reader per form: `defect.luau` reads the two sections and the Siblings answer, `lessons.parseHarvest` the entry. The defect checklist and the challenge's checklist name the same parts, and the docs spec pins them together.
### Usability
Each refusal names the missing part and gives its form. The owner sees an unreproducible report or an unknown cause as one ruling, before any design is spent on it.
