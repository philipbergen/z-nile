# f0448: Defect path proven and root caused

## Problem
- A defect can reach design without a failing reproduction or a written root cause, because `z-nile/skills/z-design/checklists/defect.md` asks for its Reproduction and Root cause items only at design.
- The escape entry c0316 designs for a defect (`- escape rework 1 (<stage>): …` in `harvest.md`) names one stage, so a defect can land with no record of what the other two of idea, design and implementation could have done to stop it.
- No command or gate reads a defect's Design for the answers to the defect checklist, so a design that leaves out the Siblings item is stopped only if the reviewer notices.

## Non-goals
- The path for features and chores does not change.
- No landing gate, gate helper or record outside `harvest.md` is built here: the refusal is c0316's at `status prepared`, `ready` and `done`, and the ticket holds no lesson or per-stage answer.
- The rule of c0316 that an issue correcting merged work names it with a `reworks:` line and is an escape is used as it stands, not rewritten.
- The short design for easy tickets that c0435 proposes is not built here.
- The wording of the defect checklist's Siblings, Expected behaviour, Regression guard and Smallest change items does not change.

## Solution
- A defect's idea stage is complete only when the ticket holds a reproduction that fails, kept as the test the fix must turn green.
- A defect's idea stage is complete only when the ticket states the root cause, naming the change that introduced it when that can be named.
- The defect checklist's Reproduction and Root cause items are rewritten to have the Design confirm and refine the ticket's reproduction and root cause, so each is written in one place and not asked for twice.
- This issue names c0316 in `depends_on`, and c0316's gates refuse a defect until its rework escape in `harvest.md` gives, for each of idea, design and implementation, a lesson id or a `one-off: <reason>`.
- A defect's design is refused as prepared by the engine unless its Design answers the checklist's Siblings item as that item stands, with every other place that makes the same mistake fixed in this issue or captured as its own.
