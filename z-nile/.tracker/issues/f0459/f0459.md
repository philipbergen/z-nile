# f0459: Agy session adapter and Gemini lineup

## Problem
- Antigravity (`agy`) currently requires manual `ZN_CLI=agy` and `ZN_MODEL=<id>` environment variables, stamps every session row `(declared)`, locks the worker's model across mid-session `/model` changes, and leaves interactive `agy` token spend unmeasured even though `agy 1.5.18` exports `ANTIGRAVITY_CONVERSATION_ID` and `ANTIGRAVITY_APP_DATA_DIR` and flushes live NDJSON usage and model IDs to `transcript.jsonl`.
- `z-nile` has no `agy-cli` headless adapter on `main` (`c0175` is blocked behind `c0251` and `c0325`), `[clis.agy]` in `.models.toml` maps `xlight`, `light`, and `heavy` writers all to `gemini-4-neon-medium` while leaving `gemini-4-hydrogen` and `gemini-3.8-flash` unused, and interactive lifecycle commands (`status preparing`, `status prepared`, `commit`, `finish`, `done`) do not mechanically refuse wrong-model AI sessions or unverified review models.

Decided with the user (2026-11-27): eliminate `ZN_MODEL` completely by reading `agy`'s live `transcript.jsonl`, wire the `agy-cli` headless adapter now ahead of `c0175`, map the full 5-family Gemini lineup at `-high` / `-xhigh` (`xlight = gemini-3.8-flash-high`, `light = gemini-4-hydrogen-high`, `heavy = gemini-4-hydrogen-xhigh`, `ultra = gemini-4-carbon-high`, with `[clis.agy.review]` `light = gemini-4-neon-high` and `heavy = gemini-4-argon-high`, and `[activities.idea] all = "light"`), delegate mismatched-model activities from interactive `agy` sessions via `./zn dispatch <step> <id> --cli agy`, and mechanically guard `./zn` write/review lifecycle commands against wrong-model execution.

## Non-goals
- Implementing `opencode-cli` or `kimi` leftovers, which remain in `c0175`.
- Blocking plain human terminal commands when no AI session environment marker (`agentenv`) is set.
- Changing model aliases for `claude`, `grok`, `agent`, or `kimi` outside lowering `[activities.idea]` to `light`.

## Solution
- Auto-detect `agy` via `ANTIGRAVITY_CONVERSATION_ID` in `modelcmd.SESSION_VARS` and `agentenv.OWNERS` (clearing `ANTIGRAVITY_CONVERSATION_ID` and `ANTIGRAVITY_APP_DATA_DIR` in `fence.CLI_VARS`), add `sessions.agy` in `sessions.luau` and `sessionread.luau` to read live model IDs and `input_tokens + output_tokens` from `transcript.jsonl` (`models.SESSION_READ.agy = true`), and remove `ZN_MODEL` and `(declared)` across Luau, Go, specs, `AGENTS.md`, skills, and `.models.toml`.
- Add the `agy-cli` headless adapter (`agy -p <prompt> --output-format stream-json --model <model> --dangerously-skip-permissions`) in `adapters.luau` and `fakecli.luau`, configure the 5-family Gemini lineup in `.models.toml` (updating `models-pin` and `models-review-table` tooling specs), and instruct interactive `agy` sessions in `AGENTS.md` and `z-work` to delegate mismatched-model steps via `./zn dispatch <step> <id> --cli agy`.
- Add `z-nile/engine/modelguard.luau` so `status preparing`, `status prepared`, `commit`, and `finish` refuse interactive AI sessions whose live `SESSION_READ` model disagrees with `.models.toml`, and `status prepared` and `done` verify that required review files have a recorded spend row on `.models.toml`'s resolved reviewer model.
