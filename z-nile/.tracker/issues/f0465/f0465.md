# f0465: Pre-code approval and zn rework

## Problem
- Building `normal` and `risky` tickets optimistically before the owner approves their design risks wasting multi-million-token implementation and review runs when a design guess is rejected, and `./zn approve --apply` still lacks `./zn rework` (`f0423` steps 3-5) to send a `change` ruling back to `idea`, `design`, or `code`.

Decided with the user (2026-11-27): supersede earlier optimistic-all-the-way rulings (`c0267`, `f0417`, `f0420`) for `normal` and `risky` tickets by gating them on human design approval *before coding* (batchable via `./zn approve`), while keeping optimistic post-code guess-mode approval for `easy` tickets only, and deliver `./zn rework` under `z-nile/engine/sendback.luau` (avoiding `f0453`'s `rework.luau`).

## Non-goals
- Blocking `easy` tickets before coding or changing `f0453`'s shipped-defect rework metric module (`z-nile/engine/rework.luau`).

## Solution
- Gate `./zn ready`, `./zn work next`, and `./zn dispatch` so `normal` and `risky` designs wait in the `./zn approve` queue after `status prepared` until `approval.md` holds for the prepared contract, while `easy` tickets continue to ready and build optimistically and require approval at `./zn done`.
- Implement `./zn rework <issue-id> --stage idea|design|code` in `z-nile/engine/sendback.luau` and wire it into `./zn approve --apply` so confirmed `change` rulings automatically transition items back to the requested stage.
