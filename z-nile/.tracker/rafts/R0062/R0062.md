# R0062: Check guards

## Theme
Two z-nile chores that make the gate's own checks trustworthy before they act: **c0141** (Check paths touched from home: check-paths' Touched exemption compares a path's repository-relative form with what each Touched entry names, one convention per document, from its text) and **c0139** (Rename script refuses conflicts: writing `./zn` commands and c0115's tree-rewriting scripts refuse over unmerged paths and whole merge conflicts, and audit reports a committed one). Both guard checks against misreading the tree they judge. They share no Touched path (c0141: `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0120/{checkpaths.luau,check-paths.spec.luau}`, `z-nile/skills/z-design/SKILL.md`; c0139: `z-nile/engine/{conflicts,main,audit}.luau`, c0115's two experiment scripts, `z-nile/tests/tooling/repo-fence.spec.luau`). User decisions their designs quote: none (both chores were designed from their captures; c0139's capture is the user's request after c0115's rebase).

No module is touched by both members, so there is no shared interface to agree on.
- c0141 moves check-paths' scanner into `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0120/checkpaths.luau` (`repoPath`, `owned`, `scan`, …) and rewires c0120's promoted spec to it; no engine code.
- c0139 adds `z-nile/engine/conflicts.luau` (`outsideRepo`, `unmerged`, `marked`, `folders`, `refusal`), a dispatch guard in `main.luau` (`conflictOk`), audit's `conflict-markers` error, the scripts' guards, and repo-fence `ALLOWED` entries (conflicts.luau; the promoted conflicts.spec, before `done`: c0139 step 5).
