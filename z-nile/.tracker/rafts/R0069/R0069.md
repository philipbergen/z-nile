# R0069: Lessons backfill

## Theme
One member, **c0112** (Lessons backfill, size 1,400): every review finding already on `main` gets a harvest line naming the step that erred, the recurring ones become lessons, `design-first-pass-checklist`'s twelve points are seeded as notes in R0039's `harvest.md`, and whole-tree `lessons --uncovered` and `--unattributed` become a gate invariant. It is a raft by itself because it `depends_on` **c0136** (R0068): grouping it with unblocked chores would hold them behind R0068. Size 1,400 sits under the ~1,500 cap, so nothing else fits (c0160, 220 lines, would pass it and waits for a different raft). Scope decision quoted from c0112: "c0112 takes them" (2026-11-01): the backfill also harvests the 19 past stream review files (40 findings).

## Whole-raft design
Single member: no interface between members. Interfaces it takes:
- **From c0136 (R0068)**: the attribution rule ("The rule": `erred`/`upstream`, `DESIGN_STEPS`, `lessons.reviewPhase`), `lessons.parseHarvest` with the step group, `./zn lessons --check`, `--check-review <path>`, `--unattributed [<id>...]`, the stems `stream-review-<id>-NNN` and `focused-review-NNN`. c0112's workers, validator (`backfill-check.sh`) and gate spec (`z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0112/coverage.spec.luau`) call those.
- **To later rafts**: once R0069 lands, the gate fails any raft whose reviews are not all harvested and attributed, so every raft that lands after it (including this rafting's R0068 to R0077 coherence reviews, harvested by their leads under the rule of that date) must have clean ledgers.
- Shared files: `z-nile/engine/lessons.luau` (one pattern), the skills' lesson files and the `harvest.md` of every reviewed item (R0039's included). c0181 (R0074) also edits `lessons.luau` and the lesson set; its design lists c0112 as a reconcile item.

## Order & dependencies
One member. **Cross-raft: R0069 starts only after R0068 is done** (`depends_on: [c0136]`; z-ready enforces it). No other raft depends on it. Landing relative to R0074 (c0181) and R0072 is free in both orders (c0181's design says merges stay inside one skill and a merge never moves a lesson to another step); the executor merges `origin/main` before the straggler pass and reruns `batches.sh`, so items that landed in between are picked up.

## Experiments phase
None at raft level; the member's E1 to E10 ran in prep (c0112 Unknowns & experiments). The method's tools (`batches.sh`, `held.sh`, `backfill-check.sh`, `sources-kept.luau`) sit in the member's `experiments/` and `acceptance/` folders.

## Exit criteria
`./zn done R0069` succeeds: RC0 (z-nile's full gate, c0112's check.sh, `promote-check R0069`). The member's check.sh holds the whole-tree `--check`, `--uncovered` and `--unattributed` invariants, so the raft adds no RCn.

## Risks & rollback
- The backfill is large and mechanical (35 batches of review text, Sonnet workers, one merge pass per wave); a worker's attribution can be wrong. The validator (`backfill-check.sh`) and the sample judged in JC1 catch format and source loss, not every misjudgment; the code review reads a sample.
- Items held by an open PR or live worktree are skipped in a wave (`held.sh`, fail-closed), and a straggler pass takes what main added. The member's AC1 is the whole-tree invariant, so an item still held at the end is a stop, not a skip.
- c0136's open question about stream reviews is closed by the 2026-11-01 decision above; the executor needs c0136's engine already on `main`.
- Rollback: revert the PR (the ledgers are `.tracker` text; lessons are files). The gate invariant goes with it.
