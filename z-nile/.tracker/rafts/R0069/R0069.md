## Risks & rollback
- The backfill is large and mechanical (35 batches of review text, Sonnet workers, one merge pass per wave); a worker's attribution can be wrong. The validator (`backfill-check.sh`) and the sample judged in JC1 catch format and source loss, not every misjudgment; the code review reads a sample.
- Items held by an open PR or live worktree are skipped in a wave (`held.sh`, fail-closed), and a straggler pass takes what main added. The member's AC1 is the whole-tree invariant, so an item still held at the end is a stop, not a skip. **c0247 is held by this very raft** once it is readied (both members are under `active/`): the lead must not mistake that for another branch's hold; c0247's ledger goes in the last wave, and the one hold `held.sh` reports for it is its own raft.
- c0247's executor adds review files after c0112's last wave: its Phase 4/5 harvest and attribution must be done before `./zn done R0069`, or c0112's AC1 (and the full gate's coverage spec) is red. If c0247 runs first by accident, nothing breaks: its ledger is then complete and c0112's wave takes it as settled.
- c0256 is in re-prep (R0093 has no member): the fold adds a wait that c0112 did not have. c0247's pins name no file or flag of c0256 (its paragraph says "the reply `./zn dispatch retry` hands over"), so a redesign of c0256 does not change c0247; but the raft cannot be readied until R0093 is done. The unfold of c0247 is the fallback.
- c0136's open question about stream reviews is closed by the 2026-11-01 decision above; the executor needs c0136's engine already on `main`.
- c0260 (R0092) edits c0247's promoted `docs.spec` and the z-ticket block c0247 pins. That is safe only in the order above; the stop for R0092's lead is `./zn find c0247` showing `done/`.
- Rollback: revert the PR (the ledgers are `.tracker` text; lessons are files; c0247 is two text files). The gate invariant goes with it. c0247's pieces revert independently of c0112's (disjoint files), but reverting c0112 alone leaves c0247's ledger attributed, which is harmless.
