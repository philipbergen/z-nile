# R0070: Temp trees fixture identity

## Theme
Two z-nile chores that keep agents' scratch work out of the places the gate and the user's machine watch, both about where a prototype or experiment may live and both written in the same skills and AGENTS.md. Size 1,300 changed lines (c0194 850, c0189 450).
- **c0194** (Temp trees under repo): every temporary tree the tooling, the gate or an agent makes lives under `<checkout>/.cache/.tmp/`, never `$TMPDIR`; one maker (`z-nile/lib/tmp.luau`, `./zn tmp new|jail|verify|rm|sweep|leftovers`), a `SessionStart` hook that sets every session's `TMPDIR`, the runner's per-run folder inside it, and the merge gate's clone made isolated by `./zn tmp verify`.
- **c0189** (Real repository refuses fixture identity commits): a `reference-transaction` hook in the real repository's common hooks refuses a commit whose author or committer ends `@example.invalid`, installed by `./zn hooks` (run by `setup.sh`, checked in the gate's static stage and in the merge's clone); prototypes go in a scratch clone or commit under the user's own identity.
They share the surface `AGENTS.md`, `SETUP.md`, the skills `z-design`, `zz-execute`, `zz-test` and `zz-rules` (the prototype rule, the Merging step 2 sentence, the main-checkout command list), `z-nile/check.sh`, `z-nile/setup.sh`, `z-nile/engine/main.luau` and `z-nile/tests/tooling/repo-fence.spec.luau`.
User decisions their designs quote: c0194 "Jail the clone", "Make them work jailed", and **"c0194 first"** (2026-11-01: c0194 lands before R0039 / c0075; its executor edits c0075's sequestered `jail-argv.spec` and `linux-jail-script.spec` to add the `--ro-bind <cache>/.tmp` carve under the conditional Overrides line, a declared exception to zz-review code-mode item 2); c0189 none beyond its capture.

## Whole-raft design
The two features are independent in code (c0189's hook and `./zn hooks` live in `z-nile/lib/repo-hooks/` and `z-nile/engine/hooks.luau`; c0194's maker and commands in `z-nile/lib/tmp*.{luau,sh}`, the runner `fence.luau` and `fixture.luau`). The shared modules and what each adds:
- **`z-nile/engine/main.luau`**: c0189 adds the `hooks [--check]` command (usage line, `READ_ONLY`/main-checkout lists as its design says); c0194 adds a `tmp` command (pre-engine branch in `zn`, plus the dispatch hook) and moves the remap scratch worktree under `.cache/.tmp`. Different commands.
- **`z-nile/check.sh`**: c0189 adds `par_start "hooks (./zn hooks --check)" ./zn hooks --check` after the skills line and before `par_wait`, with its `--plan` line; c0194 makes the gate's temp folder through the shell maker and adds its own stage lines. Both are in the static stage: c0194 keeps c0189's `skills`-then-`hooks`-then-`par_wait` order (c0189's wiring.spec pins it).
- **`z-nile/setup.sh`**: c0189 runs `./zn hooks`; c0194 writes `env.TMPDIR` / installs the SessionStart hook and the `.cache/.tmp` marker. Separate lines.
- **`z-nile/tests/tooling/repo-fence.spec.luau`**: c0189 adds allow-list entries for its hook specs; c0194 edits its `TMPDIR` assertion and adds allowances for c0194's specs. Separate blocks; the second to land goes after the first.
- **`zz-rules` Merging step 2 (the merge's clone)**: c0189's `wiring.spec` pins `and \`git checkout --detach\` the exact head. Run \`./zn hooks\` in the clone ` before `Spawn the gate there`, and `a fresh clone has no hook of its own`; c0194's `docs.spec` pins `` `dir=$(./zn tmp new gate)` ``, `` `./zn tmp verify "$dir"` must print `isolated` ``, the clone, remote-removal and detach commands and `PICKMAN_FENCE_REAL_GIT`, and that `dir=$(mktemp -d)` and the parent-directories sentence are gone. - **The prototype rule (z-design experiments bullet, zz-execute Phase 1, zz-test, AGENTS.md, zz-rules)**: c0189's pinned sentence says a prototype lives "in a scratch clone (a `git clone` of the checkout into `$TMPDIR` or the scratchpad) or commits under your own identity" (z-design, zz-execute, zz-test) and that the z-design lesson `prove-specs-pass-on-a-prototype` reads "in a scratch clone or worktree". c0194's pinned definition, in AGENTS.md, zz-rules, z-design and zz-execute, is "scratch clone (never a `git worktree add`)" under `.cache/.tmp/` and "a prototype worktree goes under `.worktree/`". - **Two pinned sentences in one paragraph.** If the combined Merging step 2 or the prototype-rule text satisfies only one member's spec, the second lander's gate is red; the executor runs both members' `docs.spec`/`wiring.spec` after writing it (RC1).
- **c0194 edits another item's sequestered specs** (c0075's `jail-argv.spec` and `linux-jail-script.spec`) and its document, and adds dated notes under c0189.md and c0157.md. The first is declared on its conditional Overrides line; the note under c0189.md is an execution edit of a member of this raft (a dated note under the design, not a change of its text or specs). Review checks that nothing else in those items changed.
- **Both change what every session and every gate sees at start** (the SessionStart hook sets `TMPDIR`; the reference-transaction hook runs on every ref update of the real repository, including `./zn` publishes): a false refusal blocks tracker work, a stale hook is caught by `./zn hooks --check`. Rollback is reverting the PR and removing the installed hook with `./zn hooks` (c0189 design) / the SessionStart key.
- A tripwire that fires during the executor's gate because of a live `.worktree` prototype ref is retried once (memory/AGENTS: tripwire retry-once rule that c0189 itself writes).
