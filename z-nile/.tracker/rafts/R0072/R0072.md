- **c0189 waits for R0070 (c0194) and R0070 waits for R0078**: R0072 cannot be readied before then, and c0192 and c0193, which would be ready now, are held with it by the folding policy. The alternative (unfold c0189) is the lead's to take; this raft does not rely on it.
