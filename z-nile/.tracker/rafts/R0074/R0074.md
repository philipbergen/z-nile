One member, **c0181** (Lessons codified and consolidated, size 17,000, `solo: true`: a raft by itself and nothing is ever folded into it). ## Risks & rollback
- Largest raft (17,000 lines, run in waves, one skill group per wave): a long-lived branch that every other edit to lessons, skills and `main.luau` conflicts with. The executor merges `origin/main` at each wave and the raft's PR is not held open longer than the waves need.
- A codified lesson whose check is wrong turns the gate red for the wrong reason; every `enforced_by` must fail on a fixture that breaks its rule (the member's guard).
- Rollback: revert the PR; lesson files return to prose-only.
