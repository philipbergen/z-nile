## Risks & rollback
- The registry-driven reader runs against the real registry in the live case; with R0075 landed it resolves five more homes from the registry, so a regression that only walks the known roots would still be caught by the fixture third project.
- **c0123 is large and wide** (about 1,200 lines over about 70 files in its prototype: it rewrites the prologue of every entry script and edits about twenty specs). A launcher that misses the allow-list breaks every gate, so its executor runs the full gate once at the merge, in a clone (zz-rules Merging), with c0194's and c0189's code merged under it.
- **A stale installed hook turns the gate red everywhere**: after this raft lands the first line of `z-nile/lib/repo-hooks/reference-transaction` differs from every installed copy, and c0189's static stage (`./zn hooks --check`) reports it until a person runs `./zn hooks` once. c0123's PR text and report say so.
- **Order across three rafts** (R0062, R0070, R0072) is enforced only by `depends_on` at z-ready; a rename in c0189's `wiring.spec` or hook path before then turns c0123's two override edits into misses, and the executor re-reads both files on `main`.
- c0123's design says R0072's c0189 hook stays `#!/bin/sh` there; if c0189's executor changes the first line itself, c0123's edit is already in place and its override reduces to `wiring.spec` (either way the executor follows the file as it is on `main`).
- Rollback: revert the PR; c0141's check-paths is unchanged by it. A revert of c0123 also reverts the two edits to c0189's hook and `wiring.spec`; a person re-runs `./zn hooks` to reinstall the `#!/bin/sh` copy.
