# R0093: Pipeline base for back-and-forth reviews

## Theme
Seven z-nile chores stacked in one raft, one layer per issue, regrouped on 2026-11-18 at the owner's request ("make a raft with 417 and its dependents"). They are the prepared chores that the feature f0417 ("Back-and-forth reviews, idea to merge ready", in design, not a member: one type per raft) builds on: f0417's Problem names c0260's idea loop, c0262's final review and c0250's blocked state and `./zn resolve`, and says it "does not redesign what c0260, c0262, c0250 ... already design or deliver". f0417 becomes its own feature raft later.

The pipeline these layers build, bottom to top:
- **Stacked delivery**: c0248 "Stacked PR delivery of a raft" (1600): a layer is complete only when reviewed, the review rules live in one pure `completion.luau`, and a raft lands as one bind or by `done --through`.
- **Restack keeps work**: c0300 "Restack drops unfinished layer commits" (320): `./zn restack` stops dropping a layer's unfinished commits (c0256 `depends_on` it).
- **Answers reach the re-run step**: c0256 "Dispatch retry passes answer" (1250): `./zn dispatch retry --answer`, the dated `exchange/` folder.
- **Confirm intent**: c0247 "Ticket confirms intent" (30): z-ticket restates the request before it creates anything.
- **Idea interview and loop**: c0260 "Capture interview and idea review" (1860): `## Ask (verbatim)`, `## Interview`, `## Confirmation`, and an idea review that loops (at most three challenges).
- **Limited-view final review**: c0262 "Full force final review" (750): once per ticket, `./zn final <id>` and a reviewer that sees only a fixed view (ask, design, contract, diff), verdict `on-course` or `off-course`.
- **Blocked state and resolve**: c0250 "Escalation to the human: blocked state and resolve" (2200): a `blocked` status, `escalation.md`, `./zn block` and `./zn resolve`.
