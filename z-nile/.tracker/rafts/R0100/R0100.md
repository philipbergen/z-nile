# R0100: Heartbeat

## Theme
Five chores that give a claim a pulse and make the lost work resumable. They are one design that the user had the prep split on 2026-11-08 (user: "yes go ahead and split the whole thing up and tie them togetjer in a raft so we can run through these a layer at a time in a PR"), so they are grouped by argument (`./zn new raft chore heartbeat c0265 c0274 c0275 c0276 c0277 --project z-nile`) and the total size (780 + 400 + 720 + 200 + 1,100, about 3,200 changed lines) is over the rafting cap on purpose: the user's grouping overrides it, and the raft is delivered a layer at a time, one ticket per layer.

| layer | member | title | what it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | c0265 | Work heartbeat and lost items | the heartbeat branch `hb/<claim stamp>`, `./zn beat`, the verdicts (live, stale, parked, lost, never beaten), `./zn lost`, the `./zn status` markers, lost work first |
| 2 | c0274 | Take over lost claim | the next claim of a lost item takes it over (atomic branch swap, claim memory `.cache/tracker-stamp-<id>`, `beat --adopt`), and the old owner is told and refused |
| 3 | c0275 | Automatic heartbeats | first beat at every claim, beats on every `./zn` command in a worktree, during long commands, from the dispatcher, and a background beater |
| 4 | c0276 | Board shows lost work | each claim's verdict on the board's cards, rows and headings |
| 5 | c0277 | Discard and recover worktree | a takeover renames the old worktree aside, the taker works in a numbered copy, and unpushed work is listed for recovery |

The surface they share: `z-nile/engine/heartbeat.luau` (c0265 creates it; c0274, c0275 and c0277 add to it), `z-nile/engine/main.luau` (all five), `z-nile/engine/dispatcher.luau` (c0265, c0275, c0277), `z-nile/engine/board.luau` and `board.html` (c0276 only), the skills `zz-rules`, `z-design`, `z-raft`, `z-run`, `z-stop` and `zz-execute`, `z-nile/README.md` and `z-nile/tests/lib/cachepaths.luau`.

## Risks & rollback
- **A clean merge that is wrong: two `local hb`** (layer 4 on layers 1 to 3). c0276's prototype forward-declares `local hb = {} :: {...}`; c0265 already declares `hb` earlier in `main.luau`. The executor of the board layer does not add a declaration: `hb` exists above `buildBoard`, and `hb.boardVerdicts` is defined on it with `function hb.boardVerdicts`. A second declaration silences every beat (callers `pcall`) and only the real-time proofs go red. RC2 counts the declarations.
- **A clean merge that is wrong: inlined requires** (layer 1 on main). c0265 inlines `sessionread` and `sessions` to free locals; main's z-work uses both in five places. The executor of layer 1 keeps those two requires as locals (stylua order: after `reapLib`), which leaves ten spare locals for the layers above; the lint and types stages catch any it misses.
- **A prototype predates main.** Three of the five prototypes (and the stack) predate the z-work change and the dispatcher's trail change. The stack was merged with current `origin/main` and checked (Experiments phase); the executor does the same merge by hand at each layer, using the resolutions recorded there.
- **Stopped and readied again, before the worktree layer lands (design-review-001 F1).** After a `./zn stop` and a new `./zn ready` the old raft worktree still remembers the old stamp, so `--adopt` and `done` are refused in it. Layer 5's re-prep makes the lookup return a numbered copy; in layers 2 to 4 the shim and zz-execute tell the session to *remove its old worktree* (`git worktree remove`, z-stop step 4's start-fresh path) before `cd "$(./zn worktree <R-id>)"`. RC3 pins `remove its old worktree`.
- **z-run is a shim.** The members pin phrases in `z-run/SKILL.md` that the long `z-run` used to hold. Resolution: the executor writes a short paragraph into the shim that carries them, and the matching text into z-work's step 10 ("Heartbeats and taking over a raft someone else holds are c0265" now says what exists); no spec changes. Whether `./zn work next` itself takes lost work first, and takes a lost raft over, is not in any member: the worker loop (the item that already depends on layers 1 to 3) owns it. Until then a person takes lost work with `./zn lost`, `./zn stop`, `./zn ready` and `cd "$(./zn worktree --takeover <R-id>)"` (the skills say so). Flagged for the user in Open to the human.
- **The top-level local cap** (Luau's 200) is shared with the provenance raft (Interfaces). Measured on the stack plus main (Experiments phase): `origin/main` alone has 4 spare top-level locals, the stack has 10, so landing the heartbeat raft first gives the provenance raft more room, and landing it second costs the heartbeat raft nothing (its layers net free locals).
- **Real-time proofs under load.** The members' real-time proofs (`proofs/race`, `proofs/beater`, `proofs/long`, `proofs/dispatch`, `proofs/discard`) fail when five checks run at once, and pass alone. Run them one at a time and bound them with `./zn run`.
- **The slow fixtures.** `takeover.spec` takes about two minutes and several others one; the affected gate runs the whole z-nile suite. Not a risk to correctness; a cost to every layer's gate.
- **Layer 5 and the `.worktree` of the executor.** `worktree --takeover` renames directories under `.worktree/`; no check may do it in the real repository (the members' specs use fixtures under `.cache`).
- **Rollback:** each layer is one commit on its own stack PR. Revert the top layer first; layers 1 and 2 stand alone, layer 3 needs 1 and 2, layer 4 needs 1, layer 5 needs 2 and 3. A claim made while the heartbeat ran is an ordinary stamped claim after a revert (its `hb/*` branch is harmless; `git push origin :refs/heads/hb/<stamp>` removes it, and the pruner removes unclaimed ones). `.cache/tracker-*` files are ignored.
