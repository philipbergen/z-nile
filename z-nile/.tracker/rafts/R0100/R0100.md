# R0100: Heartbeat

## Theme
Five chores that give a claim a pulse and make the lost work resumable. They are one design that the user had the prep split on 2026-11-08 (user: "yes go ahead and split the whole thing up and tie them togetjer in a raft so we can run through these a layer at a time in a PR"), so they are grouped by argument (`./zn new raft chore heartbeat c0265 c0274 c0275 c0276 c0277 --project z-nile`) and the total size (780 + 400 + 720 + 200 + 1,200, about 3,300 changed lines) is over the rafting cap on purpose: the user's grouping overrides it, and the raft is delivered a layer at a time, one ticket per layer.

| layer | member | title | what it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | c0265 | Work heartbeat and lost items | the heartbeat branch `hb/<claim stamp>`, `./zn beat`, the verdicts (live, stale, parked, lost, never beaten), `./zn lost`, the `./zn status` markers, lost work first |
| 2 | c0274 | Take over lost claim | the next claim of a lost item takes it over (atomic branch swap, claim memory `.cache/tracker-stamp-<id>`, `beat --adopt`), and the old owner is told and refused |
| 3 | c0275 | Automatic heartbeats | first beat at every claim, beats on every `./zn` command in a worktree, during long commands, from the dispatcher, and a background beater |
| 4 | c0276 | Board shows lost work | each claim's verdict on the board's cards, rows and headings |
| 5 | c0277 | Discard and recover worktree | a takeover renames the old worktree aside, the taker works in a numbered copy, unpushed work is listed for recovery, and the plain lookup returns a numbered copy to a session that stopped and readied its raft again |

The surface they share: `z-nile/engine/heartbeat.luau` (c0265 creates it; c0274, c0275 and c0277 add to it), `z-nile/engine/main.luau` (all five), `z-nile/engine/dispatcher.luau` (c0265, c0275, c0277), `z-nile/engine/board.luau` and `board.html` (c0276 only), the skills `zz-rules`, `z-design`, `z-raft`, `z-run`, `z-stop`, `z-work` (layer 5) and `zz-execute`, `z-nile/README.md` and `z-nile/tests/lib/cachepaths.luau`.

## Risks & rollback
- **A clean merge that is wrong: two `local hb`** (layer 4 on layers 1 to 3). c0276's prototype forward-declares `local hb = {} :: {...}`; c0265 already declares `hb` earlier in `main.luau`. The executor of the board layer does not add a declaration: `hb` exists above `buildBoard`, and `hb.boardVerdicts` is defined on it with `function hb.boardVerdicts`. A second declaration silences every beat (callers `pcall`) and only the real-time proofs go red. RC2 counts the declarations.
- **A clean merge that is wrong: inlined requires** (layer 1 on main). c0265 inlines `sessionread` and `sessions` to free locals; main's z-work uses both in five places. The executor of layer 1 keeps those two requires as locals (stylua order: after `reapLib`), which leaves ten spare locals for the layers above; the lint and types stages catch any it misses.
- **A prototype predates main.** Three of the five prototypes (and the stack) predate the z-work change and the dispatcher's trail change. The stack was merged with current `origin/main` and checked (Experiments phase); the executor does the same merge by hand at each layer, using the resolutions recorded there.
- **Stopped and readied again, before the worktree layer lands (design-review-001 F1).** In layers 2 to 4 the old raft worktree still remembers the old stamp after a `./zn stop` and a new `./zn ready`, so `--adopt` and `done` are refused in it. This text ships only if layer 5 is unfolded (the stack lands in one merge, so no `main` has layers 2 to 4 without layer 5), and unfolding it reopens round 1's F1. Then the shim and zz-execute tell the session: (1) `git worktree remove <old path>` (the memory goes with it; keep the branch and the PR), (2) `cd "$(./zn worktree <R-id>)"`, (3) `./zn beat <R-id> --adopt`, (4) z-stop's keep path (`start` on the branch); not z-stop's start-fresh path, which closes the PR and deletes the branch. Layer 5 replaces that sentence: its lookup discards the stale worktree and returns the numbered copy (its `reready.spec` runs the whole path), so the sentence is not pinned in the final state.
- **z-run is a shim.** The members pin phrases in `z-run/SKILL.md` that the long `z-run` used to hold. Resolution: the executor writes a short paragraph into the shim that carries them, and the matching text into z-work's step 10 ("Heartbeats and taking over a raft someone else holds are c0265" now says what exists); no spec changes. Whether `./zn work next` itself takes lost work first, and takes a lost raft over, is not in any member: the worker loop (the item that already depends on layers 1 to 3) owns it. Until then a person takes lost work with `./zn lost`, `./zn stop`, `./zn ready` and `cd "$(./zn worktree --takeover <R-id>)"` (the skills say so). Not a user decision: the coordinator ruled so when round 1 raised it, for the reasons in Experiments (the user's split leaves the rank to its own item).
- **The top-level local cap** (Luau's 200) is shared with the provenance raft (Interfaces). Measured on the stack plus main (Experiments phase): `origin/main` alone has 4 spare top-level locals, the stack has 10, so landing the heartbeat raft first gives the provenance raft more room, and landing it second costs the heartbeat raft nothing (its layers net free locals).
- **Real-time proofs under load.** The members' real-time proofs (`proofs/race`, `proofs/beater`, `proofs/long`, `proofs/dispatch`, `proofs/discard`) fail when five checks run at once, and pass alone. Run them one at a time and bound them with `./zn run`.
- **Existing worktrees on the day layer 5 lands (design-review-002 F7).** The first plain `./zn worktree <name>` of an existing worktree whose remembered claim ended is a discard: it is renamed `-discarded-<n>` and a numbered copy is returned. `cm.ids` also counts c0116's `tracker-claim-<id>` files, which main's engine writes at `new raft`, `fold`, `recombine`, `start` and `status <id> preparing` and removes at an issue's `prepared` but not at a raft's rafting end, so an existing rafting worktree is discarded on its first lookup. That is the user's rule; the executor of layer 5 lists the active worktrees and their owners in the PR. c0277's own text does not say so (its checklist (1) and Existing work pre-date the re-prep, and `staleMember` and `isMember` are missing from its pure list); a raft cannot edit a member, so the note lives here.
- **The slow fixtures.** `takeover.spec` takes about two minutes and several others one; the affected gate selects the fast tier only (the slow tier is the Experiments phase's "slow tier" paragraph). Not a risk to correctness; a cost to every layer's gate.
- **Layer 5 and the `.worktree` of the executor.** `worktree --takeover` renames directories under `.worktree/`; no check may do it in the real repository (the members' specs use fixtures under `.cache`).
- **Rollback:** after a revert of layer 5, discarded worktrees and numbered copies stay on disk with their markers: use the copies by path and remove the `-discarded-<n>` worktrees by hand after reading `./zn reap`'s list (a prep branch checked out in `prep-x-1` makes a plain `./zn worktree prep-x` refuse with `already used by worktree`). Each layer is one commit on its own stack PR. Revert the top layer first; layers 1 and 2 stand alone, layer 3 needs 1 and 2, layer 4 needs 1, layer 5 needs 2 and 3. A claim made while the heartbeat ran is an ordinary stamped claim after a revert (its `hb/*` branch is harmless; `git push origin :refs/heads/hb/<stamp>` removes it, and the pruner removes unclaimed ones). `.cache/tracker-*` files are ignored.
