# R0107: Refuse reviews on cheap models

## Theme
One issue, rafted alone (2026-11-15, the backlog rafting): c0306 "Commands refuse a review off the review models", a chore in z-nile's engine. It has no unfinished dependency and nothing prepared shares a PR with it, so the raft's theme is the issue's own: a review record counts only when it ran on a review model (one fixed list of four id patterns in the tooling's code, `claude-opus-`, `claude-fable-`, `kimi-code-k3`, and `grok-` at effort `xhigh`), and `./zn status <id> prepared`, `./zn ready <R-id>` and `./zn done <R-id>` refuse an item whose design, coherence, layer or final review is not on one. An item with no `reviews: judged` mark is exempt, and a stream review is never judged.

User decisions its design quotes are in its Problem and Solution (prep Q1 to Q3) and its design review's F3 ruling; its `## Open to the human` is "none".
