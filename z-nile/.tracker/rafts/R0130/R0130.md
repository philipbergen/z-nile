- **Approvals that no longer match** after PR #587: c0250 and c0394 (re-prepared), to be re-approved by the person before their rafts land; c0267 and f0339 were never approved. c0250 also meets f0399 on main in its own `work-board.spec` (a ready refusal f0399 removed outside the dispatcher); that is c0250's to resolve, not this raft's.
- **A moved base**: other z-nile rafts change `z-nile/engine/main.luau` often; the prototype patch may not apply as cut; the executor then follows the Implementation steps by hand, and stops with `questions.md` only when a rule of the Design can no longer hold against the code on main.
- **Rollback**: revert the merge; approve returns to the two commands and f0399's `land.spec` to its old assertion. `approval.md` files written meanwhile stay valid, since what an approval records does not change.
