# R0158: Findings stay in their raft

## Theme
Two z-nile chores that share a surface: the engine's `main.luau` and `work.luau`, the issue template `templates/issue/doc.md`, the `z-design`, `zz-execute` and `zz-review` skills, and the `z-nile/tests/acceptance/c0043` specs they pin.

- **c0394 Findings stay in their raft**: a finding is fixed in its raft (small), added to it as an issue that waits for approval (large) or becomes a lesson; no skill tells an agent to open a backlog ticket for one. User decisions it quotes (2026-11-16): build on c0248's `done --through`; keep it one issue; the added issue goes in front of the layer whose review found it by default; design it with guesses and wait for approval.
- **c0460 Behavioral acceptance and build mutants**: z-nile acceptance specs reach the engine only through `./zn` and `lune run`; the prep-time mutation check is replaced by a `mutants.md` the executor writes against the built code, which `./zn finish` and code review check.

The two are different problems that the policy grouped on shared Touched paths (the raft is `single: false`); neither needs the other's code.

No module is changed in a way the other depends on. Where both touch a file:

## Risks & rollback
- **Skill text collides**: both edit `zz-execute`, `zz-review` and `z-design`. Mitigation: c0394 first; c0460 re-reads the skill files before editing; the c0043 and c0111 skill-doc specs catch a lost pin.
- **Cross-raft dependencies** (c0248, c0458) land late: the raft stays unready until they are done; `./zn ready` refuses it.
- **c0460's template line arms every new ticket**: its fixture handling in `tests/lib/engine.luau` is part of c0460 and must not be dropped when rebasing.
- **Rollback**: each member is one layer and one revertable commit range; reverting c0460 leaves tickets with a `Mutants:` line owed nothing, reverting c0394 restores the Emergent issues rule.
