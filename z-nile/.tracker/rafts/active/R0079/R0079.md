## Risks & rollback
- A hook that refuses valid commands blocks every agent session; a reaper that stops the wrong process kills work. Both are bounded by the member's decisions (the session's own tree by ancestry; unowned processes outside the repository's scope never touched) and its specs. Rollback: revert the PR and remove the hook keys from `.claude/settings.json`.
- Shared-file merges with R0070, R0073, R0074, R0075, R0080 (above). Land R0079 after R0070 to keep the settings merge simple.
- `Bash(./zn run:*)` in `permissions.allow` is a user decision; the settings edit is the one step prep could not make.
- A known gap, stated in c0191 (E18): `ulimit -t` does not stop a syscall-heavy loop; the wall-clock limit, the watchdog and the reaper are the other lines.
