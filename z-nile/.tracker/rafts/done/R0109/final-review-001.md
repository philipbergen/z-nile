- **A note for z-design/rerun-skipped-checks-after-edits, for build.** A spec added in answer to a code review is type-checked only by the gate and `promote-check`. The runner and `./zn finish` do not do it. The lesson already records a two-value `string.find` passed to `t.expect` in c0115 and in c0118, both in prep. Five recorded cases of this raft have a comma in the name and a bare `it(` line, so they cannot be cited by name at all: the Phase 5 case of docs.spec, the three cases of `tests/tooling/trace.spec.luau` and the first case of trace-skeleton.spec.
