---
title: Only the user narrows captured scope
when: design
tags: [scope, process]
check: Does every outcome in the issue's capture (`./zn capture <id>`: its title, Problem and Solution ideas) still have a Design and an acceptance criterion (Checked, or Judged or Observed only where the zz-test skill allows it), and is every outcome removed, deferred or reworded away backed by a `Scope decision (user, …)` or `Decided with the user (…)` line? And does any **(prep)** choice (an answer to a question the capture left open) leave one of the Problem's outcomes only partly true, which needs a `Decided with the user` line instead of the marker? And before designing, does the document's Problem, Non-goals and Solution equal what `./zn capture <id>` prints, and when a later PR rewrote them (a "recaptured" commit or an idea-stage rerun, not a `tracker: new` capture), does a user decision cover each outcome the rewrite drops? And is any `Guess (pending user, …)` line that keeps part of a captured outcome from showing (a line under one tab only, a card left on LOADING until visited) either redesigned so the outcome holds, or put under `## Open to the human`, since a Guess is not a Scope decision?
---
The capture (title, Problem and the user's Solution ideas) is the scope. Prep adds the Design and criteria and may add detail, but it never rewrites, narrows, defers or drops an outcome the capture asks for. When an outcome is too hard, too big, or blocked by today's code, the options go to the user, and the answer is written into the Problem with its date and words. A captured scope too big for one raft is split into sequenced rafts, not shrunk.

**Why:** R0042 (z-nile, 2026-10-20) lost most of its captured scope over five design-review rounds. The move into `z-nile/` became a frontmatter tag, the skill rename became copies, the dispatcher a dry run, and the board lost its live data. Each cut began as a reviewer's recommended fix, was accepted in a refute, and was then written back into the Problem so that the design matched. The user had asked for all of it and was never asked.

**How to apply:** before each round and before `design: ready`, compare each item's document with `./zn capture <id>`: only added detail and decision lines may appear in the title, Problem and Solution ideas, and captured Solution bullets stay as they are. An earlier prep's landed rewrite is not scope: restore it, or get a decision. For a finding whose only fix is a cut, ask the user before answering it, with the options and what each costs.

R0044 design review 4 (F2): a design that meets a captured outcome except in rare failure modes (a write that fails for good, an old build) must say so in Risks and name the option that would close the gap for the user, rather than leave the limit unstated or reword the outcome.

c0118 design review 001 (F4): when a Scope decision moves outcomes to another issue, the findings and recommendations that came with them (a programme review's, a reviewer's) must move too, into that issue's document, through a capture `land`; a line in the source item's answers ("c0121's prep must take F3 whole") reaches no one, because the next prep starts from the other issue's document.
**Also:** f0147 design review 001 F1: the capture left "how the rounding keeps each level strictly faster" open for prep; prep answered that two slow ghost speeds may stay level for two levels and marked it (prep), which made "every level is 5% faster for the ghosts" only partly true. The user chose "Carry fractions" once asked.

**Note (c0121 design review 014 F2):** a reviewer can find, rounds after a design was approved, that an outcome the capture asked for ("click a card for ... its PR, its checks") was met for one kind of card only (rafts, not issues). That is a scope gap, not a detail to quietly close or cut: ask the user which way (here: add prep PRs), record the answer as a `Decided with the user` line, and build it. Ask: for each noun of a captured outcome (card, item, worktree, PR), does the design cover every kind of that noun the board shows?

**Note** (c0330 design review 001 F1): an idea-stage finding answered `revised` had narrowed the captured outcome "a raft already readied ... passes" to "gets a skeleton with no Solution refusal" and moved the rest into a Non-goal, with no `Scope decision`. A reviewer's recommendation answered `revised` is still not a decision; at prep, compare the Solution's bullets with `./zn capture` and either restore the outcome, as a prep bullet that proves what the change can prove, or ask the user. (Open question for z-idea: it calls the idea stage's outcomes the scope, while z-design measures against `./zn capture`.)

**Note** (d0295 design review 001 F2): a regression found in prep (c0067 readies a cyclic raft on purpose) made the first revision judge only the Order's text in every command, narrowing "status prepared refuses an Order start would refuse" with no decision line. The capture named one command, and that command could keep the whole outcome (c0067 never passes its raft through it); the narrowing belonged only to the commands the capture did not name. Ask: when a regression forces a narrower rule, does it apply only where the regression is, and is the captured command still held to the captured outcome?

**Note** (c0278 design review 001 F1): a guess written in the user-decision form (`Scope decision (user, …)`, its text saying "GUESS … NOT YET CONFIRMED") passes the line test while no user decided anything, and every later reader (z-raft's Theme, `./zn capture`, the final review) takes it for a ruling. A guess that drops a captured outcome is labelled as a guess, and goes under `## Open to the human`, so `prepared` and `ready` refuse until it is ruled. Check: is each `Scope decision (user, …)` line the user's own words, and not a guess recorded under their name?

**Note** (c0392 design review 001 F1): PR #526 rewrote c0392's Problem, Non-goals and Solution after c0210 split the spec c0392 narrows. Its subject was "tracker: c0392 recaptured …", and it reran the idea stage, but there was no user decision. `./zn capture` still printed the original. The rewrite turned the original's kept assertions (the start place's ids, the publish order, c0210's `check.sh` unchanged) into a Non-goal and a deletion. The prep designed the rewrite, and its Guess called the captured outcome "a scope change". Compare the document with `./zn capture` before designing. A difference that no `Scope decision` covers goes under `## Open to the human`, with both options worked out.

**Also:** f0399 design review 001 F6 and F7 (2026-11-16): a word in a captured list is an outcome too. "design it, fix it, raft it, or implement it" had no design for "fix it", and "furthest along first" was designed without the stages that are already started (a held raft, a design under way), behind a guess that the design's own rule said may never narrow scope. Read each captured sentence word by word against the action list before the review.

Two captures that disagree are the user's (c0250 design review 006 F1): when the item's captured outcome ("a worker never guesses") meets another landed item's captured rule (f0399's guess mode), a guess may pick neither: put both lines and the options under `## Open to the human`, and build on the one the design recommends only as a guess the approval waits on.
