---
title: A prep the owner directs to depart from a skill's text records the direction where the reviewer reads it
when: design
tags: [process, review-rounds, owner]
check: When the owner directs this prep to run a step differently from the skill's text (more review rounds, a step skipped), are the owner's words quoted in the issue's Design and in a harvest note, and does nothing outside the issue change the skill to match?
---
A prep sometimes runs a step differently from the skill on the owner's word, before any issue has changed the skill. Without the owner's words in the document, the reviewer and the next reader see only a prep that broke its skill.

**Why:** f0417 design review 001 F17: f0417's prep ran its design review as a loop of rounds while z-design step 5 still said one round, on the owner's direction relayed in the brief; the first harvest note named a lesson about restated rules, which did not fit.

**How to apply:** quote the owner's words (with the date and who relayed them) in the Design's sources, add a harvest note naming this lesson, and leave the skill's text to the issue that changes it.
