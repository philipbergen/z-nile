---
title: A bar set for one pair of kinds is checked for every pair
when: design
tags: [colour, coordination, measurement]
check: When another prep sets a measurable bar between two kinds (a ghost colour against a wall colour, at 30 CIE76), does every member of each kind meet it, or does each failing pair have a decision from the user? Does it cover every look a member can take (a ghost's frightened blue and blink white), not only its usual colour?
---
A prep that asks for one pair to be checked (gold against Inky) names the pair it was looking at, not the rule. The same bar applies to every pair of those kinds, and a pair argued away in the design text is a decision taken without the user.

**Why:** f0126 design review 011 F1: f0109 rejected a yellow Inky at 23.1 from the marks' gold, against a bar of 30; f0126 checked Inky only, and Clyde's orange stood at 23.6 from the same gold, with a written argument instead of a decision. The user chose a brighter gold (255, 215, 0) that clears every ghost.

**How to apply:** compute the bar for every member of each kind before writing the check; check every member in the spec (iterate the table, not one entry); put each failing pair to the user with options and their numbers.

f0126 design review 012 F3: the distance case covered the four ghost bodies but not the frightened or blink looks; a frightened colour moved next to the gold survived.
