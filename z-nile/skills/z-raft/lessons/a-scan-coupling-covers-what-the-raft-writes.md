The skills folders hold the lessons, and the raft's lead writes lessons on the branch while harvesting reviews, so a phrase scan over `skills/` also judges text no member's design lists.

**Why:** R0061 design review 002 F4 (2026-11-19): the regrouped raft's document said c0137's check ran two finished criteria and named two bans. It runs three (c0044 AC9, c0067 AC10, R0025 RC1), which between them ban more phrases, a skill folder name, and require text to be present; they read both skills folders whole. c0137's own prep had once found a banned phrase in a lesson. c0137's step for a red check says "main changed", which would have sent the executor to blame main for a phrase the raft's own lesson wrote.

**How to apply:** for each member whose check scans a folder, write in Whole-raft design every criterion it runs, what each bans and requires, and the folders read; name every text the raft will write there, the harvested lessons among them; and say that a red is checked against the raft's own diff of those folders before main is blamed.
